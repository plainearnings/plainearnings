How it works

1

We read the filing

Every number comes from the company's official earnings release filed with the SEC — never summaries, never estimates.

2

We chart the money

Revenue flows in on the left; costs flow out; profit is what's left. Hover any block to trace that dollar's whole path. If the numbers don't balance, the page doesn't ship.

3

You get the story

A one-sentence verdict, the handful of numbers that matter, and three takeaways — in plain English, in about two minutes.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+19%

AWS kept accelerating

AWS sales grew 19% to $27.5B and the segment earned $10.4B of operating profit — 60% of Amazon's total — as companies moved more computing, and new AI work, onto its cloud.

$17.4B

Operating income jumped 56%

Consolidated operating income rose 56% from a year ago: North America earned $5.7B and International swung from a small loss to a $1.3B profit as the store networks got more efficient.

$158.9B

Stores grew steadily into the holidays

Store sales grew 9% in North America and 12% internationally, helped by record-fast delivery speeds and a record Prime Big Deal Days event in October.

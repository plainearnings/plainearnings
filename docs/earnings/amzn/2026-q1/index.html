Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

AWS is re-accelerating

AWS sales grew 28% to $37.6B — the fastest in 15 quarters — and produced $14.2B of operating income: 59% of Amazon's total profit from 21% of its sales. Amazon's own chips business passed a $20B annual run rate.

$16.8B

Anthropic gains did heavy lifting in profit

Net income of $30.3B includes $16.8B of pre-tax paper gains on Amazon's stake in Anthropic. The stores and cloud operations themselves produced $23.9B of operating income.

$1.2B

AI building is swallowing the cash flow

Free cash flow for the last twelve months fell to $1.2B from $25.9B a year earlier, because spending on property and equipment jumped $59.3B year over year — mostly AI data centers — even as operating cash flow rose 30% to $148.5B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax gains of $16.8B on Amazon's investment in Anthropic are included in non-operating income — the main reason net income ($30.3B) is so much larger than operating income ($23.9B).

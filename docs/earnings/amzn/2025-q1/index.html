Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

AWS keeps carrying the profit

AWS sales grew 17% to $29.3B and the unit earned $11.5B — almost two-thirds of Amazon's total operating income comes from the cloud.

+64%

Profit grew seven times faster than sales

Net income jumped 64% to $17.1B, helped by $2.7B of other income below the operating line, on top of a 20% rise in operating income.

$1.4B

Currency masked faster growth

Reported sales rose 9%, but shifting exchange rates cut $1.4B off the total — without that drag, sales would have grown 10%.

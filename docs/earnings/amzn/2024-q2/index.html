Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+19%

AWS kept accelerating

AWS sales grew 19% to $26.3B and the segment earned $9.3B of operating profit — 64% of Amazon's total — as companies moved more computing, and new AI work, onto its cloud.

$14.7B

Operating income nearly doubled

Consolidated operating income rose 91% from a year ago: North America earned $5.1B and International swung from a $0.9B loss to a $0.3B profit as the store networks got more efficient.

$13.5B

Profit doubled

Net income doubled to $13.5B from $6.7B a year ago; about $0.4B of it was a paper gain on Amazon's investment in electric-truck maker Rivian, recorded before tax.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $0.4B pre-tax paper gain on Amazon's common-stock investment in Rivian Automotive sits inside non-operating income — it is a revaluation of shares Amazon owns, not money from customers.

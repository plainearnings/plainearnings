Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

AWS growth picked up again

AWS sales grew 20% to $33.0B and produced $11.4B of operating income — two-thirds of Amazon's total profit from 18% of its sales.

$4.3B

One-off charges in the stores business

Operating income includes a $2.5B legal settlement with the Federal Trade Commission and $1.8B of severance for planned role eliminations. Without them, operating income would have been $21.7B instead of a flat $17.4B.

$14.8B

The AI build-out swallows the cash

Free cash flow for the last twelve months fell to $14.8B, from $47.7B, because spending on property and equipment jumped $50.9B year over year — mostly AI. Operating cash flow still grew 16% to $130.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating income includes two special charges totaling $4.3B: a $2.5B legal settlement with the Federal Trade Commission and $1.8B in estimated severance costs, primarily for planned role eliminations. Excluding these charges, operating income would have been $21.7B.
  • Net income includes a $9.5B pre-tax gain from the revaluation of Amazon's investment in Anthropic, recorded within other income.

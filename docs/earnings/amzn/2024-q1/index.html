Amazon — Q1 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

AWS reaccelerated

AWS sales grew 17% to $25.0B — a $100B annual revenue run rate, per CEO Andy Jassy — and the segment earned $9.4B of operating profit at a 37.6% margin, 62% of Amazon's total, as companies renewed infrastructure modernization and bought AI work on its cloud.

$15.3B

Operating income more than tripled

Consolidated operating income rose 221% from a year ago: North America earned $5.0B (up from $0.9B) and International swung from a $1.2B loss to a $0.9B profit as the store networks got more efficient.

$10.4B

Profit tripled despite the Rivian drag

Net income rose to $10.4B from $3.2B a year ago even though $2.0B of it was wiped out by a paper loss on Amazon's investment in electric-truck maker Rivian, recorded before tax. Free cash flow over the trailing twelve months swung to a $50.1B inflow from a $3.3B outflow.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $2.0B pre-tax paper loss on Amazon's common-stock investment in Rivian Automotive sits inside non-operating expense — it is a revaluation of shares Amazon owns, not money lost to customers. A year ago the same line held a $0.5B loss.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+19%

AWS growth re-accelerated

AWS sales grew 19% to $28.8B and the segment earned $10.6B of operating profit — half of Amazon's total — as companies kept moving computing, and new AI work, onto its cloud.

$21.2B

Operating income jumped 60%

Consolidated operating income jumped 60% from a year ago: North America earned $9.3B and International swung from a loss to a $1.3B profit as the regional store networks got more efficient.

$187.8B

A record holiday quarter

The holiday quarter set a sales record at $187.8B, up 10%, and net income nearly doubled to $20.0B — about 11 cents of every sales dollar stayed as profit.

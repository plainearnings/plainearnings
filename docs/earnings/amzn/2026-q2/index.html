Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+37%

AWS is re-accelerating

AWS sales grew 37% to $42.2B — the fastest in 18 quarters, a $169B annual run rate — and produced $16.6B of operating profit, 61% of Amazon's total.

$53.4B

Most of the 'profit' is a paper gain

Net income of $62.6B includes $53.4B of non-operating income, primarily the revalued Anthropic stake. The operating business itself earned $27.5B — up a strong 43%, but a very different story from the headline.

-$7.6B

AI spending swallowed free cash flow

Free cash flow over the last twelve months was an outflow of $7.6B, as purchases of property and equipment jumped $66.1B year over year — mostly AI data centers.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-operating other income of $53.4B, primarily from Amazon's investments in Anthropic, is inside GAAP net income of $62.6B. Excluding it, the quarter's story is operating income of $27.5B, up 43%.

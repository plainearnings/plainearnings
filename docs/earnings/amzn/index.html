Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+39%

AWS is accelerating again

AWS grew 39% to $46.0B — its fastest in years — and produced 63% of Amazon's entire operating profit at a 38.7% margin.

-$42.4B

Free cash flow went deeply negative

Twelve-month free cash flow swung to -$42.4B from +$14.8B a year ago, as property and equipment purchases jumped $94.8B — the AI data-center buildout, paid in cash.

+61%

Operating profit jumped — net income didn't

Operating income rose 61% to $28.1B, but net income slipped 2%: last year's quarter included a $9.5B Anthropic investment gain that didn't repeat.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Q3 2025 non-operating income included $9.5B of pre-tax gains from Amazon's investments in Anthropic, which did not repeat — so net income fell 2% YoY despite operating income rising 61%. Total non-operating income was -$1,207M this quarter (interest income $1,187M, interest expense -$1,691M, other -$703M). The balance check also subtracts $4M of equity-method investment activity, net of tax.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

AWS growth is accelerating again

AWS sales grew 24% to $35.6B — the fastest in 13 quarters — and produced $12.5B of operating income: half of Amazon's total profit from 17% of its sales. Amazon's own chips business (Trainium and Graviton) passed a $10B annual run rate, growing at triple-digit rates.

$2.4B

One-off charges in the stores business

Operating income includes $1.1B for resolving tax disputes tied to the stores business in Italy and settling a lawsuit, $730M of severance costs, and $610M of asset impairments, mainly on physical stores. Without them, operating income would have been $27.4B.

$200B

The AI build-out swallows the cash

Full-year free cash flow fell to $11.2B because spending on property and equipment jumped $50.7B year over year — mostly AI. Amazon expects to invest about $200B in capex across 2026.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating income includes three special charges totaling about $2.4B: $1.1B for the resolution of tax disputes associated with Amazon's stores business in Italy and the settlement of a lawsuit, $730M in estimated severance costs, and $610M in asset impairments primarily related to physical stores. Excluding these charges, operating income would have been $27.4B.

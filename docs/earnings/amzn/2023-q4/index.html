Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$6.5B

North America swung to a big profit

North America earned a $6.5B operating profit, versus a $0.2B loss in the holiday quarter a year ago, and International's loss narrowed to $0.4B from $2.2B — the store networks' efficiency push showed up all at once.

+13%

AWS kept compounding

AWS sales grew 13% to $24.2B — a $97B annual revenue run rate — and the segment earned $7.2B of operating profit, 54% of Amazon's total, up from $5.2B a year ago.

$36.8B

Free cash flow flipped sign

Free cash flow over the trailing twelve months was a $36.8B inflow, versus an $11.6B outflow a year earlier, and net income rose to $10.6B from just $0.3B in the year-ago quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $0.1B pre-tax paper loss on Amazon's common-stock investment in Rivian Automotive sits inside non-operating income — it is a revaluation of shares Amazon owns, not money lost to customers. A year ago the same line held a $2.3B loss.

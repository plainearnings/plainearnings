Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

AWS growth picked up

AWS sales grew 17% to $30.9B and the unit earned $10.2B — more than half of Amazon's total operating income comes from the cloud.

+35%

Profit grew far faster than sales

Net income jumped 35% to $18.2B. International operating income rose more than fivefold to $1.5B as the overseas stores got more efficient.

$18.2B

Free cash flow is shrinking

Free cash flow over the last twelve months fell to $18.2B, from $53.0B a year earlier — Amazon is pouring cash into AI data centers and warehouses faster than the business generates it.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

A record quarter

Net revenue hit a record $12.1B, up 13%, on payments volume +9%, cross-border volume +14% and processed transactions +10%. Full-year net revenue grew 15% — the strongest in over 15 years outside the post-pandemic rebound.

$5.1B

Incentives are the toll

Client incentives — rebates paid to issuing banks and partners — rose 21% to $5.1B, about 30% of gross revenue. They grow faster than revenue because big clients negotiate harder.

$3.48

GAAP understates it

GAAP EPS of $3.19 includes a $469M litigation provision and a $282M donation to the Visa Foundation. Excluding special items, non-GAAP EPS was $3.48 on net income of $6.6B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A litigation provision of $474M (the flagged special item is $469M) for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case — Visa excludes it from its non-GAAP results.
  • A $282M donation of investments to the Visa Foundation and a $37M deferred tax expense from a change in the U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings are also excluded from non-GAAP results. Non-GAAP net income was $6,552M ($3.48 per share).

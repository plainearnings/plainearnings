Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Net revenue hit $9.5B

Net revenue rose 10% to $9.5B — international transactions +14%, data processing +9%, service revenue +8% and other revenue +32%. Payments volume grew 9% and cross-border volume 16% in constant dollars; processed transactions rose 11%.

$2.75

Underlying EPS rose 14%

Non-GAAP EPS was $2.75, up 14%, and non-GAAP net income $5.5B, up 11%. GAAP EPS rose 8% to $2.58, held back by items Visa excludes, including a $44M litigation provision.

$3.8B

Incentives keep climbing

Client incentives — the rebates Visa pays banks and merchants to issue and accept its cards — rose 13% to $3.8B, 29% of gross revenue. Personnel costs jumped 23% to $1.8B, pushing GAAP operating expenses up 22%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP results exclude the $44M litigation provision, certain acquisition-related amortization and equity-investment losses: non-GAAP net income was $5,463M ($2.75 per share) versus GAAP net income of $5,119M ($2.58).

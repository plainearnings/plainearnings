Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

Cross-border stayed hot

Payments volume rose 8% in constant dollars and processed transactions 11%, while cross-border volume — the most lucrative traffic — jumped 16%, lifting international transaction revenue 9%.

+10%

Every revenue line grew

Net revenue rose 10% to $8,775M: service revenue +7%, data processing +12%, international transaction +9% and other revenue +37%, against client incentives of $3,257M paid back to card issuers.

$2.29

A legal provision held back GAAP profit

Net income rose 10% to $4,663M and EPS 12% to $2.29 — held back by a $430M litigation provision. Excluding special items, non-GAAP EPS was $2.51, up 20%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include a $424M litigation provision for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) injunctive relief class and other legal matters, plus $57M of lease consolidation costs. Excluding these and other special items, non-GAAP net income was $5,117M, or $2.51 per share.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Volume growth stayed solid

Net revenue rose 9% to $9.6B — data processing +10%, international transactions +10%, service revenue +9% and other revenue +24%. Payments volume grew 8% and cross-border volume 13% in constant dollars; processed transactions rose 9%.

$2.76

Underlying EPS rose 10%

Non-GAAP EPS was $2.76, up 10%, and non-GAAP net income $5.4B, up 6%. GAAP EPS rose just 1% to $2.32 because of the legal charge below.

$1.0B

The legal bill spiked again

The litigation provision was $1,000M — mostly the special item for the interchange MDL case — versus $430M a year ago. GAAP operating expenses rose 22%; excluding such items, they rose 7%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A litigation provision of $1,000M sits in operating expenses — $992M of it is the special item for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case; Visa excludes it from non-GAAP results.
  • Also excluded from non-GAAP results: $96M of amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs, and $23M of net losses on equity investments. Non-GAAP net income was $5.4B ($2.76 per share).

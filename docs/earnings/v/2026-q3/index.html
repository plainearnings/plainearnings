Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Every line grew

Net revenue rose 14% to $11.6B — data processing +17%, service revenue +14%, international transactions +6% on cross-border volume +15%, and other revenue +45%. Processed transactions grew 10%.

$563M

A big severance bill

Personnel costs jumped 41% because of $563M of severance charges as Visa reshapes its workforce — the main reason GAAP profit grew only 7% while revenue grew 14%. Non-GAAP EPS was $3.32.

$4.7B

Incentives keep climbing

Client incentives — rebates paid to issuing banks and partners — rose 18% to $4.7B, about 29 cents of every gross revenue dollar. They grow fast because the biggest banks negotiate hard to stay on the network.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Severance costs of $563M (inside personnel expense) from Visa's drive for operational efficiencies — excluded from its non-GAAP results.
  • A litigation provision of $253M for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case and other legal matters ($237M flagged as the special item), also excluded from non-GAAP results. Non-GAAP net income was $6.3B ($3.32 per share).

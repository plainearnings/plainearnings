Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Volume growth stayed strong

Net revenue rose 15% to $10.9B — data processing +17%, service revenue +13%, other revenue +33% and international transactions +6%. Payments volume grew 8% in constant dollars, cross-border volume 12% and processed transactions 9%.

$3.03

EPS rose 17%

GAAP EPS rose 17% to $3.03 and net income 14% to $5.9B. Non-GAAP EPS, which strips out the litigation charge and other one-offs, was $3.17, up 15%.

$707M

The legal bill spiked again

The litigation provision included a $707M special item for the interchange MDL case — in November, Visa signed a superseding settlement agreement for the injunctive-relief class claims, still subject to court approval. A one-off $333M deferred tax benefit, from a change in U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings, pulled the GAAP tax rate down to 13.0%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A litigation provision of $707M (the special item within the $708M provision line) for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case sits in operating expenses; Visa excludes it from non-GAAP results.
  • Also excluded from non-GAAP results: $66M of amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs, and $7M of net losses on equity investments. GAAP results also include a $333M deferred tax benefit from a change in the U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings. Non-GAAP net income was $6.1B ($3.17 per share).

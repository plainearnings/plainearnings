Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Cross-border stayed hot

Payments volume rose 7% in constant dollars and processed transactions 10% to 59.3 billion, while cross-border volume — the most lucrative traffic — jumped 14%, lifting international transaction revenue 9%.

+10%

Every revenue line grew

Net revenue rose 10% to $8,900M: service revenue +8%, data processing +9%, international transaction +9% and other revenue +31%, against client incentives of $3,530M paid back to card issuers.

$2.40

Profit grew faster than revenue

Net income rose 17% to $4,872M and EPS 20% to $2.40 — helped by a litigation provision of just $13M versus $457M a year ago. Excluding special items, non-GAAP EPS was $2.42.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include three special items: a $118M release of an indirect tax reserve set aside in fiscal 2021 (a gain), a $67M donation of investment securities to the Visa Foundation, and a $10M litigation provision for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case. Excluding these and related items, non-GAAP net income was $4,909M, or $2.42 per share.

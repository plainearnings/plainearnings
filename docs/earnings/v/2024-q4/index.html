Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

A strong finish to the fiscal year

Net revenue rose 12% to $9,617M as the growth drivers held steady: payments volume +8%, cross-border volume +13% and processed transactions +10% in constant dollars. Every revenue line grew, with Other revenue up 30%.

$5.3B

Profit grew faster than revenue

Net income rose 14% to $5,318M and EPS 17% to $2.65 — helped by a small $10M litigation provision versus $129M a year ago, and by buybacks shrinking the share count. Non-GAAP EPS was $2.71.

$6.8B

Cash kept flowing back

Visa returned $6.8B to shareholders in the quarter through buybacks and dividends, and raised the quarterly dividend 13% to $0.59 a share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The GAAP litigation provision was just $10M this quarter (versus $129M a year ago); Visa excludes litigation provisions from its non-GAAP results, which is most of why non-GAAP net income of $5,425M sits above the GAAP $5,318M.

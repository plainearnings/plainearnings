Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Fastest growth since 2022

Net revenue rose 17% to $11.2B — data processing +18%, service revenue +13%, international transactions +10% on cross-border volume +12%, and other revenue +41%. Payments volume grew 9% and processed transactions 9%.

$3.14

EPS jumped 36%

GAAP EPS rose 36% to $3.14, largely because the litigation provision fell to $329M from $1,000M a year ago. Non-GAAP EPS, which strips out the legal charge and other one-offs, was $3.31, up 20%.

$4.2B

Incentives keep climbing

Client incentives — rebates paid to issuing banks and partners — rose 14% to $4.2B, about 27 cents of every gross revenue dollar. They grow fast because the biggest banks negotiate hard to stay on the network.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A litigation provision of $329M sits in operating expenses; $311M of it is the special item for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case and other legal matters (a year ago the special item was $992M). Visa excludes it from non-GAAP results.
  • Also excluded from non-GAAP results: $86M of amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs, and $15M of net losses on equity investments. Non-GAAP net income was $6.3B ($3.31 per share).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Volume growth stayed strong

Net revenue rose 12% to $10.7B — data processing +17%, other revenue +21%, service revenue +10% and international transactions +10%. Payments volume grew 9% in constant dollars, cross-border volume 12% and processed transactions 10%.

$2.98

Underlying EPS rose 10%

Non-GAAP EPS was $2.98, up 10%, and non-GAAP net income $5.8B, up 7%. GAAP EPS slipped 1% to $2.62 because of the legal charge below. Visa also raised its quarterly dividend 14% to $0.67 a share.

$903M

The legal bill spiked again

The litigation provision was $903M — mostly a $899M special item for the interchange MDL case and other uncovered legal matters, vs just $10M a year ago. It cut GAAP net income 4% to $5.1B; Visa excludes it from non-GAAP results.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A litigation provision of $903M sits in operating expenses, $899M of it the special item for the interchange multidistrict litigation (MDL) case and other uncovered legal matters; Visa excludes it from non-GAAP results.
  • Also excluded from non-GAAP results: $54M of amortization of acquired intangible assets and $12M of acquisition-related costs, and $46M of net gains on equity investments. Non-GAAP net income was $5.8B ($2.98 per share).

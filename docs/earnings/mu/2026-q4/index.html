Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+379%

The memory supercycle

Revenue went from $11.3B to $54.2B in a year. Full-year revenue was $133B — three and a half times the year before.

87%

Scarcity pricing

Gross margin hit 87%, up from 45% a year ago: when every chip is spoken for before it is made, the maker sets the price.

$61.5B

Next quarter: bigger

Micron guided the current quarter to about $61.5B of revenue — it expects the shortage, and the pricing power, to continue.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $500M patent-license charge sits inside 'other operating expense' — a one-off legal cost of the quarter, unrelated to selling memory. Excluding such items, non-GAAP net income was $38.4B ($33.42 a share) versus $37.7B GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

>50%

Data centers became the majority

Data-center revenue passed half of all sales for the first time, growing more than 40% in a single quarter and over 400% from a year ago. AI servers need DRAM and HBM in quantities consumer devices never did.

38%

Margins snapped back

Gross margin hit 38.4%, from −0.7% a year earlier, as memory prices kept recovering from the 2023 crash. In memory chips, price — not volume — is the profit lever, and it swung Micron from a $1.23B loss to a $1.87B profit.

$3.13B

Spending heavily to keep up

Capital spending of $3.13B nearly matched the $3.24B of cash the operations generated, leaving adjusted free cash flow of just $112M — record profits, but almost all of them reinvested in new capacity.

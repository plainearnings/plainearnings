Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$4.5B

Cloud Memory is the engine

High-bandwidth memory for AI servers brought in $4.5B — more than triple a year ago — at a 59% gross margin, the richest business Micron runs.

45%

Margins widened fast

Gross margin hit 45%, up from 35% a year ago — with memory supply this tight, price increases fall almost straight through to profit. Full-year revenue was a record $37.4B.

$3.2B

Profit more than tripled

Net income of $3.2B, up from $887M a year ago. Non-GAAP net income was $3.5B, and Micron guided the next quarter's revenue to $12.5B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.4B

Cloud Memory leads

High-bandwidth memory for AI servers brought in $3.4B at a 58% gross margin — already Micron's largest business unit, and by far its most profitable per dollar of sales.

38%

Margins widened

Gross margin hit 38%, up from 27% a year ago — with AI memory in short supply, higher prices fall almost straight through to profit. Non-GAAP gross margin was 39%.

$1.9B

Profit jumped five-fold

Net income rose to $1.9B from $332M a year ago, and Micron guided the next quarter's revenue to $10.7B as the AI build-out accelerated.

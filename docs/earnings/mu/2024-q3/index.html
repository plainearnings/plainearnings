Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+82%

AI demand ignites data center

Revenue rose to $6.81B from $3.75B a year ago and beat Micron's guidance, as AI demand drove data-center revenue up 50% in a single quarter to a record share of the mix — with share gains in high-bandwidth memory and record data-center SSD revenue.

27%

Margins swung from red to green

Gross margin reached 26.9%, up from -17.8% a year ago when a memory glut forced write-downs — and reported operating income was $719M, versus a $1,761M operating loss in the same quarter last year.

$332M

Profit again — after a heavy tax bill

Net income was $332M ($0.30 a share) versus a $1,896M loss a year ago. A $377M tax provision on just $715M of pre-tax income held GAAP profit well below the $702M non-GAAP figure ($0.62 a share).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The GAAP tax bill was unusually heavy — a $377M provision on $715M of pre-tax income (about 53%); Micron's non-GAAP reconciliation includes $150M of estimated tax effects and other tax adjustments, a large part of the gap between GAAP net income of $332M and non-GAAP net income of $702M (which also excludes $217M of stock-based compensation).
  • There were no restructure and asset-impairment charges this quarter (versus $68M a year ago) and no inventory write-down charge — a clean quarter after the glut-era charges of fiscal 2023.

Business units are shown as originally reported for fiscal 2024 — Compute and Networking, Storage, Mobile, and Embedded (Q3 FY24 Form 10-Q segment note; operating margins are from that note, as Micron reported no gross margin by unit that quarter) — because no SEC filing reports this quarter on the recast structure Micron adopted in fiscal 2026 (Cloud Memory, Core Data Center, Mobile and Client, Automotive and Embedded). The reorganization took effect in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025 (FY25 Form 10-K, Note 27), and the Q3 FY25 earnings release contains no business-unit table; on the recast basis Micron has filed only full-year FY24 unit revenue (FY25 10-K: Cloud Memory $3,792M, Core Data Center $4,984M, Mobile and Client $11,667M, Automotive and Embedded $4,631M, All other $37M) and the FQ4-24 quarter (Q4 FY25 earnings release), so no quarterly recast split of FQ3-24 exists.

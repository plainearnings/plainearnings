Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+57%

A record quarter

Revenue rose from $8.7B a year ago to a record $13.6B — and Micron guided the next quarter to $18.7B, another huge step up.

56%

Margins widened fast

Gross margin hit 56%, up from 38% a year ago and 45% last quarter — with memory supply this tight, price increases fall almost straight through to profit.

$5.2B

Profit nearly tripled

Net income of $5.2B, up from $1.9B a year ago. Cloud Memory — the AI unit — doubled its revenue to $5.3B at a 66% gross margin. Non-GAAP net income was $5.5B.

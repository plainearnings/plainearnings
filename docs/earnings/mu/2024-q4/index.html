Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+93%

Revenue nearly doubled

Revenue rose to $7.75B from $4.01B a year ago, on record data center revenue — robust AI demand drove a strong ramp of data center DRAM and high-bandwidth memory, and data center SSD sales passed $1B in a quarter for the first time.

35%

Margins swung back

Gross margin reached 35%, up from -11% a year ago when Micron was still digging out of a memory glut — tighter supply and AI pricing turned each sale profitable again. Full-year revenue was $25.1B.

$887M

Profit is back

Net income of $887M, versus a $1.43B loss a year ago; non-GAAP net income was $1.34B. Micron guided the next quarter's revenue to $8.7B — another record.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructure and asset-impairment charges of $1M sit inside operating expenses.
  • The GAAP tax bill was unusually heavy — a $623M provision on $1,510M of pre-tax income; Micron's non-GAAP reconciliation adds back $236M of estimated tax effects and other tax adjustments.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+196%

Revenue nearly tripled

Sales rose from $8.1B a year ago to $23.9B — and Micron guided the next quarter to $33.5B, another huge step up.

74%

Margins exploded

Gross margin hit 74%, double the 37% of a year ago — with supply this tight, price increases fall almost straight through to profit.

$13.8B

A record profit quarter

Net income of $13.8B in a single quarter — more than Micron earned in most full years before the AI boom. Non-GAAP net income was $14.0B.

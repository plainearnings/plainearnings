Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1B+

HBM crossed $1 billion

Revenue from high-bandwidth memory — the chips stacked beside AI processors — passed $1 billion in a single quarter for the first time, and data-center revenue overall tripled from a year ago. AI demand is now the center of Micron's business.

37%

Margins rebuilt

Gross margin reached 36.8%, double the 18.5% of a year ago, as prices for both DRAM and NAND kept recovering. In memory chips, price swings matter more than volumes — and prices were Micron's friend this quarter.

-8%

A sequential step down, guided back up

Revenue dipped from the prior quarter's $8.71B, a pause after a run of records. Management said to expect a new record in the next quarter, with demand growing in both data centers and consumer devices.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+58%

The memory recovery arrives

Revenue rose 58% from a year ago to $5.82B and gross margin swung from -33% to 19%, as pricing recovered from the 2023 glut. Gross profit also benefited from $382M of lower costs on selling inventory that had been written down earlier.

$622M

A tax benefit made the bottom line

Net income of $793M was far bigger than the $170M of pre-tax profit: Micron booked a $622M income-tax benefit after switching to an estimated annual effective tax rate applied to year-to-date results, as its 10-Q explains.

$200M

A patent deal padded the quarter

Other operating income of $224M included a $200M gain from a patent cross-license agreement. Setting that gain and the tax benefit aside, the core memory business was roughly break-even — reported operating income was $191M.

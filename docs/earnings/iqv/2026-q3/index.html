Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.2B

Bookings are surging

R&D Solutions won $3.2B of net new business, up 15% — a 1.2x book-to-bill — and last-twelve-month bookings reached $11.7B.

$667M

Cash conversion is strong

Free cash flow of $667M was 120% of adjusted net income; net debt stood at $13.8B, or 3.45x adjusted EBITDA.

$12.90–13.00

Guidance raised

IQVIA lifted its full-year outlook: revenue of $17.35–17.50B and adjusted EPS of $12.90–$13.00.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

~$800M

China controls bit hard

US export controls on the Instinct MI308 AI chip led to roughly $800M of inventory and related charges inside cost of sales — GAAP gross margin was 40%, versus about 54% without the charges.

+67%

PC chips had a record quarter

Client revenue hit a record $2.5B, up 67% YoY on 'Zen 5' Ryzen desktop processors, and Gaming rose 73% — together they carried the quarter while Data Center absorbed the China hit.

$834M

A tax benefit flipped the bottom line

AMD booked an $834M income-tax benefit, so despite a $134M operating loss the quarter ended with $872M of net income — $768M from continuing operations plus $104M from the business being sold.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Export-control charges: ~$800M — inventory and related charges on Instinct MI308 AI chips blocked from sale to China, booked inside cost of sales. Excluding them, gross margin would have been about 54% instead of 40%.
  • Discontinued operations added $104M below the line — profit from the server-rack manufacturing business (acquired with ZT Systems) that AMD agreed to sell; profit from continuing operations was $768M.
  • An $834M income-tax benefit — largely non-cash — turned an operating loss into a net profit; it is drawn as a green 'Tax benefit' source in the chart.

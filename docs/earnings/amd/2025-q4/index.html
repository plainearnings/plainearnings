Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+39%

Data Center is the engine

Data Center brought in $5.4B — 52% of all revenue — and $1,752M of segment operating profit, as demand for EPYC server chips and Instinct AI accelerators stayed strong.

54%

Gross margin widened

Gross margin reached 54%, up from 51% a year ago, as high-value data-center products became a bigger share of the mix.

$2.1B

Cash piled up

Free cash flow was $2,082M — 20% of revenue — taking cash and short-term investments to $10.6B against just $3.2B of debt.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $109M loss from discontinued operations sits below the continuing-operations line; profit from continuing operations was $1,620M.
  • Stock-based compensation was $486M inside the expense lines; on AMD's non-GAAP view (which excludes it and acquisition amortization), net income was $2,519M instead of $1,511M.

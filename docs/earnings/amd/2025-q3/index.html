Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+73%

Client and Gaming surged

Client and Gaming together brought in $4.0B, up 73% YoY — Ryzen PC chips up 46% and Gaming up 181% — while Data Center grew 22% to $4.3B.

52%

Gross margin widened

Gross margin reached 52%, up from 50% a year ago, as data-center products became a bigger share of the mix — and the quarter included no revenue from MI308 AI-chip shipments to China.

$1.5B

Record free cash flow

Free cash flow was a record $1,530M — 17% of revenue — as the quarter's profits converted straight into cash.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Discontinued operations added $71M below the line — profit from the server-rack manufacturing business (acquired with ZT Systems) that AMD agreed to sell; profit from continuing operations was $1,172M.
  • Stock-based compensation sits inside the expense lines; on AMD's non-GAAP view (which excludes it and acquisition amortization), net income was $1,965M instead of $1,243M.

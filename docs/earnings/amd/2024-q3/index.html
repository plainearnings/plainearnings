Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+122%

Data Center more than doubled

Data Center revenue hit a record $3.5B, up 122% YoY, on the ramp of Instinct AI GPUs and EPYC server CPUs — it is now more than half of everything AMD sells.

50%

Margins healed

Gross margin reached 50%, up from 47% a year ago, as high-margin data-center chips became the mix — operating income more than tripled to $724M and net income reached $771M.

-69%

Gaming and Embedded still shrinking

Gaming revenue fell 69% to $462M late in the console cycle and Embedded fell 25% as customers digested inventories — together they masked how fast the AI-driven businesses are growing.

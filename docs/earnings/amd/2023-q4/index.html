Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+38%

Data Center carried the quarter

Data Center revenue hit $2,282M, up 38% YoY, on the ramp of Instinct MI300 GPUs and 4th Gen EPYC CPUs — while Embedded fell 24% to $1,057M as customers worked through inventory.

$297M

A tax benefit flattered profit

GAAP net income was $667M, but $297M of it was an income-tax benefit rather than operating performance; operating income itself was $342M, a 6% margin. Non-GAAP net income was $1,249M.

+62%

PC chips bounced back

Client revenue rose 62% YoY to $1,461M on Ryzen 7000 Series sales as the PC market recovered; Gaming fell 17% to $1,368M on weaker semi-custom revenue.

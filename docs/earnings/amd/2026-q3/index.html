Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+97%

Data Center is the company now

$8.5B of the $13.4B total, on record Instinct GPU and EPYC CPU sales; management expects growth to accelerate as Helios systems ramp.

$2.09

Two versions of profit

Non-GAAP EPS was $2.09; GAAP EPS was $1.46. Almost the whole gap is the non-cash warrant charge described below.

-40%

Gaming is the weak spot

Gaming revenue fell 40% to $773M — console chips and graphics cards are shrinking while everything AI-adjacent booms.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $1,097M warrant charge is non-cash: AMD granted two large customers warrants for up to 160M shares each (at $0.01) tied to purchase milestones; as AMD's share price climbed, the accounting value of that promise jumped, and the increase is booked as a loss. It will keep swinging with the share price until the warrants vest.
  • Income tax was a $555M benefit this quarter instead of the usual expense, which also lifted GAAP net income; AMD's non-GAAP results use a normalized 13% tax rate.

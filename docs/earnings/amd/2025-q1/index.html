Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+57%

Data Center carried the growth

Data Center revenue rose 57% YoY to $3.7B — half of everything AMD sells — on EPYC server processors and Instinct AI accelerators.

+68%

PC chips boomed too

Client revenue jumped 68% to $2.3B on 'Zen 5' Ryzen demand, more than offsetting a 30% drop in Gaming as console chip sales faded.

$806M

Profits finally leveraged

Operating income jumped from just $36M a year ago to $806M — revenue grew 36% while costs grew far more slowly, lifting the gross margin to 50% from 47%.

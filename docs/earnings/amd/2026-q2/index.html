Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+107%

Data Center is now the company

Data Center revenue more than doubled to $6.7B — 58% of everything AMD sells — and its operating profit swung from a $155M loss a year ago to $2.1B.

-31%

Gaming is shrinking

Gaming revenue fell 31% to $779M as console-chip demand faded — the old business is shrinking while the AI business replaces it.

$808M

Capex nearly tripled

Spending on property and equipment jumped from $282M to $808M as AMD builds out capacity for AI demand; free cash flow still rose to $1.6B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income includes $483M of net gains on long-term investments (per the cash-flow statement) — paper gains, not cash from selling chips.
  • An $8M loss from discontinued operations sits below the continuing-operations line; profit from continuing operations was $2,305M.

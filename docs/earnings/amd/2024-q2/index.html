Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+115%

Data Center more than doubled

Data Center revenue hit a record $2.8B, up 115% YoY, on the steep ramp of Instinct AI GPUs and strong 4th Gen EPYC sales — it is now nearly half of everything AMD sells.

49%

Margins and profit recovered

Gross margin reached 49%, up from 46% a year ago — operating income swung to $269M from a $20M loss a year ago and net income reached $265M, up from $27M.

-59%

Gaming and Embedded still shrinking

Gaming revenue fell 59% to $648M on lower semi-custom console revenue and Embedded fell 41% to $861M as customers normalized inventories — together they masked how fast the AI-driven businesses are growing.

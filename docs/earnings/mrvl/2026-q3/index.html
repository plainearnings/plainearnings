Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+38%

Data Center set the record

Data Center revenue hit $1.52B — 73% of the company — on AI demand, and total revenue set a record at $2.07B, up 37% YoY.

$1.8B

A one-time gain did the lifting

The $1,830.4M gain on the automotive ethernet sale is most of the quarter's $1,901.3M GAAP profit. Underlying non-GAAP net income was $655.0M.

$582M

Cash kept flowing

Operating cash flow was $582.3M in the quarter, and Marvell spent $1.3B buying back its own shares after banking the sale proceeds.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Marvell closed the sale of its automotive ethernet business to Infineon on August 14, 2025 for $2.5B in cash, booking a $1,830.4M pre-tax gain this quarter — the entire reason GAAP EPS ($2.20) dwarfs non-GAAP EPS ($0.76). Net cash proceeds were $2,478.6M.
  • GAAP results also include $152.1M of stock-based compensation and $229.0M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP.

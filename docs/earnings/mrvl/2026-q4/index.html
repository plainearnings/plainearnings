Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Data Center set the record

Data Center revenue hit $1.65B — 74% of the company — on AI demand, and total revenue set a record at $2.22B, capping a record $8.19B fiscal year, up 42%.

$0.46 vs $0.80

Two very different profits

GAAP EPS was 46 cents against 80 cents non-GAAP: $143M of stock pay and $224M of acquisition amortization sit in the GAAP column only.

$374M

Solid cash generation

Operating cash flow was $373.7M in the quarter despite a $640M jump in receivables as sales surged into the year-end.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit is below non-GAAP profit ($396M vs $685M) mainly because GAAP includes $143.0M of stock-based compensation and $223.6M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP.
  • The tax line was a $14.5M benefit, not a charge, in the quarter.

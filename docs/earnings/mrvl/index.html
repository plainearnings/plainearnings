Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+46%

Data Center is carrying the growth

Data Center revenue hit $2,171.5M, up 46% from a year ago and 18% from last quarter — 79% of all revenue. Marvell said AI-related bookings remain exceptionally robust and raised its outlook for fiscal 2027 and 2028.

$0.33 vs $0.94

GAAP profit looks much smaller than non-GAAP

GAAP EPS was $0.33 against $0.94 non-GAAP. The gap is mostly $326.2M of stock-based compensation and $214.9M of amortization on past acquisitions, plus a net $52.0M fair-value loss tied to the Celestial deal — all real costs that non-GAAP leaves out.

$3.15B

Next quarter is guided to another record

Marvell guided Q3 FY27 revenue to $3.15B (±5%), with GAAP EPS of $0.53 (±$0.05) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.10 (±$0.05) — a big step up from this quarter's $2.74B as its Custom business accelerates.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A net $52.0M fair-value loss sat inside interest and other loss, net: a $101.9M increase in the fair value of the contingent consideration owed for the Celestial acquisition, partly offset by a $49.9M unrealized gain on a forward stock purchase contract. Both are excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • GAAP profit is far below non-GAAP profit ($308.0M vs $865.9M) mainly because GAAP includes $326.2M of stock-based compensation and $214.9M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP.

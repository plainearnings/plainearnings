Marvell kept only $1.43 of every $100 this quarter — a $331.8M paper charge on the Celestial AI deal price, plus heavy stock pay and acquisition amortization, ate most of the operating profit.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+27%

Data Center set the record

Data Center revenue hit a record $1.83B — 76% of the company — on AI demand for custom XPU silicon, optics and scale-out switches.

$0.04 vs $0.80

Two very different profits

GAAP EPS was just 4 cents against 80 cents non-GAAP: a $331.8M Celestial deal fair-value charge, $207.6M of stock pay and $225.2M of acquisition amortization sit in the GAAP column only.

$639M

Record operating cash flow

Operating cash flow was a record $638.8M — the business generated plenty of cash even though GAAP profit was only $34.5M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Inside the $203.3M other expense, net: the fair value of the contingent consideration owed for Celestial AI rose $331.8M — a non-cash charge — partly offset by an $81.1M unrealized gain on a forward stock purchase contract. Both are excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • GAAP profit is far below non-GAAP profit ($34.5M vs $718.0M) mainly because GAAP includes $207.6M of stock-based compensation and $225.2M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP.
  • Marvell closed two acquisitions in the quarter — Celestial AI on February 2, 2026 and XConn on February 10, 2026 — and their results are included from those dates.

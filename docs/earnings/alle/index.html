Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18.8%

Clean double-digit growth

EPS rose 18.8% to $2.59 — and adjusted EPS was $2.60, so for once the GAAP and adjusted stories nearly match (the $19.9M tariff refund inside GAAP is excluded from adjusted).

30.1%

Americas is a profit engine

The Americas segment runs at a 30.1% adjusted operating margin on institutional door hardware — pricing power plus volume leverage as non-residential construction demand holds up.

$9.05–9.10

Guidance raised

Full-year outlook raised: reported revenue growth of 9.5–10.5% and adjusted EPS of $9.05–$9.10, with available cash flow targeted at 85–95% of adjusted net income.

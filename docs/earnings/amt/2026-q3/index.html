Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2.5%

Revenue rose to $2.79B

Property revenue grew 4.5% to $2.73B; services revenue halved after last year's large projects wound down, which held total growth to 2.5%.

-14%

Net income fell to $781M

Higher interest expense ($366M) and a swing in other items cut accounting profit, even though the underlying rent business grew.

+2.4%

AFFO — the cash measure — grew

Adjusted funds from operations rose 2.4% to $1,335M and management raised its full-year outlook, citing data-center outperformance.

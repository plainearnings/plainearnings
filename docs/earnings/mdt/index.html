Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

Heart treatments led the surge

Cardiovascular sales rose 20% to $3.9B, with electrophysiology — catheter treatments for irregular heartbeats — growing in the high 20s on pulsed-field ablation demand.

+14%

Growth was broad, not one product

All four portfolios grew at double-digit rates: Cardiovascular +20%, Diabetes +17%, Neuroscience +10% and Medical Surgical +10%.

$1.45

The non-GAAP gap is mostly amortization

Non-GAAP EPS was $1.45 versus $1.14 GAAP. The biggest difference is $412M of amortization of acquired technology — a real accounting cost, but not cash out the door this quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

Data centers found Generac

Commercial & Industrial external sales rose 28% to $552M, and management cited significant additional commitments from data-center customers — backup power is becoming an AI-infrastructure product.

+9%

Residential came back

Residential sales rose 9% to $743M as 'lower power quality mega-trends' — an unreliable grid — lifted shipments of home standby and portable generators after a soft stretch.

39.9%

Margins expanded too

Gross margin rose to 39.9% from 38.3% on favorable price/cost, including lower tariff costs — so operating income grew 56%, much faster than sales.

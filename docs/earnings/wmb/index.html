Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Growth projects are paying off

Service revenues rose $198M year on year on new power generation revenues, the Momentum acquisition, projects placed in service and higher storage revenues.

$2.04B

On track for the top half of guidance

Adjusted EBITDA rose 6% to $2.04B in Q3 and 9% year to date to $6.2B, and Williams says it is tracking toward the upper half of its $8.3B–$8.5B full-year 2026 range.

2.42x

Dividend well covered

Available funds from operations rose 10% to $1.59B, covering the dividend 2.42 times, while Williams placed four pipeline expansions into service and upsized Transco projects in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating income includes an $83M gain on the sale of a Permian gathering system; excluding this and other adjustments, adjusted net income was $663M, or $0.54 per share (non-GAAP), versus GAAP EPS of $0.56.
  • Williams closed the acquisition of Momentum Midstream (M6 Midstream) on September 3, 2026, strengthening its position in the Haynesville shale basin.

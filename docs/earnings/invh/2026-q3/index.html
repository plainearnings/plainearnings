Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$0.48

Core profit per share inched up

Core FFO per share rose 2.4% to $0.48 — helped by buybacks shrinking the share count — and full-year guidance midpoint was raised to $1.96.

$136M

Home sales padded the profit

Net income of $159M includes $136M of gains on homes sold. FFO, which strips those gains out, was $212M — down from $274M a year ago.

~$1B

Buybacks are the strategy

Invitation Homes has repurchased nearly $1B of stock since December 2025, funded by home sales, and the board just authorized another $500M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $136M gain on sale of property (versus $46M a year ago) is included in net income; REIT yardstick FFO removes it.

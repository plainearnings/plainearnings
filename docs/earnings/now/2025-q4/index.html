Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20.5%

Growth held up

Total revenue grew 20.5% to $3.57B and subscription revenue 21% — ServiceNow beat its Q4 guidance and finished 2025 with $13.28B of revenue, up 21%.

$28.2B

Future revenue is booked

Remaining performance obligations — revenue customers have signed for but not yet been billed as sales — grew 26.5% to $28.2B, with $12.85B due in the next 12 months.

$0.38 vs $0.92

Two very different profits

GAAP EPS was $0.38 against $0.92 non-GAAP: $494M of stock-based compensation is the biggest single difference between the two profit pictures.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation was $494M in the quarter (including $89M inside cost of revenues) — the main reason non-GAAP net income ($959M) is more than double the GAAP figure ($401M). The cost is real; it is paid in shares instead of cash.
  • ServiceNow closed its Moveworks acquisition on December 15, 2025; a 5-for-1 stock split also took effect in the quarter (December 17), so per-share figures are split-adjusted.

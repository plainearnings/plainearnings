Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20.5%

Growth held above 20%

Subscription revenue grew 21% and contracted-but-not-yet-delivered revenue (RPO) reached $29.7B, up 22% — much of the next few years' growth is already pre-sold.

$1.7B

AI deals are the new engine

ServiceNow raised its 2026 target for AI net new contract value to $1.7B, the second raise this year — AI agents are becoming a real product line, not a demo.

$0.27 vs $1.00

Two very different profits

GAAP EPS was $0.27; excluding stock pay and one-offs it was $1.00. Stock-based pay is the single biggest gap between the two profit measures.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Interest expense jumped to $81M from $6M a year ago after ServiceNow issued new debt — a big reason GAAP profit fell even as revenue grew 20.5%.
  • Stock-based compensation was $633M in the quarter — the main reason non-GAAP net income ($1,039M) is nearly 4× the GAAP figure ($277M). The cost is real; it is paid in shares instead of cash.
  • Subscription delivery costs rose 58% YoY (to $1,054M) as AI features run on expensive computing capacity — GAAP gross margin narrowed from 77% to 71%.

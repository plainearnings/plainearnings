Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Growth accelerated

Total revenue grew 24% to $3.99B and subscription revenue 24.5% — ServiceNow beat the high end of its guidance on every headline metric and raised its full-year subscription outlook.

$29.0B

Future revenue is booked

Remaining performance obligations — revenue customers have signed for but not yet been billed as sales — grew 21% to $29.0B, with $13.20B due in the next 12 months.

$0.29 vs $0.90

Two very different profits

GAAP EPS was $0.29 against $0.90 non-GAAP: $655M of stock-based compensation is the biggest single difference between the two profit pictures.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation was $655M in the quarter (including $109M inside cost of revenues) — the main reason non-GAAP net income ($930M) is more than 3× the GAAP figure ($298M). The cost is real; it is paid in shares instead of cash.
  • Other income, net swung to $206M from a $3M expense a year ago, and interest income added $70M — together they more than doubled pre-tax profit relative to the $162M operating line.
  • ServiceNow AI crossed $1B in annual contract value in Q2 2026, and the company counted 123 transactions over $1M in net new ACV, up nearly 40% YoY.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Growth held up

Total revenue grew 22% to $3.77B and subscription revenue 22% — ServiceNow beat the high end of its guidance and raised its full-year subscription outlook.

$27.7B

Future revenue is booked

Remaining performance obligations — revenue customers have signed for but not yet been billed as sales — grew 25% to $27.7B, with $12.64B due in the next 12 months.

$0.45 vs $0.97

Two very different profits

GAAP EPS was $0.45 against $0.97 non-GAAP: $547M of stock-based compensation is the biggest single difference between the two profit pictures.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation was $547M in the quarter (including $96M inside cost of revenues) — the main reason non-GAAP net income ($1,012M) is more than double the GAAP figure ($469M). The cost is real; it is paid in shares instead of cash.
  • ServiceNow closed its Armis acquisition early in the quarter, expanding its security business; the company raised its full-year subscription revenue outlook in this release.

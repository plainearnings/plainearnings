Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$292B

Backlog jumps to a record

Backlog rose 27% in a single quarter to $292B, including a $52.5B multi-year award to build PAC-3 MSE interceptors — years of work already sold.

+24%

Missiles lead the growth

Missiles and Fire Control sales rose 24% to $4.5B on PAC-3, THAAD, GMLRS and PrSM production ramps, and its profit rose 24% too. All four segments grew.

$30.55

Guidance raised again

Lockheed raised its 2026 outlook for the second straight quarter: sales of about $80–82B, EPS of about $30.35–30.75 (midpoint $30.55) and free cash flow of about $7.8–8.0B. The dividend was raised for the 24th straight year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Watch item, not a charge: costs on the F-35 Lots 20–22 advance contract began exceeding the U.S. Government's authorized funding in Q3. Lockheed warns that without additional authorization and funding, future results, cash flows and financial condition will be hurt.

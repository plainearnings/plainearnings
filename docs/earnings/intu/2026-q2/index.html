Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Growth before the main event

Revenue rose 17% to $4,651M even though Q2 is only the warm-up to tax season. Global Business Solutions grew 18% and Consumer 15%, and Intuit reiterated its full-year guidance.

$616M

Credit Karma is the growth engine

Credit Karma revenue jumped 23% to $616M on personal loans, credit cards and auto insurance, while TurboTax grew 12% to $581M on early-season filers.

+44%

Profit grew much faster than sales

GAAP operating income rose 44% to $855M and net income 47% to $693M, as revenue growth outran a 16% rise in running costs. GAAP EPS was $2.48; non-GAAP EPS was $4.15.

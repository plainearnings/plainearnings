Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+43.8%

Faster payments is taking off

Faster-payments revenue jumped 43.8% on expanding transaction volumes, while digital revenue rose 13.3% and card revenue 9.4%.

+11.7%

The cloud shift continues

Hosting revenue grew 11.7% as new and existing clients migrate from on-premise systems to Jack Henry's private cloud.

$71M

Steady buybacks

Jack Henry repurchased $71M of stock in the quarter at an average of $153.47 per share, with only $85M of debt outstanding.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Revenue includes $18M of deconversion fees — one-time payments from institutions that left the platform, nearly double last year's $9M. Excluding them (and a small acquisition), non-GAAP adjusted revenue grew 7.2%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

2.95%

The margin keeps widening

Net interest margin rose 20 basis points year over year to 2.95% on cheaper deposits, reinvested securities proceeds and a shift toward higher-yielding commercial loans.

$61M

Credit costs fell hard

The provision for credit losses dropped to $61M from $107M a year ago, with lower net charge-offs and nonperforming assets.

$320M

Buybacks resumed at pace

Key repurchased $320M of common shares in the quarter while keeping its CET1 capital ratio at an estimated 11.1%.

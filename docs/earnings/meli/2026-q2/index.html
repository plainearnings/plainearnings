Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+50%

Fastest growth in four years

Revenue passed $10B in a quarter for the first time. Shoppers bought more often — items per buyer rose 14% — a year after the free-shipping threshold was cut in Brazil.

6.7%

Margin is a choice, for now

Operating margin fell to 6.7% from 12.1% a year ago as MercadoLibre spends on shipping, warehouses and credit-card issuance. Management calls it deliberate: users who use both marketplace and Pago spend far more.

$16.4B

The credit book is ballooning

The loan and credit-card portfolio grew 75% YoY to $16.4B, with 2.6 million new cards issued in the quarter. The bad-debt provision — $1.28B this quarter — grows with it.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+49%

Growth re-accelerated

Revenue grew 49% in dollars — 84 million unique buyers bought 722 million items, and Mercado Pago's monthly active users reached 83 million.

6.9%

Margin is a choice, for now

Operating margin fell to 6.9% from 12.9% a year ago. Two-thirds of the squeeze is the bad-debt provision growing with the credit book; the rest is deliberate spending on shipping and logistics.

$14.6B

The credit book is ballooning

The loan and credit-card portfolio grew 87% YoY to $14.6B, its largest-ever quarterly increase; credit cards alone more than doubled to $6.6B. The bad-debt provision — $1.24B this quarter — grows with it.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+45%

Growth stayed very fast

Revenue reached $10,750M, up 45% in dollars and 41% ignoring currency moves, with Brazil the strongest of the big markets. Commerce revenue rose 47% in dollars and Fintech 42%, so both halves of the business grew at a similar pace.

6.3%

Margin was spent on purpose

Income from operations fell 6% to $676M, a 6.3% margin, as more free shipping, new fulfillment space added ahead of peak season and extra affiliate marketing in Brazil cost more than they returned this quarter. Net income still rose 6% to $448M on a lower tax bill and smaller currency losses.

~$18B

The credit book keeps scaling

The credit portfolio reached almost $18B, up 62% year over year, with credit cards now 47% of it. Late loans stayed contained — the 15–90 day non-performing ratio was 7.0%, broadly stable and close to all-time lows — while the lending spread after losses (NIMAL) was 20.1%.

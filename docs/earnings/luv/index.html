Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Record revenue, record yields

Operating revenue was a Q3 record $8,422M and unit revenue rose 21.6% to an all-time record 18.55 cents per available seat mile, on strong demand for the revamped product.

$821M

Fuel was the big headwind

Aircraft fuel and related taxes cost $2,152M, up $821M or 62% from a year ago — yet operating margin still expanded 4.0 points to 4.5% because revenue grew faster.

$328M

Clean profit was higher

Excluding $73M of pre-tax special items, adjusted net income was $328M ($0.66 per share) versus the GAAP $270M ($0.55). Southwest also returned $176M to shareholders in dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Special items cost $73M pre-tax in operating expenses ($58M after tax): excluding them, operating income was $452M instead of $379M and net income was $328M ($0.66 per share) instead of $270M ($0.55).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Growth came from deals and North America

Revenue rose to $9.45B. North America jumped 24% to $2.40B, helped by ChampionX, which added $956M of revenue in the quarter.

$0.49

One-time charges cut into profit

GAAP EPS fell 2% to $0.49. Excluding charges and credits, EPS was $0.64, as restructuring and a pension settlement charge weighed on reported results.

$828M

Cash generation stayed solid

Free cash flow was $828M in the quarter, and the board kept the quarterly dividend at $0.295 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges of $187M in the quarter, including a $187M non-cash pension settlement charge.
  • Merger and integration costs of $55M, mainly for ChampionX. Net income excluding charges and credits was $962M versus $735M GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Record revenue, led by residential

Revenue reached a record $952M, up 14%. Residential Real Estate jumped 23% to $460M, CoStar rose 8% to $344M and LoopNet rose 13% to $89M, while Other Commercial Real Estate fell 8% to $59M. Net new bookings were $37M in the quarter.

$229M

Profitability surged as spend was reined in

Adjusted EBITDA doubled to $229M, the highest ever. Selling and marketing fell to $366M from $418M a year ago even as revenue grew, and total operating expenses fell to $657M from $713M. Management put this year's cost savings at about $150M and raised full-year Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $820M-$840M.

$74M

Back in profit after last year's loss

Net income was $74M, or $0.18 per diluted share, vs a $31M loss a year ago — a $105M swing. Operating income was $84M vs a $51M loss, and the Residential segment's EBITDA turned positive at $16M, vs a $101M loss a year ago, as the heavy Homes.com investment phase eased.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating costs include $15M of acquisition and integration-related costs (the Zonda acquisition closed in August 2026) and $3M of settlements and impairments that the company excludes from Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Net Income.

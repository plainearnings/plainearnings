Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5%

Volume, not just price, grew

Global unit case volume rose 5% and concentrate sales rose 7%, with price/mix adding 1% — growth came mainly from selling more drinks. Sparkling soft drinks grew 5%, led by Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, up 16%.

33.4%

Margins widened

Operating margin rose to 33.4% from 32.0% a year ago as gross profit grew 10% to $8,450M, faster than revenue. Net income attributable to shareowners rose 8% to $4,009M.

$11.4B

Cash flow is strong year to date

Year-to-date free cash flow (non-GAAP) was $11.4B on $12.5B of operating cash flow, and the company raised its full-year guidance alongside the results.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter included $192M of settlement charges and special termination benefits for a non-U.S. pension plan, recorded in items impacting comparability; comparable (non-GAAP) EPS was $0.91 versus $0.93 GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.0%

Subscriptions keep compounding

Organic annual subscription value (ASV) — the forward revenue locked in from subscriptions — rose 7.0% to $2,568.2M, a record increase, with retention above 95%.

24.7%

GAAP margin took a one-time hit

GAAP operating margin fell to 24.7% from 29.7% a year ago, mainly on $26.9M of restructuring/severance and $4.6M of CEO onboarding costs. Adjusted margin was 33.0%.

$177.3M

Cash still flows, and gets returned

Free cash flow was $177.3M in the quarter, and FactSet returned over $179M to shareholders — $138M of buybacks plus more than $41M of dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and severance charges of $26.9M and CEO onboarding costs of $4.6M weighed on GAAP operating income; excluding them and intangible amortization, adjusted operating income was $209.4M, up 3.8%.
  • A year ago, results included a gain on the divestiture of a business, which makes this year's GAAP EPS comparison ($3.41 vs $4.03) look worse than the underlying trend — adjusted EPS rose 11.6% to $4.52.

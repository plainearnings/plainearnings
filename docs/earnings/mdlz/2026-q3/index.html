Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

39.4%

The margin jump is accounting, not pricing power

Gross margin leapt from 26.8% to 39.4% almost entirely on mark-to-market swings in cocoa and currency hedges. The underlying (adjusted) gross profit grew much more modestly.

+2.1%

Steady underlying growth

Organic revenue rose 2.1%: selling slightly more snacks (volume/mix +1.8 points) with near-flat pricing (+0.3 points) after last year's big chocolate price rises.

-2.1%

Europe is the soft spot

European organic sales fell 2.1% — shoppers pushed back on chocolate prices (pricing subtracted 3.7 points there) — while emerging markets grew 4.1% organically.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gross profit includes a large favorable swing in mark-to-market accounting on cocoa and currency derivatives — the same mechanism depressed last year's quarter (gross margin was 26.8% then, 39.4% now). Adjusted results strip these swings out, which is why adjusted EPS grew only 5.5% at constant currency while GAAP EPS rose 79%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.58

Adjusted EPS up 27%

Adjusted earnings were $610M, or $2.58 a share, up from $475M and $2.03 a year ago — warmer weather, growth in demand, large-customer revenue and recovery of regulated investments all helped.

1.4 GW

Data centers keep coming

Evergy signed amendments adding about 250 MW to existing large-customer service agreements, plus a letter of intent with Digital Realty for a 1.4 GW expansion next to an existing 600 MW project.

$4.35–$4.50

Guidance raised, dividend up 4%

Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance rose to $4.35–$4.50, and the quarterly dividend increased 4% to $0.7225 a share ($2.89 annualized). Long-term adjusted EPS growth of 6–8%+ through 2030 was reaffirmed.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted earnings strip out a $5M gain from a transmission-revenue-requirement settlement and $1M of losses on early-stage clean-energy investments (plus their tax effects) — which is why adjusted profit of $610M sits slightly below the $613M GAAP figure (both attributable).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.24B

TAVR keeps growing

TAVR sales rose 8.0% (8.8% at constant currency) on global procedure growth and modest share gains, with stable prices. Edwards raised full-year TAVR growth guidance to 9.0–9.5%.

+44%

TMTT is the growth engine

Mitral and tricuspid therapies surged 44% to $209M as PASCAL, EVOQUE and SAPIEN M3 adoption expanded. Full-year TMTT guidance rose to $780–$790M of sales.

$0.80

Adjusted EPS beat the GAAP story

Adjusted EPS of $0.80 grew 19%, while GAAP EPS of $0.53 was weighed down by the $220M impairment. Edwards raised full-year sales growth guidance to about 11.5% and adjusted EPS guidance to $2.97–$3.05.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $220M impairment of an in-process research-and-development asset and fixed assets acquired in an acquisition is the main reason GAAP operating margin was 17.4% versus 30.3% adjusted.
  • A $13M net litigation insurance recovery this quarter compares with a $90M litigation expense a year ago — a swing that flatters the year-on-year comparison.

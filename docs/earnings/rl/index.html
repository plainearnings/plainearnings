Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Revenue rose to $2.19B

Net revenues rose 9% to $2.19B, with growth in every region, led by Asia at +28%.

74.2%

Gross margin widened sharply

Gross profit was $1.63B on $2.19B of sales — a 74.2% gross margin, up from 67.9% a year earlier — helped by strong full-price demand and lower product costs this quarter.

$297M

Net income jumped 43%

Net income rose to $297M from $208M a year earlier, and the company raised its fiscal 2027 revenue and operating margin outlook.

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

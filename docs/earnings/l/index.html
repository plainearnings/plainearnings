Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$125M

A reserve charge hit CNA

CNA's annual long-term care reserve review produced an unfavorable $125M impact (after tax and noncontrolling interests). CNA's core income fell to $203M from $409M, and the P&C combined ratio rose to 97.0% from 92.8% on higher catastrophe losses and loss-cost trends. Loews notes the charge should be almost entirely offset over time by higher investment yields.

+13%

Boardwalk kept growing

Pipeline revenue rose 13% to $616M on higher gas transportation rates and utilization. Net income of $85M included about $14M (after tax) of one-off charges — unrealized losses at Continuum and an office-relocation impairment; excluding them, results improved.

$167M

Buybacks continue

Loews repurchased 1.6 million of its own shares for $167M in the quarter and held $4.4B of cash and investments at the parent. Book value per share excluding AOCI rose to $100.67, from $95.89 at the end of 2025.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • CNA's annual long-term care reserve assumption update: an unfavorable $125M impact on Loews' share of CNA's earnings (after tax and noncontrolling interests) — the single biggest reason profit fell year over year.
  • Boardwalk took about $14M (after tax) of charges: $7M of net unrealized losses at Continuum and a $7M impairment tied to relocating its corporate office.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

Bookings value accelerated

Gross booking value rose 16% to $27.2B on 10% more nights and seats booked and a moderate rise in nightly rates — growth accelerated in core markets like the U.S., France, the UK and Australia.

$816M

Profit jumped on two boosts

Net income rose from $642M to $816M (a 23% margin). Higher operating income did most of the work, and a $77M benefit related to prior-year taxes — from newly published guidance — helped too.

$1.3B

A third of revenue became free cash

Free cash flow was $1.3B, 35% of revenue, and $4.8B over the last twelve months — guests pay upfront, hosts are paid after check-in.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The tax line includes a $77M benefit recorded in the quarter related to recently published guidance impacting prior-year taxes; without it, the tax bill would have been higher and net income lower.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

155M

Bookings accelerated

Nights and seats booked grew 16% — faster than last quarter — and gross booking value rose 21% to $27.7B as the average daily rate climbed to $179.

39%

A very profitable model

Net margin was 39%. Airbnb collects guest money before the stay and owns no hotels, so growth converts unusually cleanly into profit and $1.4B of free cash flow.

$283M

Tax helped the comparison

Taxes fell to $283M from $418M a year ago, when Airbnb booked a $213M valuation allowance against tax credits — part of why net income grew 38% on 19% revenue growth.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

121.9M

Bookings accelerated

Nights and seats booked grew 10% — the strongest growth quarter of the year — and gross booking value rose 16% to $20.4B as the average daily rate climbed 6% to $168.

12%

Profit shrank while revenue grew

Net income fell to $341M from $461M a year ago as planned investments in new growth and policy initiatives, plus about $90M of non-income tax matters, outweighed 12% revenue growth. Net margin fell to 12% from 19%.

13.6%

A smaller cut of each booking

Airbnb's implied take rate — revenue divided by gross booking value — slipped to 13.6% from 14.1% a year ago, as it simplified fees by moving hosts to a single 15.5% service fee, alongside FX and booking-timing effects.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • About $90M of non-income tax matters weighed on the quarter — Airbnb says planned investments in new growth and policy initiatives plus these tax charges are why net income fell even as revenue grew 12%.

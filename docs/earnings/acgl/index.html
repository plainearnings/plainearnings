Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$702M

Underwriting carried the quarter

Underwriting income of $702M (insurance $108M, reinsurance $393M, mortgage $201M) with a combined ratio excluding catastrophes and prior-year development of 81.2%.

$3.00

Profit down on investment marks

EPS of $3.00 was down from $3.56 a year ago: investments swung to $99M of realized losses (vs $210M of gains) as rates rose, even though underwriting improved.

$1.4B

Huge buyback quarter

Arch returned $1.4B via buybacks in one quarter — a big reason book value per share slipped 0.2% sequentially to $67.90 despite a profitable quarter.

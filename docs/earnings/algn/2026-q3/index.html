Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

16.7%

Profit grew from the cost line

Operating margin jumped from 9.7% to 16.7% — almost all of it cost: manufacturing cost fell $39M and restructuring charges halved. GAAP EPS more than doubled to $1.90.

690.6k

Volumes are back

Aligner case volume rose 6.6% — record Q3 shipments, record doctor submitters, and North America orthodontist shipments grew year over year for the first time in six quarters.

-7.6%

Scanners: units up, revenue down

Scanner and software revenue fell 7.6% even as placements grew double digits — more systems are leased/rented/pre-owned, which spreads the revenue out instead of booking it upfront.

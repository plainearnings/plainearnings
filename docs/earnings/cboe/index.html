Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Options are booming

Net revenue — what Cboe keeps after paying liquidity and clearing costs — rose 18% to a record $712M. Options net revenue rose 23% as index options volumes grew 24% on record same-day-expiry (0DTE) trading.

$4.08

A record quarter, with help

GAAP EPS hit a record $4.08, up 43%. About $0.68 of that came from the one-time $71.5M gain on selling Cboe Australia; adjusted EPS of $3.63 was still up 36%.

High teens

Guidance went up

Cboe raised its 2026 organic net revenue growth target to 'high teens' from 'mid to high teens', lifted Data Vantage to 'mid teens', and lowered its adjusted expense guide to $830–845M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $71.5M gain on the sale of Cboe Australia, completed in the quarter, is included in GAAP results. Excluding it and other adjustments, adjusted EPS was $3.63 (non-GAAP) versus GAAP EPS of $4.08.
  • Operating expenses include a $14.0M reserve on notes receivable connected with funding the Consolidated Audit Trail (the SEC's market-surveillance system), and a $1.4M asset impairment.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

Plumbing carries the growth

Plumbing Products sales rose 4% to $1.3B (5% in local currency before a divestiture), while paint and coatings were flat at $628M.

17.9%

Margins did the heavy lifting

Adjusted operating margin widened to 17.9% from 15.9% a year ago — pricing and cost discipline turned 3% sales growth into 23% adjusted EPS growth.

$322M

Cash keeps flowing back

Masco returned $322M to shareholders in the quarter through dividends and share repurchases.

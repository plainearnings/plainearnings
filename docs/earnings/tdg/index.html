Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Growth in every channel

Net sales rose 23% to $2,741M — 13% organic plus acquisitions — with commercial aftermarket up 17% and double-digit growth in commercial OEM and defense as well.

52.8%

Margins stay exceptionally high

EBITDA As Defined was $1,447M, or 52.8 cents of every sales dollar, down slightly from 54.4% only because newly bought businesses carry lower margins at first.

$514M

Interest is the big cost after parts

Interest expense, net, rose 30% to $514M as borrowings grew to fund deals such as the $2.2B Jet Parts Engineering and Victor Sierra purchase — the price of TransDigm's debt-funded model.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $35M of acquisition transaction and integration-related expenses were added back in the company's adjusted figures (GAAP results include them).

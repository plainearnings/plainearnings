Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

Health premiums are the growth engine

Health premium rose 16% to $447M — Family Heritage health underwriting margin rose 12% and United American had an underwriting loss on Medicare Supplement claims timing — while life premium grew a steadier 2%.

$4.84

Operating EPS vs $4.62 GAAP

Net operating income — Globe Life's preferred measure, which excludes realized investment results — was $379M, or $4.84 a share. GAAP EPS of $4.62 includes a $10.0M after-tax realized loss and $6.8M of legal-proceedings costs.

$146M

Reserve reviews flatter benefits

Policyholder benefits include about $146M of pre-tax favorable remeasurement gains from the annual assumption update ($117M life, $29M health) — slightly less than last year's $158M, which is the main reason profit dipped.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Benefits are net of total remeasurement gains of $145.8M before tax from the annual reserve-assumption update ($116.7M life, $29.1M health), versus $158.3M a year ago.
  • Realized investment losses were $12.6M before tax; legal-proceedings costs were $6.8M after tax (both excluded from net operating income).
  • Globe Life repurchased 1.1M shares in the quarter for $185M.

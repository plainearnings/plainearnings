Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

3.19%

The lending margin widened

Net interest margin (fully taxable-equivalent) rose to 3.19%, up 19 bps from a year ago, driving net interest income up 13% to $1,675M. Average loans grew, led by the Private Bank.

0.37%

Credit stayed calm

Net charge-offs were 0.37% of average loans, stable vs last quarter, and the provision for credit losses fell to $125M from $154M a year ago. The allowance covers 1.47% of loans.

$0.52

More cash back to owners

Citizens raised its quarterly dividend 13% to $0.52 per share and expanded its buyback capacity to $1.95B. The Private Bank contributed $0.16 of the quarter's $1.41 EPS, with spot deposits of $18.5B.

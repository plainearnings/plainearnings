Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$53.2B

Record backlog

Orders of $19.3B (book-to-bill 3.2x) took the backlog to a record $53.2B, led by Missile Solutions at 11.8x — including $6.3B for THAAD propulsion and $4.7B for PAC-3 MSE propulsion, both seven-year deals to scale up production.

13.0%

Margins expanded

Operating margin rose to 13.0% from 11.0% a year ago as G&A fell to $826M from $873M and volumes grew. Segment operating margin held at 15.9%.

$1.3B

Cash flow surged

Free cash flow was $1.3B, up 195% year over year, and management raised full-year guidance: revenue of $23.5B–$23.7B and GAAP EPS of $12.25–$12.35.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net income was $632M, but $19M of subsidiary preferred stock deemed dividends reduced income available to common shareholders to $613M — the diluted EPS of $3.28 is calculated on that $613M.

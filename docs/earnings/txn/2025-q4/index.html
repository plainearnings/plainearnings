Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Analog did the lifting

Analog revenue rose 14% to $3.62B — 82% of all sales — while Embedded Processing grew 8% to $662M and Other fell 34% to $146M.

55.9%

Margins eased

Gross margin slipped to 55.9% from 57.8% a year ago, but the revenue growth still lifted operating profit 7% to $1.47B.

$1,693M

Cash returned to owners

TI returned $1,693M in the quarter — $1,290M of dividends and $403M of buybacks — against $1,329M of free cash flow.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Earnings per share included a 6-cent reduction that was not in the company's original guidance, as stated in the release.
  • The $32M of restructuring charges/other is reported inside the 'Other' segment, whose operating profit fell to $7M from $82M a year ago.

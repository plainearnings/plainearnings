Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Analog is driving the quarter

Analog revenue rose 22% to $3.92B with growth led by industrial and data center demand — TI's biggest business did the heavy lifting.

58.0%

Margins expanded

Gross margin reached 58.0% (from 56.9% a year ago) as revenue grew 19% while R&D and SG&A slightly fell — operating profit jumped 37%.

$1,449M

Cash returned to owners

TI returned $1,449M in the quarter — $1,291M of dividends and $158M of buybacks; trailing-12-month free cash flow was $4.4B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Earnings per share included a 5-cent benefit that was not in the company's original guidance, as stated in the release.
  • Free cash flow of $1,399M includes $555M of CHIPS Act incentive proceeds received in the quarter.

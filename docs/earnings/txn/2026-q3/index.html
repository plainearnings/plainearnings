Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+32%

Analog is driving the quarter

Analog revenue hit $4,911M, up 32% YoY — 81% of all revenue — with $2,505M of operating profit. Embedded Processing also grew 21% to $858M.

64.2%

Margins expanded sharply

Gross profit was $3,887M, or 64.2% of revenue, up from 57.4% a year ago, because revenue grew 28% while cost of revenue rose only about 7%.

$2,956M

Cash generation stayed very strong

Cash flow from operations was $3,419M in Q3 and free cash flow (operations less capital spending, plus any CHIPS Act incentives) was $2,956M, after $463M of capital expenditures.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquisition charges were $7M in the quarter and restructuring charges/other were $0 (versus $85M a year ago) — both are included in the $2,874M operating profit.
  • Earnings per share included a 1-cent reduction that was not in the company's original guidance, as stated in the release.

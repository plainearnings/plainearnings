Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$24.2B

Services hit another record

Services revenue reached $24.2B, an all-time high, up 14% — more than a quarter of everything Apple sells now comes from subscriptions, the App Store and other services.

+24%

iPad bounced back

iPad sales jumped 24% to $7.2B after the May launch of the redesigned iPad Pro and iPad Air — the lineup's first new models in over a year.

$1.40

Profit rose even with iPhone flat

Net income rose 8% to $21.4B and EPS reached $1.40, helped by the high-margin Services mix; Apple Intelligence, unveiled in June, was still to come in the fall.

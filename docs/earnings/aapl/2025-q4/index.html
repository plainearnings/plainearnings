Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$28.8B

Services set another all-time high

Services reached $28.8B, up 15%, and it is Apple's most profitable money: delivering those services cost $7.1B, against $47.0B of cost on $73.7B of product sales. The installed base of active devices hit a new all-time high.

$416.2B

A record fiscal year

Fiscal 2025 revenue was a record $416.2B and net income was $112.0B. Apple bought back $90.7B of its own stock during the year.

+13%

EPS growth, on a like-for-like basis

GAAP EPS of $1.85 is up 91% — but only because the year-ago quarter absorbed a $10.2B one-time tax charge. Excluding that charge from the comparison, Apple says EPS grew 13%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago comparison is distorted: in Q4 FY24 Apple booked a one-time $10.2B income-tax charge after the European Commission's State Aid decision was reinstated, which cut that quarter's EPS to $0.97. This quarter's GAAP EPS of $1.85 is up 91% against it; on the adjusted basis Apple cites, EPS grew 13%.

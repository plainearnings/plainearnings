Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Services set another record

Services revenue reached an all-time high of $26.3B, up 14% — more than a fifth of everything Apple sells, at much higher margins than hardware.

46.9%

Margins hit a record

Gross margin reached 46.9% of revenue, and EPS rose 10% to an all-time record $2.40 — the mix keeps shifting toward Services, which keeps far more of each dollar than hardware.

-1%

iPhone took a small step back

iPhone sales slipped 1% to $69.1B and Greater China revenue fell 11%, even as Mac (+16%) and iPad (+15%) more than covered the gap.

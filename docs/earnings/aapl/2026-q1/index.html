Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$85.3B

iPhone's best quarter ever

iPhone revenue hit $85.3B, up 23% — an all-time record, with records in every geographic segment. iPhone alone was 59% of everything Apple sold.

$30.0B

Services set another all-time high

Services reached $30.0B, up 14%, and it is Apple's most profitable money: delivering those services cost $7.0B, against $67.5B of cost on $113.7B of product sales. The installed base passed 2.5 billion active devices.

$53.9B

Record cash funded big returns

Operating cash flow was nearly $54B in the quarter, and Apple returned almost $32B to shareholders — $24.7B of buybacks plus dividends.

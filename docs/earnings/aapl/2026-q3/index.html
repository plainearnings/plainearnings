Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

The iPhone had a record June quarter

iPhone revenue rose 22% to $54.3B — half of Apple's total sales — and Mac jumped 29%. Only iPad fell, down 6% against last year's launch-boosted quarter.

$30.7B

Services set another record

Services revenue hit $30.7B, up 12%. Services cost only $7.5B to deliver, so this segment quietly supplies an outsized share of Apple's profit.

50.1%

Half of every sales dollar survives direct costs

Gross margin was $54.8B on $109.4B of sales — a 50.1% gross margin — and Apple kept $29.8B as net profit, 27 cents of every revenue dollar.

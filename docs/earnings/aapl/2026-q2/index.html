Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$57.0B

A record March quarter for iPhone

iPhone revenue hit $57.0B, up 22% — a March-quarter record, powered by the iPhone 17 lineup. iPhone alone was 51% of everything Apple sold.

$31.0B

Services set another all-time high

Services reached $31.0B, up 16%, and it is Apple's most profitable money: delivering those services cost $7.2B, against $49.2B of cost on $80.2B of product sales.

$100B

A fresh buyback and a higher dividend

The board authorized a new $100B share-repurchase program and raised the dividend 4% to $0.27 a share. Operating cash flow topped $28B in the quarter — both it and EPS were March-quarter records.

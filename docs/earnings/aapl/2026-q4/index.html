Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

iPhone 17 drove a record quarter

iPhone revenue hit a September-quarter record $57.6B, up 17% on the iPhone 17 lineup — half of everything Apple sold.

$31.6B

Services is the quiet giant

Services set an all-time high at $31.6B, up 10% — nearly 28% of revenue, and the steadiest growth Apple has.

+1pt

The margin got a one-off lift

Gross margin of 47.9% included about 1 point from tariff refunds, worth $0.05 of EPS — a one-off, not the run rate.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Tariff refunds added approximately 1 percentage point to gross margin and $0.05 to diluted EPS in the quarter — a one-off benefit called out in the release, not recurring run-rate profit.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

Ready-to-drink is the growth engine

Ready-to-drink sales rose 20% to $154M, led by New Mix, up 48% on strong demand in Mexico and its U.S. launch — enough to offset much of the weakness elsewhere.

72%

Whiskey still pays the bills

Whiskey was flat at $658M — 72% of sales. The Jack Daniel's Tennessee Blackberry launch and flat Tennessee Whiskey offset declines in Tennessee Honey (-10%) and Gentleman Jack (-16%).

+11%

Emerging markets carried the map

Emerging-market sales rose 11% (Mexico +26% on New Mix), while the United States fell 3% and Developed International fell 6% on weaker Jack Daniel's volumes in Germany, France and Spain.

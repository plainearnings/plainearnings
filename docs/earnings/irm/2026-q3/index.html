Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30%

Growth businesses are carrying growth

Data center, digital and asset lifecycle management together grew over 30%; Iron Mountain signed 102MW of data-center leases in the quarter, 137MW year to date.

$1.53

Cash profit hit a record

AFFO — the cash yardstick REIT investors watch — rose 17% to a record $458M, or $1.53 per share.

+15%

Dividend raised

Full-year guidance was raised, and the quarterly dividend goes up 15% to $0.993 per share, paid January 6, 2027.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-52%

Profit more than halved

Net earnings fell to $284M ($1.19 per share) from $591M ($2.29) a year ago. With the 30-year mortgage rate near 6.8% at quarter-end, Lennar kept volume up with incentives — which protected deliveries but squeezed profit per home.

15.8%

Margins are the story

The gross margin on home sales was 15.8%, down from about 17.5% a year ago, even as construction cost per square foot improved 6% year over year and build cycle time hit a record-low 116 days.

20,879

Orders fell 9%

New orders dropped 9% to 20,879 homes, and the backlog stands at 16,857 homes worth $6.3B — a thinner cushion if high rates keep buyers on the sidelines.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Lennar Other lost $84M, driven by $53M of mark-to-market losses on technology investments (a year ago the same line gained $99M).
  • Financial Services costs include $39M, net, of one-time items, primarily a litigation accrual reversal from a favorable court judgment — a benefit, not a cost.
  • Excluding the technology-investment losses and the Financial Services one-time items, net earnings were $294M ($1.23 per share) versus $284M ($1.19) GAAP.

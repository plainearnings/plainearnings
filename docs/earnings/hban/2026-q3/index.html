Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+37%

Interest income surged on scale

Net interest income rose 37% to $2.07 billion as the Cadence and Veritex acquisitions added loans and deposits, and the margin widened to 3.25%.

$0.38

Adjusted EPS excludes deal costs

Excluding notable items, mostly acquisition-related expenses worth $0.05 a share, adjusted EPS was $0.38 and adjusted return on tangible common equity was 16.6%.

$146M

Charge-offs rose

Net charge-offs increased to $146 million from $75 million a year ago, a credit cost to watch as the combined loan book seasons.

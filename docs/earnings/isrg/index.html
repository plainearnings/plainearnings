Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Procedure growth is the story

Worldwide procedures grew about 15% (da Vinci +14%, Ion +31%), pulling instrument revenue up 18% and expanding the installed base 12% to 12,094 systems.

$450M

One charge halved GAAP profit

Settling antitrust class-action litigation cost $450M before tax ($347M, or $0.99 a share, after tax). Non-GAAP net income, which excludes it, rose to $1.05B from $867M.

$2.94B

Buybacks at full speed

Intuitive repurchased 7.8 million shares for $2.94B in the quarter — more than five times its GAAP net income.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $450M pre-tax charge (inside SG&A, which shows $455M of litigation charges) to settle previously disclosed antitrust class-action litigation; $347M after tax, or $0.99 per share.
  • Cost of revenue includes a $28M benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds for tariffs paid in prior periods ($22M of benefit to net income after tax).

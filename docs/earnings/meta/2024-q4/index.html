Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Ads did the lifting

Advertising revenue grew 21% to $46.8B — 97 cents of every dollar Meta made — helped by 6% more ad views and a 14% higher average price per ad.

$1.55B

A legal adjustment flattered profit

Meta cut what it had set aside for certain legal cases by $1.55B, a one-off boost that sits inside G&A and flatters this quarter's profit; the underlying business was still strongly profitable.

-$5.0B

Reality Labs still burns cash

Reality Labs lost $5.0B in the quarter on $1.1B of revenue — the metaverse and headset bet is still funded entirely by the ad business.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Inside the small $761M G&A line is a favorable $1.55B adjustment: Meta reduced what it had previously set aside for certain legal cases. Without it, operating income would have been about $21.8B instead of $23.4B.

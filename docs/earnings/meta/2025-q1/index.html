Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Higher ad prices did the work

Ad impressions rose 5% and the average price per ad rose 10%, powered by AI ad tools — advertising brought in $41.4B of the $42.3B total.

41%

Margins widened again

Operating margin reached 41%, up from 38% a year ago, because revenue grew 16% while total costs grew only 9%.

$4.2B

Reality Labs still burns cash

The headsets-and-glasses unit lost $4,210M in one quarter on just $412M of revenue — the price Meta pays to build its next platform while the ad business funds it.

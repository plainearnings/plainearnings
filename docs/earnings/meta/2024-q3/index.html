Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+19%

Ads did the lifting

Advertising revenue grew 19% to $39.9B — 98 cents of every dollar Meta made — helped by 7% more ad views and an 11% higher average price per ad.

-$4.4B

Reality Labs still burns cash

Reality Labs lost $4.4B in the quarter on just $270M of revenue, and Meta said its losses would grow meaningfully in 2024 as it keeps building headsets and glasses.

$15.5B

Cash generation stayed enormous

Free cash flow was $15.5B even after $9.2B of capital spending, and Meta raised its full-year capex outlook to $38–40B, mostly for AI computing.

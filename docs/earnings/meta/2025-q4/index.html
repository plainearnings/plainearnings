Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Ads kept growing fast

Advertising revenue rose 24% to $58.1B: ad impressions grew 18% and the average price per ad rose 6%. The Family of Apps earned $30.8B of operating profit.

-$6.0B

Reality Labs losses widened

Reality Labs lost $6.0B at the operating level on $955M of revenue, which fell 12%. Across 2025 the unit lost $19.2B; Meta expects similar losses in 2026.

$22.1B

The AI bill keeps climbing

Capital spending was $22.1B in the quarter ($72.2B for 2025), and free cash flow was $14.1B. For 2026 Meta plans capex of $115-135B and total expenses of $162-169B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Ads did the lifting

Advertising revenue grew 22% to $38.3B — 98 cents of every dollar Meta made — helped by 10% more ad views and a 10% higher average price per ad.

-$4.5B

Reality Labs still burns cash

Reality Labs lost $4.5B in the quarter on just $353M of revenue, and Meta said it expects the segment's operating losses to keep growing as it builds headsets and glasses.

$10.9B

Cash generation stayed enormous

Free cash flow was $10.9B even after $8.5B of capital spending, and Meta raised its full-year capex outlook to $37–40B, mostly for AI computing.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1.4B charge for Meta's settlement with the State of Texas (over its past use of facial-recognition data) sits inside general & administrative costs. Without it, operating income would have been about $16.2B instead of $14.8B.

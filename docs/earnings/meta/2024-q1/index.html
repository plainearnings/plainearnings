Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+27%

Ads did the lifting

Advertising revenue grew 27% to $35.6B — 98 cents of every dollar Meta made — helped by 20% more ad views and a 6% higher average price per ad.

38%

The efficiency year paid off

Costs rose just 6% while revenue rose 27%, so operating margin widened to 38% from 25% a year ago and net income more than doubled to $12.4B — free cash flow was $12.5B.

-$3.8B

Reality Labs still burns cash

Reality Labs lost $3.8B in the quarter on $440M of revenue — slightly less than the $4.0B it lost a year ago, but still the one big drag on an otherwise highly profitable company.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Impairment charges of $240M for facilities consolidation — part of Meta's ongoing shrink of its office footprint — are inside the quarter's costs, per the cash-flow statement in the release.

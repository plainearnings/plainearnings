Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+25%

Ads surged in the holiday quarter

Advertising revenue rose 24% to $38.7B — 97 cents of every dollar Meta made — on 21% more ad impressions and a 2% higher average price per ad. Reality Labs chipped in $1,071M of revenue but lost $4,646M.

41%

The efficiency year paid off

Revenue rose 25% while total costs and expenses fell 8%, so operating margin widened to 41% from 20% a year ago and net income tripled to $14.0B — free cash flow was $11.5B, and Meta ended the year with $65.4B of cash and marketable securities.

$0.50

A first-ever dividend

Meta declared its first-ever dividend — $0.50 a share, payable March 26, 2024 — and added $50B to its buyback authorization, after repurchasing $6.32B of stock in the quarter. Headcount was 67,317, down 22% from a year earlier.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges of $1,148M in the quarter — mostly facilities consolidation as Meta shrank its office footprint — are inside the quarter's costs; the 'year of efficiency' total for 2023 was $3,452M.

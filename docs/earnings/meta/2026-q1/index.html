Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+33%

Ads are surging

Advertising revenue rose 33% to $55.0B: ad impressions grew 19% and the average price per ad rose 12%. The Family of Apps earned $26.9B of operating profit, while Reality Labs lost $4.0B.

$8.0B

A one-time tax benefit inflated profit

Net income includes an $8.03B tax benefit created by a U.S. Treasury notice on how the corporate minimum tax treats past R&D spending. Without it, EPS would have been $3.13 lower — $7.31 instead of $10.44.

$19.8B

The AI build-out keeps accelerating

Capital spending was $19.8B in the quarter, and Meta raised its full-year 2026 capex guide to $125-145B, citing higher component prices and more data-center capacity for future years.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $8.03B income tax benefit (U.S. Treasury Notice 2026-7, on the Corporate Alternative Minimum Tax treatment of previously capitalized U.S. R&D costs) is inside this quarter's tax line, turning it into a $5,021M benefit. It partly offsets the $15.93B non-cash tax charge Meta recorded in Q3 2025 when the One Big Beautiful Bill Act was enacted. Excluding the benefit, diluted EPS would have been $3.13 lower.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+26%

Ads are still a money machine

Advertising grew 26% to $63.1B — 98% of all revenue — on 10% more impressions and 14% higher prices.

$9.9B

One settlement cut profit by a third

A $9.9B charge to settle teen mental-health litigation with state attorneys general cut operating profit 37%. Without it, EPS would have been $7.40, not $3.83.

-$4.1B

Reality Labs still burns billions

The metaverse division lost $4.1B this quarter on just $443M of revenue — and Meta now expects $18-19B of Reality Labs losses for the full year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Q3 2026 G&A includes a $9,890M charge for the August 2026 agreement with US State Attorneys General resolving teen mental-health/social-media litigation (payments of up to ~$18,590M in total). Excluding it: operating income would have been $22,880M, diluted EPS $7.40, effective tax rate 17% (vs 24% reported). Prior-year Q3 net income was itself depressed by an outsized $18,954M tax provision (87% effective tax rate), so the +264% net-income growth overstates the underlying trend.

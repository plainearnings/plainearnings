Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

The ad machine is still growing fast

Revenue rose 28% to $60.8B — impressions up 14%, price per ad up 12%, and 3.60 billion people use a Meta app daily.

-8%

But profit fell anyway

Total costs jumped 55%: R&D up 67% to $21.7B, plus $2.4B of legal charges and $1.18B of severance from the May layoffs (about 8,000 roles). Operating income fell 8% to $18.8B, and Reality Labs lost another $4.6B.

$784M

Free cash flow nearly vanished

Operations generated $31.9B of cash, but data-center and AI spending consumed almost all of it — free cash flow was just $784M. Meta now guides 2026 capex (incl. finance leases) to $130–145B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $2.40B of charges related to legal proceedings are inside this quarter's costs (in general & administrative), and $1.18B of severance from the May 2026 headcount reduction is spread across the cost lines. Meta raised its full-year expense outlook to $165–169B to absorb the legal charges.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

The ad engine is running hot

Ad impressions rose 11% and the average price per ad rose 9%, powered by AI ad tools — advertising brought in $46.6B of the $47.5B total.

43%

Margins widened sharply

Operating margin reached 43%, up from 38% a year ago, because revenue grew 22% while total costs grew only 12%.

$4.5B

Reality Labs still burns cash

The headsets-and-glasses unit lost $4,530M in one quarter on just $370M of revenue — the price Meta pays to build its next platform while the ad business funds it.

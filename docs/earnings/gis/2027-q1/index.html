Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.05B

Last year's gain haunts the comparison

A year ago General Mills booked a $1,054M gain on the U.S. yogurt divestiture. Without it, operating profit fell 11% in constant currency (adjusted) rather than the reported 63% — still a decline, driven by the lost yogurt sales.

0%

Organic sales were flat

Organic net sales were flat: volume fell 4 points and price/mix added 1 point (with a divestiture drag on reported volume). North America Retail fell 7% reported; International grew 4%.

$0.75

Guidance held

Adjusted EPS of $0.75 fell 13% in constant currency, and the company reaffirmed its full-year fiscal 2027 outlook — betting on new products and brand investment to restart growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter included a $1,054.4M gain on the divestiture of the U.S. yogurt business; this quarter has no divestiture gain, which explains most of the reported profit decline.
  • Restructuring, transformation, impairment and other exit costs were $21.4M in the quarter.

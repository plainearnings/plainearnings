Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8.6B

Keytruda still carries the company

Keytruda and its new QLEX version grew 6% to $8.6B — 48% of revenue. The QLEX launch ($830M already) matters: it can extend the franchise as the original's patents near expiry.

+93%

The next generation is arriving

WINREVAIR nearly doubled to $694M and GARDASIL grew 14% to $2.0B — the products Merck is counting on to soften the eventual Keytruda cliff.

-12%

Deal costs hit GAAP profit

Net income fell 12% despite 5% sales growth, on $1.5B of acquisition and restructuring charges inside cost of sales. The 8.9% effective tax rate (vs 14.2%) cushioned the fall.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Cost of sales includes $1,140M of acquisition- and divestiture-related costs and $396M of restructuring — the main reason GAAP EPS fell while sales grew. Non-GAAP EPS, which excludes these and a $393M equity-investment gain, was $2.49.

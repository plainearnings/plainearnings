Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2%

The core engine is steady

Net interest income rose 2% to $1.8B — loan and deposit balances are growing slowly, and the taxable-equivalent margin held up.

$135M

Credit is a watch item, not a problem

The provision for credit losses rose to $135M from $125M a year ago — a modest build as the loan book grows, and the main reason profit dipped despite steady revenue.

$5.04

EPS held up better than profit

Diluted EPS was $5.04 on $732M available to common shareholders; buybacks keep shrinking the share count, cushioning small profit declines.

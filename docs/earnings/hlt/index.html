Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.8%

Rooms earned more

System-wide revenue per available room (RevPAR) rose 5.8% currency-neutral, led by the U.S. at +7.0% — guests paid more and filled more rooms.

543,100

The pipeline keeps growing

Hilton approved 30,500 more rooms for development, taking the pipeline to 543,100 rooms (+5% YoY), and added a net 23,000 rooms in the quarter — net unit growth of 6.0%. Future fee income is being built now.

$879M

Shareholders got paid

Hilton returned $879 million in the quarter through buybacks (2.6 million shares) and dividends, and expects about $3.5 billion of capital return for the full year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Hilton announced on October 20, 2026 that it will move its stock listing to Nasdaq (same ticker, HLT) effective November 5, 2026 — a listing change only, with no effect on the numbers.

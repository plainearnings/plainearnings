Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+112%

Revenue more than doubled

Revenue hit $2,078M, up from $982M a year ago, and the revenue backlog — work customers have committed to — reached about $99.4B at quarter-end, with new Meta and Anthropic agreements signed in the quarter.

$536M

Interest is what makes the loss bigger

Operations lost $144M, but net interest expense was $536M. After $24M of other income and $84M of tax, the net loss was $740M — the price of borrowing to build ahead of demand.

$7.7B

Spending to build is enormous

CoreWeave spent $7,695M on property and equipment in the quarter — about 3.7 times its revenue — while operating cash flow was $2,984M. Active power passed 1 GW, with over 3.5 GW contracted.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation of $153M is stripped out of the adjusted figures: the non-GAAP reconciliation removes $153M of stock-based compensation, $11M of amortization of acquired intangibles and $1M of acquisition-related costs on the way from the GAAP net loss of $740M to the adjusted net loss of $589M. There was no fair-value gain or loss this quarter.

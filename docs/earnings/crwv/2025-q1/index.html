Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+420%

Growth exploded in the first public quarter

Revenue rose 420% to $982M from $189M a year ago, and revenue backlog reached $25.9B — including the $11.2B strategic deal with OpenAI announced in the quarter.

$606M

The cloud itself is profitable before interest

Adjusted EBITDA was $606M, a 62% margin, and adjusted operating income was $163M. The GAAP operating loss of $27M includes the one-off $177M IPO share-pay charge.

$264M

Debt costs turned operations into a net loss

Net interest expense of $264M on the borrowing behind CoreWeave's GPU fleet drove the $315M net loss (EPS -$1.49 diluted), even as demand kept accelerating.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation: $177M of share-pay expense came from awards whose liquidity-event vesting condition was satisfied at CoreWeave's IPO in March 2025 — a one-off spike that sits mostly in general and administrative costs.

Interest on the debt that funds CoreWeave's datacenters is the big leak: other expense — mostly $267M of net interest — took $21.60 of every $100 of revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+207%

Revenue more than tripled

Revenue rose 207% to $1,213M as CoreWeave raced to add GPU capacity, ending the quarter with about 470 MW of active power. Revenue backlog reached $30.1B, including a $4B expansion deal with OpenAI on top of the earlier $11.9B agreement.

$267M

Interest turned a profit into a loss

The core business earned $19M of operating income, but $267M of net interest expense — the cost of borrowing to build datacenters — plus a $48M tax charge produced a $291M GAAP net loss. On the company's adjusted basis, operating income was $200M.

$753M

The rental economics still show through

Adjusted EBITDA was $753M, a 62% margin — the cash earnings of the datacenters before interest, tax and the heavy upfront cost of the machines themselves, which is exactly the bill the interest line represents.

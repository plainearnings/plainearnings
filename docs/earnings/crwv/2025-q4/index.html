Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+110%

Revenue more than doubled

Revenue hit $1,572M, up from $747M a year ago, and the revenue backlog — work customers have committed to — reached $66.8B at quarter-end, more than four times where the year began.

$388M

Interest is what makes the loss bigger

Operations lost $89M, but net interest expense was $388M. After $10M of other income and a $15M tax benefit, the net loss was $452M — the price of borrowing to build ahead of demand.

$4.1B

Spending to build is enormous

CoreWeave spent $4,060M on property and equipment in the quarter — about 2.6 times its revenue — while operating cash flow was $1,559M. Active power passed 850 MW after adding about 260 MW in the quarter, with about 3.1 GW contracted.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation of $157M is stripped out of the adjusted figures: the non-GAAP reconciliation removes $157M of stock-based compensation, $11M of amortization of acquired intangibles, $9M of acquisition-related costs and a $4M loss on extinguishment of debt on the way from the GAAP net loss of $452M to the adjusted net loss of $284M. There was no fair-value gain or loss this quarter. Adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) was $88M and adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $898M, a 57% margin.

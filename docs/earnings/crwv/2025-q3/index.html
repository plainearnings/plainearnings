Interest and other non-operating costs took $21.15 of every $100 — more than the quarter's entire operating profit. A $127M tax benefit added back $9.28, but the business still lost $8.07 of every $100 it took in.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+134%

Revenue more than doubled, again

Revenue hit $1,365M, up from $584M a year ago, and the revenue backlog — work customers have committed to — almost doubled in one quarter to more than $55B.

$52M

An operating profit, swallowed by interest

The core business earned $52M of operating income, but interest expense, net of $311M on the debt used to build data centers pushed the quarter to a $237M pre-tax loss. A $127M tax benefit cut the final net loss to $110M.

$838M

Cash earnings look very different

Adjusted EBITDA — a non-GAAP cash-earnings view that strips out interest, taxes and non-cash costs — was $838M, a 61% margin, and adjusted operating income was $217M versus $52M on a GAAP basis.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The tax line was a $127M benefit, not a bill — without it, the net loss would have been $237M instead of $110M.
  • Unlike a year ago, there was no fair-value adjustment this quarter; Q3 2024's results carried a $341M fair-value loss, which is a big reason the year-ago net loss was $360M.

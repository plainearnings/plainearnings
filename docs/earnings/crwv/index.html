The striking thing: interest and other non-operating costs took $20 of every $100 — almost as much as the $24.31 lost overall. The business was nearly breakeven before the cost of its debt.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+112%

Revenue more than doubled

Revenue hit $2,575M, up from $1,212M a year ago, and the revenue backlog — work customers have committed to — reached about $104B at quarter-end, with over $25B more added in early Q3.

$640M

Interest is what makes it a loss

Operations lost only $49M, but net interest expense was $640M. After $125M of other income and $62M of tax, the net loss was $626M — the price of borrowing to build ahead of demand.

$6.4B

Spending to build is enormous

CoreWeave spent $6,422M on property and equipment in the quarter — about 2.5 times its revenue — while operating cash flow was $679M. Active power grew by nearly 500 MW to 1.5 GW, with about 3.7 GW contracted.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income included an unrealized investment gain: the non-GAAP reconciliation removes $109M of 'other adjustments' — described as primarily a net unrealized gain on investments — on the way from the GAAP net loss of $626M to the adjusted net loss of $567M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Every segment grew

Revenue rose to $4,531M from $4,146M. Natural gas transport volumes rose to 49,273 BBtu/d, and management credited favorable contributions across all business segments for the earnings growth.

$764M

Profit rose 22% despite a charge

Net income attributable to Kinder Morgan rose to $764M ($0.34 per share) from $628M, even after a $138M legal and environmental accrual. Adjusted EPS was $0.35.

$545M

Free cash flow covered most of the dividend

Free cash flow was $545M after $1,045M of capital spending; dividends paid were $665M, leaving FCF after dividends of -$120M for the quarter (positive $226M year to date).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Certain Items cut $28M from adjusted earnings on a net basis: a $138M legal, environmental and other accrual (mainly in Products Pipelines) and $3M of risk-management losses, partly offset by a $71M gain on a divestiture and $42M of related tax benefits. Adjusted net income was $792M ($0.35 per share).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+46%

Higher prices, more barrels

Revenue rose 46% to $8,549M. The composite crude price rose about 31% to $86.72 a barrel and total production rose 10% to 1,433 thousand barrels of oil equivalent a day. Crude oil alone brought in $4,392M, up 35%.

$2.8B

Profit nearly doubled

Net income rose to $2,781M from $1,471M a year ago, and diluted EPS to $5.33 from $2.70. Operating income was $3,586M on total operating expenses of $4,963M — production costs per barrel stayed controlled even as output grew. Operating cash flow was $3,813M.

$368M

Part of revenue is gains, not sales

Revenue includes $242M of gains on asset dispositions and $105M of mark-to-market derivative gains — hedging contracts repriced at quarter-end — inside the $368M 'other revenue & gains' line. Those items can swing either way from quarter to quarter, unlike production sales.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Revenue includes $242M of gains on asset dispositions and $105M of net mark-to-market gains on financial commodity and other derivative contracts — both are included in the 'Other revenue & gains' segment line rather than in production sales.
  • Expenses include $24M of impairments and $29M of dry hole costs.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

AI spending is the growth engine

Revenue rose to $4,136M from $3,210M a year ago. Semiconductor Process Control — the core business — brought in $3,754M, and management pointed to AI-driven investment in foundry, logic and advanced packaging.

$1.55B

More than a third became profit

Net income was $1,549M, a 37% margin, even after $68M of interest expense. Free cash flow was $1,336M in the quarter.

$1.24B

Cash went back to shareholders

KLA returned $1,241M in the quarter — $936M of share buybacks plus $305M of dividends — while guiding next-quarter revenue to about $4.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP net income was $1,603M ($1.22 per share) after adding back $43M of acquisition-related charges, mainly amortization of acquired intangibles.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$28.9B

Record backlog

Backlog hit a record $28.9B, up 27%, with a trailing book-to-bill of 1.4x — the AI build-out (data centers, chips, power, water) is filling the order book.

43%

Tax ate the profit growth

Operating profit rose 22%, but a temporarily elevated 43% tax rate — a side effect of the PA Consulting deal structure — left GAAP net earnings of $137M, down from $181M a year ago.

3rd raise

Guidance raised again

Jacobs raised its FY26 adjusted EPS and net revenue guidance midpoints for the third consecutive quarter, and bought back $142M of stock in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter's 43% effective tax rate was temporarily elevated by the PA Consulting acquisition transaction — the main reason GAAP earnings fell despite higher operating profit.
  • SG&A includes roughly $14M of restructuring charges for the PA Consulting transaction and $8M for the separation program.

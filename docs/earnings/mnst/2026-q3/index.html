Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

The growth is international

Sales outside the U.S. jumped 28% to $1.2B — now 47% of the company — while the core Monster Energy segment grew 17% overall.

54.6%

Margins are under pressure

Gross margin slipped to 54.6% from 55.7%, and operating expenses grew faster than sales — which is why 17% sales growth became only 8% profit growth.

-14%

The alcohol experiment keeps shrinking

Alcohol Brands sales fell 14% to $29M and lost $9M from operations. Monster excludes the segment from its adjusted results as it winds the business down.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $20M impairment on land and property in Kona, Hawaii, reclassified as held for sale, sits inside operating expenses. The Alcohol Brands segment also lost $9M at the operating line; Monster's adjusted results (EPS $0.30) exclude both.

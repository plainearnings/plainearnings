Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+25%

Eliquis and cancer drugs are carrying growth

Eliquis revenue rose 25% to $2.5B and oncology rose 10% to $4.3B, led by Padcev (+55%) and Lorbrena (+44%). Launched and acquired products grew 32% operationally.

-63%

COVID is still shrinking

Paxlovid fell 63% to $449M and Comirnaty fell 30% to $808M. Excluding both COVID products, Pfizer's revenue grew 7% operationally — the flat headline hides a growing core.

$62–64B

Guidance went up

Pfizer raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance by $1.5B at the midpoint to $62.0–$64.0B and narrowed adjusted EPS guidance to $2.95–$3.05, citing strong year-to-date performance.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $669M of acquired in-process R&D charges, including a one-time $650M charge for the in-licensing and collaboration agreement with Innovent Biologics — worth about $0.10 per share. Pfizer is buying pipeline and expensing it immediately.
  • $791M of restructuring charges and certain acquisition-related costs, up from $286M a year ago. Together with $1,128M of intangible amortization, these are the main reason GAAP EPS ($0.56) sits well below adjusted EPS ($0.87).

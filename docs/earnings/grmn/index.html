Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Record quarter, guidance raised

Revenue hit a third-quarter record of $1.99 billion, and Garmin raised its 2026 outlook to about $8.15 billion of revenue.

63.3%

Margins hit a record too

Gross margin expanded to 63.3%, helped by product mix, lower costs and about $33 million of refunds of previously paid tariffs.

-20%

Auto OEM is the one weak spot

Car-system revenue fell 20% as domain controller sales wind down ahead of the expected Mercedes-Benz program launch.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gross profit includes about $33 million of refunds of tariffs paid in earlier periods, which flattered the record 63.3% gross margin.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

25.4%

Data centers need power — Cummins sells it

Power Systems earned a 25.4% EBITDA margin on $2.3B of total sales, up 15%. Demand for power generation, especially for data centers in the US, China and Asia Pacific, is the strongest thread in the whole quarter.

$8.25

A record quarter, with a tax assist

Diluted EPS more than doubled to $8.25 — helped by $0.54 a share of favorable one-off tax items, and by comparison with a year-ago quarter that carried $240M ($1.73 a share) of Accelera write-down charges.

$661M

Cash went back to shareholders

Cummins returned $661M in dividends and buybacks in the quarter and still expects about 10% revenue growth for the full year, with EBITDA margins around 18.2%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Diluted EPS includes $0.54 per share of favorable discrete tax items — one-off tax benefits that will not necessarily repeat.
  • The year-ago quarter included $240M ($1.73 per diluted share) of non-cash Accelera charges, which is why this quarter's profit growth looks so large.

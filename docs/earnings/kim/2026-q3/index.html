Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

96.6%

Fullest centers on record

Portfolio occupancy hit an all-time high of 96.6%, small-shop occupancy matched its record at 92.9%, and new leases signed at cash rents 31.5% above the old ones on comparable spaces.

$89M

Property sales drove the profit jump

Net income available to common shareholders rose to $272M ($0.41 per share) from $130M, mainly on $122M more of property-sale gains than a year ago, including selling The Witmer apartments for $211.5M.

$0.47

The rent engine grew too

FFO — the REIT measure that strips out sale gains and depreciation — rose 6.8% per share to $0.47, same-property NOI grew 4.9%, and Kimco raised its full-year outlook again.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gains on sales of properties were $89M in the quarter, versus $4M a year ago — the main reason net income more than doubled. FFO, which excludes property-sale gains, was $316M ($0.47 per share).
  • Impairment charges were $8M in the quarter, versus $0.4M a year ago.

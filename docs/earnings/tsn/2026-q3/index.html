Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$389M

Chicken carried the quarter

Chicken earned $389M at a 9.1% margin and Prepared Foods earned $312M at 12.2% — together they more than covered Beef's $142M loss, its seventh difficult quarter on tight cattle supplies.

$362M

GAAP profit rose 39%

Operating income rose to $362M from $260M a year ago, when a $343M goodwill impairment weighed on results. Adjusted operating income, excluding this quarter's one-offs, was $547M.

$98M

A legal charge cut the top line

A $98M legal contingency accrual was booked as a reduction of Chicken sales, and a $73M leadership-transition charge sat in SG&A — together they explain most of the gap between GAAP and adjusted profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $98M legal contingency accrual was recognized as a reduction of Sales in the Chicken segment; excluding it, sales would have grown 0.6%.
  • Executive leadership transition charges of $73M were included in SG&A and are not deductible for tax — a main reason the effective tax rate was an unusually high 30.1%.
  • Restructuring and related charges added $14M in the quarter; adjusted (non-GAAP) operating income excluding all three items was $547M, vs $362M GAAP.

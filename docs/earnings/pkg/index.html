Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Growth came from Greif — and price

Sales rose 12% to $2.6B. Corrugated shipments rose 10.6% including Greif, while legacy shipments slipped 0.5%; favorable price and mix in Packaging added $0.79 a share vs a year ago.

$398M

Packaging did the heavy lifting

Packaging segment operating income rose to $398M from $328M a year ago ($408M excluding special items). Paper earned $29M, down from $36M.

$3.04

Profit ex-charges was higher

Reported EPS of $2.93 includes $0.11 of special items. Excluding them, EPS was $3.04 and net income $272M — the number to compare with a normal quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Special items totaled $13M before tax ($10M after tax, $0.11 per share): $6M of facility closure costs, $3M of acquisition and integration costs, $3M for a fire at a corrugated products facility, and $0.3M of Wallula mill restructuring charges. Excluding them, net income was $272M ($3.04 per share).

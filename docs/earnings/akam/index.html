Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$14B

The backlog is the headline

Akamai has signed more than $14B of multi-year Cloud Infrastructure commitments this year — the backlog behind its claim that 2027 revenue growth jumps to 17–22% and 2028 revenue tops $7.0B.

10%

Profit is being spent on the pivot

GAAP operating margin compressed to 10% (from 16%) — the cost of building a cloud business before its revenue arrives. GAAP EPS fell 21% to $0.77.

$622M

Security is the core now

Security is now the majority of the company and compounding at 9%; legacy delivery shrinks slowly at −3%. The mix shift is nearly complete.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.1%

The domain base is growing again

The .com and .net base reached 182.5M names, up 6.1% YoY, with 13.1M new registrations processed in the quarter versus 10.6M a year ago.

67%

A registry is a toll booth

Operating income was $296M on $443M of revenue — a 67% margin. Running the registry costs little; most of each renewal fee drops through to profit.

$189M

Profits go back to shareholders

Verisign repurchased 0.7M shares for $189M in the quarter and declared a $0.81 per share dividend, payable November 24, 2026.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $7.5M pre-tax operating loss related to certain assets held for sale, which reduced diluted EPS by $0.06.

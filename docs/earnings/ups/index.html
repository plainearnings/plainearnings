Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.7%

Growth in every segment

Revenue rose to $22.9B with all three segments growing — Supply Chain Solutions led at +18.2%, and U.S. and international pricing per piece rose 6.1% and 15.0%.

$1.37

GAAP profit took one-off hits

GAAP EPS fell to $1.37 from $1.55 as a $182M goodwill impairment and transformation costs landed in the quarter. Adjusted EPS was $1.69.

~$92.6B

Guidance raised anyway

Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to about $92.6B of revenue and roughly $7.28 of adjusted EPS, heading into the holiday peak with its most automated network ever.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include a net charge of $279M, or $0.32 per share: after-tax transformation strategy costs of $130M and a $182M goodwill impairment charge in Supply Chain Solutions, partly offset by a $33M gain on selling MD-11 aircraft assets. Adjusted EPS was $1.69 vs $1.37 GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1M

The wildfire swing

Marshall wildfire litigation cost $1M this quarter versus a $287M charge a year ago — that comparison alone explains most of the jump in GAAP profit, from $524M to $960M.

+23%

Underlying earnings grew

Ongoing EPS — Xcel's non-GAAP measure without the wildfire charge — rose to $1.52 from $1.24, on recovery of electric infrastructure investment and unseasonably warm weather, partly offset by higher financing costs.

$4.48–$4.62

2027 guidance initiated

Xcel narrowed 2026 ongoing EPS guidance to $4.10–$4.16 and guided 2027 at $4.48–$4.62, targeting 6–8%+ annual EPS growth as it builds clean generation and transmission.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Marshall Wildfire litigation cost just $1M this quarter, versus a $287M charge in Q3 2025 — that swing is the main reason GAAP net income rose 83% year over year. Xcel's 'ongoing' EPS excludes the charge in both periods.
  • Earnings from equity method investments jumped to $77M from $6M a year ago, adding to the year-over-year increase.

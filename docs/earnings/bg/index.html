Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$547M

Soybean processing drove the quarter

Soybean Processing and Refining EBIT rose to $547M from $337M, and Softseed EBIT to $353M from $236M — processing margins, helped by the expanded Viterra network, did the heavy lifting.

$2.56

Adjusted profit is well above GAAP

Adjusted EPS was $2.56 vs GAAP $1.64. The gap is $0.28 of mark-to-market timing — paper swings on commodity contracts that reverse later — and $0.64 of certain charges, including the Brazil mill sale.

-$74M

Grain merchandising lost money

Grain Merchandising and Milling posted a $74M EBIT loss despite $6.76B of sales, after the $82M Brazil sugarcane-mill charges. Excluding certain charges, its adjusted EBIT was $87M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $82M of charges in Grain Merchandising and Milling tied to the announced sale of two Brazilian sugarcane mills — $66M of impairment charges and $16M of hedge losses, recorded in cost of goods sold.
  • Viterra acquisition and integration costs in Corporate and Other reduced net income by $21M in the quarter (spread across cost of goods sold, SG&A, interest and tax).
  • Adjusted EPS of $2.56 excludes $0.28 per share of mark-to-market timing differences and $0.64 of certain gains and charges; GAAP EPS was $1.64.

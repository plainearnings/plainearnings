Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11%

Growth in every business

Sales reached $25.0B. Raytheon grew fastest at +14% on naval and air-defense work, while Collins and Pratt & Whitney each grew about +10%.

$340B

Record backlog

More than $81B of new orders in the quarter took the backlog to a record $340B, and RTX raised its full-year sales, EPS and cash-flow outlook.

-$363M

Bankruptcy charge at Pratt & Whitney

Reported Pratt profit fell 19% because of a $363M charge tied to a customer bankruptcy. Without it, segment profit rose 29%, helped by about $160M of tariff cost recovery.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $363M charge at Pratt & Whitney related to a customer bankruptcy is included in reported operating profit.
  • Adjusted results include roughly $0.5B of IEEPA tariff cost recovery across the segments (about $250M at Collins and $160M at Pratt & Whitney), worth $0.25 of adjusted EPS.
  • GAAP profit also includes $448M of acquisition-accounting adjustments and a $159M gain on investments recorded in eliminations and other.

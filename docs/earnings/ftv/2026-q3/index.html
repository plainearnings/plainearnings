Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

The industrial side is carrying growth

Intelligent Operating Solutions grew 9.8% with a 29.1% GAAP operating margin, while Advanced Healthcare Solutions grew 3.7% and its segment operating profit fell 10% on mix.

$18M

A tariff refund flattered the quarter

After the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs, Fortive booked an $18.0M refund recovery inside cost of sales. It is excluded from adjusted results — one reason GAAP EPS rose 63% while adjusted EPS rose 13%.

~$300M

Buybacks are the capital story

Fortive spent about $300M repurchasing roughly 5M shares — about 2% of the share count — in a single quarter, funded by $313M of operating cash flow.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $18.0M IEEPA tariff-refund recovery is included in cost of sales (and therefore in gross profit); Fortive excludes it from adjusted earnings.
  • A $6.2M non-cash impairment of capitalized software and $4.9M of foreign-currency gains on euro-denominated debt also sit inside the GAAP figures.

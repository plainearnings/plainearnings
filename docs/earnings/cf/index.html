Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Prices did the lifting

Sales rose 12% to $1.86B. Ammonia prices rose 27% per ton and UAN volumes jumped 27%; excluding lost Yazoo City supply, volumes would have been up about 13% overall.

-45%

Yazoo City is still a drag

AN sales fell 45% to $67M and the segment's gross margin shrank to $3M because the Mississippi complex remains partly out after last November's incident. Insurance recoveries — $62M this quarter — are cushioning the loss.

$2.03B

Cash keeps compounding

Free cash flow over the last twelve months was $2.03B. CF spent $367M on buybacks in Q3 alone, and construction of the low-carbon Blue Point One ammonia plant began in August.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $62M business-interruption insurance recovery (for the November 2025 incident and ongoing outage at the Yazoo City complex) is counted inside operating results — without it, operating costs net would have been about $85M and operating earnings about $639M on this page's basis.
  • Nine-month 2026 results (not this quarter) include a gain of about $170M from a litigation settlement and a $23M asset impairment.

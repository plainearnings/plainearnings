Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$115M

Tariff refunds were the big story

Gross profit included about $115M of tariff refunds received in the quarter, worth $0.84 of EPS. Adjusted EPS was $3.24, up 71%.

+13%

Coffee carried sales growth

Coffee sales rose 13% to $808M on higher prices, helping total sales grow 5% to $2.22B even as Hostess sweet baked snacks fell 7%.

$324M

A swing back to profit — with help

Net income was $324M versus a $44M loss a year ago. The comparison is flattered by a $29M derivative gain this year versus a $253M derivative loss a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • About $115M of tariff refunds received in the quarter, recorded mainly in gross profit, plus $4M of related interest income.
  • A $29M gain from the change in net cumulative unallocated derivative gains was included in GAAP gross profit (a year ago the same line was a $253M loss).

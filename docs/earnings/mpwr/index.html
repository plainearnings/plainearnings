Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+165%

AI is the whole story

Enterprise Data revenue nearly tripled to $507M as AI servers need far more power conversion than ordinary servers. Communications (+135%) rode the same wave through optical modules and switches.

$1.2B

First billion-dollar quarter

Revenue crossed $1B for the first time, up 23% in a single quarter. Management says installed capacity can already support $6B of annual revenue, heading for $8B by late 2027.

$8.19

Two profit numbers, one debate

Non-GAAP EPS was $8.19 vs $5.51 GAAP. The difference is mainly stock-based pay — real dilution for shareholders, but not cash out the door.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • No one-off gains or charges — but note the GAAP/non-GAAP gap: non-GAAP net income was $403M vs $271M GAAP, almost entirely because GAAP subtracts roughly $120M+ of stock-based compensation. Whether that is a 'real' cost is the standing debate with fast-growing chip companies.

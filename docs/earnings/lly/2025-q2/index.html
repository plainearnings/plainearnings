Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8.6B

Two drugs are now most of the company

Mounjaro ($5.2B) and Zepbound ($3.4B) together brought in 55% of Lilly's revenue — Mounjaro grew 68% and Zepbound 172%.

84.3%

Margins widened

Gross margin rose 3.5 points to 84.3% on better production costs and a richer product mix — so profit grew 91%, much faster than revenue.

$60–62B

Guidance raised

After the strong first half, Lilly lifted its full-year 2025 revenue guidance to $60.0–62.0B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+125%

Mounjaro more than doubled

Mounjaro revenue rose 125% to $8.7B. Growth came from volume — more prescriptions — even as prices fell, including after Mounjaro joined China's National Reimbursed Drug List.

$584M

Smaller one-off charges

Acquired IPR&D charges fell to $584M ($0.52 of EPS) from $1,572M a year ago — one reason reported profit nearly tripled. Asset impairment and restructuring added $279M.

$82–85B

Guidance raised

Lilly raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $82–85B and won FDA approval for Foundayo, its GLP-1 pill for weight loss — the next product in the franchise.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired IPR&D of $584M — research bought via acquisitions and expensed immediately. It cost $0.52 of EPS; a year ago the same line was $1,572M ($1.72 of EPS).
  • Asset impairment, restructuring and other special charges of $279M, versus $35M a year ago.
  • Jardiance revenue outside the U.S. included a one-time $250M benefit tied to the Boehringer Ingelheim collaboration; a year ago the same benefit was $370M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$5.4B

Two drugs drove the growth

Mounjaro ($3.5B, +60%) and Zepbound ($1.9B, up from $176M in its launch quarter) together brought in $5.4B — 40% of all revenue. Overall volume rose 48%, partly offset by 4% lower realized prices.

82.2%

Margins widened again

Gross margin rose 1.3 points to 82.2% on a favorable product mix, partly offset by lower realized prices — so operating income more than doubled, to $5.1B.

$58–61B

2025 guidance issued

Lilly guided 2025 revenue to $58.0–61.0B with reported EPS of $22.05–23.55, and said it would produce at least 1.6 times as many salable incretin doses in the first half of 2025 as a year earlier.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Asset impairment, restructuring and other special charges of $344M — primarily an intangible asset impairment associated with Vitrakvi. They cost $0.30 of the quarter's EPS on a non-GAAP comparison basis.
  • Acquired IPR&D charges of $189M ($0.19 of EPS), down from $623M a year ago, when the charges mainly related to Mablink Biosciences and a Beam Therapeutics transaction.
  • Jardiance revenue included a one-time benefit of $300M from an amendment to the Boehringer Ingelheim collaboration that adjusted who sells Jardiance in certain markets — that benefit sits inside 'All other medicines' revenue.

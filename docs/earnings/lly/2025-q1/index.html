Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$6.2B

Two drugs drove the growth

Mounjaro ($3.8B, +113%) and Zepbound ($2.3B) together brought in $6.2B — 48% of all revenue. Volume rose 53% overall, partly offset by lower realized prices.

$1.72

The bought-research charge

Acquired IPR&D charges of $1.57B — mainly for Scorpion Therapeutics' STX-478 program — cost $1.72 of the quarter's $3.06 reported EPS. Because the charge is largely non-deductible, the effective tax rate rose to 20.2% from 11.6% a year ago.

82.5%

Margins widened, guidance held

Gross margin rose 1.6 points to 82.5% on improved production costs and a favorable product mix, partly offset by lower realized prices. Lilly reaffirmed full-year 2025 revenue guidance of $58.0–61.0B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired IPR&D charges of $1,572M — primarily the acquisition of Scorpion Therapeutics' PI3Kα inhibitor program STX-478 — expensed in full this quarter. They account for $1.72 of EPS and, being largely non-deductible, pushed the effective tax rate up to 20.2%.
  • Jardiance revenue included a one-time benefit of $370M from an amendment to the Boehringer Ingelheim collaboration that adjusted who sells Jardiance in certain markets — that benefit sits inside 'All other medicines' revenue.
  • Asset impairment, restructuring and other special charges of $35M, related to intangible asset impairments.

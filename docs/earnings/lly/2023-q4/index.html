Eli Lilly — Q4 2023 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Reported 2024-02-06

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.2B

Mounjaro exploded, Zepbound arrived

Mounjaro brought in $2,205.6M, up from $279.2M a year ago, and Zepbound — launched in November 2023 — added $175.8M in its first partial quarter. Together the two tirzepatide shots were about a quarter of Lilly's revenue; U.S. revenue rose 39% to $6.46B.

$623M

Bought-in research weighed on profit

Acquired in-process R&D charges of $622.6M, plus $67.7M of asset impairment, restructuring and other special charges, held reported EPS to $2.42 (non-GAAP $2.49). Even so, operating income rose 30% to $2,387.8M on an 80.9% gross margin.

$40.4–41.6B

2024 guidance set

Lilly guided 2024 revenue to $40.4–41.6B and reported EPS to $11.80–$12.30, with Mounjaro and Zepbound growth hinging on how fast it can expand production capacity.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired in-process R&D of $622.6M ($0.62 of EPS), mainly the acquisition of Mablink Biosciences and a collaboration agreement with Beam Therapeutics. Lilly counts IPR&D in both its reported and its non-GAAP figures.
  • U.S. Mounjaro revenue included a favorable one-time change in estimates for rebates and discounts — reported U.S. Mounjaro sales rose 65% from the third quarter, versus underlying growth of about 30%.

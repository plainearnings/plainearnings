Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$4.3B

Two shots are 38% of revenue

Mounjaro ($3.1B, +215%) and Zepbound ($1.2B, launched November 2023) together brought in $4.3B of the $11.3B total — two versions of the same drug, tirzepatide.

$45.4–$46.6B

Guidance raised by $3B

Lilly lifted full-year 2024 revenue guidance from $42.4–$43.6B to $45.4–$46.6B, and reported EPS guidance by $2.05 to $15.10–$15.60, on the strength of Mounjaro and Zepbound.

$435M

A litigation charge in the quarter

Lilly booked a $435M asset impairment, restructuring and other special charge related to anticipated litigation payments, plus a $154M acquired IPR&D charge — and operating income still rose 75% to $3.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired in-process R&D of $154M ($0.14 of EPS), up from $97M a year ago. Lilly counts IPR&D in both its reported and its non-GAAP figures.
  • Asset impairment, restructuring and other special charges of $435M ($0.38 of EPS), related to anticipated litigation payments, with no such charge a year ago. Lilly's non-GAAP net income of $3,541M (EPS $3.92) excludes this charge, along with net losses on equity securities and amortization of intangible assets.
  • The year-ago quarter's revenue included $579M from the sale of rights for Baqsimi — excluding it, Q2 2024 revenue grew 46% rather than the reported 36%.

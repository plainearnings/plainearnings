Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+39%

Two drugs are the company

Mounjaro ($10.7B, +65%) and Zepbound ($5.4B, +49%) brought in $16.1B — two-thirds of all revenue. Lilly also raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $91–92B.

$4.2B

The IPR&D charge

Buying AtaiBeckley and Curevo cost a $4.2B immediate research charge — $4.57 per share, roughly the size of the entire quarterly R&D budget. Reported EPS of $7.22 already includes it.

83.9%

Medicines are cheap to make

Gross margin hit 83.9% — it costs Lilly about 16 cents to manufacture each $1 of medicine it sells, helped by a favorable product mix.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired IPR&D charges of $4,181M — mostly the AtaiBeckley and Curevo acquisitions — expensed in full this quarter. They account for $4.57 of EPS and, being largely nondeductible, pushed the effective tax rate up to 23.3%.
  • Revenue included about $900M of one-off benefits: $410M of Jardiance sales milestones (the last of them — no further milestones remain) plus favorable adjustments to rebate and discount estimates.

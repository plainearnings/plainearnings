Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.40

EPS jumped two-thirds

Diluted EPS rose from $2.04 to $3.40. Buybacks helped — DaVita repurchased 0.6M shares for $111M in the quarter, and the diluted share count is down from 73.8M to 65.5M in a year — and a smaller share of profit went to joint-venture partners.

$62M

China write-down hit operating profit

Operating income was $499M, slightly below last year's $506M, because of a $62M non-cash impairment on the China business. Without it, adjusted operating income was $561M, a 15.7% margin.

$415

Steady treatments, better rates

Revenue per U.S. treatment was $415.25, up from $405.15 a year ago, while treatment volume grew just 0.4% on a normalized basis. Revenue growth is coming from price and mix, not from many more patients.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $62M non-cash goodwill and asset impairment charge on DaVita's China business sits inside operating costs. Excluding it, adjusted operating income was $561M (vs $499M reported) and adjusted EPS was $3.82 (vs $3.40).

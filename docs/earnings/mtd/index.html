Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

Asia is pulling the growth

Sales rose 16% in Asia/Rest of World versus 3% in the Americas and flat Europe — demand for lab and industrial instruments is recovering fastest outside the West.

59.8%

Pricing power in precision

Gross margin widened to 59.8% — customers pay up for instruments that labs and factories depend on for accuracy, and Mettler's service army keeps them loyal.

$12.29

Big EPS, tiny share count

EPS rose 16% — faster than the 13% profit growth — because decades of buybacks have shrunk the share count to about 20 million.

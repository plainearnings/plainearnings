Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15.5%

Big customers are driving growth

Daily sales rose 15.5%. Contract customers — large accounts with signed deals — grew 18.8% and are now 76.5% of sales, as Fastenal keeps winning share inside factories.

21.5%

Overhead leverage beat margin pressure

Gross margin slipped to 44.8% because big customers get lower prices, but SG&A fell to 23.3% of sales from 24.6%, so operating margin rose to 21.5% — and operating income jumped 20%, the biggest dollar gain in Fastenal's history.

$328M

Most profit went back to shareholders

Fastenal returned $328M — 82% of net income — as $298M of dividends and $30M of share buybacks, while keeping total debt at just $100M.

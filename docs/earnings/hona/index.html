Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$0.40

A first dividend

In its first quarter as a standalone company (spun off from Honeywell on June 29, 2026), Aerospace declared its inaugural quarterly dividend of $0.40 per share, payable December 4, 2026.

$20B+

Wins are stacking up

The company has secured more than $20 billion of new wins (estimated lifetime value) year to date, with orders up 9% over the last 12 months — demand is not the problem; supplier output is.

+11% / −16%

Two businesses, two directions

Electronic Solutions' adjusted profit rose 11% while Engines and Power Systems fell 16% on unfavorable mix and higher costs — the quarter's story is which product line, not whether demand exists.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • First quarter as a standalone public company: the balance sheet now carries $15.9 billion of long-term debt issued around the separation (versus $4M at the end of 2025), and results include incremental standalone corporate costs.
  • Prior-year figures are carve-out results from when Aerospace was part of Honeywell, and pre-spin EPS is calculated on the 317 million shares issued at the spin-off.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+322%

The AI factory business arrived

Revenue jumped from $80.9M to $341.9M. HPC Hosting — data centers built for AI tenants — produced $262.6M, mostly $183.5M of tenant fit-out work plus the first full quarters of rent. The two reported segments together earned $46.8M of segment profit.

-$62.4M

Head-office costs swamped segment profit

Consolidated operating result was a $62.4M loss, because $114.7M of general and administrative costs — including $59.4M of stock-based pay — sit above the segment lines. Excluding ChronoScale and one-offs, the company's adjusted operating income was $37.8M.

$77.4M

Financing the build-out is expensive

Interest expense was $77.4M in one quarter, and mark-to-market losses on derivatives and an investment added another $60.9M. After a $16.1M discontinued-operations loss, the consolidated net loss was $184.1M; the loss attributable to common stockholders was $237.1M after preferred dividends and the noncontrolling share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Losses on the change in fair value of derivatives ($49.5M) and of an investment ($11.4M) are non-cash mark-to-market moves, not operating costs.
  • A $16.1M loss from discontinued operations is included in other expense in the flow below so the bottom line is the full consolidated net loss; continuing operations lost $168.0M.

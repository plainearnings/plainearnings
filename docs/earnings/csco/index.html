Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Record revenue, led by networking

Revenue hit a record $17,252M, up 18%. Product revenue rose 24% while services were flat. Networking jumped 28% to $9,791M, Security rose 14%, Collaboration 12% and Observability 6%. Total product orders rose 35%, and AI infrastructure orders from hyperscalers were $4B in the quarter.

$3.9B

Profit grew much faster than revenue

Operating income rose 38% to $4,264M, a 24.7% margin, and net income rose 51% to $3,859M, or $0.97 per diluted share. Interest and other income added a net $669M, helped by investment gains, and cash flow from operations was $5,386M, up 27%.

$511M

A big restructuring charge sits in the costs

Restructuring and other charges jumped to $511M from $35M a year ago under the plan announced on May 13, 2026, which Cisco expects to be substantially complete by the end of fiscal 2027. Total GAAP operating expenses rose 10% to $6,799M; leaving out restructuring, share-based pay and similar items, they rose 5%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges were $511M in Q4, up from $35M a year ago, under the restructuring plan announced on May 13, 2026. Cisco excludes these charges from its non-GAAP results and expects the plan to be substantially complete by the end of fiscal 2027.
  • Other income, net of $822M was boosted by about $869M of gains on investments (before smaller offsetting losses) that Cisco also excludes from non-GAAP results. It is the main reason interest and other income swung to a $669M gain from an $88M loss a year ago.

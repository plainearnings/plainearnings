Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-1.3%

Organic sales slipped

Net sales were $4,134M, down 0.4%. Organic sales fell 1.3%, including a roughly 270-basis-point hit from a China social-media disruption and retailer inventory swings in North America.

$516M

Operating profit fell on one-off costs

Operating profit fell 17% to $516M, including $106M of Kenvue deal costs and $55M of transformation charges. Excluding items, adjusted operating profit was $677M, down 0.9%.

$1.4B

A deal gain swamped the quarter

Completing the IFP joint-venture transaction produced a gain of about $1.4B inside discontinued operations, taking total net income to $1,800M and total EPS to $5.37 — most of it one-time.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Income from discontinued operations was $1,395M, including a pre-tax gain of about $1.4B on completing the IFP Transaction (the Arbex joint venture with Suzano) on July 1, 2026, and $52M of pre-tax separation costs.
  • Operating profit included $106M of Kenvue acquisition costs and $55M of 2024 Transformation Initiative charges; excluding these and other items, adjusted operating profit was $677M. Kimberly-Clark closed its Kenvue acquisition on November 2, 2026, after quarter-end.

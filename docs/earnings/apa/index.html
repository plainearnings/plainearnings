Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-22%

Revenue fell to $1.99B

Almost all of the drop is the absence of last year's $620M UK tax-settlement gain in the comparison, not weaker operations.

+4%

Production rose to 488 Mboe/d

Permian growth, the Granite Ridge merger and new Suriname output more than offset declines in Egypt and the North Sea, while realized gas prices fell sharply.

$1.22

EPS of $1.22 vs $3.49 a year ago

Profit fell with the one-off gain gone and prices lower, though operating cash flow of $1.26B stayed strong. The growth bet is Suriname: a final investment decision on the larger GranMorgu project is expected in Q2 2027.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

Bookings growth won't slow down

Gross Bookings — everything riders and eaters spent on the platform — grew 20% to $59.8B, the fifth straight quarter at 20%+, with record audience and engagement.

$2.3B

Core profit hit a record

Non-GAAP operating income rose 38% to $2.3B (3.9% of bookings, a record) and Adjusted EBITDA grew 31% to $3.0B — the underlying business has never been more profitable.

$0.25

GAAP profit tells a noisier story

GAAP EPS was $0.25 vs $0.88 non-GAAP, almost entirely because of a $476M paper loss on equity investments. A year ago the same line (plus a $4.9B tax benefit) flattered GAAP profit instead.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $476M pre-tax paper loss from revaluing equity investments sits in other expense — the main reason GAAP EPS ($0.25) trails non-GAAP EPS ($0.88).
  • The year-ago comparison is flattering in reverse: Q3 2025 net income of $6.6B included a $4.9B one-time tax benefit and a $1.5B investment gain.

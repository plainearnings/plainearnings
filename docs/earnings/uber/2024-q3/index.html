Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.1B

First billion-dollar operating profit

Income from operations reached $1,061M — the first time Uber has cleared $1B in a quarter — up from $394M a year ago, as revenue growth outpaced costs. Adjusted EBITDA rose 55% to $1.7B.

+20%

Growth stayed broad

Revenue rose 20% to $11.2B on 2.9 billion trips (+17%). Mobility revenue jumped 26% and Delivery 18%; Freight, still facing a soft trucking market, grew just 2%.

$1.7B

A paper gain padded the bottom line

Net income attributable to Uber was $2.6B, but $1.7B of it (pre-tax) was an unrealized gain from marking up the value of Uber's equity investments — not cash from rides or deliveries. Free cash flow was a more grounded $2.1B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net income includes a $1.7B pre-tax benefit from net unrealized gains on the revaluation of Uber's equity investments — a paper gain that could reverse if those stocks fall.

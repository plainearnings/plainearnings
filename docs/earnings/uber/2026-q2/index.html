Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Bookings still booming

Gross Bookings — everything riders and eaters spent on the platform — grew 24% to $58.0B, with trips up 18%. Revenue grew 12% to $14.2B; business-model changes in how some trips are billed held reported revenue growth about 8 points below bookings growth.

$1.9B

Core profit up 30%

Operating income rose 30% to $1.9B and Adjusted EBITDA 33% to $2.8B. Free cash flow over the last twelve months passed $10B for the first time in Uber's history.

$1.6B

A paper gain did heavy lifting

GAAP net income of $2.4B includes a ~$1.6B pre-tax benefit from revaluing Uber's equity investments — and much of the quarter's unusually large $840M tax bill came with it. The cleaner read on the business is the $1.9B operating line.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A ~$1.6B pre-tax benefit from revaluations of Uber's equity investments sits in other income — a paper gain, not cash from rides or deliveries. It also drove most of the quarter's $840M tax provision, which includes the deferred U.S. tax on those securities.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$172M

The core business turned an operating profit

Income from operations was $172M, up from a $262M loss a year ago, and Adjusted EBITDA jumped 82% to a record $1,382M as revenue growth outpaced costs.

+20%

Bookings kept growing

Gross Bookings rose 20% to $37.7B on 2.6 billion trips (+21%). Mobility revenue jumped 30% to $5,633M and Delivery rose 4% to $3,214M; Freight fell 8% to $1,284M in a soft trucking market.

$721M

A paper loss flipped the bottom line to red

Net loss was $663M only because Uber marked down the value of its equity investments by $721M (pre-tax) — a paper loss, not cash out the door. Free cash flow was $1,359M, up 148%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net loss includes a $721M pre-tax loss from net unrealized losses on the revaluation of Uber's equity investments — a paper loss that could reverse if those stocks rise.
  • General & administrative expense included $527M of legal, tax and regulatory reserve changes and settlements; excluding these and other items, non-GAAP G&A was $582M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Trips still booming

Trips grew 22% to 3.5 billion and Gross Bookings 21% to $49.7B, with Monthly Active Platform Consumers up 17%. Revenue grew 20% to $13.5B — Mobility +20%, Delivery +29%, Freight flat.

$479M

Legal reserves hit operating profit

Income from operations rose just 5% to $1.1B: general & administrative costs jumped to $1,183M as Uber booked $479M of legal, tax and regulatory reserve changes and settlements. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes them, rose 33% to $2.3B.

$4.9B

A tax release made the bottom line

Net income attributable to Uber was $6.6B ($3.11 a share) — but $4.9B of it was a non-cash benefit from releasing a tax valuation allowance, and other income included a $1.5B net gain on revaluations of Uber's equity investments.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $4.9B non-cash tax benefit from releasing valuation allowances against deferred tax assets sits in the tax line (a $4,046M net tax benefit for the quarter) — it reflects Uber's sustained profitability, not cash received.
  • Other income includes a $1.5B net benefit (pre-tax) from revaluations of Uber's equity investments — paper gains on stakes it holds.
  • General & administrative expense includes $479M of legal, tax and regulatory reserve changes and settlements, which Uber excludes from Adjusted EBITDA.

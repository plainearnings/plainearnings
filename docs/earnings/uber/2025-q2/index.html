Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Trips kept climbing

Trips grew 18% to 3.3 billion and Gross Bookings 17% to $46.8B, with Monthly Active Platform Consumers up 15%. Revenue rose 18% to $12.7B — Mobility +19%, Delivery +25%, Freight −1%.

$1.5B

Operating profit nearly doubled

Income from operations rose to $1,450M from $796M a year ago. Adjusted EBITDA rose 35% to $2.1B — 4.5% of Gross Bookings, up from 3.9%.

$20B

A new buyback, funded by cash flow

Uber authorized a new $20B share repurchase program. Free cash flow was $2.5B in the quarter and a record $8.5B over the last twelve months.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net income includes a $17M net headwind (pre-tax) from revaluations of Uber's equity investments; a year ago the same line contributed a $333M benefit.

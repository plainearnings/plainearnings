Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$652M

The core business swung to profit

Income from operations was $652M, up $794M from a $142M loss a year ago, and Adjusted EBITDA nearly doubled to $1,283M as Mobility and Delivery growth outpaced costs.

+22%

Bookings kept growing

Gross Bookings rose 22% to $37.6B on 2.6 billion trips (+24%). Mobility revenue jumped 34% to $5,537M and Delivery rose 6% to $3,119M; Freight fell 17% to $1,280M in a soft trucking market.

$1.0B

Paper gains padded the bottom line

About $1.0B of the profit was a net unrealized gain on Uber's stakes in other companies — Aurora up $659M and Didi up $414M, partly offset by a $91M drop in Grab — plus a $204M gain on the Careem sale. Free cash flow was $768M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income includes a $1,000M net unrealized gain (pre-tax) from revaluing Uber's equity investments — a paper gain that could reverse if those stocks fall — and a $204M gain on the sale of Uber's interest in the Careem non-ridesharing business.

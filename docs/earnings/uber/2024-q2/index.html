Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$796M

Operating profit more than doubled

Income from operations jumped 144% to $796M, from $326M a year ago, as revenue growth outpaced costs. Adjusted EBITDA rose 71% to $1,570M.

+19%

Bookings kept growing

Gross Bookings rose 19% to $40.0B on 2.8 billion trips (+21%). Mobility revenue jumped 25% to $6,134M and Delivery 8% to $3,293M; Freight was flat at $1,273M in a soft trucking market.

$333M

A paper gain padded the bottom line

Net income attributable to Uber was $1,015M, but $333M of it (pre-tax) was an unrealized gain from marking up the value of Uber's equity investments — not cash from rides or deliveries. Free cash flow was $1,721M, up 51%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net income includes a $333M pre-tax benefit from net unrealized gains on the revaluation of Uber's equity investments — a paper gain that could reverse if those stocks fall.

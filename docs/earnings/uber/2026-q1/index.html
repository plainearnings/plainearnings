Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+25%

Bookings still booming

Gross Bookings — everything riders and eaters spent on the platform — grew 25% to $53.7B, with trips up 20% to 3.6 billion and 50 million Uber One members. Revenue grew 14% to $13.2B; billing-model changes held reported Mobility revenue growth to 5% even as ride bookings rose 25%.

$1.9B

Core profit up 57%

Operating income rose 57% to $1.9B and Adjusted EBITDA 33% to $2.5B. The cleaner read on this quarter is the operating line — the bottom line was decided by investments, not by rides or deliveries.

-$1.5B

A paper loss did the damage

Other expense, net, was $1,494M — mostly a $1,474M unrealized loss on debt and equity securities. It cut reported net income to $282M ($263M to Uber shareholders, $0.13 a share), down from $1,774M a year ago, when a tax benefit flattered results instead.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An unrealized loss of about $1,474M on debt and equity securities sits in other income (expense), net — a paper loss on the value of Uber's investment stakes, not cash lost from rides or deliveries.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Bookings still booming

Gross Bookings grew 22% to $54.1B, with trips up 22% to 3.8 billion and Monthly Active Platform Consumers up 18%. Revenue grew 20% to $14.4B.

$1.8B

Operating profit more than doubled

GAAP income from operations rose 130% to $1.8B and Adjusted EBITDA 35% to $2.5B. Free cash flow was $2.8B — the core business had a very strong quarter.

-$1.6B

A paper loss did the damage

Other expense, net was $1,568M, driven by a roughly $1.6B net headwind (pre-tax) from revaluations of Uber's equity investments. It cut net income to $317M ($296M to Uber shareholders, $0.14 a share), even with a $40M tax benefit helping.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A net loss of roughly $1.6B (pre-tax) from revaluations of Uber's equity investments sits in other income (expense), net — paper losses on the value of stakes Uber holds, not cash lost from rides or deliveries.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Trips kept climbing

Trips grew 18% to 3.0 billion and Gross Bookings 14% to $42.8B, with Monthly Active Platform Consumers up 14% to 170 million. Revenue rose 14% to $11.5B — Mobility +15%, Delivery +18%, Freight −2%.

$1.2B

Operating profit took off

Income from operations rose to $1,228M from just $172M a year ago, as revenue grew 14% while total costs and expenses rose only about 3%. Adjusted EBITDA rose 35% to $1.9B — 4.4% of Gross Bookings, up from 3.7%.

$2.3B

Cash flow matched profit

Operating cash flow and free cash flow were both $2.3B in the quarter. Uber ended March with $6.0B of unrestricted cash and short-term investments, and guided Q2 Gross Bookings to $45.75–47.25B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net income includes a $51M net benefit (pre-tax) from revaluations of Uber's equity investments.
  • Income taxes were a $402M benefit this quarter rather than an expense — almost all of it deferred (deferred income taxes were a $412M benefit in the cash-flow statement) — so reported net income came in above pre-tax income.

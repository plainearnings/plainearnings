Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Underlying growth is stronger than the headline

Reported revenue rose 3% to $4.1B, but that is after losing revenue from sold businesses. Organic growth — the same business, like for like — was 6%, led by Commercial Risk (+7% organic) on new business wins and high retention.

$93M

A growth program is eating reported profit

Operating income was flat at $809M even though revenue grew, because spending on the Accelerating Aon United program nearly tripled to $93M. Excluding that program and acquisition amortization, adjusted operating income rose 5% to $1,106M.

-21%

Wealth shrank after a business sale

Wealth Solutions revenue fell 21% to $429M, almost entirely because Aon sold NFP's wealth business. The flip side: the sales recycle cash into Aon's faster-growing risk and health franchises.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses include $93M for the Accelerating Aon United restructuring program (vs $32M a year ago) and $175M of amortization and impairment of acquisition intangibles. Aon's adjusted EPS of $3.36 excludes these items.

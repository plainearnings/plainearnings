Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+0.2%

Comps stay positive

Comparable sales rose 0.2% — the fifth straight positive quarter — as Pro, home services and online (+15.7%) outweighed persistent pressure on discretionary DIY spending.

33.0%

Margin did the damage

Gross margin slipped to 33.04% from 33.81% a year ago, so an 8% sales jump produced only 2% more operating income ($3,549M) and flat net earnings of $2,399M.

$92B

Outlook trimmed to the low end

Full-year fiscal 2026 guidance now points to about $92B of sales (was $92–94B), flat comparable sales, GAAP EPS of about $11.75 and adjusted EPS of about $12.25. Lowe's paid $673M of dividends in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Lowe's booked $96M of pre-tax expenses tied to its acquisitions of Foundation Building Materials (FBM) and Artisan Design Group (ADG). Excluding them, adjusted EPS was $4.40 vs GAAP $4.27. Both figures include an $0.11-per-share benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds.

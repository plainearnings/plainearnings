Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Products drove the quarter

Product revenue rose 23% to $510M — systems up 29% and software up 19% — the ninth straight quarter of double-digit product growth.

23.1%

GAAP margin narrowed

GAAP operating margin was 23.1%, down from 25.4% a year ago, as sales & marketing and other costs grew faster than revenue. Non-GAAP operating margin was 33.7%.

$1.5B

More buybacks authorized

The board added $1.5B to the share repurchase program, on top of $322M still remaining — while fiscal 2027 guidance calls for slower revenue growth of 8% to 9%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include $7.4M of restructuring charges and stock-based compensation of $67.5M; excluding these and other items, non-GAAP net income was $255M, or $4.42 per share.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Record revenue, real volume

Revenue hit a record $7.2B, up 15%. Carloads rose 6% and pricing rose too — only part of the jump was fuel surcharges passed through to customers.

59.1%

Costs stayed controlled

The operating ratio — operating costs per $100 of revenue — was 59.1%, a touch better than a year ago, even though fuel costs jumped 69%.

$3.49

Profit per share up 16%

Net income rose 16% to $2.1B and EPS to $3.49. Adjusted EPS was $3.53 after adding back $22M of Norfolk Southern merger costs.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include $22M of acquisition-related expense for the planned Norfolk Southern merger — non-deductible for tax and worth $0.04 of EPS. Excluding it, adjusted net income was $2,098M and adjusted EPS was $3.53.

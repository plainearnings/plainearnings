Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$10.8B

Orders surged 31%

Orders rose 31% YoY to $10.8B. Industrial & Energy Technology took $5.9B — its second-largest order quarter ever, including 1.1 GW of power equipment for a data-center customer — pushing total backlog (RPO) to a record $46.4B.

$888M

Chart's first quarter

Chart, acquired July 16 for about $14.2B in cash paid during the quarter, added $888M of revenue and $156M of segment EBITDA in its first partial quarter, and $3.6B of backlog.

-33%

GAAP profit fell on deal costs

Net income attributable to Baker Hughes fell 33% YoY to $411M, even as revenue rose 14% — the gap is deal costs, Chart amortization and restructuring. On the company's adjusted basis, profit rose 2% to $695M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Transaction-related costs of $158M, mainly for the Chart acquisition and business disposals — reported inside other expense.
  • Amortization of Chart intangible assets of $143M and a $25M inventory step-up charge from the Chart deal, plus $28M of restructuring costs.
  • Leaving these items out, adjusted net income was $695M ($0.70 per share) and adjusted EBITDA was $1,415M, up 14% YoY — versus GAAP net income attributable to Baker Hughes of $411M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.1%

Organic growth beat the headline

Reported sales rose only 1.0% to $1,602M because last year's strategic portfolio actions removed whole product lines. Organic sales — the brands Church & Dwight still owns — grew 5.1%, with volume up 4.6%, well ahead of the company's 3% outlook, and all three divisions grew.

46.8%

Margins widened sharply

Gross margin rose 170 basis points to 46.8%, helped by higher volume, productivity, tariff refunds and a richer mix from acquisitions and portfolio actions, which more than offset inflation and transport costs. That margin gain is the main reason profit grew 16% on 1% sales growth.

$0.92

Outlook raised for the year

Adjusted EPS was $0.92, up 13.6%. The company raised its 2026 outlook to about 0.5% reported sales growth and about 5% organic growth, tightened adjusted EPS growth to about 7%, and approved a new $1B share repurchase program.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • SG&A includes $6M of charges for restricted stock issued for the TOUCHLAND acquisition. Excluding that and similar items, adjusted operating income was $298M and adjusted EPS was $0.92, vs $0.89 on a GAAP basis.

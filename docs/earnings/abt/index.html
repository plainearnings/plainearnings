Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$0.53

GAAP profit halved by deal charges

GAAP EPS fell 44% to $0.53 as specified items hit $1.56B after tax — amortization, legal settlements and Exact Sciences deal costs. The adjusted number Abbott steers by was $1.42, up modestly.

+7.0%

Underlying growth is 7%

Reported sales rose 14.5% with Exact Sciences included; on the comparable basis Abbott prefers (both years incl. Exact) growth was 7.0% — balanced across all four divisions.

$5.50–5.55

Guidance tightened, not cut

Abbott tightened full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $5.50–$5.55 and expects Q4 adjusted EPS of $1.62–$1.67, with comparable sales growth of about 6% for the year.

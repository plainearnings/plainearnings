Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

3.8M tons

Record shipments meet better prices

Steel operations shipped a record 3.8 million tons at $1,400 per ton while scrap costs eased, lifting steel operating income 43% from the prior quarter to $1,033M.

$476M

Aluminum is becoming real revenue

Aluminum sales jumped from $71M to $476M as the new flat-rolled mill ramped up. It still lost $26M in the quarter on startup costs, though it was breakeven before depreciation.

$846M

Cash funds buybacks and growth

Operations generated $846M of cash; the company spent $150M on capital projects, paid $76M of dividends and repurchased $276M of its own shares, keeping $2.4B of liquidity.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Aluminum operations recorded a $26M operating loss in the quarter from startup and ramp-up costs at the new mill — an improvement from a $33M loss in the prior quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1.9%

Comparable sales stayed positive

Global comparable sales rose 1.9% — a 13th consecutive positive quarter: the U.S. grew 2.5% on higher average checks, International Operated Markets 2.7% led by the U.K. and Germany, and Developmental Licensed Markets slipped 0.2% on the war in the Middle East.

$6.2B

Revenue grew 5%

Total revenue rose 5% to $6,169M (+4% in constant currencies): franchised rent and royalties rose 4% to $3,723M and company-run restaurant sales rose 6% to $2,355M.

$2.66

Charges trimmed EPS slightly

Operating income rose 8% to $2,736M despite $35M of pre-tax restructuring charges for 'Accelerating the Organization.' Excluding charges, EPS was $2.70 versus the reported $2.66.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax charges of $35M ($0.04 per share), primarily restructuring charges for 'Accelerating the Organization,' the company's internal modernization program. Excluding them, EPS was $2.70.

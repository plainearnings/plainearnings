Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.8%

Comps bounced back

Global comparable sales rose 3.8% — the U.S. +3.9% (versus -3.6% a year ago) on higher checks, International Operated Markets +3.9% and licensed markets +3.4%. Systemwide sales across all restaurants rose 11% to over $34B.

$2.78

EPS rose 7%

Diluted EPS was $2.78, up 7% (currency flattered it by $0.13). Excluding $47M of restructuring charges tied to the 'Accelerating the Organization' program, it was $2.83. Operating income rose 12% to $3.0B.

61%

Franchisees carry the model

Franchise revenue — rent and royalties from other people's restaurants — was $4.0B, 61% of all revenue, and produced $3.3B of margin. Company-run stores brought in most of the rest at much thinner margins ($285M).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax charges of $47M ($0.05 per share) for restructuring under the 'Accelerating the Organization' program; the year-ago quarter had $66M of similar charges. Excluding them, operating income rose 11%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1.5%

Comps grew — barely, at home

Global comparable sales rose 1.5%, but U.S. comps slipped 0.2% as guest counts fell; international licensed markets led with +4.2%.

$38B

The system dwarfs the P&L

Systemwide sales topped $38B, up 4% — McDonald's books only the fees on most of it. Loyalty members alone spent more than $11B in the quarter.

+10%

Profit per share outran sales

Diluted EPS rose 10% to $3.51 on cost cuts and a shrinking share count, and the quarterly dividend was raised 4% to $1.93 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Results include $56M of pre-tax restructuring charges from the 'Accelerating the Organization' program (about $0.06 per share). Excluding them, EPS was $3.57.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-1.0%

Comps dipped, mostly the calendar

Global comparable sales fell 1.0% — the U.S. -3.6% on fewer guest visits, International Operated Markets -1.0% with the U.K. weak, licensed markets +3.5% on the Middle East and Japan. Against a Leap Day quarter a year ago, comps were essentially flat.

$2.60

EPS eased 2%

Diluted EPS was $2.60, down 2%. Excluding $66M of pre-tax restructuring charges under 'Accelerating the Organization', it was $2.67, down 1%. Net income was $1,868M.

61%

Franchisees carry the model

Franchise revenue — rent and royalties from other people's restaurants — was $3.7B, 61% of all revenue, and fell only 2%. Company-run store sales fell 9% to $2.1B, at much thinner margins.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax charges of $66M ($0.07 per share), primarily restructuring charges under the 'Accelerating the Organization' program to modernize how the company works; excluding them, operating income fell 2% and EPS was $2.67.

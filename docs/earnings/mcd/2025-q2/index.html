Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.8%

Comps rose across the board

Global comparable sales rose 3.8% — the U.S. +2.5%, International Operated Markets +4.0% and licensed markets +5.6%, led by Japan. Systemwide sales rose 8%.

$3.14

EPS rose 12%

Diluted EPS was $3.14, up 12%. Excluding $43M of pre-tax restructuring charges under 'Accelerating the Organization', it was $3.19. Net income was $2,253M.

62%

Franchisees carry the model

Franchise revenue — rent and royalties from other people's restaurants — was $4.2B, 62% of all revenue, and grew 7%. Company-run store sales were flat at $2.5B at much thinner margins.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax charges of $43M ($0.05 per share), primarily restructuring charges under the 'Accelerating the Organization' program to modernize how the company works; excluding them, operating income rose 7% and EPS was $3.19.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.6%

Comps rose everywhere

Global comparable sales rose 3.6% — the U.S. +2.4% on positive check growth, International Operated Markets +4.3% led by Germany and Australia, and licensed markets +4.7% led by Japan. Systemwide sales rose 8% to over $36B.

$3.18

EPS rose 2%

Diluted EPS was $3.18, up 2%. Excluding $39M of pre-tax restructuring charges under 'Accelerating the Organization', it was $3.22. Operating income rose 5% to $3.4B.

62%

Franchisees carry the model

Franchise revenue — rent and royalties from other people's restaurants — was $4.4B, 62% of all revenue, and grew 7%. Company-run stores shrank 3% to $2.6B at much thinner margins.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net pre-tax charges of $39M ($0.04 per share), primarily restructuring charges under the 'Accelerating the Organization' program to modernize how the company works; excluding them, operating income rose 3%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+0.4%

Comparable sales barely grew

Global comparable sales rose 0.4%: the U.S. fell 1.4% on fewer guests and a lower average check, International Operated Markets were nearly flat at +0.1%, and Developmental Licensed Markets grew 4.1%. Total revenue was flat at $6.4B.

$30B

Loyalty is becoming the engine

Systemwide sales topped $130B for the full year; loyalty members accounted for about $30B of that — roughly $8B in this quarter alone — across 60 loyalty markets, with more than 175 million 90-day active users.

$2.80

Profit held up on franchised margins

Operating income rose 2% to $2.9B as higher franchised margins offset weaker company-owned restaurant margins, and EPS was flat at $2.80 ($2.83 excluding $18M of net charges).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Results included net pre-tax charges of $18M ($0.03 per share) — restructuring charges under the 'Accelerating the Organization' program and a write-off of impaired software no longer in use, partly offset by gains. Excluding them, EPS was $2.83.

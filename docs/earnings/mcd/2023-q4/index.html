Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.4%

Comparable sales stayed positive everywhere

Global comparable sales rose 3.4% — U.S. +4.3% and International Operated Markets +4.4% — while International Developmental Licensed Markets managed only +0.7% as the war in the Middle East weighed on sales.

+8%

Revenue and profit both grew 8%

Revenue rose 8% to $6,406M and operating income 8% to $2,802M. For the full year, global comparable sales grew 9% — more than 30% above 2019 levels — and systemwide sales to loyalty members passed $20B.

$2.80

Charges trimmed EPS

EPS rose 8% to $2.80 despite $138M of pre-tax charges — a $72M write-off of software no longer in use and $66M for the Accelerating the Arches restructuring. Excluding them, EPS was $2.95, up 14%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax charges of $138M ($0.15 per share): $72M to write off impaired software no longer in use, and $66M related to the Accelerating the Arches growth strategy, including restructuring for 'Accelerating the Organization,' the company's internal modernization program. Excluding them, EPS was $2.95.

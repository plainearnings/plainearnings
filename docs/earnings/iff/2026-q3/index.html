Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

All three divisions grew

Taste ($687M), Scent ($689M) and Health & Biosciences ($566M) all grew sales, with currency-neutral comparable growth of 5% overall.

$236M

The loss is a write-down, not trading

Operations earned $160M. The $127M continuing loss came from marking the soon-to-be-sold Food Ingredients business down by $236M, plus a $23M goodwill impairment.

$7.5–7.6B

Guidance nudged up

IFF raised the low end of its full-year sales outlook to $7.5–7.6B and completed a $500M share buyback in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $236M loss on assets classified as held for sale — writing the Food Ingredients disposal group down to what IFF expects to get for it; the sale is on track to close by the end of Q2 2027.
  • A $23M goodwill impairment inside operating costs.
  • The discontinued businesses being sold (Food Ingredients and SCL) actually made $72M this quarter, so IFF's total net loss was $55M (EPS -$0.22) rather than the $127M continuing loss shown in the chart.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+40%

AI demand lit the fuse

A record quarter: revenue up 40% to $4.02B, operating income nearly doubled to $1.61B (40.1% margin), and management guided to a record Q4.

84%

Data centers are the growth story

Communications revenue — the bucket that holds data-center sales — grew 84%. Industrial, still the biggest bucket, grew 53%.

$1.7B

Cash machine running

Shareholders got $1.7B back in the quarter via dividends and buybacks; trailing-twelve-month free cash flow was $4.9B, 36% of revenue.

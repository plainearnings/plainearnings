Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$18.3B

Orders rose 71% organically

Orders rose 71% organically to $18.3B with growth in all segments, and total backlog grew $13B in one quarter to $163B. Gas Power equipment backlog plus slot reservation agreements leapt from 83 GW to 100 GW, and the company now expects at least 110 GW by year-end 2026.

$4.0B

A paper gain made the profit

Net income of $4.7B includes a $3,992M pre-tax gain from remeasuring GE Vernova's old 50% Prolec GE stake when it bought the rest, plus a $330M gain on selling the Proficy software business. Stripping out one-offs, adjusted EBITDA was $896M, up 87%.

-23%

Wind kept shrinking

Wind revenue fell 23% to $1.4B on lower onshore deliveries and the segment's EBITDA loss widened to $382M, while Electrification revenue jumped 61% to $3.0B with Prolec GE included and Power rose 12% to $5.0B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gains on purchases and sales of business interests were $4,405M pre-tax: a $3,992M gain remeasuring the previously held Prolec GE stake, a $330M gain on the sale of the Proficy manufacturing-software business, and an $86M gain on selling an equity-method investment (components per the 10-Q note).
  • Net interest and investment income of $263M included $251M of gains on the China XD Electric stake ($231M unrealized and $20M realized).
  • GE Vernova bought the remaining 50% of Prolec GE on February 2, 2026 for about $5.3B of cash, issued $2.6B of senior notes, and incurred $23M of separation costs and $41M of costs under its restructuring plan in the quarter.
  • Free cash flow was $4.8B in the quarter — more than all of 2025 — and management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $44.5–45.5B of revenue and $6.5–7.5B of free cash flow.

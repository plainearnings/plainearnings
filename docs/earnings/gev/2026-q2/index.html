Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$24.2B

Orders nearly doubled

Orders rose 88% organically to $24.2B, driven by Power equipment, and total backlog grew $13B in one quarter to $176B. Gas Power equipment backlog plus slot reservation agreements rose from 100 GW to 116 GW, and the company now expects at least 125 GW under contract by year-end 2026.

+68%

Grid gear is the growth star

Electrification revenue jumped 68% to $3.6B (+29% without the Prolec GE acquisition) as utilities buy substations and transmission gear, while Power revenue rose 14% to $5.5B. Wind was the drag: revenue fell 10% and the segment EBITDA loss widened to $275M.

$5.1B

Cash poured in, guidance went up

Free cash flow was $5.1B in the quarter — more than all of 2025 — lifting the cash balance to $13.1B. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $45.5–$46.5B of revenue and $11.5–$12.5B of free cash flow.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges of $9M and separation costs of $38M (from the spin-out from GE) were excluded from adjusted EBITDA of $1,250M, as were $48M of losses on purchases and sales of business interests.
  • Cost of equipment includes a $35M fair-value adjustment to Prolec GE inventory. GE Vernova bought the remaining 50% of Prolec GE in February 2026 for about $5.3B of cash.
  • GE Vernova monetized its remaining ownership stake in China XD Electric for about $0.6B of pre-tax proceeds, and voluntarily contributed about $0.5B to the GE Energy Pension Plan.

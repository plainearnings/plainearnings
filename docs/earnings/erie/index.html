Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2.6%

Core fees grew

Management fees for policy issuance and renewal — the engine of the business — rose 2.6% to $847 million as the Exchange's direct written premiums grew 1.8%.

$485M

Agents took a bigger share

Commissions and agent incentives together rose about 5% to $485 million on incentive pay tied to better underwriting results, which is why operating income slipped 0.7% despite higher fees.

$3.48

EPS essentially flat

Net income was $182.0 million, or $3.48 a share, versus $182.9 million and $3.50 a year ago; for the first nine months, net income is up 3.4% to $513 million.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

61M

Fans showed up in record numbers

Attendance grew 19% to 61 million, international markets supplied 60% of the growth, and 170 million tickets for 2026 shows had been sold through October — already ahead of all of 2025.

+14%

All three businesses grew AOI

Adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) rose 14% to $1,179M: Concerts $603M (+17%), Ticketing $316M (+10%) and Sponsorship & Advertising $350M (+12%). GAAP operating income was $892M, up 13%.

$448M

Shareholders' share of profit

Consolidated net income was $567M; after $119M attributable to noncontrolling interests, $448M belonged to Live Nation common stockholders. After $43M of accretion on redeemable interests, diluted EPS available was $1.69.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $7M loss on disposal of operating assets this quarter compares with a $15M gain a year ago.
  • Nine-month corporate expenses include a $450M charge for governmental investigations and litigation recorded earlier in 2026 — it is not in this quarter's $114M corporate line, but it is why nine-month GAAP operating income is down 25% despite the record third quarter.

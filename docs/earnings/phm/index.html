Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-9%

Fewer homes closed

Closings fell 9% to 6,876 homes and the average price slipped 1% to $561,000, so home sale revenue fell 9% to $3.9B. Net new orders also fell 6% to 6,208 homes.

24.8%

Margin held up thanks to buyer mix

Home sale gross margin was 24.8%, down from 26.2% a year ago and including $26M of land impairments. More than 60% of sales are now higher-margin move-up and active-adult homes.

$6.4B

The backlog still grew

Backlog rose 4% to 10,298 homes worth $6.4B, and PulteGroup ended the quarter with $1.4B of cash and a low 12% debt-to-capital ratio after buying back $350M of shares.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $26M of land impairments are inside home sale cost of revenues this quarter — about 0.7 percentage points of the reported 24.8% home sale gross margin.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Industrial is outgrowing Auto

Industrial (Motion) sales rose 6.1% to $2.41B on 5.5% comparable growth, while total Automotive sales rose about 3% to $4.11B — North America +2.2%, International +4.5%.

$2.07

Adjusted EPS vs $1.62 GAAP

Excluding $61M of after-tax adjustments ($0.45 a share) — restructuring and split-related costs — adjusted EPS was $2.07 and adjusted net income about $285M.

2027

A split is coming

Genuine Parts is separating into Global Automotive and Global Industrial, targeted for Q1 2027 — the restructuring line ($64.5M this quarter) is the visible cost of becoming two companies.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other costs were $64.5M in the quarter; total after-tax adjustments excluded from adjusted earnings were $61M ($0.45 per share).

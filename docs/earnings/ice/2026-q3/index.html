ICE headlines net revenue — the $2,765M left after $865M of transaction-based expenses (SEC Section 31 fees and liquidity payments to traders). This chart starts one step earlier, from $3,630M of gross revenue, so the pass-through costs are visible. Net income attributable to ICE shareholders was $940M, after $16M belonging to non-controlling interests.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Exchanges had a record quarter

Exchange net revenue rose 22% to $1,542M — energy up 25%, financials up 37%, ags and metals up 62% — as customers traded and hedged through moving markets.

$1.37B

Recurring revenue keeps compounding

Recurring revenues hit a record $1,365M, up 7%, and ICE raised 2026 recurring growth guidance for both exchanges (9%) and fixed income & data (8%).

2%

Mortgage is still the soft spot

Mortgage Technology earned just $13M of GAAP operating income — a 2% margin — because of heavy acquisition amortization; on an adjusted basis it earned $219M (40% margin).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted EPS of $2.02 vs $1.67 GAAP mainly excludes amortization of acquisition-related intangibles and $49M of transaction and integration costs sitting in operating expenses.
  • Regulators cleared the MarketAxess acquisition early (HSR waiting period terminated Oct 21); ICE now expects it to close in Q1 2027.

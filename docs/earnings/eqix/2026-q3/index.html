Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$533M

Record bookings

Annualized gross bookings hit a record $533 million, up 35% year over year, on strong demand and firmer pricing — future recurring revenue stacking up.

+27%

Operating profit jumped

Operating income rose to $600 million as revenue grew faster than costs; adjusted EBITDA reached $1.32 billion, a 51% margin.

$11.64

AFFO per share

Adjusted funds from operations — the cash-earnings yardstick for REITs — rose 18% per share, helped by a one-time atNorth fee; excluding it, normalized growth was 12%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A non-recurring fee from Equinix's investment in the atNorth joint venture lifted other income ($54M) and AFFO this quarter; excluding it, AFFO per share grew 12% on a normalized basis.
  • Operating expenses include $6M of restructuring charges, $20M of transaction costs and $6M of impairment charges.

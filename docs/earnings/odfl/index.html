Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

Pricing drove growth

LTL revenue per hundredweight rose 13.3% while LTL tons per day fell 0.1% — growth was price, not volume.

71.5%

Operating ratio improved

Operating expenses were 71.5% of revenue, down from 74.3% — salaries and overhead both leveraged the revenue gain.

$1.65

EPS up 29%

Diluted EPS rose to $1.65 from $1.28, on net income of $344M.

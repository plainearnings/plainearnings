Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+112%

Revenue more than doubled

Revenue rose 112% from a year ago to $1.64B as the January launch of spot bitcoin ETFs pulled traders back: total trading volume hit $312B, and transaction revenue alone was $1.08B, up 187%.

$737M

Paper crypto gains did the heavy lifting

A new accounting rule (ASU 2023-08) let Coinbase mark the crypto it holds to market prices, adding $737M of pre-tax gains — $650M on crypto held for investment and $86M on crypto held for operations. Most of it is unrealized, and it made up the bulk of the quarter's $1.18B net income.

$1.01B

Adjusted EBITDA topped $1B

Adjusted EBITDA was $1,014M — 62% of revenue — as costs grew far more slowly than revenue. Subscription and services revenue, the steadier half of the business, rose 41% to $511M, led by $197M of stablecoin revenue.

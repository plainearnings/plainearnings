Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-44%

Trading revenue took the hit

Transaction revenue fell 44% to $590M in a quiet crypto market. Subscriptions and services held up better (−20%), and stablecoin revenue of $307M barely fell — the steadier business is becoming the bigger one.

$715M

The profit is an investment gain

Operating profit was just $62M. Net income of $719M exists because crypto Coinbase holds on its own balance sheet gained $715M — if crypto prices fall, that line reverses just as fast.

$333M

Costs were cut to match the market

Total operating expenses fell 17% YoY, and Adjusted EBITDA rose 60% from Q2 to $333M — Coinbase's 15th straight positive quarter, through boom and bust alike.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $715M mark-to-market gain on crypto assets held for investment accounts for essentially all of the quarter's $719M net income. Coinbase's operating business, by contrast, earned $62M.

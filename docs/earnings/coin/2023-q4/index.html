Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+83%

Trading roared back

Transaction revenue jumped 83% from the third quarter to $529M as crypto prices and volatility climbed into year-end. Consumer trading supplied $493M of it; institutions $37M.

$375M

The steadier business kept growing

Subscription and services revenue reached $375M — 39% of the total — led by $172M of stablecoin revenue and $95M of blockchain rewards. It is the cushion that keeps Coinbase profitable when trading cools.

$141M

A tax benefit padded the profit

The tax line was a $141M benefit, $121M of it a non-cash release of the valuation allowance Coinbase had held against its deferred tax assets. That is why net income of $273M is more than double the $133M pre-tax profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $121M non-cash income-tax benefit from releasing part of Coinbase's valuation allowance against its deferred tax assets — the main reason the quarter's $141M tax benefit is so large.
  • An $18M gain on buying back Coinbase's own debt below face value, inside other income.
  • $15M of legal settlement costs inside general & administrative expense.

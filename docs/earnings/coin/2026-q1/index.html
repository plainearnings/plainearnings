Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-40%

Trading slowed with the market

Transaction revenue fell 40% year over year to $756M as consumer trading volumes dropped 54%. Subscriptions and services fell only 14%, cushioning the downturn.

-$482M

Paper losses on Coinbase's own crypto

The crypto Coinbase holds as an investment lost $482M on paper in the quarter — more than the entire $394M net loss. The core business ran close to breakeven, with an operating loss of just $21M.

$10.2B

Cash covered the loss

Coinbase ended the quarter with $10.2B of cash and equivalents, so the loss came out of a deep cushion — but it shows how quickly profits swing with crypto prices.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $482M mark-to-market loss on crypto assets held for investment (versus a $597M loss a year ago) — non-cash, and it reverses if crypto prices rise.
  • Losses of $35M on crypto held for operations, also mark-to-market.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-22%

Trading slowed with the market

Transaction revenue fell 22% year over year to $599M as crypto spot volumes shrank. Subscriptions and services — 48% of net revenue — fell only 12%, cushioning the downturn.

-$209M

Paper losses on Coinbase's own crypto

A year ago this line was a $362M gain; this quarter the crypto Coinbase holds as an investment lost $209M on paper. That one swing is bigger than half the net loss.

$208M

The core business still earned its keep

Adjusted EBITDA was positive for the 14th straight quarter at $208M, and total operating expenses fell 12% year over year as Coinbase cut costs to match the quieter market.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $209M mark-to-market loss on crypto assets held for investment (versus a $362M gain a year ago) — non-cash, and it reverses if crypto prices rise.
  • A $52M restructuring charge from the plan announced in May 2026 to realign the cost base — severance and related one-off costs.
  • Losses of $32M on crypto held for operations, also mark-to-market.

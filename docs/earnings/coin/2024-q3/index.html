Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+98%

Trading nearly doubled from a year ago

Transaction revenue rose 98% year over year to $573M — consumer trading alone was $483M — even though volume fell 18% from Q2 to $185B as crypto volatility eased.

$556M

Steadier revenue nearly matched trading

Subscription and services revenue rose 66% year over year to $556M, almost as much as trading brought in — stablecoin revenue was the biggest piece at $247M, ahead of blockchain rewards at $155M.

$121M

Paper crypto losses capped profit

A $121M pre-tax loss on crypto Coinbase holds as an investment — mostly unrealized, $92M after tax — held net income to $75M, even though Adjusted EBITDA was $449M for a 7th straight positive quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $121M mark-to-market loss on crypto assets held for investment — mostly unrealized and non-cash ($92M after tax), and it reverses if crypto prices recover.
  • $9M of net operating gains (crypto held for operations, and other operating income) that Coinbase reports inside its expense lines; the chart shows them in the other-income step — see the note under the chart.

Coinbase — Q2 2025 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q2 2025 · Quarter ended 2025-06-30
Reported 2025-07-31

Coinbase's core business ran at a small operating loss in a quieter crypto quarter — revenue fell 26% from Q1 as trading cooled — but paper gains on its investments, above all a roughly $1.5B mark-up on its Circle stake, turned the quarter into a $1.43B net profit.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.5B

A paper gain did the heavy lifting

Coinbase's stake in Circle — the stablecoin company that went public in June — was marked up by about $1.5B, and the crypto it holds as an investment gained another $362M. Neither is cash, and both reverse if prices fall: adjusted net income, which strips both out, was just $33M.

-26%

Trading cooled from Q1

Revenue fell 26% from the first quarter to $1.50B as crypto volatility — the fuel of trading fees — dropped. Transaction revenue fell 39% to $764M, while subscription and services revenue held up better, slipping 6% to $656M.

$308M

The data-theft bill arrived

Other operating costs were $308M, substantially all tied to the May 2025 data-theft incident — customer reimbursements, remediation and legal costs. That one item is what pushed the core business to a $25M operating loss.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A roughly $1.5B pre-tax gain on strategic investments, mostly an unrealized mark-up of Coinbase's stake in Circle after its June 2025 IPO — non-cash.
  • A $362M pre-tax gain on crypto assets held for investment — largely unrealized.
  • $308M of costs tied to the May 2025 data-theft incident, inside other operating expense.

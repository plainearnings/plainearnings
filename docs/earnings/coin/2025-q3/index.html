Of every $100 Coinbase took in, paper gains on the crypto it holds as an investment added $22.68, while paper losses on its strategic investments took $20.36 back out — the two nearly cancelled. Rows may not add exactly due to rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+83%

Trading roared back

Transaction revenue rose 83% year over year to $1.05B — consumer trading alone was $844M — as crypto prices and volumes recovered from a soft second quarter.

$424M

Paper gains beat paper losses

The crypto Coinbase holds as an investment gained $424M on paper, while its strategic investments lost about $400M. Adjusted net income was $421M — close to the reported $433M, because the two swings nearly cancelled.

$61M

Data-theft costs lingered

Other operating costs included $61M tied to the May 2025 data-theft incident — voluntary customer reimbursements and legal costs. Coinbase also closed its Deribit acquisition in August, adding costs across its main expense lines.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $424M mark-to-market gain on crypto assets held for investment — non-cash, and it reverses if crypto prices fall.
  • About $400M of net losses on strategic and other investments, inside the $381M of other expense, net.
  • $61M of costs from the May 2025 data-theft incident — customer reimbursements and legal bills — inside other operating expense.

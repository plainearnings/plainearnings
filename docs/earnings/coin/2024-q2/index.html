Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+139%

Trading more than doubled from a year ago

Transaction revenue rose 139% year over year to $781M — consumer trading alone was $665M — even though volume fell 28% from Q1 to $226B as crypto volatility eased from the ETF-launch peak.

$599M

Steadier revenue kept diversifying

Subscription and services revenue rose 79% year over year to $599M — 41% of all revenue — led by stablecoin revenue of $240M and blockchain rewards of $185M. It grew 17% from Q1 even as trading fell.

$319M

Paper crypto losses capped profit

A $319M pre-tax loss on crypto Coinbase holds as an investment — the vast majority unrealized, $248M after tax — plus a $96M tax benefit left net income at just $36M, even though Adjusted EBITDA was $596M for a 6th straight positive quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $319M mark-to-market loss on crypto assets held for investment — the vast majority unrealized and non-cash ($248M after tax), and it reverses if crypto prices recover.
  • $65M of net operating losses (a $31M loss on crypto held for operations and $34M of other operating expense) that Coinbase reports inside its expense lines; the chart shows them in the other-expense step — see the note under the chart.

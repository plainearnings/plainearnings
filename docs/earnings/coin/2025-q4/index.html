Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-$718M

Paper losses drove the red ink

The core business earned a $274M operating profit, but the crypto Coinbase holds as an investment lost $718M on paper and its strategic investments lost another $395M — together they turned a profitable quarter into a $667M net loss. Adjusted net income was $178M.

+13%

Subscriptions kept growing

Subscription and services revenue rose 13% year over year to $727M — stablecoin revenue alone was $364M — cushioning a 37% drop in trading revenue as crypto markets cooled.

$11.3B

Cash covered the loss

Coinbase ended 2025 with $11.3B of cash and equivalents, and still bought back $850M of its own shares in the quarter — the loss came out of a deep cushion.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $718M mark-to-market loss on crypto assets held for investment — non-cash, and it reverses if crypto prices rise.
  • A $395M loss on strategic investments, including Coinbase's stake in Circle (CRCL).
  • Losses of $31M on crypto held for operations, also mark-to-market.

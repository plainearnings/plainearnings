Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$439B

Trading exploded after the election

Trading volume reached $439B in the quarter, and transaction revenue rose 172% from Q3 to $1.56B as crypto prices and volatility surged in November and December. Trading is still the engine: it brought in 68 cents of every revenue dollar.

$1.29B

A record profit

Net income was $1.29B, nearly five times a year ago. About $476M of it was a paper gain on crypto Coinbase holds as an investment — real only if prices hold — but the core business was strongly profitable on its own, with $1.03B of reported operating income.

$641M

The steady side kept growing too

Subscription and services revenue — stablecoins, staking rewards, custody — rose 15% from Q3 to $641M. It is smaller than trading but far less dependent on market mood swings.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $476M pre-tax gain on crypto assets held for investment — unrealized, and it reverses if crypto prices fall.
  • $37M of net operating gains (crypto held for operations, and other operating income) that Coinbase reports inside its expense lines; the chart shows them in the other-income step — see the note under the chart.

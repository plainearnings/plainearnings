Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8.5B

Orders and backlog hit records

Total orders rose about 35% and data-center orders about 300%, taking backlog above $8.5B, up about 60% YoY. Carrier also signed long-term agreements worth multiple billions with minimum volume commitments, on top of backlog.

$3.1B

A Europe write-down drove the GAAP loss

A non-cash $3.1B goodwill impairment on Climate Solutions Europe turned a profitable operating quarter into a $2,273M GAAP operating loss. Excluding it and other items, adjusted operating profit was $1,091M, up 33%, at a 17.4% margin.

$0.87

Underlying EPS grew 30%

Adjusted EPS was $0.87 vs $0.67 a year ago, helped by about $130M of tariff (IEEPA) refunds in operating profit. Free cash flow was $674M and Carrier returned about $600M to shareholders.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Goodwill impairment of $3,100M (non-cash) on the Climate Solutions Europe reporting unit — the entire reason for the GAAP loss; it is excluded from adjusted results.
  • About $130M of operating-profit benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds in the quarter (about $120M of it in Climate Solutions Americas); adjusted EPS includes about a $0.10 benefit from the refunds, net of a $0.02 noncontrolling-interest effect.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-25%

Revenue shrank — services most of all

Revenue fell 25% to $52.4M. Subscription revenue fell 18% to $49.2M, and professional services fell 68% to $3.2M as paid feature-acceleration work dried up. Management guided next quarter to $51M–$55M and the full year to $210M–$240M.

32%

Costs fell faster than revenue

Total costs and running expenses dropped from $195.1M to $150.6M in a year, so the operating loss narrowed from $124.8M to $98.3M. GAAP gross margin was 32% (50% before share-based pay). On the company's non-GAAP basis the operating loss was $36.2M.

+$2.1M

Cash flow turned positive

Operating cash flow and free cash flow were both about +$2.1M, versus a $34.3M free-cash outflow a year ago. With $651.1M of cash and marketable securities, the company has a long runway — though $59.2M of the quarter's costs were share-based pay, a real cost paid in stock rather than cash.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges were $0.7M this quarter, part of the turnaround plan that reorganised sales, services and products.
  • Share-based compensation was $59.2M — more than the quarter's revenue. Excluding it, employer payroll taxes on it and restructuring, the non-GAAP net loss was $30.7M.

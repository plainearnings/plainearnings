Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8.1%

Both segments grew

Sales reached $3.12B as Freight rose 6.9% and Transit rose 11.3%, with the Frauscher and Dellner acquisitions adding to demand that was already healthy.

20.0%

Margins widened

GAAP operating margin hit 20.0%, up 3.0 points, as gross margin improved to 37.3% — the company kept more of each sales dollar than a year ago.

$31.4B

A record order book

Backlog reached $31.4B, up 23% from a year ago — years of locomotive, parts and transit work already ordered by customers.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted EPS of $2.83 excludes $89M of non-cash amortization of acquisition intangibles (worth $0.39 a share after tax) and $4M of after-tax restructuring and portfolio-optimization costs.

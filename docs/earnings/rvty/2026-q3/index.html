Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11%

Life Sciences led the quarter

Life Sciences revenue rose to $380M, with pro forma organic growth of 9% (growth from the existing business, stripping out currency and deals).

+4%

Diagnostics grew once the sale is stripped out

Reported Diagnostics revenue fell 2% to $349M because it still includes the China immunodiagnostics business being divested. On a pro forma basis excluding it, Diagnostics grew 4%.

$5.40–$5.45

Guidance raised

Revvity now expects pro forma adjusted EPS of $5.40–$5.45 for 2026 and pro forma revenue of about $2.86B with roughly 5% organic growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges of $28M in the quarter weighed on GAAP operating income of $95M; adjusted operating income was $209M.
  • Revvity has agreed to sell its China immunodiagnostics business (about 6% of 2025 revenue), expected to close by the end of 2027; results above are as reported, with pro forma figures excluding that business noted where relevant.

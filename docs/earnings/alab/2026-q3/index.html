Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+154%

Scorpio took off

Revenue hit a record $584.9M, up 154% from a year ago and 49% from Q2. The Scorpio fabric switches — chips that link racks of AI accelerators together — ramped to high-volume production and became the company's largest product family.

72.3%

Very profitable growth

Gross margin was 72.3% and operating income was $179.1M, a 31% operating margin. Net income was $182.9M, helped by $13.0M of interest income, against a small $9.1M tax bill.

53%

One customer is half the business

A single customer accounted for 53% of revenue in the quarter (by billing address — the chips flow through manufacturing partners to cloud end-customers, so this can swing between quarters). Management guided Q4 revenue to $730M–$750M, which would be another jump of about 25%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating costs include $81.7M of stock-based compensation in the quarter (pay in shares rather than cash). Leaving that and similar items out, the company's own non-GAAP operating income was $261.6M and non-GAAP EPS was $1.32, versus $179.1M and $0.99 on a GAAP basis.

For every $100 TSMC took in, $50.48 survived as profit. AI data-center chips (high-performance computing) supplied $61.26 of the $100 — and advanced nodes (7-nanometer and below) made up 74% of wafer revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

61%

AI chips run the show

High-performance computing — mostly AI accelerators — was 61% of revenue, and 3-nanometer plus 5-nanometer chips together were 61% of wafer revenue. Leading-edge demand is the whole story.

66.2%

Margins at record levels

Gross margin reached 66.2% and net margin 50.5% — TSMC charges premium prices for capacity nobody else can supply, and more than half of revenue now lands as profit.

$39–40B

Guidance points higher

For Q2 2026 TSMC guided revenue of $39.0–40.2B with gross margin of 65.5–67.5% — another step up, on continued leading-edge demand.

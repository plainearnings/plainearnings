Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

68%

AI chips are the business

High-performance computing — mostly AI accelerators — was 68% of revenue and grew 22% from Q2. Advanced chips (7nm and below) were 79% of wafer revenue, and the newest 2nm node already contributed 8%.

66.4%

Margins most companies dream of

Gross margin of 66.4%, operating margin of 58.7% and a net margin of 51.1% — TSMC keeps more than half of every sales dollar as profit, even while spending heavily on new fabs.

$52.3B+

Next quarter guided higher still

Management guided Q4 2026 revenue to US$52.3–54.3B, driven by the steep ramp of 2nm production — the quarter ahead is expected to be bigger than this record one.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-operating income fell to NT$34.6B from NT$95.8B in Q2 2026, when TSMC booked unusually large non-operating gains — that is why net income grew only 8% from Q2 while operating income grew 14%.

For every $100 TSMC took in, $55.62 survived as profit — the fattest margin of any company on this site. Advanced chips (7-nanometer and below) were 77% of wafer revenue. Rows may not add exactly due to rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

66%

AI chips are the business now

High-performance computing — AI accelerators and server chips — reached 66% of revenue, up 20% in a single quarter. Smartphone chips fell 4% ahead of autumn phone launches.

67.7%

Margins keep climbing

Gross margin hit 67.7%, up from 58.6% a year ago, as premium 3nm and 5nm wafers made up 63% of wafer revenue. The new 2nm node shipped its first revenue — 3% of wafers.

+77%

Profit rose much faster than sales

Net income rose 77% to NT$706.6B on 36% revenue growth (in NT$) — operating leverage, plus a jump in non-operating income helped by the Vanguard stake sale.

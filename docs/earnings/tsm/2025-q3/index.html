Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

74%

Leading edge is the business

Chips made on 7-nanometer and more advanced processes were 74% of wafer revenue — 3nm alone was 23% as the newest process ramped, with 5nm at 37%.

59.5%

Margins near record levels

A gross margin of 59.5% and an operating margin of 50.6% mean TSMC kept about half of every revenue dollar after running the fabs.

$32.2–33.4B

A slightly cooler Q4 ahead

Management guided fourth-quarter revenue to $32.2–33.4B with a gross margin of 59–61% — a touch below Q3's level, at a stronger assumed NT dollar.

TSMC — Q3 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q3 2024 · Quarter ended 2024-09-30
Reported 2024-10-17

TSMC's revenue rose 39% to NT$759.7B ($23.5B) on strong smartphone and AI demand for its 3- and 5-nanometer chips — and with a 57.8% gross margin, it kept NT$325.3B ($10.1B) as profit, up 54% in a year.

For every $100 TSMC took in, $42.82 survived as profit. AI data-center chips (high-performance computing) supplied $51.00 of the $100 — and advanced nodes (7-nanometer and below) made up 69% of wafer revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

51%

AI chips are the majority now

High-performance computing — mostly AI accelerators — was 51% of revenue and grew 11% from the second quarter; smartphone chips were another 34%, up 16%. By node, 3-nanometer was 20% of wafer revenue, 5-nanometer 32% and 7-nanometer 17%.

57.8%

Margins expanded again

Gross margin reached 57.8%, up from 54.3% a year ago, and the net margin was 42.8% — TSMC charges premium prices for leading-edge capacity nobody else can supply, so profit grew 54% on 39% revenue growth.

$26.1–26.9B

Guidance pointed up

TSMC guided fourth-quarter 2024 revenue of $26.1–26.9B with a gross margin of 57–59% — another sequential jump, on continued strong demand for its leading-edge process technologies.

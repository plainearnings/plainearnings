TSMC — Q2 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q2 2024 · Quarter ended 2024-06-30
Reported 2024-07-18

TSMC's revenue rose 40% to NT$673.5B ($20.8B) as strong demand for its 3nm and 5nm chips outweighed soft smartphone season — and with a 53.2% gross margin, it kept NT$247.8B ($7.7B) as profit, up 36%.

Of every $100 of revenue, $36.80 stayed as profit; high-performance computing alone brought in $52 of it, and advanced 7nm-and-below nodes were 67% of wafer revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

52%

AI chips are now the majority

High-performance computing passed half of revenue for the first time, jumping 28% in one quarter on demand for AI accelerators, while smartphone chip sales slipped 1% in their seasonal lull.

53.2%

Margins held above 53%

A 53.2% gross margin and 42.5% operating margin — 3nm and 5nm together were half of wafer revenue, and advanced nodes (7nm and beyond) were 67%.

$22.4–23.2B

A bigger Q3 guided

Management guided third-quarter revenue to $22.4–23.2B with a gross margin of 53.5–55.5%, expecting strong smartphone and AI-related demand for its leading-edge technologies.

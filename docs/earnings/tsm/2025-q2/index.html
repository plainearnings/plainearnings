Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

74%

Leading edge is the business

Chips made on 7-nanometer and more advanced processes were 74% of wafer revenue — 3nm alone was 24% and 5nm 36% as AI accelerators ramped.

58.6%

Margins at very high levels

A gross margin of 58.6% and an operating margin of 49.6% mean TSMC kept about half of every revenue dollar after running the fabs.

$31.8–33.0B

A stronger Q3 ahead

Management guided third-quarter revenue to $31.8–33.0B with a gross margin of 55.5–57.5% — a touch below Q2's level, at a stronger assumed NT dollar.

TSMC — Q4 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q4 2024 · Quarter ended 2024-12-31
Reported 2025-01-16

For every $100 TSMC took in, $43.14 survived as profit. AI data-center chips (high-performance computing) supplied $53.00 of the $100 — and advanced nodes (7-nanometer and below) made up 74% of wafer revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

53%

AI chips run the show

High-performance computing — mostly AI accelerators — was 53% of revenue and rose 19% from the third quarter; smartphone chips were another 35%, up 17% on seasonal launches.

59.0%

Margins jumped in a year

Gross margin reached 59.0%, up from 53.0% a year ago, and net margin was 43.1% — TSMC charges premium prices for leading-edge capacity nobody else can supply, so profit grew 57% on 39% revenue growth.

$38–42B

A huge building budget

TSMC set its 2025 capital budget at $38–42B and guided Q1 2025 revenue of $25.0–25.8B with a gross margin of 57–59% — a seasonal step down, with AI demand still growing underneath.

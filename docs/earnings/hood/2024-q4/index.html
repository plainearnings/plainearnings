Crypto alone supplied $35.31 of every $100 of revenue — $358M of the $1,014M total — as digital-asset trading rose over 700% from a year ago. The tax benefit added back another $35.31 of every $100.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$358M

Crypto revenue exploded

Transaction revenue rose 236% to $672M — crypto revenue jumped over 700% to $358M, options rose 83% to $222M and equities rose 144% to $61M as customers traded actively across asset classes.

$16.1B

Record net deposits

Net deposits hit $16.1B in the quarter — an annualized growth rate of 42% — and assets under custody rose 88% to $193B, helped by higher equity and cryptocurrency valuations.

2.6M

Gold subscribers hit a record

Robinhood Gold subscribers rose 86% to a record 2.6M, lifting other revenues 31% to $46M — steady subscription income growing alongside the trading business.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $369M deferred tax benefit ($0.41 of diluted EPS) from releasing the valuation allowance on most of Robinhood's deferred tax assets — management concluded the company is now likely to earn enough to use them. It is a one-time accounting benefit, not cash from the business, and it is most of why the tax line shows a $358M benefit.
  • A $55M benefit ($0.06 of diluted EPS) from reversing an accrual as part of a regulatory settlement. It sits inside general & administrative expenses, which is why that line fell 47% from a year ago even as the company grew.

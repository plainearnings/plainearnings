Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+72%

Trading revenue surged

Transaction-based revenue rose 72% to $319M — options revenue grew 63% to $202M, crypto jumped 165% to $61M and equities rose 37% to $37M, as customers traded actively across asset classes.

$150M

A swing to profit

Net income was $150M versus an $85M loss a year ago: revenue rose 36% while total operating expenses fell 10%, with general & administrative costs down 42%. The tax bill was just $3M — most deferred tax assets still sat under a valuation allowance.

2.2M

Gold subscribers climbed 65%

Robinhood Gold subscribers rose 65% to a record 2.2M, funded customers reached 24.3M, and net deposits were $10.0B in the quarter — a 29% annualized growth rate against assets under custody at the end of Q2.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported revenue is after $27M of matches paid to customers on transfers and deposits — an incentive Robinhood nets against revenue (it was $13M in Q2 2024), so the trading growth underneath was slightly stronger than the headline.
  • The $3M tax provision is unusually small: Robinhood still carried a valuation allowance against most of its deferred tax assets in Q3 2024. It released that allowance the following quarter, booking a one-time $369M tax benefit in Q4 2024.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

10x

Event contracts exploded again

Event-contract revenue hit $156M, up more than 10x, with a record 13.6 billion contracts traded. Options revenue rose 29% to $342M and equities 95% to $129M; crypto fell 38% to $100M.

+32%

Growth with record deposits

Revenue rose 32% to $1.31B, net deposits hit a record $22B, and Gold subscribers reached a record 4.8M — the customer base kept growing even as crypto trading cooled.

$129M

A one-off gain flatters profit

Net income rose 48% to $573M, but $129M came from deconsolidating the Robinhood Ventures fund — a paper gain, not trading revenue. Profit attributable to Robinhood was $561M.

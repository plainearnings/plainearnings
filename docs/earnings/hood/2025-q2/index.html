Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$539M

Trading boomed

Transaction revenue rose 65% — crypto revenue nearly doubled to $160M and options brought in $265M as customers traded actively.

$279B

Assets on the platform doubled

Total platform assets rose 99% to $279B, helped by $13.8B of net deposits in the quarter and higher market values.

3.5M

Gold keeps growing

Robinhood Gold subscribers rose 76% to 3.5M, and in June the company closed its Bitstamp acquisition, expanding its crypto business worldwide.

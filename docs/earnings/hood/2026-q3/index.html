Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

6x

Event contracts are a real business now

Event-contract revenue hit $173M, up more than 6x in a year, alongside options revenue of $399M (+31%) — prediction markets went from experiment to a top-three revenue line.

+173%

Credit losses are the watch item

The provision for credit losses nearly tripled to $71M as the Gold Card loan book grows. It is the cost of becoming a lender — fine while defaults stay low, painful if they don't.

-9%

Interest revenue is fading as rates fall

Net interest revenue fell 9% to $415M on lower short-term rates and weaker securities lending — a reminder that a big slice of Robinhood's income depends on the Fed, not just on traders.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$324B

Customers keep piling in assets

Total Platform Assets rose 68% to $324B, with $15.9B of net deposits in the quarter alone and $68.1B over the year. Funded customers reached 27.0M.

4.2M

Gold becomes a real business

Robinhood Gold subscribers rose 58% to a record 4.2M, and Gold subscription revenue rose 56% to $50M — part of why 'other revenues' more than doubled to $96M.

−34%

Profit is down — on paper

Net income fell from $916M to $605M, but last year's quarter included a $424M benefit from a deferred-tax item and a reversed regulatory accrual. Underneath, revenue grew 27%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago comparison is flattered by a $424M benefit in Q4 2024 — a deferred-tax benefit plus the reversal of a regulatory accrual — worth $0.47 of that quarter's $1.01 EPS. Q4 2025 has no equivalent item.
  • General and administrative costs jumped 154% to $178M, driven by acquisition-related expenses for the TradePMR and Bitstamp deals and by growth investments; total operating expenses rose 38% to $633M.

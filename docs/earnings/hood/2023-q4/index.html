Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+41%

Interest became the biggest line

Net interest revenues rose 41% year-over-year to $236M — more than trading fees — driven by growth in interest-earning assets and higher short-term interest rates.

$30M

Back in the black

Net income was $30M versus a $166M loss a year ago. Total operating expenses fell 17% to $445M, and the tax line was a $1M benefit, so profit came out slightly above the $29M pre-tax figure.

$102.6B

Customer assets jumped 65%

Assets under custody rose 65% year-over-year to $102.6B on $4.6B of net deposits and higher market values. Gold subscribers rose 25% to 1.42M and funded customers reached 23.4M.

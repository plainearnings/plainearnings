Unlike last quarter there was no one-off gain in 'other income' — the $32.43 Robinhood kept of every $100 came from the business itself. Event contracts alone supplied $13.78 of every $100 of revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

320%

Event contracts quadrupled

Event-contract revenue rose 320% to $147M and options rose 8% to $260M, more than covering a 47% drop in crypto revenue to $134M as digital-asset trading cooled.

$17.7B

Customers kept depositing

Net deposits were $17.7B in the quarter and total platform assets rose 39% to $307B; Gold subscribers grew 36% to 4.3M.

+18%

Spending grew faster than revenue

Operating expenses rose 18% to $656M — marketing, new products and acquisition costs — which is why profit rose only 3% despite 15% revenue growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses included $14M of costs tied to launching Trump Accounts and the Rothera acquisition, flagged by Robinhood in its adjusted expense figures.

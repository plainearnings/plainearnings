Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+69%

Trading revenue surged

Transaction-based revenue rose 69% to $327M — options revenue grew 43% to $182M, crypto jumped 161% to $81M and equities rose 60% to $40M, as customers traded actively across asset classes.

$188M

Profit jumped more than sevenfold

Net income was $188M versus $25M a year ago: revenue rose 40% while total operating expenses rose just 6% to $493M. The tax bill was just $3M — Robinhood still carried a full valuation allowance against its U.S. deferred tax assets.

$13.2B

Record net deposits

Net deposits hit a record $13.2B in the quarter — a 41% annualized growth rate against assets under custody, which rose 57% to $139.7B. Gold subscribers rose 61% to a record 2.0M and funded customers reached 24.2M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported revenue is after $13M of matches paid to customers on transfers and deposits — an incentive Robinhood nets against revenue (options revenue alone was reduced by $8M in the quarter), so the trading growth underneath was slightly stronger than the headline.
  • The $3M tax provision is unusually small: Robinhood carried a full valuation allowance against its U.S. federal and state deferred tax assets in Q2 2024. It released that allowance later in 2024, booking a one-time $369M tax benefit in Q4 2024.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$333B

Customers keep piling in assets

Total Platform Assets rose 119% to $333B, with a record $20.4B of net deposits in the quarter and $68.3B over the past twelve months.

3.9M

Gold becomes a real business

Robinhood Gold subscribers rose 77% to a record 3.9M, helping Other revenues double to $88M; average revenue per user rose 82% to $191.

$268M

Crypto is the swing factor

Crypto revenue rose more than 300% to $268M — over a third of all transaction revenue — alongside options at $304M, up 50%, and equities at $86M, up 132%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • General and administrative costs rose 39% to $185M, partly on payroll taxes triggered when the CEO's 2019 market-based stock awards vested as the share price climbed — a cost that grows with the stock price, not with the business.

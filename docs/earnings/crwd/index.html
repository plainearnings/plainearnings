Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$333M

Record new business

Net new ARR — new subscription value signed in the quarter — hit a record $333M, up 51%, taking total ARR to $5.84B. AI-driven attacks are pushing companies to consolidate security onto Falcon.

$5M

GAAP profit is a rounding error

GAAP net income was $5M on $1.47B of revenue. Excluding stock-based pay, profit was $323M — the gap is shares handed to employees, a real cost that simply doesn't use cash.

$377M

Cash flow tells another story

Free cash flow was a Q2 record $377M — customers pay subscriptions upfront, so cash arrives long before the revenue is counted.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The core business ran at a $33M operating loss; interest earned on the company's $5.0B cash pile more than covered it, leaving the small $5M profit. A $1M tax benefit (the tax line added money back) helped too.

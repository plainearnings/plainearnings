Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

ARR accelerated

Ending ARR grew 23% to $4.92B, with a record $265.3M of net new ARR added in the quarter — net new ARR growth accelerated to 73% YoY.

-$69M

In the red on paper

The GAAP operating loss was $69.4M as $293.3M of stock-based compensation weighed; non-GAAP operating income was a record $264.6M.

$296M

Cash flow tells another story

Free cash flow was a Q3-record $295.9M on $397.5M of operating cash flow — CrowdStrike's cash generation is far stronger than its GAAP result suggests.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Costs tied to the July 19 Incident and related matters were $26.2M in the quarter, up from $33.9M a year ago.
  • Stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes were $293.3M — the main reason non-GAAP net income attributable to CrowdStrike ($245.4M) contrasts with the GAAP attributable net loss ($34.0M).
  • The $34.0M net loss is the consolidated total; $0.0M of income belonged to a non-controlling interest, leaving a $34.0M net loss attributable to CrowdStrike.

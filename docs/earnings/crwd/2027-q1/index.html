Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

ARR keeps compounding

Annual recurring revenue grew 24% to $5.51B, with $255.8M of net new ARR added in the quarter — demand for the Falcon platform stayed strong.

-$31M

Operating loss shrank fast

The operating loss narrowed to $30.6M from $118.7M a year ago; interest and investment gains of $69.7M, plus a $6.9M tax benefit, turned the quarter profitable overall.

$469M

Cash flow tells another story

Free cash flow was $468.5M on $590.9M of operating cash flow — CrowdStrike's cash generation is far stronger than its GAAP profit suggests.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Costs tied to the July 19 Incident and related matters were $18.1M in the quarter (mostly inside G&A), down from $39.7M a year ago.
  • Stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes were $317.6M — the main reason non-GAAP net income attributable to CrowdStrike ($283.4M) is about 10× the GAAP attributable figure ($27.8M).
  • $18.2M of the quarter's $46.0M consolidated net income belonged to a non-controlling interest; net income attributable to CrowdStrike was $27.8M.

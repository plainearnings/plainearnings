Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

ARR crossed $5B

Annual recurring revenue grew 24% to $5.25B, with a record $330.7M of net new ARR added in the quarter — demand for the Falcon platform stayed strong.

-$7M

Operating loss nearly gone

The operating loss narrowed to $6.9M from $79.3M a year ago; interest income of $44.1M net, plus a $3.6M tax benefit, turned the quarter profitable overall.

$376M

Cash flow tells another story

Free cash flow was $376.4M on $497.9M of operating cash flow — CrowdStrike's cash generation is far stronger than its GAAP profit suggests.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Costs tied to the July 19 Incident and related matters were $16.2M in the quarter, down from $21.0M a year ago.
  • Stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes were $302.2M — the main reason non-GAAP net income attributable to CrowdStrike ($289.1M) is far above the GAAP attributable figure ($38.7M).
  • $2.1M of the quarter's $40.8M consolidated net income belonged to a non-controlling interest; net income attributable to CrowdStrike was $38.7M.

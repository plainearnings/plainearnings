Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Merchant is now the engine

Merchant revenue rose 15% to $5.1B as Booking collects more travelers' payments itself, while agency revenue fell 7% to $1.9B — the ongoing shift from agency to merchant at Booking.com.

325M

Travel demand stayed resilient

Room nights rose 5% to 325 million and gross bookings rose 9% to $51.0B. Alternative-accommodation room nights at Booking.com rose 4% and connected-trip bookings kept growing.

$195M

A currency swing flattered profit

A $195M foreign-currency gain on remeasuring euro-denominated debt — versus a $961M loss a year ago — is the main reason GAAP net income jumped 118%, while adjusted net income rose a steadier 8% to $2.0B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Foreign currency: a $195M gain on the remeasurement of euro-denominated debt and related derivatives, versus a $961M loss in Q2 2025; equity securities lost $25M in the quarter.
  • Discrete tax items added $79M of tax expense, including a one-time deemed-repatriation item and the tax impact of intercompany asset transfers.
  • Transformation costs were $30M in the quarter; the company raised the program's expected annual run-rate savings to about $650M, to be realized by the end of 2027.
  • Stock split: Booking completed a 25-for-1 stock split on April 2, 2026, and all share and per-share figures in the filings are retroactively adjusted.

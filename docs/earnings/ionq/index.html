Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+336%

Revenue quadrupled, backlog doubled

Revenue rose 336% to $173.8M — IonQ Platform up 127% to $90.3M on Tempo deployments, plus $83.5M from the newly acquired SkyWater foundry. Remaining performance obligations more than doubled to $1.2B, and full-year revenue guidance was raised to $505-515M.

-$433M

Operations are still deeply loss-making

The reported operating loss was $433M: total operating costs of $607M against $174M of revenue, including $149M of stock pay and $128M of depreciation and amortization. On the company's own adjusted basis, the EBITDA loss was $134M.

+$896M

A paper gain made the profit

Net income was $563M only because warrant liabilities fell in value by $896M — a non-cash accounting gain — plus a $142M tax benefit. A year ago the same warrant line was an $882M loss. Cash, equivalents and investments were $1.7B at quarter end, $3.7B pro forma for October's equity raise.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gain on change in fair value of warrant liabilities: +$896.2M — a non-cash paper gain (the warrants IonQ owes became cheaper as the share price moved). It is shown as a source in the chart, with the layout adapted and documented, because it is 5.2x revenue.
  • Income tax was a $141.7M benefit this quarter, mostly deferred — it added to the bottom line instead of reducing it.
  • Stock-based compensation was $148.6M in the quarter, and the SkyWater acquisition closed in the quarter, adding a whole foundry segment (and its costs) that was not there a year ago. Elisity was acquired on November 3, after quarter end, for about $1.3B.

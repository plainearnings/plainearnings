Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-8%

U.S. pet products are the problem

U.S. companion-animal revenue fell 8% as dermatology products faced competition and pet owners showed price sensitivity. U.S. livestock rose 5%, cushioning the segment's 6% decline.

+4%

International and livestock grew

International revenue rose 4% to $1,116M and worldwide livestock revenue rose 7% to $797M, offsetting much of the U.S. pet weakness.

$1.75

Underlying EPS still rose

GAAP net income fell 18% to $591M on higher restructuring and other deductions, but adjusted EPS rose 3% to $1.75 — and Zoetis raised its full-year adjusted EPS guidance while narrowing its revenue guidance.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges and certain acquisition/divestiture-related costs jumped to $43M from $4M a year ago.
  • Adjusted net income of $720M excludes a net $129M impact from purchase-accounting adjustments, acquisition and divestiture-related costs and certain significant items — which is why adjusted EPS rose 3% while GAAP EPS fell 12%.
  • The divested medicated feed additive portfolio cut revenue growth by about 1 percentage point in the quarter.

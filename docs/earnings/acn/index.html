Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.29

EPS up 46% (with an easy comparison)

Q4 EPS rose 46% — flattered by comparison with a year-ago quarter that absorbed $615M of restructuring. Versus adjusted EPS last year, growth was 9%.

$84.5B

Bookings stayed strong

Full-year bookings hit $84.5B with a record 141 quarterly client bookings of $100M+; Q4 bookings were $22.2B, led by managed services at a 1.4 book-to-bill.

3–6%

FY27 outlook is measured

Fiscal 2027 guidance: revenue growth of 3–6% in local currency and GAAP EPS of $14.39–14.81 — steady, not spectacular, with at least $9.5B of planned shareholder returns ($11.5B in FY26).

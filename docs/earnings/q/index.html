Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Both segments grew double digits

Net sales rose to $1,556M from $1,276M a year ago. Interconnect Solutions grew 30% to $759M and Semiconductor Technologies grew 15% to $797M.

$1.46

Adjusted profit surged

Adjusted EPS was $1.46, up 55% year over year, and adjusted operating EBITDA rose to $503M. Qnity raised full-year 2026 guidance for sales, EBITDA and EPS.

$0.87

GAAP EPS fell despite the sales jump

GAAP EPS was $0.87, down from $1.01 a year ago, as interest expense rose to $64M and transformation and spin-off costs weighed on profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Transformation, integration and other charges of $53M, including about $35M for IT independence from DuPont
  • Indirect legacy costs of $39M from DuPont spin-off cost-sharing arrangements, recorded in other expense

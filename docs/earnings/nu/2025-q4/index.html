Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$895M

A record profit, at a 33% ROE

Net income reached $894.8M, up 62% in dollars from $552.6M a year ago, for a record 33% return on equity. Full-year net income was $2.9B, up from $2.0B in 2024.

131M

17 million customers added in 2025

Nu added 4 million customers in the quarter to reach 131 million — 113 million in Brazil, where it is now the largest private financial institution by customer count, about 15% of Mexico's adult population, and over 4 million in Colombia. Monthly revenue per active customer reached $15.

4.1%

Credit quality improved at year-end

The 15–90 day delinquency ratio fell to 4.1% and the 90+ ratio to 6.6%, helped by Brazil's 13th-salary seasonality, while the credit portfolio grew 40% to $32.7B and deposits rose 29% to $41.9B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • This was the first quarter reported under Nu's new Managerial P&L framework. This page uses the IFRS accounting P&L from the KPMG-assured reconciliation bridge: managerial revenue was $4,857.3M, but net income is identical ($894.8M) on both bases.
  • Risk-adjusted NIM of 10.5% included a one-off extraordinary contribution to Prosofipo, the sector-wide deposit-protection fund all Mexican Sofipos must fund.
  • In January 2026 Nu received conditional OCC approval for a U.S. national bank charter — the first of three clearances for its planned U.S. expansion.

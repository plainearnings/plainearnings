Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.1B

First billion-dollar profit quarter

Net income reached $1,061M, up from $637M a year ago — the first time Nu has earned more than a billion dollars in a single quarter. Return on equity was 33%.

139M

Still adding millions of customers

Nu added about 4 million customers in the quarter to reach 138.9 million — almost 118 million in Brazil, 15.8 million in Mexico and over 5 million in Colombia. In August it launched as a full bank in Mexico.

$39.4B

Lending is growing faster than deposits

The credit portfolio grew 37% year over year to $39.4B, while deposits rose to $45.3B. Loan-loss costs remain the swing factor: expected credit losses were $1,482M in the quarter, the single biggest cost after interest paid.

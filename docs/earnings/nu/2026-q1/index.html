Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$871M

Profit jumped 56% in dollars

Net income reached $871M, up from $557M a year ago in dollar terms, with return on equity of 29%. On Nu's managerial view, revenue passed $5 billion for the first time ($5,315.5M).

135M

Still adding millions of customers

Nu added about 4 million customers to reach 135.2 million — over 115 million in Brazil, 15 million in Mexico and approaching 5 million in Colombia. Mexico reached break-even in the quarter.

$1.79B

Loan-loss costs spiked seasonally

Credit loss allowances closed at $1.79B, up 33% in the quarter on seasonality, portfolio growth and mix. Expected credit losses of $1,718M were the biggest single cost after funding, and the 15–90 day delinquency ratio rose to 5.0% on the usual first-quarter pattern.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

29.4M

Record trading, in every asset class

Average daily volume hit a Q3 record of 29.4 million contracts, with double-digit growth in all six product lines — interest rates (15.1M a day) and equity indexes (7.7M) are the giants. Uncertainty is good business for an exchange.

+20%

Data is the quiet growth line

Market data revenue hit a record $243M, up 20% — selling prices and data now brings in 14% of revenue and grows even when trading slows.

66%

A toll road's margins

Operating income was $1,141M — 66 cents of every revenue dollar — because one more trade costs CME almost nothing to process. Net income rose 14% to $1,037M.

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

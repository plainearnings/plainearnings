Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

An acquisition did the growing

Buying the rest of McCormick de Mexico (closed January 2026) contributed about 15 points of the 17% sales growth. Organic growth was 2%, driven by price.

39.3%

Margins expanded anyway

Gross margin widened 190 basis points to 39.3% on the Mexico contribution, cost savings from the CCI program, and pricing — absorbing higher commodity and freight costs.

$0.50

Deal costs dominate the GAAP line

Special charges of $141.5M — mostly for the planned Unilever Foods combination — took $0.50 off EPS. Adjusted EPS of $0.86 was up just 1%, as higher interest and tax offset 22% adjusted operating income growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $141.5M of special charges — transaction and integration costs for the planned combination with Unilever Foods plus a $43.1M non-cash impairment and $1.8M of exit costs on a development-stage pepper project in Malaysia — cut EPS by $0.50. Excluding special charges, adjusted operating income rose 22% to $359M and adjusted EPS was $0.86.

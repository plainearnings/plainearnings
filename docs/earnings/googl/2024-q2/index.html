Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

Cloud is compounding

Google Cloud revenue jumped 29% to $10.3B, and its operating profit nearly tripled to $1.2B from $0.4B a year ago — the cloud has become a real second engine, not just a growth story.

$48.5B

Search still pays for everything

Search & other revenue rose 14% to $48.5B and YouTube ads grew 13% to $8.7B — the advertising core still supplies the great bulk of Alphabet's money.

$23.6B

Profit rose 29%

Net income rose 29% to $23.6B despite the $714M paper loss on equity investments inside other income; free cash flow was $13.5B in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $714M loss on equity securities — paper losses on stakes Alphabet holds — is netted inside the small $126M of net other income; together with a $128M reversal of previously accrued performance fees, it cut net income by $463M (about $0.04 a share), per the release's own footnote.

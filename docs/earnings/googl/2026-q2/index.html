Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$99.0B

A paper gain tripled the bottom line

Net unrealized gains on equity securities added $99.0B before tax. Strip them out and net income would have been roughly $35B — still strong, but the $112.2B headline is an investment story, not an ads story.

+82%

Cloud is booming

Google Cloud revenue jumped 82% to $24.8B and its operating profit more than tripled to $8.8B, while Search grew 17% to $63.3B.

-$5.9B

Free cash flow went negative

Capital spending hit $44.9B in one quarter — more than the $39.1B of operating cash flow — as Alphabet builds AI data centers. It also raised $49.6B of new equity and $20.3B of debt in the quarter to fund the build-out.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income includes a $99.0B net gain on equity securities (mostly unrealized). Alphabet itself quantifies the effect: the gain raised the tax bill by $21.9B, net income by $77.1B and EPS by $6.26.
  • In June 2026 Alphabet raised $49.6B by issuing stock and mandatory convertible preferred shares, plus $20.3B of senior notes — war-chest funding for AI infrastructure, not an operating result.

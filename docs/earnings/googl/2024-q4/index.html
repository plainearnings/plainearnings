Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30%

Cloud is compounding

Google Cloud revenue jumped 30% to $12.0B, and its operating profit more than doubled to $2.1B — the cloud has become a real second engine, not just a growth story.

$54.0B

Search still pays for everything

Search & other revenue rose 13% to $54.0B and YouTube ads grew 14% to $10.5B — the advertising core still supplies the great bulk of Alphabet's money.

$26.5B

Profit kept 28 cents per dollar

Net income rose 28% to $26.5B — about 28 cents of every revenue dollar — even after spending $14.3B in the quarter on property and equipment, mostly data centers.

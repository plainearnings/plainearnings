Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+48%

Cloud is surging

Google Cloud revenue jumped 48% to $17.7B and its operating profit more than doubled to $5.3B. Cloud ended 2025 at an annual run rate above $70B, driven by enterprise AI demand.

$2.1B

A Waymo pay charge sat in the costs

Operating income includes a $2.1B employee compensation charge for Waymo, based on its estimated stock valuation and recorded mostly in R&D. Operating income still rose 16% to $35.9B.

$400B

A $400 billion year

Full-year 2025 revenue was $402.8B — the first time Alphabet has passed $400B. Paid subscriptions passed 325 million, and 2026 capex is planned at $175-185B to keep up with AI demand.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating income includes a $2.1B employee compensation charge for Waymo, based on estimated stock valuation, primarily reflected in research and development expenses.
  • Other income includes a $2.3B net gain on equity securities (stakes Alphabet holds in other companies), which added $1.8B to net income after tax.

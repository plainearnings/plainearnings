Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+63%

Cloud is booming

Google Cloud revenue jumped 63% to $20.0B and its operating profit more than tripled to $6.6B. The cloud order backlog nearly doubled in a single quarter to over $460B.

$37.7B

A paper gain bigger than most companies earn

Other income was a $37.7B net gain, mostly unrealized gains on stakes Alphabet owns in private companies. That is why net income rose 81% while the operating business grew its profit 30%.

350M

Subscriptions keep compounding

Paid subscriptions across Google reached 350 million, led by YouTube and Google One, and the Gemini app had its strongest quarter yet — a growing cushion of recurring revenue next to ads.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income includes a net gain of $37.7B, primarily unrealized gains on non-marketable equity securities (stakes in private companies). It is a paper gain — the main reason GAAP net income of $62,578M is so far above the $39,696M the operations earned before tax.

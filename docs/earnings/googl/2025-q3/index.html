Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Cloud is surging

Google Cloud revenue jumped 34% to $15.2B and its operating profit nearly doubled to $3.6B, driven by enterprise demand for AI infrastructure and AI solutions.

$3.5B

A European fine sat in the costs

Operating income includes a $3.5B European Commission fine. Excluding it, operating income rose 22% and the operating margin was 33.9%, helped by strong revenue growth and a leaner expense base.

$91-93B

Capex guidance raised again

Alphabet raised expected 2025 capital spending to $91-93B, up from $85B, as Cloud customers' demand strains capacity. Third-quarter capex was $24.0B and free cash flow was $24.5B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating income includes a $3.5B charge related to the European Commission (EC) fine, recorded within general and administrative expenses. Excluding the fine, operating income increased 22% and operating margin was 33.9%.
  • Other income includes a $12.8B net gain, primarily net unrealized gains on non-marketable equity securities (stakes Alphabet holds in private companies).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+32%

Cloud is compounding

Google Cloud revenue grew 32% to $13.6B and its operating profit more than doubled to $2.8B — the AI buildout is turning into real earnings, not just sales.

$1.4B

A legal charge sits in the costs

General & administrative costs include a $1.4B charge for a settlement in principle of legal matters. Operating income still rose 14% to $31.3B despite it.

29%

Nearly 30 cents of every dollar became profit

Net income rose 19% to $28.2B — a 29% margin — even while Alphabet spent $22.4B in the quarter on data centers and other capital projects.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1.4 billion charge in general & administrative costs for a settlement in principle of certain legal matters. The charge is not tax-deductible, which also pushed the quarter's tax bill up; without it, operating income would have been $32.7B instead of $31.3B.

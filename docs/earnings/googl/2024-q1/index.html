Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

Cloud is compounding

Google Cloud revenue jumped 28% to $9.6B, and its operating profit rose to $900M from $191M a year ago — the cloud is becoming a real second engine.

32%

Margins widened sharply

Operating margin expanded to 32% from 25% a year ago as revenue grew 15% and operating expenses actually fell 2% — severance charges dropped to $716M from $2.0B a year ago.

$0.20

Alphabet started paying a dividend

The board declared Alphabet's first-ever cash dividend, $0.20 a share, and authorized up to $70B more in buybacks; net income rose 57% to $23.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $2,243M gain on equity securities — paper gains on stakes Alphabet holds — is inside the $2,843M of net other income; together with a $104M reversal of previously accrued performance fees, it added $1.9B to net income (about $0.15 a share), per the release's own footnote.
  • Employee severance and related charges of $716M were spread across cost of revenues and operating expenses, down from $1,994M a year ago.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+26%

Cloud swung to a profit

Google Cloud revenue jumped 26% to $9.2B, and its operating result swung to an $864M profit from a $186M loss a year ago — the cloud is becoming a real second engine next to ads.

$48.0B

Search and subscriptions carried Services

Search & other revenue rose 13% to $48.0B, YouTube ads reached $9.2B, and subscriptions, platforms and devices grew 23% to $10.8B — Google Services earned $26.7B of operating profit in total.

27%

Margins widened despite the charges

Operating margin reached 27%, up from 24% a year ago, even with $1.2B of office-space exit charges inside the quarter's costs — net income rose 52% to $20.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Office-space exit charges of $1.2B (plus $62M of accelerated rent and depreciation) sit inside the quarter's costs, mostly in unallocated corporate costs, as Alphabet shrinks its real-estate footprint; employee severance added just $7M this quarter, after $2.1B for the full year.
  • A January 2023 change that stretched the assumed life of Alphabet's servers from four to six years cut depreciation expense by $983M in this quarter alone, which flatters the profit comparison.

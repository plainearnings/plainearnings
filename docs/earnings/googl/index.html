Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+103%

Cloud more than doubled

Google Cloud grew 103% to $30.7B, and its operating income tripled to $11.1B — a 36% margin. Cloud is now a quarter of Alphabet's revenue.

-$8.7B

The paper loss that sank the bottom line

An $8.7B mark-to-market loss on equity securities swung other income from +$12.8B to -$8.2B. Operating profit rose 30%, but net income fell 24%.

1B

Gemini hit a billion users

The Gemini app reached one billion users — called the fastest-growing product in Google's history — and YouTube Music and Premium passed 150 million subscribers.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income swung to a net expense of $8,221M from $12,759M of income a year ago, driven by an $8,664M loss on equity securities (vs a $10,734M gain a year ago) — this $19.4B swing is why net income fell 24% despite operating income rising 30%. Note: the income statement's net income of $26,468M is before $298M of preferred stock dividends; net income available to common stockholders was $26,170M, on which the $2.12 EPS is based.

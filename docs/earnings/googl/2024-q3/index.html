Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+35%

Cloud is compounding

Google Cloud revenue jumped 35% to $11.4B, and its operating profit leapt to $1.9B from just $0.3B a year ago — the cloud has become a real second engine, not just a growth story.

$49.4B

Search still pays for everything

Search & other revenue rose 12% to $49.4B and YouTube ads grew 12% to $8.9B — the advertising core still supplies the great bulk of Alphabet's money.

$26.3B

Profit rose 34%

Net income rose 34% to $26.3B, helped by a $1.8B paper gain on equity investments inside other income; free cash flow was $17.6B in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1.8B gain on equity investments — paper gains on stakes Alphabet holds — sits inside the $3.2B of other income and flatters net income; it is not revenue from customers.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

Cloud is compounding

Google Cloud revenue grew 28% to $12.3B and its operating profit rose to $2.2B from $0.9B a year ago — the AI buildout is turning into real earnings, not just sales.

$8.0B

A paper gain did heavy lifting

Other income included an $8.0B unrealized gain on a private-company stake; with related items it added about $7.7B to net income after tax — most of the 46% jump — without anything being sold.

38%

Over a third of revenue became profit

Net income hit $34.5B, a 38% margin — but strip out the investment gain and the underlying margin was closer to 30%, still strong.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $8.0 billion unrealized gain on non-marketable equity securities — Alphabet's stake in a private company — sits in other income. Together with related items it added $7.7B to net income (and $0.62 to EPS) after tax; it is a paper gain, not cash earned by the business.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$616M

Magic passes $600M in a quarter

Magic: The Gathering revenue grew 34% to $616 million — its first quarter above $600 million — driving Wizards & Digital Gaming up 31%.

32.4%

Operating margin leapt

GAAP operating margin rose to 32.4% from 24.6% a year ago on favorable mix, cost savings and about $55 million of tariff refunds.

7-9%

Outlook raised again

Hasbro now expects 2026 revenue up 7-9% in constant currency and adjusted EBITDA of $1.575-1.625 billion, and doubled its buyback target to $400 million.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Profit includes about $55 million of refunds of tariffs paid earlier — $48 million in Consumer Products and $7 million in Wizards — which will not repeat every quarter.
  • Adjusted operating profit of $541 million excludes items such as acquired-intangible amortization and transformation costs.

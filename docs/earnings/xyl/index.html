Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.33B

Orders outran sales

Orders rose 7% to $2.33B (up 10% organically) even as revenue fell — customers are delaying project spending decisions, not cancelling the work.

23.9%

Margins expanded

Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 23.9%, up 70 basis points, as productivity savings and pricing beat inflation and lower volumes; operating margin rose to 17.5% from 14.7%.

$8.95B

Guidance cut, portfolio slimmed

Full-year revenue guidance fell to about $8.95B and adjusted EPS to $5.40–$5.45 (from $5.55–$5.70), and Xylem will sell Applied Water International and Specialty Flow Control and move from four segments to three in 2027.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and asset impairment charges were $8M in the quarter, down from $23M a year ago.
  • A $5M loss on the sale of businesses (versus $37M a year ago) sits below operating income; adjusted EPS of $1.44 excludes that loss, restructuring, purchase-accounting amortization and special charges, which included IEEPA tariff refunds.

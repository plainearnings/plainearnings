Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

91.3%

Combined ratio up on storms, not slippage

The combined ratio worsened from 86.8% as catastrophe losses nearly tripled to $283M and reserve releases shrank — but the accident-year ratio ex-CAT held at 88.4%, so the core book is steady.

$844M

Investments carried GAAP profit

Net income rose 63% to $844M ($1.60/share) even as underwriting income fell 29% — fair-value investment gains and 9% higher GI investment income more than filled the gap.

$787M

Capital returns continue

AIG returned $787M in the quarter ($527M buybacks, $260M dividends), declared a $0.50 dividend, and grew adjusted book value per share 5% to $81.25.

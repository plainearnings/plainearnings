Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7%

Every business grew

Net sales rose 7% to $5,201M, with all five businesses up: Control Systems & Software and Test & Measurement each grew 14%, Final Control 5%, Sensors 4% and Safety & Productivity 2%. Management said underlying orders rose 14% on exceptional long-cycle project activity. Full-year sales were $18,982M, up 5%.

19.2%

Profit grew much faster than sales

Pre-tax earnings rose to $997M from $760M, and pre-tax margin widened to 19.2% from 15.6%. Adjusted segment EBITA — segment profit before interest, tax and amortization — was $1,511M at a 29.1% margin. Net earnings were $744M, or $1.33 a share, vs $609M and $1.07 a year ago (full-year EPS $4.79).

$1.86

Adjusted EPS, and a higher dividend

Adjusted EPS was $1.86 vs $1.33 GAAP; the gap is mainly $203M of intangible amortization and $46M of restructuring in 'other deductions'. Emerson raised its quarterly dividend 7% to $0.595 a share and guided to fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS of about $7.25, with about $2.5B of planned buybacks.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other deductions, net of $274M include $203M of amortization of intangible assets and $46M of restructuring costs — the main items Emerson excludes to get from GAAP EPS of $1.33 to adjusted EPS of $1.86.

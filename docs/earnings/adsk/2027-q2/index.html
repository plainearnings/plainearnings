Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.33

GAAP profit caught up

GAAP EPS leapt from $1.46 to $2.33 — a year ago Autodesk was absorbing sales-model transition costs. Non-GAAP EPS was $3.30, and GAAP operating margin hit 29%.

+26%

Make is the growth engine

The 'Make' portfolio (construction + manufacturing execution) is small but growing 26% — the strategic second engine behind the Design core.

$561M

Cash flow compounding

Free cash flow of $561M in the quarter, up 24%. FY27 FCF guidance was narrowed: stronger underlying cash generation, partly spent on MaintainX deal and financing costs.

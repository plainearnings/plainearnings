Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

86.8%

Underwriting stayed strong

The combined ratio was 86.8% (84.1% excluding catastrophes and reserve development), and pre-tax underwriting income was $1,432M — profitable insurance, not just investment gains.

$1.1B

Investments set a record

Net investment income hit a record $1,098M before tax, up 6%, as higher yields and a larger portfolio lifted what Travelers earns on premiums held before claims are paid.

$538M

Storms cost more than last year

Catastrophe losses were $538M before tax, up from $402M a year ago, mostly severe wind and hail. Favorable reserve development of $234M — past claims costing less than reserved — more than offset the increase.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net favorable prior-year reserve development was $234M before tax — reserves for past claims proved more than enough — even after a $295M addition to asbestos reserves.
  • Net realized investment losses were $34M before tax ($30M after tax); excluding them, core income was $1,947M, or $9.18 per diluted share.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.1%

The shopping centers themselves grew

Same Property NOI rose 5.1% year over year, the portfolio ended the quarter 97.0% leased, and leases were renewed at blended cash rent spreads of +11.9%.

$65M

An impairment cut GAAP profit

Net income attributable to common shareholders fell to $75M, or $0.41 per share, from $106M a year ago, mainly because of a $65.1M provision for impairment of real estate, partly offset by a $23.8M gain on sales.

$1.23

FFO rose and the dividend went up

Nareit FFO was $231M, or $1.23 per diluted share, up from $214M a year ago. Regency raised full-year FFO guidance and lifted its quarterly dividend 6% to $0.80 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Provision for impairment of real estate of $65M ($0.35 per diluted share)
  • Gain on sale of real estate, net of tax, of $24M ($0.13 per diluted share)

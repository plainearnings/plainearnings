Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+32%

International is the growth engine

International Omnipod revenue rose 32% to $266M — nearly a third of all sales — as Omnipod 5 gained sensor integrations in more countries. U.S. revenue grew a solid 15% to $570M.

22.5%

Margins leapt

Operating income reached 22.5% of revenue, up from 16.7% a year ago. Gross margin hit 75% and running costs grew much slower than sales, so GAAP operating income rose 61% to $190M.

$1B

A big buyback

The board approved a $1 billion share repurchase program, with about $400M planned for the fourth quarter of 2026, funded from the company's $584M of cash.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include a favorable $4.5M adjustment to warranty estimates tied to the voluntary medical device corrections issued in March and May 2026. Excluding that and a $0.3M debt-refinancing loss, adjusted operating income was $185M and adjusted EPS was $1.99 (both non-GAAP).

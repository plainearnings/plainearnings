Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$20.8B

Client money is flowing in again

Long-term net inflows were $20.8B in the quarter and $84.1B for the fiscal year, with inflows in every quarter and across equity, multi-asset and alternatives. Assets under management reached $1,795B, up 8% YoY.

+317%

Operating income jumped — mostly an easy comparison

Operating income rose to $356M from $85M, but a year ago the quarter carried a $202M intangible-asset impairment. On Franklin's adjusted basis, operating income rose a more modest 27% to $599M.

$305B

Alternatives are the growth corner

Alternative assets — private markets and other non-traditional strategies — reached a record $305B, with $48.7B raised during the fiscal year, as Franklin pushes beyond traditional stock and bond funds.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • No impairment charge this quarter. A year ago Franklin booked a $202M impairment of intangible assets, which is the main reason GAAP operating income rose 317% YoY.
  • Adjusted (non-GAAP) operating income was $599M and adjusted EPS was $0.94; the adjusted figures exclude amortization and impairment of intangibles, the consolidated investment products and certain other items. GAAP EPS was $0.72.
  • Of the $554M of consolidated net income, $174M belonged to noncontrolling interests — outside investors in consolidated funds. Net income attributable to Franklin Templeton shareholders was $380M.

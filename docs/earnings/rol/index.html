Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.2%

Sales still growing

Revenue hit $1.09B, the 100th quarter in a row of growth. Commercial work grew fastest at +7.7%, while residential grew +4.9%.

-3.4%

Insurance costs squeezed profit

Net income fell to $158M. Insurance and claims costs roughly doubled to about $37M across service and office costs, and management called residential customer sign-ups disappointing.

$235M

Cash flow was strong

Free cash flow (cash left after running costs and small investments) rose 29% to $235M, and Rollins spent $120M buying back its own shares.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquisition-related charges of $8M (intangible amortization and revaluing earn-out promises from the Fox, Saela and Romex deals) are excluded from the company's adjusted profit.
  • Operating cash flow included a $63M tax refund tied to a tax-credit strategy, which flattered the free-cash-flow growth rate.

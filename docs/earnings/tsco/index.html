Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.1%

Growth came from new stores

Net sales rose to $3,910M, driven by 23 new Tractor Supply stores opened in the quarter, the VIP Petcare acquisition, and comparable sales up just 0.4% — new stores did most of the work.

36.4%

Margins were squeezed

Gross margin fell a full point to 36.4% on higher transportation and fuel costs and deliberate price investments, while SG&A rose to 28.6% of sales, including startup costs for a new distribution center.

$225M

Profit fell despite higher sales

Operating income fell 10% to $308M and net income fell 13% to $225M. The company still returned $213M to shareholders — $89M of buybacks and $125M of dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Tractor Supply closed 74 Petsense by Tractor Supply stores in the quarter, as previously announced; the related impairment charges ($63M over the first nine months) were recorded earlier in the year, not in Q3.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

The Southeast is booming

Revenue rose 13% to $8,843M. Retail electricity sales rose 7.7%, with commercial sales up 10.1% on large customers — management calls Southeast growth 'unprecedented and transformational'.

$1.94

Adjusted profit beat the headline

Excluding flagged items, EPS was $1.94 versus GAAP diluted EPS of $1.76. The gap is mainly $118M of accelerated wind-repowering depreciation and debt-extinguishment costs.

$842M

Interest is the big drag below the line

Interest expense of $842M — funding a huge build-out of generation and grid — was partly offset by $140M of allowance for funds used during construction and $77M of equity earnings.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $118M pre-tax accelerated depreciation and decommissioning for repowering Southern Power wind facilities ($92M after tax).
  • $43M of debt-extinguishment costs, plus a $57M change in related tax benefits, and a $9M estimated loss on disallowed Nicor Gas capital investments.

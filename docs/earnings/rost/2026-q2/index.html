Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Shoppers drove the growth

Comparable store sales rose 10%, mainly because more customers visited and bought, with both new and existing customers spending more.

$851M

Profit up 67% — with a one-off tailwind

Net income rose from $508M to $851M. About $253M of operating profit came from IEEPA tariff refunds; even without them, operating margin rose 205 basis points (2.05 percentage points).

$8.61–$8.77

Outlook raised

Ross raised full-year EPS guidance to $8.61–$8.77 and lifted its 2026 store-opening plan to 115 new stores after opening 47 in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • IEEPA tariff refunds of about $253M were recognized in the quarter, adding roughly $0.60 to EPS and about 405 basis points (4.05 percentage points) to operating margin.

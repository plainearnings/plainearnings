Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+44%

Truck profits snapped back

Net income rose 44% to $848M, driven by truck segment margins: Truck pretax profit jumped to $455M from $103M a year ago on 28% higher truck sales.

$1.78B

Parts set another record

Parts revenue hit a record $1.78B with $404M of pretax profit — a steady, high-margin business that cushions PACCAR when truck sales swing.

40,100

Fleets are buying again

Global deliveries rose to 40,100 trucks from 31,900, and PACCAR expects healthy U.S. Class 8 demand to continue into 2027.

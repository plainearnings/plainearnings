Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+54%

Record quarter on AI demand

Revenue hit $8,219M, up from $5,324M a year ago and $6,722M last quarter. Management credited AI driving higher semiconductor demand and greater device complexity.

52.3%

Margins expanded too

GAAP gross margin rose to 52.3% from 50.4% a year ago and the operating margin reached 40.0%, so net income grew even faster than revenue, to $2,753M.

$8.6B

Guidance points higher

For the December 2026 quarter Lam guided to revenue of $8.60B plus or minus $400M, with a GAAP gross margin of about 52.5% and operating margin of about 40.5%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP results were only slightly above GAAP: non-GAAP gross profit was $4,305M versus $4,299M GAAP and non-GAAP net income was $2.77B ($2.20 per share) versus $2,753M ($2.19) GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$818M

Record sales in every segment

Sales hit an all-time quarterly record, up 10% (about 12% organically), with record quarters in all three segments. Advanced Technology Solutions led, up 28% on electronics demand.

55.5%

Best-in-class margins held

Gross margin widened to 55.5% and EBITDA reached a record $262M — 32 cents of every sales dollar before interest, tax and depreciation. That margin discipline is Nordson's signature.

$3.0B+

Guidance raised

Management raised full-year guidance to $3,035–3,075M of sales and $11.80–12.00 of adjusted EPS, pointing to backlog and order momentum — putting Nordson on track for its first $3B revenue year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $15M non-cash loss on minority investments (marked to fair value) sits inside 'other expense' this quarter. Nordson excludes it — and acquisition-related amortization and costs — from adjusted results: adjusted EPS was $3.25 versus $2.73 GAAP.

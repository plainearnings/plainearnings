Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30.3%

Specialty is the growth engine

Specialty rentals — trench, power, climate and fluids — grew 30.3% to $1.83B and now bring in 37% of revenue, at a richer 47.6% rentals gross margin.

$920M

Record profit

Net income was a record $920M, an 18.8% margin, and adjusted EBITDA reached $2.33B — 47.6 cents of every revenue dollar before interest, tax and depreciation.

$14.77

EPS up 35%

GAAP EPS rose to $14.77 from $10.91, helped by growth and by buybacks — the company returned $1.5B to shareholders in the first nine months.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter includes a $15M restructuring charge in operating expenses. Adjusted EPS was $15.55 vs $14.77 GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11%

Growth steadied, backlog accelerated

Revenue rose 11% to $805M. Subscription backlog due within a year (cRPO) grew 14% — faster than revenue — pointing to steadier growth ahead.

13%

A real GAAP operating margin now

GAAP operating income was $107M, a 13% margin, up from 6% a year ago. Stock-based pay is still large ($114M in the quarter) but shrinking as a share of revenue.

$227M

Cash flow is the standout

Free cash flow was $227M — 28 cents of every revenue dollar — and Okta ended the quarter with $2.3B of cash and short-term investments.

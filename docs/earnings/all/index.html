Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$9.76

Core underwriting is excellent

Adjusted EPS of $9.76 vs GAAP $8.03 — the gap is mostly the $545M of investment/derivative losses that adjusted earnings exclude. Underwriting itself was strong: combined ratio 86.6 even with heavy catastrophes.

217.6M

Growth, not just price

Total policies in force hit 217.6 million — Property-Liability policies up 3.3%, with auto and home both gaining share on new 'Affordable, Simple and Connected' products.

-45%

Headline profit down on one-offs

Net income to common fell 45% to $2.05B — but the comparison is against a quarter with a $720M disposal gain and investment gains. Premiums, the controllable part, rose 4%.

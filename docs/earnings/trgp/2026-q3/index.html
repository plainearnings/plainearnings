Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+35%

Commodity sales drove growth

Revenue rose 35% to $5.6B. Higher NGL, gas and condensate prices added about $802M and higher volumes about $383M versus a year ago, and fee revenue rose 29% to $875.7M.

$1.5B

Adjusted EBITDA up 18%

Adjusted EBITDA rose 18% to $1,504M on record Permian inlet volumes and record NGL transportation and fractionation volumes. Targa now expects at least $6.0B of adjusted EBITDA for 2026.

$6.00

Dividend set to rise again

The quarterly dividend is $1.25 a share, up 25% year over year, and management expects to recommend $6.00 a share annualized for 2027 — another 20% rise — while spending about $5.0B on growth projects in 2026.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Revenue includes $18.1M of net derivative gains that are not sales to customers — $54.7M of gains on derivatives not designated as hedges, partly offset by $36.6M of hedge losses — which can swing either way with gas prices.

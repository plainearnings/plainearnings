Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.42

Clean quarter, no adjustments

EPS of $2.42 matched the adjusted figure and rose versus adjusted $2.17 a year ago (GAAP $2.35 included a one-off tax benefit). Infrastructure investment and warm-weather sales did the work.

8–10%

Growth guidance stands out

Ameren raised 2026 guidance to $5.35–$5.55, set 2027 at $5.70–$5.90, and guided to 8–10% annual EPS growth for 2027–2031 — top-tier for a regulated utility.

$216M

Debt funds the build

Interest charges of $216M ate about a quarter of operating income — the flip side of funding a multi-year grid build with debt.

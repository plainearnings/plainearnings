Berkshire Hathaway — Q4 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q4 2024 · Quarter ended 2024-12-31
Reported 2025-02-22

Berkshire's operating earnings jumped 71% to $14.5B as insurance underwriting quadrupled to $3.4B — and $6.6B of investment gains lifted headline profit to $19.7B, though that was still only half of a year ago's $37.6B, when the stock portfolio soared.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.4B

Underwriting quadrupled

After-tax underwriting earnings rose to $3.4B from $0.8B a year ago, led by GEICO: higher average premiums per policy, lower claims frequencies and improved efficiency took GEICO's full-year pre-tax underwriting profit to $7.8B. Hurricane losses from Helene and Milton were about $360M at GEICO.

$14.5B

Operating earnings jumped 71%

The businesses themselves earned $14.5B after tax, up from $8.5B — insurance investment income rose to $4.1B after tax on the bigger cash pile, and 'Other' earnings got a roughly $1.2B boost from currency gains on foreign-denominated debt.

$171B

The float kept growing

Insurance float — the premiums Berkshire holds and invests before claims come due — reached about $171B at year-end, up $2B in the year. Berkshire also sold down investments during 2024, realizing about $79.6B of after-tax gains, much of it from trimming Apple.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Investment gains of $6,552M before tax are mostly paper gains. Of the after-tax investment gains of $5,167M, about $2.1B was an increase in unrealized gains on stocks Berkshire still owns and $3.1B was realized gains on stocks sold. Next quarter the unrealized part can just as easily reverse — a year ago this line was $29.1B after tax, which is why headline profit then was $37.6B.
  • Currency gains on foreign debt: revaluing Berkshire's euro-, pound- and yen-denominated borrowings produced a gain of about $1.2B after tax in the quarter, versus a loss of about $684M a year ago. It sits inside the 'Other' operating earnings line and flatters the 71% jump.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8.5B

Operating earnings rose 28%

The businesses themselves earned $8,481M after tax, up 28% from $6,625M a year ago — insurance underwriting swung to an $848M profit on GEICO's recovery, and insurance investment income rose to $2,759M as Treasury Bill yields lifted earnings on the float.

$37.6B

Headline profit was mostly the stock market

Headline profit attributable to Berkshire was $37,574M, versus $18,080M a year ago — but $29,093M of it was after-tax investment and derivative gains, mostly unrealized paper gains on the stock portfolio that can reverse in any quarter.

$167.6B

The cash pile set a record

Cash, equivalents and Treasury Bills reached $167.6B at December 31, 2023, and insurance float was about $169B. Berkshire repurchased about $2.2B of its own shares in the quarter, $9.2B for the year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Investment and derivative gains of $36,814M before tax ($29,093M after tax) were mostly paper: after-tax unrealized gains were about $29.5B in the quarter as stock prices rose, partly offset by about $330M of after-tax realized losses on sales. The unrealized part can just as easily reverse next quarter.
  • Operating earnings included an after-tax foreign-currency loss of about $684M from revaluing Berkshire's non-dollar-denominated debt, inside the 'Other' segment line.
  • Insurance underwriting earned $848M after tax, up from $160M a year ago, led by GEICO's recovery; for the full year, underwriting earned $5,428M after a $30M loss in 2022.

Berkshire does not report a standalone fourth quarter, so the figures here are the year ended December 31, 2023 minus the nine months ended September 30, 2023, both on the original 2023 presentation — Pilot Travel Centers inside Railroad, Utilities and Energy. Later filings reclassify Pilot into Insurance and Other (the basis the 2024 pages here use), which moves revenue and costs between the sales-and-service and utilities lines but changes no totals, so those two lines are not directly comparable with the 2024 pages and no growth rates are shown for the revenue lines.

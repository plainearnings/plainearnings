Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

China carried the quarter

China Mainland revenue rose 24% to $528M with comparable sales up 30%, and Rest of World rose 10% to $432M. The Americas fell 4% to $2,681M — international growth is the only reason total revenue grew at all.

54.9%

Margin fell 550 points

Gross margin dropped to 54.9% from 60.4%: product margin fell 560 points on higher tariffs and heavier markdowns, partly offset by efficiency gains. Operating margin fell to 22.3% from 28.9%.

$12.10–12.30

Next year's EPS was guided down

For fiscal 2026 management guided revenue of $11.350–11.500B (up 2–4%) but EPS of $12.10–$12.30, down from $13.26 in fiscal 2025, with Americas revenue expected to fall 1–3%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Fiscal 2024 had a 53rd week and fiscal 2025 had 52, which distorts every growth rate in this quarter: excluding the extra week, total revenue rose 6% (4% in constant currency) instead of 1%.
  • Lululemon repurchased 1.4 million of its shares for $269.1M in the quarter and opened 15 net new company-operated stores, ending the year with 811 stores and $1.8B of cash; inventories rose 18% to $1.7B.

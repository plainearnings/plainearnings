Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-5%

The home market kept shrinking

Americas comparable sales fell 5% and regional revenue fell 3% to $1,621M. China Mainland grew 30% to $478M and Rest of World 13% to $372M, which is the only reason total revenue grew at all.

54.2%

Margin fell 410 points

Gross margin dropped to 54.2% from 58.3% on higher tariffs and heavier markdowns, while SG&A rose to 42.9% of revenue. Operating margin nearly halved to 11.2% from 18.5%.

$11.0B

The full-year outlook was cut

Management cut fiscal 2026 guidance to revenue of $11.000–11.150B (down 1% to flat) and EPS of $10.95–$11.15, down from $11.35–11.50B and $12.10–$12.30 guided in March.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Lululemon repurchased 2.2 million of its shares for $358.3M in the quarter and opened five net new company-operated stores, ending the quarter with 816 stores and $1.5B of cash.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Broad, subscription-led growth

Revenue rose 10% to $1,833M, all of it organic (not from deals or currency). Recurring revenue — 88% of the total — grew 9%, and transaction revenue grew 27%. Legal Professionals, Corporates and Tax & Accounting, the 'Big 3', each grew about 10% and together are 88% of revenue.

-22%

Profit fell on a tough comparison

Operating profit dropped 22% to $402M, mainly because last year's quarter included a $160M gain on selling the remaining minority stake in Elite, and because software amortization rose. Adjusted EBITDA, which strips those out, rose 10% to $651M.

$339M

Net earnings included a business being sold

Net earnings were $339M, of which $50M came from discontinued operations — Global Print, whose 51% stake was sold to KKR funds for about $500M in gross proceeds on October 1, just after quarter end. Continuing operations earned $289M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A year ago, other operating gains of $160M — mostly the gain on selling the remaining minority interest in the Elite business — flattered operating profit; this quarter that line was a $1M loss.
  • Earnings include $50M from discontinued operations (Global Print). The company also issued about $2.0B of new notes in September and finished a $600M share buyback.

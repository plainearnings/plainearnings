Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

International did the growing

Every international segment grew — Latin America Foods +14%, Asia Pacific +10%, EMEA and the franchise drinks business +8% each. International brought in $11.1B of the $25.3B total.

0%

North America foods is stuck

PepsiCo Foods North America was flat at $6.5B and its operating profit fell 13%. Management said it is acting 'with urgency' to improve North America with more innovation and sharper execution.

$2.34

Core profit grew much slower than the headline

GAAP EPS rose 17% to $2.23, but core EPS — PepsiCo's cleaned-up measure — rose only 2% to $2.34, and core operating profit rose 3% while its margin shrank. The gap is one-time items, listed below.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP operating profit was lifted by one-time items: a $96M net credit from acquisition and divestiture items and mark-to-market gains on commodity derivatives, partly offset by $192M of restructuring and impairment charges. Stripping them out, core operating profit was $4,277M, up 3%.
  • Last year's quarter carried a $133M impairment of the Rockstar brand ($2.0B year-to-date), which flatters this year's 19% operating-profit growth comparison.

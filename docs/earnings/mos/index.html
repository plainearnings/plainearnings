Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-$105

Phosphate lost money per tonne

Phosphate gross margin swung from +$92 to -$105 per tonne in a year as sulfur costs spiked and prices lagged. The segment lost $194M from operations.

$189M

Potash paid the bills

Potash earned $189M from operations on $643M of sales — a reminder that Mosaic's two nutrient businesses can move in opposite directions.

2.7%

Almost no cushion at the top line

Consolidated gross margin was just 2.7% (from 16.0%), so ordinary running costs were enough to push the quarter into a loss despite a $38M currency gain and a $38M tax benefit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Phosphate economics were the story: the segment's gross margin was negative $105 per tonne in the quarter (vs +$92 a year ago) as sulfur — a key input Mosaic must buy — stayed scarce and expensive, and Mosaic curtailed production to match.

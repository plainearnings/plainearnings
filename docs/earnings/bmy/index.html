Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Newer medicines are carrying growth

The Growth Portfolio rose 12% to $7.7B — Breyanzi +46%, Camzyos +42%, Reblozyl +22% and the new under-the-skin Opdivo Qvantig — and is now about 59% of all revenue.

$4.7B

Eliquis is doing the heavy lifting

Eliquis alone brought in $4.7B, up 25% — more than the rest of the Legacy Portfolio combined — while Revlimid and Pomalyst shrank by $755M combined under generic competition.

$1.6B

Buying future drugs cost $1.6B this quarter

Acquired research charges of $1,558M cut into profit now in exchange for pipeline rights later. The company still raised full-year revenue guidance to $49.75–$50.25B, while trimming non-GAAP EPS guidance to $6.35–$6.55 because of these charges.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired IPRD charges of $1,558M — upfront payments for licensed and collaboration drug research. GAAP and non-GAAP EPS both include a net -$0.58 per share from these charges and licensing income.
  • A $187M provision for restructuring, inside other expense.
  • Gross margin also included a one-time charge related to a contract termination (the company did not quantify it separately), and fell from 71.9% to 68.3% mainly on product mix.

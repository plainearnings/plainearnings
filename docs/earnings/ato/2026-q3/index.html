Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30%

Net income rose to $243M

Rate increases already approved by regulators ($355M annualized) flowed through, lifting EPS 23% to $1.43 in the seasonally smallest quarter.

+22%

Pipeline & storage is the growth engine

Segment revenue rose 22% to $333M (before internal eliminations) on new Texas infrastructure entering service.

$8.40–$8.50

Full-year guidance reaffirmed

Atmos kept its fiscal 2026 EPS guidance and expects roughly $4.2B of capital spending, recovered through future rates.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Commercial is carrying growth

Building Climate Solutions revenue rose 24% to $635M — 15% organic growth plus acquisitions — with profit up 19% to $167M. Home Comfort grew only 3%.

18.7%

Home margins squeezed hard

Home Comfort's margin fell 490 basis points to 18.7% and profit dropped 18% to $176M, hit by $32M of product-cost inflation and tariffs, lower factory absorption and price actions to defend share.

$21.25

Full-year guidance cut

Lennox cut full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $21.00–$21.50 (midpoint $21.25) from $23.00–$24.00, while raising free cash flow guidance to $700–750M. Q3 operating cash flow rose to $395M as inventories fell.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted net income was $236M ($6.84 per share) vs GAAP $221M ($6.41) — the gap is restructuring charges, special litigation charges and acquisition costs, partly offset by a $4M gain on selling previous dispositions.

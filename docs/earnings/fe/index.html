Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9.9%

Demand did the lifting

Revenue rose to $4,557M from $4,148M a year ago as electricity sales grew — FirstEnergy has 7.9 GW of contracted large-load demand in its pipeline.

$0.98

Core earnings topped GAAP

Core (non-GAAP) EPS was $0.98 vs GAAP $0.96 — the 2-cent gap is special items like investigation costs and pension credits. Full-year Core guidance of $2.62–$2.82 was reaffirmed.

$4.9B

Spending heavily on the grid

FirstEnergy deployed $4.9B in the first nine months under its Energize365 capital plan, the investment program behind its 6–8% Core EPS growth target through 2030.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Special items added a net $0.02 per share of costs in GAAP results — mainly $18M (pre-tax) of investigation and related costs and reorganization costs, partly offset by net pension/OPEB credits.
  • Net income in the chart is the consolidated $615M; after the $58M earned by noncontrolling interests, $557M was attributable to FirstEnergy shareholders — the basis for the $0.96 GAAP EPS.

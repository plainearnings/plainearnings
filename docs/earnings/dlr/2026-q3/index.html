Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Revenue jumped on new capacity

Revenue rose 21% to $1,902M. Rental and other services grew 19% to $1,827M as new data centers leased up, and renewal leases signed in the quarter carried rent increases of 11.8% on a cash basis.

$627M

Record bookings and backlog

The company signed record bookings expected to bring in $627M of annual rent at its share ($855M at 100% share), including a record $152M of smaller 0–1 megawatt and interconnection deals. The signed-but-not-yet-billing backlog hit a record $1.8B at its share.

$2.10

Cash earnings rose, outlook raised

Core FFO — the REIT measure that adds back property depreciation — was $2.10 per share, up from $1.89 a year ago. Management raised its full-year 2026 Core FFO outlook to $8.30–$8.35 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter includes an $8M gain on selling properties, counted in other income below — a one-off, not rent.

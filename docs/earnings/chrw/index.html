Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29.5%

Pricing drove the top line

Revenue rose to $5,358M as truckload, ocean, LTL and air pricing all rose and truckload volume grew. North American Surface Transportation — 71% of revenue — grew 29%, and Global Forwarding grew 46%.

$808M

Each load earned more

Adjusted gross profit — what Robinson keeps after paying carriers — rose 14.5% to $808M. Truckload adjusted gross profit per shipment rose 17% as disciplined repricing outran a 33.5% rise in linehaul cost per mile.

-10.4%

More freight, fewer people

Average headcount fell 10.4% while volume grew, as manual processes were turned into automated, AI-assisted workflows. Operating income rose 23% to $272M and net income rose 22% to $198M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Results include $19M of specified items the company strips out of its adjusted figures — severance and other personnel costs, plus other SG&A items including an impairment charge on the Kansas City regional center lease after subleasing the remaining space. Adjusted operating income was $291M vs $272M GAAP, and adjusted EPS was $1.80 vs $1.67.

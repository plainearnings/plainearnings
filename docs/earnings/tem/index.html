Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Both engines grew

Revenue rose 23% to $411.5M. Diagnostics brought in $303.5M (up 20%) as oncology test volumes grew 32% and minimal-residual-disease tests — checking whether cancer remains after treatment — roughly quadrupled to about 12,000. Data and applications grew faster, up 33% to $108.1M. Management raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $1.60–1.605B.

65%

The core business still loses money

Gross margin was a healthy 65%, but running costs of $323.6M — $231.9M of it selling, general and administrative — left a $56.9M operating loss, only slightly better than a year ago. On the company's non-GAAP basis (leaving out stock pay and similar items) Tempus made a $13.4M profit, so much of the GAAP loss is non-cash pay and deal accounting.

-$15M

Investment income shrank the loss

The net loss narrowed to $14.9M from $80.0M a year ago — mostly because other income jumped to $47.1M, from $2.6M, on gains in the value of marketable equity securities and licence income. Those gains sit outside the testing and data business and can reverse, so the smaller loss is not yet an operating turnaround.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income of $47.1M (vs $2.6M a year ago) consists of gains and losses on marketable equity securities, foreign-currency moves and SB Tempus licence income, per the 10-Q — market-driven items, not customer revenue.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

Fees grew in all three businesses

Trust, investment and servicing fees rose 8% to $1,373.0M, with organic fee growth in Asset Servicing, Wealth Management and Asset Management alike, helped by assets under custody/administration rising 9% to $19.9 trillion.

+40%

Costs barely grew, so profit leapt

Revenue rose 13% while expenses rose only 5% — strong operating leverage. Excluding notable items, management said revenue grew 11% and pre-tax income 21%. Net income rose 40% to $642.9M and return on average common equity was 20.1%.

19.3%

A low tax rate helped too

The effective tax rate fell to 19.3% from 26.1% a year ago, mainly on a lower tax impact from international operations — part of why EPS grew 48%, faster than pre-tax income's 29%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other noninterest income included $48.7M of pre-tax notable gains: a $34.0M gain on selling the guardianship services business and a $14.7M incremental gain from Northern Trust's participation in the second Visa Exchange Offer (combined after-tax value $36.8M).

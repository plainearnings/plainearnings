Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+77%

AI testing is the whole story

Semiconductor Test revenue rose 77% to $1,072M — 82 cents of every revenue dollar — on record memory and integrated-systems test sales tied to AI chips.

$342M

Profit nearly tripled

Net income attributable to Teradyne was $342M ($2.17 per share) vs $120M ($0.75) a year ago, as the extra sales dropped through at a 31% operating margin.

$96M

Robotics turned the corner

Robotics posted its highest third-quarter revenue yet, $96M, up 28%, and reached profitability after last year's restructuring cut about 150 jobs.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP EPS was $2.29 vs GAAP $2.17 — the gap is $5M of acquired-intangible amortization, $2M of restructuring and other charges, and their tax effects.
  • Cost of revenue included a $10.7M net charge for excess and obsolete inventory — a $10.9M provision, partly offset by selling stock that had previously been written down.

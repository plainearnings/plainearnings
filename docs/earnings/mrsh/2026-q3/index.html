Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

Both halves grew

Risk & Insurance Services revenue rose 7% to $4.2B and Consulting 9% to $2.7B — no single deal or region carried the quarter; underlying growth was 5%.

$2.03

Adjusted EPS outran GAAP again

Adjusted EPS rose 10% to $2.03, ahead of GAAP's $1.69. The gap is mostly amortization of intangibles from Marsh's many acquisitions, which management excludes.

+14%

Oliver Wyman stood out

Management consulting revenue jumped 14%, the fastest-growing piece of the portfolio, while the big broking business compounded steadily.

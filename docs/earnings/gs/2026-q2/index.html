Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+39%

The boom was in banking & markets

Global Banking & Markets revenue rose 53% to $15.5B: investment banking fees +55%, equities +72% and FICC +32% as IPOs, deals and trading all fired at once.

$20.98

Earnings per share nearly doubled

EPS rose 92% to $20.98 and annualized return on equity was 23.5%. Profit grew much faster than revenue because the credit-loss provision fell to just $102M.

$5.00

Dividend raised

Goldman raised its quarterly dividend to $5.00 per share (from $4.50) and returned $5.4B to shareholders in the quarter — $4.0B of buybacks plus $1.4B of dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Platform Solutions revenue fell 64% to $221M, mostly on markdowns of the Apple Card loan portfolio (moved to held-for-sale in late 2025) — the last drag from Goldman's abandoned consumer push.
  • Preferred stock dividends of $229M sit between net earnings ($6,628M) and the $6,399M attributable to common shareholders.

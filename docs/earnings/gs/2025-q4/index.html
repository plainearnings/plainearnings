Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$10.4B

Banking & markets had a record quarter

Global Banking & Markets revenue rose 22% to $10.4B — investment banking fees +25%, equities +25% with record financing, FICC +12% — the engine more than offset the Apple Card markdown.

-$2.3B / +$2.5B

Apple Card exit cut both ways

Agreeing to hand Apple Card to another issuer cut revenue by $2,258M (a markdown on the loan portfolio) but released $2,480M of credit reserves in the provision line — a net $145M boost to net earnings after tax and related costs.

$14.01

Profit per share jumped

Net earnings rose 12% to $4.6B and diluted EPS rose 17% to $14.01, a 16.0% return on equity — helped by $3.0B of buybacks in the quarter shrinking the share count.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The Apple Card transition is the quarter's big one-timer: -$2,258M in net revenues, -$2,481M in provision for credit losses (a benefit), +$38M in operating expenses — a net +$185M pre-tax, +$145M after tax. Excluding it, revenue was $15.7B and EPS was $13.55.
  • Preferred stock dividends of $233M sit between net earnings ($4,617M) and the $4,384M applicable to common shareholders.

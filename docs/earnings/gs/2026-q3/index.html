Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+72%

Equities carried the quarter

Equities revenue hit $6,441M — financing up 96% and intermediation up 52% — while FICC was flat at $3,471M. Clients traded and borrowed against stocks at a pace Goldman rarely sees.

$4.10T

The wealth business hit a record

Assets under supervision reached a record $4,097B, up from $3,452B a year ago, and Asset & Wealth Management fees rose 22%. Dealmaking helped too: investment banking fees of $3,224M rose 21%, with equity underwriting up 43%.

-72%

The consumer hangover is nearly over

Platform Solutions — the remains of the consumer experiment — shrank to $170M of revenue on Apple Card markdowns. It's now less than 1% of the firm; the quarter shows Goldman fully back to its wholesale roots.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Platform Solutions revenue fell 72% to $170M, mostly on markdowns of the Apple Card loan portfolio (moved to held-for-sale in late 2025) — the last drag from Goldman's abandoned consumer push.
  • Provisions for litigation and regulatory proceedings were just $23M, versus $131M a year ago.
  • 'Other expenses' jumped because charitable contributions to Goldman Sachs Gives were significantly higher — the firm's own presentation estimates the drag at roughly $1.00 of EPS.

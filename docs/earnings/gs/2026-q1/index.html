Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+48%

Dealmaking woke up

Investment banking revenues jumped 48% from a year ago to $2.8B — advisory fees of $1.5B led the way — as companies returned to buying, selling and raising money.

$17.55

EPS up 24%

Diluted EPS rose 24% to $17.55 and return on equity was 19.8% — profit growth plus a shrinking share count from $5.0B of buybacks in the quarter.

$3.65T

Client money keeps growing

Assets under supervision reached $3.65T, with $87B of net inflows in the quarter — the wealth business gives Goldman steadier fee income between trading booms.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Platform Solutions revenue fell 33% to $411M as Goldman's exit from consumer banking winds down.
  • Preferred stock dividends of $227M sit between net earnings ($5,630M) and the $5,403M attributable to common shareholders.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-$3.4B

Athene's portfolio marks drove the loss

Retirement Services booked $3.4B of investment-related losses (versus a $2.3B gain a year ago) as falling values on its holdings flowed through earnings — even though its underlying investment income grew 14% to $5.7B.

+25%

The fee machine set records

Management fees rose 25% to $758M and advisory and transaction fees jumped 67% to $633M; origination reached a record $114B in the quarter.

$1.07T

Scale keeps compounding

Assets under management reached about $1.07 trillion, and clients entrusted Apollo with $270B of inflows over the last twelve months.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $3.4B of investment-related losses at Athene are mark-to-market movements on its investment portfolio and related derivatives — they can reverse in later quarters and are excluded from Apollo's preferred 'spread related earnings' measure.

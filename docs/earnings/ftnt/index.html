Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+37%

Hardware came roaring back

Product revenue grew 37% to $765M — a strong appliance refresh cycle — while the larger service business grew 13% to $1.31B on subscriptions and support.

+29%

Billings point to more growth

Billings of $2.34B grew 29%, faster than revenue, and management raised full-year billings guidance for the third time this year — deferred revenue built up for future quarters.

34%

Profitability stayed elite

GAAP operating margin was 34% (38% non-GAAP) and operating cash flow was $946M — Fortinet converts an unusual share of sales into cash for a company growing this fast.

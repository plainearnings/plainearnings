Cost of services is mostly the pay of the agency staff who serve clients, plus client costs billed through. This quarter it includes $85M of merger severance and repositioning costs and a $107M gain on businesses held for sale.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.2%

Underlying growth is solid, not +59%

Reported revenue jumped 59% because IPG is now included. In core operations, organic growth was 7.2% — healthy for a global agency group.

$900M

Merger savings are the story to watch

Omnicom says it is on track to deliver $900M of cost savings from the Interpublic combination — the main reason profit can grow faster than revenue.

17.6%

Adjusted EBITA margin widened

Adjusted EBITA was $1.1B, a 17.6% margin — up from 16.1% a year ago on the combined basis — as savings began to offset integration costs.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating costs include $84.7M of severance and repositioning costs tied to the Interpublic (IPG) merger, and a $106.8M gain on assets held for sale and dispositions — the two roughly offset this quarter.
  • Year-ago comparisons are Omnicom alone: IPG closed on November 26, 2025, so reported growth rates mostly reflect the acquisition.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

39.0%

Margin jumped — with help

Gross margin rose to 39.0% from 31.4% a year ago. About 430 basis points of that came from net IEEPA tariff refunds (a refund gain, partly offset by related incentive pay, growth spending and taxes), on top of real productivity and mix gains.

$1.83

Guidance raised

EPS of $1.83 beat last year's $0.34, and the company raised full-year 2026 GAAP EPS guidance to $5.45–$5.85 and free cash flow guidance to $1.0–$1.2B, partly on the tariff refunds received.

$585M

Cash funded buybacks

Free cash flow was $585M in the quarter. The company spent $250M repurchasing shares, using up its old authorization, and the board approved a new $1B buyback program.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net IEEPA tariff refunds were a major one-off tailwind: about 430 basis points of gross margin and about $0.67 of EPS in the quarter (the refund gain, net of directly related incentive compensation, growth investments and, for EPS, taxes).
  • Pre-tax non-GAAP adjustments totalled $89.3M, mainly restructuring charges ($29.5M), a non-cash asset impairment ($19.7M), a $20.4M loss on the sale of a small Engineered Fastening business, and footprint-action charges inside gross profit and SG&A.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+37%

AI networks are the story

Revenue hit a record $1,671M, up 37%. Optical Networking alone was $1,191M — 71% of revenue — up 46%, as AI keeps driving compounding waves of network investment by carriers and cloud providers. Two customers each accounted for more than 10% of revenue, 41.7% combined.

$266M

Profit grew much faster than sales

Net income rose to $266M from $50M a year ago. Gross margin widened to 45.4% from 41.3% while operating expenses grew only 7% to $458M, so operating margin expanded to 18.0% from 6.1% — classic operating leverage.

$1.75B

Guidance raised again

Ciena guided fiscal Q4 revenue to $1.75B plus or minus $50M and raised full-year fiscal 2026 revenue guidance to $6.42B plus or minus $50M — about 35% growth at the midpoint. It also bought back about 0.4M shares for $172M in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter includes a $7M loss on extinguishing and modifying debt, booked below operating profit, and $1M of significant asset impairments and restructuring costs inside operating expenses. Adjusted (non-GAAP) EPS was $2.11 vs $1.83 GAAP.

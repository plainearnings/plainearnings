Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

Software did the lifting

Software revenue rose 8% to $7.8B — Red Hat up 11% and data up 19% — while consulting was flat, which is why total growth was 5% and not more.

$7.8B

Cash flow is the story

Free cash flow reached $7.8B in the first nine months, and IBM now expects it to grow by more than $1B this year — funding the $1.69 quarterly dividend and $11.4B of acquisitions so far in 2026.

14.6%

GAAP margins still lag the adjusted ones

GAAP pre-tax margin was 14.6% vs 19.3% on IBM's operating (non-GAAP) view — the gap is acquisition-related amortization and retirement-plan costs that adjusted figures exclude.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Discontinued operations lost $10M ($0.01 per share), so total net income was $2,190M vs $2,199M from continuing operations.
  • IBM ended the quarter with $63.8B of debt, including $12.7B of financing debt, after spending $11.4B on acquisitions this year.

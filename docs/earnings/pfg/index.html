Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$818M

A paper gain tripled headline profit

GAAP net income was $632M versus $214M a year ago, driven by an $818M increase in the fair value of a derivative tied to a reinsurance deal for a business Principal has exited. It is a valuation swing, not cash from selling anything.

$2.49

The operating business grew slowly

Non-GAAP operating earnings were $2.22 per share (+6%), or $2.49 excluding significant variances (+7%). Retirement & Income Solutions earned $298M before tax and Investment Management $168M; life insurance swung to a $32M profit from a $69M loss on better mortality experience.

$429M

Cash went back to shareholders

Principal returned $429M in the quarter — $250M of share buybacks and $179M of dividends — and raised the quarterly dividend to $0.85 per share for the fourth quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $818M pre-tax gain from the change in fair value of the funds-withheld embedded derivative on an exited business — excluded from Principal's non-GAAP operating earnings.
  • A $277M pre-tax loss on the disposition of Principal's Chilean annuities business, also inside the exited-business results.
  • $388M of net realized capital losses (GAAP), versus gains of $86M a year ago — excluded from operating earnings, which is why non-GAAP net income excluding the exited business was $355M ($1.64 per share) even as GAAP EPS hit $2.91.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+44%

Revenue surged, mostly commodity sales

Revenue hit $12.5B, up from $8.6B a year ago. Most of it — $11.2B — is commodity sales priced off energy markets, so the top line moves with prices as much as with volumes.

$336M

A one-time tax benefit flattered profit

A reorganization tied to the $9B minority equity investment created a one-time, noncash tax benefit of $336M ($0.53 a share). Without it, the tax line would have been a normal expense.

$2.24B

The pipelines themselves kept growing

Adjusted EBITDA rose 6% to $2.24B on record Permian volumes — NGL throughput up 6% — and ONEOK raised its full-year 2026 guidance again.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A one-time, noncash income tax benefit of $336M ($0.53 per diluted share) from the ONEOK, Inc. reorganization related to the $9B minority equity investment — it is why the quarter's tax line is a small net benefit ($26M) instead of an expense.
  • Results also include a $53M net benefit tied to the minority-equity and Brazos Midland transactions, mainly gains on early debt repayment; ONEOK repaid nearly $5.5B of debt principal in September 2026.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Government work drove solid growth

Revenue rose 31% to $43.6M. US government customers grew 34% to $39.2M — 90% of the total — while non-US government and commercial work grew 10% to $4.3M. Gross margin improved to 31% from 22%.

$76.7M

An impairment, not operations, caused the loss

The quarter includes a $76.7M goodwill impairment — a non-cash write-down of value from past acquisitions. Without it, the operating loss would have been about $23.2M instead of $100.0M. General and admin costs of $31.3M remain high relative to $43.6M of revenue.

-$91.9M

Bottom line, with a small tax benefit

Interest income and a $4.2M gain on derivative liabilities softened the loss, and taxes were a $0.6M benefit rather than a cost. The net loss was $91.9M, or $0.19 a share, versus a small profit a year ago. Nine-month operating cash outflow was $49.8M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Goodwill impairment of $76.7M in the quarter (the same amount for the nine months) — a non-cash charge that accounts for most of the net loss.
  • A $4.2M gain from the change in fair value of derivatives (warrant and note liabilities) is a mark-to-market move, not cash earned.

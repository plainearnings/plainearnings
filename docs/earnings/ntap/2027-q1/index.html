Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+51%

Hardware is booming again

Product revenue jumped 51% to $987M as customers bought all-flash arrays for AI workloads; services revenue rose 15% to $1,038M. Total revenue was a record $2,025M, up 30%.

$2.58

GAAP profit understates the run-rate

GAAP EPS was $1.88, but non-GAAP EPS — excluding stock-based pay, restructuring and similar items — was a record $2.58, and non-GAAP net income was $515M vs $375M GAAP.

$8.1B

Guidance raised for the year

NetApp raised its fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to $7,975–$8,225M (about $8.1B at the midpoint) and guided Q2 FY27 revenue to $2,025–$2,175M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $56M of restructuring charges sit inside GAAP operating expenses (vs $2M a year ago). NetApp's non-GAAP figures exclude them, along with stock-based compensation and intangible amortization.

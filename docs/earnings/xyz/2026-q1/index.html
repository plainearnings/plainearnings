Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+27%

Gross profit growth accelerated

Gross profit rose 27% to $2,909M — Cash App contributed $1.91B (+38%) and Square $982M (+9%). Block raised its full-year gross profit guidance to $12.33B, up over 19%.

$728M

Record adjusted profit

Adjusted operating income hit a record $728M — a 25% margin on gross profit — and adjusted EPS rose 52% to a record $0.85. The adjusted figures strip out the quarter's restructuring and legal charges.

-$0.52

One-time charges hid the progress

GAAP EPS was a $0.52 loss. A February reorganization ('Workforce Plan') loaded severance into every expense line, G&A carried roughly $254M of extra litigation and regulatory accruals, and bitcoin revaluation cost $173M — none of it from the core business slowing down.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A February 2026 reorganization — the 'Workforce Plan' — put severance and restructuring costs into product development, sales & marketing and G&A; management does not break out the total, but personnel costs rose sharply in all three lines.
  • General and administrative expense includes roughly $254M of additional accruals for litigation and regulatory matters, the main reason G&A jumped 74% year over year.
  • A $173M non-cash loss from revaluing Block's bitcoin holdings (a $93M loss a year ago) sits below operating income.
  • Adjusted figures strip out these items: adjusted operating income was a record $728M versus a $172M GAAP operating loss, and adjusted EPS was $0.85 versus -$0.52 GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+25%

Gross profit is the growth story

Gross profit rose 25% to $3,166M — Cash App contributed $1,974M (+31%) and Square $1,157M (+13%). Block raised its full-year gross profit guidance to $12.51B.

$864M

Record adjusted profitability

Adjusted operating income hit a record $864M — a 27% margin on gross profit — and adjusted EPS rose 65% to $1.02. Block's own AI tools are part of the story: in June, agentic AI helped write and review nearly all of its production code changes.

$0.15

Tax and bitcoin hid the progress

GAAP EPS fell to $0.15 from $0.87 a year ago. An $88M paper loss on bitcoin (a $212M gain a year ago) and a $214M tax provision on $301M of pre-tax income — a 71% effective rate — did the damage, not the operating business.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $88M non-cash loss from revaluing Block's bitcoin holdings — versus a $212M gain in the same quarter last year — sits below operating income and is the biggest single reason net income fell to $89M from $538M.
  • The $214M tax provision on $301M of pre-tax income is a 71% effective rate, far above Block's usual level — taxes, not operations, squeezed this quarter's bottom line.
  • Adjusted figures strip out share-based compensation and other items: adjusted operating income was $864M versus $447M GAAP, and adjusted EPS $1.02 versus $0.15 GAAP.

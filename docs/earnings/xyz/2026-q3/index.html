Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Profit is growing much faster than revenue

Gross profit — what Block keeps after the direct cost of each transaction — rose 22% to $3,235M. Cash App contributed $2,070M (+27%) and Square $1,157M (+14%). Block raised its full-year gross profit guidance to $12.63B.

60M

Cash App is becoming a bank

Cash App reached 60 million monthly transacting users in September, and lending through Borrow and Afterpay grew originations 40% to $19.0B. Block is quietly becoming a consumer lender, with the loan losses ($602M) to prove it.

$435M

The headline profit hides a tug-of-war

GAAP profit of $564M mixes a $435M contingency and restructuring charge with a $230M paper gain on bitcoin. Strip out the noise and adjusted operating income was $923M, up 92% — the underlying business had a very strong quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $435M charge for contingencies, restructuring and related items sits inside G&A — the main reason GAAP operating income ($429M) is less than half the adjusted figure ($923M, up 92%).
  • Other income includes a $230M non-cash gain from revaluing Block's own bitcoin holdings as prices rose.
  • Share-based compensation was $260M in the quarter — a real cost that GAAP counts and Block's adjusted numbers set aside.

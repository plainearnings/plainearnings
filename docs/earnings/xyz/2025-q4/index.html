Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Gross profit growth accelerated

Gross profit rose 24% to $2,872M — Cash App contributed $1,831M (+33%) and Square $993M (+7%) — beating Block's guidance. Block raised its full-year 2026 gross profit guidance to $12.20B, up 18%.

$588M

Adjusted profit hit a new gear

Adjusted operating income grew 46% to $588M — a 20% margin on gross profit — putting Block's Rule of 40 at 44% (24% growth plus 20% margin). The same day, Block announced plans to cut headcount nearly in half, from over 10,000 to just under 6,000, betting on AI-led productivity.

$234M

Bitcoin's paper loss shrank the bottom line

A $234M non-cash loss from revaluing Block's bitcoin holdings, versus a $252M gain a year ago, pulled GAAP net income to $116M (EPS $0.19) against $0.65 adjusted. The year-ago $1,946M net income also included a $1.9B one-time tax benefit, so the drop overstates any slowdown.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $234M non-cash loss from revaluing Block's bitcoin holdings (a $252M gain a year ago) sits below operating income — the main reason GAAP net income of $116M trails adjusted net income of $402M.
  • Operating expenses include $54M of contingencies, restructuring and other charges.
  • Adjusted figures strip out share-based compensation, amortization, contingencies and restructuring charges: adjusted operating income was $588M versus $485M GAAP, and adjusted EPS was $0.65 versus $0.19 GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-25%

Headline revenue fell sharply

Revenue was $673M, down from $894M a year ago. License revenue fell 45% on contract timing, while support and cloud revenue was almost flat at $382M.

+8%

The contract base still grew

ARR excluding the divested Kepware and ThingWorx businesses rose 8% to $2,508M, showing the underlying subscription book kept growing.

$1.44

Profit halved, and a takeover is pending

GAAP EPS was $1.44 versus $2.88 a year ago. On October 5, 2026 PTC agreed to be acquired by Schneider Electric and suspended guidance and earnings calls.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Impairment and other charges of $6M in the quarter
  • About $75M of divestiture-related cash taxes and $24M of divestiture-related costs weighed on Q4 cash flow (not on GAAP profit the same way)

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+$1.6B

The swing is mostly last year's pain

A year ago the quarter absorbed $972M of goodwill impairments and $230M of other impairments on the way to a $890M loss. With no impairments this quarter, net income was a $758M profit on revenue up 10% to $8,499M.

$720M

Intermediates led the segments

Intermediates & Derivatives EBITDA was $720M (before identified items $795M), ahead of O&P–Americas at $641M, as oxyfuels strength and higher profitability offset softer O&P–Americas polymer and olefin prices versus last quarter.

$828M

Clean earnings were $828M

Excluding $70M of identified items net of tax ($0.22 per share) — mainly European propylene-oxide joint-venture closure costs — net income was $828M, or $2.54 per diluted share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Identified items totaled $70M net of tax ($0.22 per share): pre-tax site closure costs of $77M (European propylene oxide joint venture), $2M of Cash Improvement Plan costs and a $9M European transaction credit, plus discontinued-operations effects. Excluding identified items, net income was $828M ($2.54 per share).
  • The year-ago quarter included $972M of goodwill impairments and $230M of other impairments, which is why the year-ago result was a $890M net loss.

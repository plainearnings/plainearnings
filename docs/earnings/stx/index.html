Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+61%

Pricing, not just volume, drove growth

Revenue rose $1.6B year over year, mainly on favorable pricing and more nearline exabytes shipped (198 of 220 total) as data-center demand for mass-capacity drives strengthened.

57.3%

Margins transformed

Gross margin jumped from 39.4% to 57.3% in a year on pricing and a mix shift to higher-capacity HAMR-based drives, turning the revenue surge into $1,761M of net income.

$1.2B

Cash is being used to cut debt

Seagate retired about $1.2B of debt in the quarter, cutting gross debt to $2.4B (and $1.6B after a further $750M retirement in October), while still returning $568M to shareholders.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $44M net loss from debt transactions, from retiring about $1.2B of debt principal during the quarter.
  • Restructuring and other charges were $4M.

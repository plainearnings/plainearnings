Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.2B

Fees set a record

Fee-related earnings hit a record $1.2B ($1.32 per adjusted share), up 37%, and total operating earnings were $1.5B. Management fees grew as fee-paying AUM reached $638B.

$796B

Money kept coming in

Assets under management rose 16% to $796B, with $34B of new capital raised in the quarter and $133B over twelve months — and the quarter was KKR's strongest ever for returning capital to clients.

$660M

GAAP profit is the noisy number

GAAP net income for common stockholders was $660M, after $3,309M of insurance benefit payments and $703M of interest expense on KKR's own balance sheet. Adjusted net income — KKR's preferred lens — was a record $1,493M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • KKR closed its acquisition of Arctos Partners (sports-franchise investing, $20B of AUM) on June 30, 2026 — it is reported inside Private Equity from this quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Power chips are carrying growth

Power Solutions revenue rose 21% to $890M. AI data-center revenue grew 175% from a year ago, and onsemi raised its 2030 AI data-center target to over $3B.

41.4%

Margins expanded a fifth straight quarter

GAAP gross margin reached 41.4%, up from 37.9% a year ago — richer power products and factory discipline turned 11% revenue growth into roughly 40% EPS growth.

$489M

A cash machine while it invests

Free cash flow was $489M, 28% of revenue. Big announced deals (Ferric, and Synaptics on revised ~$5.7B cash terms) had not closed yet in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses include $4.6M of restructuring and asset-impairment charges — far less than the $41.2M last quarter and the heavy fab-consolidation charges earlier in 2026.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

3.4 GW

Data centers drive the plan

Alliant has 3.4 gigawatts of contracted data-center demand and expects peak demand to rise about 60% by 2031 — an industry-leading growth rate that is reshaping its building plans.

$15.9B

Building plan raised 19%

The 2027–2030 capex forecast rose 19% to $15.9B. Alliant narrowed 2026 ongoing EPS guidance to $3.41–3.46 (the top half of its range) and set 2027 guidance at $3.63–3.73 with a $2.25 dividend target.

$1.52

A tax-timing tailwind

EPS rose to $1.52 from $1.09 on rate-base growth at IPL and WPL, higher equity earnings, higher AFUDC and the timing of income tax expense — a benefit Alliant says reverses by year-end. Income tax was a $67M benefit this quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

AI demand powers Industrial

Industrial Solutions sales rose 19.8% to $2,799M — now the larger segment — as customers building AI data centers bought more power and data connectivity, from inside the rack to the energy grid.

$6.1B

Orders point to more growth

Orders hit $6.1B in the quarter, up 29% year over year, and TE guides for mid-teens sales growth and over 20% EPS growth in the first quarter of fiscal 2027.

$87M

Tariff refunds will go back to customers

Reported sales are after an $87M deduction for IEEPA tariff refunds TE received during fiscal 2026 that it expects to return to customers — a one-off reconciling item, not lost business.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $87M of IEEPA tariff refunds received in fiscal 2026, deducted from sales as a reconciling item because TE expects to return them to customers.
  • $14M of restructuring charges and $12M of acquisition and integration costs inside operating expenses.

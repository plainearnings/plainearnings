Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

406K

Customers came back

Verizon added 406,000 postpaid phone customers and 137,000 net accounts — the best account growth in over a decade — while postpaid phone churn improved to 0.94% and promotional acquisition costs fell 41%.

$23.5B

Service revenue is the engine

Mobility and broadband service revenue grew 2.9% to $23.5B. Equipment revenue fell 9% as people keep phones longer and Verizon spends less on device subsidies — a deliberate trade for margin: adjusted EBITDA hit a record $13.8B.

$2B+

An AI infrastructure bet

Verizon signed a $2B+ deal with Amazon Web Services to scale distributed compute for AI workloads over its fiber network — and raised full-year guidance for postpaid adds, adjusted EPS and free cash flow for the third straight quarter.

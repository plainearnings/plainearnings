Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$61.40

Prices, not volumes, drove the quarter

The average realized price rose 32% to $61.40 per barrel of oil equivalent while production fell 97 MBOED to 2,302 MBOED — after closed sales and purchases, output was down 2%, with Middle East conflict effects in Qatar and higher Canadian royalties partly offsetting Lower 48 growth.

$7.2B

Cash flow funded big returns

Cash from operations was $7.2B. ConocoPhillips spent $3.1B on capital projects and returned $3.4B to shareholders — $2.4B of buybacks and $1.0B of dividends — staying on track to return 45% of operating cash flow in 2026. The quarterly dividend was raised 11% to $0.93 a share.

2029

Willow is the long bet

The Willow project in Alaska passed 75% completion on module fabrication and stays on track for first oil in early 2029. The company also grew its commercial LNG offtake portfolio to 13 million tonnes per year and ended the quarter with $10.4B of cash and short-term investments.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP earnings of $4.0B were slightly above adjusted earnings of $3.9B ($3.27 a share) — the special items the company adjusts for primarily reflect the reversal of a tax reserve.

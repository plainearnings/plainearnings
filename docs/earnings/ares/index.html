Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Management fees hit a record $1.06B

The recurring fee base keeps growing with assets under management, even in a quarter when performance income faded.

-65%

Carried interest fell to $162M

Profit-sharing from funds dropped by two-thirds versus an unusually strong quarter a year ago, pulling total revenue down 14%.

$524M

Fee related earnings of $524M

On the recurring measure Ares emphasizes, earnings stayed strong; GAAP net income attributable to the listed company was $85M after sharing profits with fund investors and operating-group partners.

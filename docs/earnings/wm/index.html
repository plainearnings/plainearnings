Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.3%

Pricing did the heavy lifting

Revenue growth came mainly from price — core price rose 5.8% and Collection and Disposal yield 3.4% — while collection volumes slipped 0.4% as WM deliberately shed low-margin residential work.

$41M

Healthcare Solutions swung to profit

The medical-waste business lifted operating income by $41M year on year (to a $20M profit from a $21M loss) on 3.7% revenue growth, cost cuts and synergy capture.

$957M

Cash flow funded big returns

Nine-month operating cash flow rose 12% to $4.87B and free cash flow rose over 42% to $3.01B; WM returned $957M to shareholders in Q3 alone and affirmed full-year guidance.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported profit includes an $18M net loss from divestitures, asset impairments and unusual items and $9M of restructuring costs; excluding adjustments, operating income was $1,329M and EPS was $2.15 (non-GAAP).
  • The year-ago quarter carried a much larger $213M impairment and unusual-items charge, which flatters this quarter's year-on-year profit growth.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

Clinical demand drove the beat

Revenue of $1.13B grew 4% and came in above guidance on healthy clinical demand and strong NovaSeq X placements; service revenue grew 17%.

$79M

A refund is inside the profit

Expected IEEPA tariff refunds added a $79M benefit inside cost of revenue (and cut revenue by $37M for customer credits). Excluding them, operating profit was $292M, not $305M.

$378M

Cash generation improved

Free cash flow rose to $378M from $253M a year ago, and Illumina raised full-year guidance: revenue of $4.64–4.66B and non-GAAP EPS of $5.40–$5.45.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Expected refunds of IEEPA tariffs: a $79M benefit recognized as a reduction of cost of revenue, and a $37M reduction of revenue for the portion expected to be credited back to customers. Excluding both, revenue was $1.166B, operating profit $292M and non-GAAP EPS $1.44.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+409%

Data Center became the company

Data Center revenue hit a record $18.4B, up 409% from a year ago and 27% from the prior quarter — about $83 of every $100 Nvidia took in — as cloud builders raced to train and run generative AI on Hopper chips.

76.0%

Margins reached record levels

A 76.0% gross margin — up from 63.3% a year ago — turned the sales surge into $12.3B of net income, up 769% from a year ago.

$24.0B

Guidance pointed higher still

Nvidia guided next-quarter revenue to $24.0B, plus or minus 2%; full-year fiscal 2024 revenue reached $60.9B, up 126%.

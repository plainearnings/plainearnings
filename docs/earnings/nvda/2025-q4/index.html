Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+93%

Data Center did the heavy lifting

Data Center revenue hit a record $35.6B, up 93% — about $90 of every $100 Nvidia took in — as cloud builders raced to install AI systems.

$11.0B

Blackwell's first quarter

The new Blackwell AI chips shipped $11.0B in their first quarter — the fastest product ramp in Nvidia's history — with large cloud providers taking about half of Data Center revenue.

-22%

Gaming took a breather

Gaming revenue fell 22% from the prior quarter to $2.5B because both new and older graphics cards were in short supply, even as the GeForce RTX 50 series launched.

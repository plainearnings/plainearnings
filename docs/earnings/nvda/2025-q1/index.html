Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+427%

Data Center exploded

Data Center revenue hit a record $22.6B, up 427% from a year ago — about $87 of every $100 Nvidia took in — as cloud builders raced to train and run generative AI on Hopper chips.

57¢

Well over half of sales became profit

Net income reached $14.9B — 57 cents of every revenue dollar — because a 78.4% gross margin held while revenue nearly quadrupled.

$28.0B

Guidance pointed higher still

Nvidia guided second-quarter revenue to $28.0B and said the Blackwell platform was in full production; it also announced a ten-for-one stock split effective June 2024.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+75%

Data Center sets another record

Data Center revenue was a record $62.3B — 91 cents of every revenue dollar. Networking revenue nearly quadrupled to $11.0B (+263%) as NVLink fabrics for GB200/GB300 systems ramped, and hyperscalers were slightly over half of Data Center sales.

$5.5B

Investment gains padded the bottom line

Gains on stakes Nvidia holds in other companies added $5.5B to other income. That is the main reason GAAP net income ($43.0B) came in above non-GAAP ($39.6B) — the gap is paper gains, not chip sales.

$36.2B

Cash generation stayed enormous

Operating cash flow was $36.2B in the single quarter. Across fiscal 2026 Nvidia returned $41.1B to shareholders in buybacks and dividends, and still had $58.5B of buyback authorization left.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gains on equity securities of $5,491M (mostly unrealized) sit inside GAAP other income and are excluded from non-GAAP results — the main reason GAAP net income of $42,960M tops non-GAAP net income of $39,552M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+56%

Data Center is nearly the whole company

Data Center revenue was a record $41.1B — 88 cents of every revenue dollar. Compute brought in $33.8B (+50%) and networking $7.3B (+98%) as Blackwell systems ramped.

$54.0B

Guidance points higher still

Nvidia guided third-quarter revenue to $54.0B, plus or minus 2%, with gross margin around 73.3% — and assumed no H20 chip sales to China at all.

$10.0B

Cash kept pouring in

Operating cash flow was $15.4B in the quarter. Nvidia returned $10.0B to shareholders ($9.7B of buybacks plus dividends) and the board added $60B to the buyback plan.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $180M release of previously reserved H20 chip inventory flattered gross profit: without it, non-GAAP gross margin would have been 72.3% rather than 72.7%. There were no H20 sales to China-based customers in the quarter; about $650M of unrestricted H20 chips were sold to a customer outside China.

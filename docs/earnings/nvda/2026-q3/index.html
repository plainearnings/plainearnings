Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+66%

Data Center is nearly the whole company

Data Center revenue was a record $51.2B — 90 cents of every revenue dollar. Compute brought in $43.0B (+56%) and networking a record $8.2B (+162%) as NVLink fabrics for GB200/GB300 systems ramped.

$23.8B

Cash kept pouring in

Operating cash flow was $23.8B in the quarter. Nvidia returned $12.7B to shareholders ($12.5B of buybacks plus dividends) and still had $62.2B of buyback authorization left.

$65.0B

Guidance points higher still

Nvidia guided fourth-quarter revenue to $65.0B, plus or minus 2%, with gross margin around 74.8% — another step up from this quarter's record.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gains from non-marketable and publicly-held equity securities of $1,354M sit inside GAAP other income and are excluded from non-GAAP results. GAAP and non-GAAP net income were nearly identical this quarter ($31,910M vs $31,767M).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+117%

Data Center is the whole story

Data Center revenue hit $89.0B, up 117% — 93% of all revenue. Hyperscalers alone bought $48.7B of AI platforms, and AI clouds, industrial and enterprise customers another $40.3B.

$7.8B

A big chunk of profit isn't from chips

Net gains on equity securities added $7,771M to other income. GAAP net income of $59.7B beats non-GAAP's $54.0B almost entirely because of those paper gains.

~$108B

Next quarter is already guided higher

Nvidia guided Q3 FY27 revenue to ~$108B (±2%) at a 74% gross margin — and assumes zero data-center revenue from China in the outlook.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net gains from equity securities of $7,771M lifted GAAP other income (excluded from non-GAAP results) — the main reason GAAP net income of $59,688M exceeds non-GAAP net income of $53,954M.

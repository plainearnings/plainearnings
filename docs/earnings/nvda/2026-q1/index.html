Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+73%

Data Center is nearly the whole company

Data Center revenue hit $39.1B, up 73% — almost $89 of every $100 Nvidia took in — as Blackwell AI systems went into full production.

$4.5B

The China export ban cost real money

A new U.S. license rule for H20 chips bound for China forced a $4.5B charge for unsold inventory and purchase commitments, and $2.5B of H20 sales that could not ship.

71.3%

Underneath the charge, margins held up

Excluding the H20 charge, gross margin would have been 71.3% and non-GAAP earnings $0.96 a share — the core AI business is still highly profitable.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $4.5 billion charge for H20 excess inventory and purchase obligations after the U.S. government required a license to export H20 products to China — sales of H20 chips were $4.6B in the quarter, and a further $2.5B of H20 revenue could not ship. Excluding the charge, non-GAAP gross margin would have been 71.3% (vs 60.5% GAAP) and diluted EPS $0.96 (vs $0.76 GAAP).

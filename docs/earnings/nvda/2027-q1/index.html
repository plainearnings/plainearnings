Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+92%

Data Center is nearly the whole company

Data Center revenue hit a record $75.2B, up 92% — 92% of all revenue. Hyperscalers bought $37.9B of AI platforms, and AI clouds, industrial and enterprise customers another $37.4B. No Hopper products were shipped to China in the quarter.

$15.9B

A big slice of profit is paper gains

Net gains on equity securities added about $15.9B to other income. That is why GAAP net income of $58.3B sits far above non-GAAP's $45.5B — the gap is investment gains, not chip sales.

$48.6B

Cash generation is enormous

Operating cash flow was $50.3B and free cash flow $48.6B in a single quarter. Nvidia returned about $20.0B to shareholders and added an $80B buyback authorization.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net gains from equity securities of about $15.9B lifted GAAP other income (excluded from non-GAAP results) — the main reason GAAP net income of $58,321M exceeds non-GAAP net income of $45,548M.
  • The year-ago quarter carried $4.5B of H20 charges (export restrictions on China-bound chips), which is why gross margin jumped from 60.5% to 74.9% year over year — the comparison flatters this quarter.

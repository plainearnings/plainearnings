Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+154%

Data Center is the company now

Data Center revenue hit a record $26.3B, up 154% — about $87 of every $100 Nvidia took in — as cloud builders raced to train and run AI models on Hopper chips.

55¢

More than half of sales became profit

Net income reached $16.6B — 55 cents of every revenue dollar — because the 75.1% gross margin held while revenue more than doubled.

$32.5B

Guidance pointed higher

Nvidia guided third-quarter revenue to $32.5B as Blackwell samples shipped to partners and customers, with the new chips set to ramp in the following quarter.

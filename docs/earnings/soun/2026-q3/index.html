Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+48%

Record revenue, bigger company

Revenue rose 48% to $62.4M, a record, helped by closing the LivePerson acquisition on September 4. GAAP gross margin fell to 34% from 43% a year ago as lower-margin acquired businesses joined and some upfront licence deals shift to hosted models; on the company's non-GAAP basis gross margin was 50%.

+$23M

A paper gain flattered the loss

Operating costs include a $23.4M gain from revaluing earn-out liabilities (shares owed to sellers of bought companies, re-priced each quarter with the stock). A year ago the same line was a $66.2M charge. Leaving it out, the operating loss would have been about $70M, not $46M.

-$46M

Still loss-making, with a dated target

The net loss narrowed to $46.4M from $109.3M a year ago, and adjusted EBITDA (the company's cash-style profit measure) was a $20.7M loss. SoundHound guided 2026 revenue to $250–260M and 2027 to $350–400M, and says it expects sustained adjusted EBITDA profit in the second half of 2027, helped by $120M of identified annual cost savings.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $23.4M gain on contingent acquisition liabilities is non-cash and swings with SoundHound's share price; the company's non-GAAP figures exclude it (non-GAAP net loss $21.2M also excludes stock pay and other items).
  • SoundHound closed its acquisition of LivePerson on September 4, 2026, so Q3 includes less than a month of LivePerson's results plus deal-related costs in general and administrative expenses.

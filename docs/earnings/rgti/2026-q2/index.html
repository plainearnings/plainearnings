Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+185%

Revenue nearly tripled — off a tiny base

Revenue rose to $5.1M from $1.8M a year ago, driven by US customers ($4.1M, up 327%). One customer alone was 64% of the quarter's revenue, so single contracts swing the numbers hard. Rigetti also signed an expanded collaboration with HPE and the Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center, and a letter of intent for up to $100M of potential US Department of Commerce funding.

$30M

Running costs are 6x revenue

Operating costs were $30.3M against $5.1M of revenue: $20.7M of research and development and $9.5M of selling, general and administrative costs. The operating loss was $28.1M. Leaving out stock pay and the warrant charge, the company's own non-GAAP net loss was $16.0M.

-$52.6M

A paper charge doubled the loss

The GAAP net loss was $52.6M, of which $29.6M was a non-cash charge from revaluing warrant liabilities — no money left the company; the liability's paper value rose with the share price. Rigetti ended the quarter with $541M of cash and investments and no debt, which is what funds losses at this scale.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $29.6M change in fair value of derivative warrant liabilities is a non-cash mark-to-market charge (revaluing warrants at the current share price). It was a $24.1M gain in the first half as a whole, so it swings sign quarter to quarter.

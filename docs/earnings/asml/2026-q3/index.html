Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+55%

A record quarter

Total net sales hit a record €11.7B ($13.3B), up 55% from a year ago and near the top of guidance. ASML sold 121 lithography systems, and service and upgrade sales jumped 68% as chipmakers squeezed more output from installed machines.

57.5%

Margins beat the plan

Gross margin reached 57.5%, above guidance, helped by those high-value upgrades. Operating income was $5.9B, a 44% operating margin, even after $1.3B of research spending.

2029–30

Demand runs past supply

Customers are raising their equipment forecasts on AI investment, and some are already asking about orders for 2029 and 2030. ASML says its planned supply for 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet demand and it is looking for ways to add capacity. Q4 sales are guided to €14.2B–€15.2B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net income includes a €509.7M (about $581M) gain on equity investments — the value of stakes ASML holds went up. It is not money from selling machines, and there was no such gain a year ago.

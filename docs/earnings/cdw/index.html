Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+73%

Financial services went all-in on AI

Sales to financial-services customers rose 73% YoY. Demand centered on data storage, servers, notebooks and software as enterprises build out AI infrastructure.

20.5%

Growth came at thinner margins

Gross margin fell to 20.5% from 21.9% because the mix shifted into lower-margin product categories — gross profit still grew 10% to $1,385M on the bigger sales base.

$3.26

Buybacks amplified EPS

Non-GAAP EPS rose 20% to $3.26, faster than profit growth, because buybacks cut diluted shares to 126M from 132M a year ago. CDW also declared a $0.68 quarterly dividend.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP operating income was $620M vs $490M GAAP — the roughly $130M gap is mainly stock-based pay, amortization and the workplace-optimization and acquisition costs CDW excludes from its non-GAAP figures.

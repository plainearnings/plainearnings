Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.3%

Best service growth in four years

Revenue rose 5.3% to $32.3B — the strongest consolidated service revenue growth in more than four years — as Advanced Connectivity service revenue grew 6.1% and its operating income jumped 18.9% to $7.6B.

$2.6B

Mexico write-down sinks GAAP profit

A $2.6B non-cash impairment on the Mexico wireless business (now held for sale) plus separation charges cut operating income to $4.1B from $6.1B. Adjusted operating income was $7.6B, up from $6.6B.

769,000

Record internet additions

AT&T added 769,000 fiber and fixed-wireless internet customers — a record — and 379,000 postpaid phones, passing 39 million fiber locations on the way to 40 million by year-end.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $2.6B non-cash impairment charge on the held-for-sale Mexico wireless business, inside the $3,372M impairments, abandonments and restructuring line.
  • Employee separation charges — together with the impairment and other items, adjustments total $0.52 per share (adjusted EPS $0.65 vs $0.13 GAAP).
  • Year-ago comparison is distorted the other way: Q3 2025 included a large gain on the sale of the DIRECTV investment in other income.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+36%

Revenue surged to $5.12B

Total revenue rose 36% from a year earlier to $5.12B, driven by a much bigger profit share from the Sanofi partnership.

$3.00B

Collaboration money is now the biggest stream

Collaboration revenue was $3.00B, up 52%. Sanofi sells Dupixent and Regeneron keeps a share of the profit; that share rose after the development balance was repaid in full last quarter.

$1.95B

Profit rose to $1.95B

Net income was $1.95B, up 33% from a year earlier, even though research spending rose 22% to $1.79B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$82M

Charges crushed GAAP profit

Restructuring, impairment and other charges jumped to $82.1M from $1.6M a year ago, including a $66.2M asset impairment. Operating income fell to $22M from $111M, and GAAP EPS to $0.15 — versus non-GAAP EPS of $1.28.

$1.98B

The Qorvo deal redraws the company

The Qorvo combination closed after quarter-end, so these results are Skyworks standalone. Together the two did about $1.98B of September-quarter revenue, and management targets $500M or more of annual cost savings within 24–36 months.

-$61M

Cash flow went negative

Operating cash flow fell to $34M from $200M a year ago while capital expenditures — money spent on buildings and equipment — rose to $95M, leaving free cash flow at -$61M for the quarter (still +$229M for the full year).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $82.1M of restructuring, impairment and other charges (vs $1.6M a year ago), including a $66.2M asset impairment charge shown in the cash-flow statement.
  • The non-GAAP reconciliation also flags $66.4M of settlements, gains, losses and impairments and $35.7M of acquisition-related expenses for the quarter, largely tied to the Qorvo combination — these overlap with, and are not additional to, the GAAP charge lines above.

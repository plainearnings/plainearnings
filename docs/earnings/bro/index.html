Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1.2%

Real growth was slow

Strip out acquisitions and organic revenue grew just 1.2% (1.5% including contingent commissions) — management called it a challenging rate environment, meaning insurers are cutting the prices that drive Brown & Brown's commissions.

$1.7B

Deals still drive the headline

Commissions and fees rose 10% to $1.7B, and nine-month revenue is up 23% — almost all of it bought, led by Accession, which added about 5,000 teammates just over a year ago.

34.6%

Margins slipped a little

Adjusted EBITDAC margin fell from 36.6% to 34.6% as costs from the bigger, acquired business grew faster than organic revenue, and diluted GAAP EPS dipped to $0.67 from $0.68 because the Accession deal issued more shares.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Change in estimated acquisition earn-out payables: a $27M expense — the company raised what it expects to pay sellers of past acquisitions (worth $0.06 of EPS in its adjusted figures).
  • Acquisition and integration costs of $36M, mainly tied to Accession, excluded from adjusted results.
  • A $14M noncash gain from marking the Accession escrow liability to market, partly offsetting the items above.
  • Excluding these items and $110M of amortization, adjusted EPS was $1.07 vs $0.67 GAAP, and EBITDAC — Adjusted was $597M.

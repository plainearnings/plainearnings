Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7%

The supply chain is the growth engine

Supply-chain revenue rose 7% to $746M — 63% of all revenue — on more orders and higher food-basket prices, and its gross margin widened 0.9 points to 12.2% on procurement savings. Royalty income, the high-margin core, grew 5% in the U.S. and 7% internationally.

+0.3%

Same-store sales barely grew

U.S. same-store sales rose just 0.3% (company stores +1.0%, franchised +0.3%) and international 0.2% excluding currency. Growth came from new stores — 175 net openings took the system to 22,706 stores — not from existing stores selling much more.

$4.60

Buybacks flatter the per-share line

Net income rose 9% to $152M, but EPS rose 13% to $4.60 because buybacks cut the diluted share count 3% to 33.1M. Operating income grew 8% to $242M despite the drag from selling company-owned stores to franchisees.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Domino's refranchised certain U.S. company-owned store markets in Q2 2026, booking a small $0.2M refranchising loss in Q3. That portfolio change — not weaker demand — is why company-owned store revenue fell 26% YoY.

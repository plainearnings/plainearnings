Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

84%

Players keep spending in old games

Recurrent consumer spending — virtual currency, add-on content and in-game purchases — was $1,290M, 84% of revenue, led by NBA 2K, Grand Theft Auto Online and Zynga mobile titles such as Toon Blast and Empires & Puzzles.

-$34M

A GAAP loss, by design of the calendar

The $34M net loss includes $161M of non-cash amortization of intangibles from past acquisitions and a $43M impairment on a cancelled title. The big release — Grand Theft Auto VI, due November 19, 2026 — lands in the next quarter but one.

$1.39B

Bookings dipped slightly

Net Bookings — what was actually sold in the quarter, before revenue deferrals — fell 3% to $1,386M. Mobile was half of revenue at $762M, console $641M and PC and other $131M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Cost of revenue included a $43.4M impairment charge after Take-Two decided not to proceed with an unannounced title from a third-party developer — the main reason software development costs and royalties jumped to $135M from $30M a year ago.

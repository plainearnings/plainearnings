Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+42%

A demand surge, led by AI freight

Revenue rose 42% to $4.11B. Airfreight revenue jumped 51% and ocean 48%, as AI hyperscalers and other technology customers shipped far more, and as tariff-related customs work grew.

+1%

Headcount barely moved

Staffing rose just 1% while operating income rose 55% to $445M — operating margin expanded to 10.8% from 10.0%. That leverage is why EPS rose even faster, up 62% to $2.65.

$946M

Cash back to shareholders

Expeditors returned $197M in the quarter and $946M year to date through dividends and share repurchases, including buying back 1.1 million shares in Q3.

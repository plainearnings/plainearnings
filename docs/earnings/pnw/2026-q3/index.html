Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

9,166 MW

Record demand, met reliably

APS customers set an all-time peak demand record on August 2 and broke last year's record a dozen times during the quarter. The grid held up through one of the more active monsoon seasons in years.

+9.5%

Growth is the story

Weather-normalized electricity sales grew 9.5% and the customer count rose 1.8%. APS expects customers' annual electricity needs to more than double by 2041, and its new long-term plan requires extra-large new users to pay for the infrastructure they need.

$5.05–$5.25

Guidance raised, bills credited

Pinnacle West raised its full-year 2026 EPS guidance to $5.05–$5.25. Separately, APS is returning over $33M in bill credits this November — about $14 off a typical home's bill — plus $5M of hardship assistance funded by the company.

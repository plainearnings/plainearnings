Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

Growth accelerated, led by Water

Sales rose 13% to $4,695M, with organic growth accelerating to 7% — volume up 2% and pricing up 5%. Global Water sales jumped 21% to $2,481M as data-center and microelectronics demand drove 63% organic growth in its high-tech business, helped by the Ovivo and CoolIT deals.

$2.18

Underlying earnings rose

Adjusted EPS was $2.18, up 5%, and adjusted operating income rose 14% to $893M — despite an estimated 10% headwind from CoolIT acquisition costs. Ecolab guided full-year 2026 adjusted EPS to $8.10–$8.20, up 8–9%.

$551M

GAAP profit still fell

GAAP net income fell 6% to $551M and GAAP EPS fell 5% to $1.95. Special charges rose to $81M and net interest expense more than doubled to $125M on acquisition debt — the cost of the deal spree that is driving the growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Special charges of $81M in the quarter, primarily acquisition and integration costs. Leaving out these and similar items, Ecolab's adjusted operating income was $893M (up 14%) and adjusted EPS was $2.18 (up 5%), versus GAAP EPS of $1.95.
  • Corporate costs include $79M of amortization for acquired brands and technology — $28M from the Nalco merger, $21M from Purolite, $20M from CoolIT and $10M from Ovivo Electronics.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$29.7B

Record order value

Marketplace GOV rose 39% to $29.7B on 903 million orders (+32%) — growth in diners and order frequency, plus a first full quarter of Deliveroo, which closed on October 2, 2025.

+38%

Revenue kept pace

Revenue rose 38% to $4.0B. DoorDash keeps a slice of every order as its fee, so revenue grows with the value of what is ordered; excluding Deliveroo, revenue still rose 26%.

$213M

Profit rose with scale

Net income attributable to shareholders rose 51% to $213M and Adjusted EBITDA rose 38% to $780M, even as depreciation climbed to $267M after the Deliveroo deal.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The chart's net profit is $213M — net income including redeemable non-controlling interests, which was also the figure attributable to DoorDash common stockholders this quarter (no amount was attributable to those interests in Q4).
  • General and administrative costs include $57M of transaction-related costs, mostly for the Deliveroo acquisition, and $29M of certain legal, tax and regulatory settlements, reserves and expenses.

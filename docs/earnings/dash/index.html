Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$33.9B

The marketplace keeps compounding

Gross order value rose 36% to $33.9B on 993M orders. DoorDash keeps only a slice of each order — $4.6B of revenue from $33.9B of goods is the whole model in one line.

+34%

Profit is scaling now

Net income rose to $465M from $243M a year ago — revenue grew 34% while most cost lines grew slower. That is the operating leverage delivery was always supposed to produce.

$1.06

EPS doubled

Diluted EPS was $1.06 vs $0.55 a year ago. The release also flags a proposed acquisition of Instacart — the next chapter, if shareholders and regulators agree.

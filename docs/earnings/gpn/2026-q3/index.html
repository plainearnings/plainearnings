Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+64%

Worldpay changed the company's size

GAAP revenue rose 64% to $3.31 billion with Worldpay consolidated; on a normalized like-for-like basis, adjusted net revenue grew over 4% to $3.18 billion.

$246M

GAAP operating profit was squeezed

Operating income fell to $246 million because cost of service includes $763 million of acquisition-related amortization and adjustments, and SG&A includes $344 million of deal and transformation costs. Adjusted operating income was $1,353 million.

$650M

Synergy target raised

Management raised the expected annual run-rate Worldpay cost synergies to $650 million and kept 2026 guidance unchanged: adjusted EPS of $13.60-13.80.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results carry over $1.1 billion of Worldpay-related items: $763 million of adjustments in cost of service (mostly $755 million of acquired-intangible amortization) and $344 million in SG&A (acquisition/integration $222M, transformation $51M, employee termination $63M, other $8M).
  • Income from discontinued operations was $202 million, and the quarter's tax line was a $67 million benefit rather than a charge — both lift total net income of $290 million well above continuing-operations income of $88 million.

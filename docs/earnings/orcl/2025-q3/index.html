Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$130B+

The backlog jumped 63%

Remaining performance obligation — contracts signed but not yet delivered — jumped 63% in a year to more than $130B. Oracle said the first Stargate contract, the giant AI-infrastructure project, had not even been signed yet.

+49%

Cloud infrastructure is the growth engine

Cloud infrastructure revenue (IaaS — renting computing power) grew 49% to $2.7B, and total cloud revenue rose 23% to $6.2B. AI training workloads are the driver: customers rent Oracle's data centers to build and run AI models.

-10%

Old license sales keep shrinking

New on-premise license sales fell 10% to $1.13B. That is the old Oracle shrinking on schedule — customers who once bought software outright now rent it as a cloud subscription, which shows up in the support line above instead.

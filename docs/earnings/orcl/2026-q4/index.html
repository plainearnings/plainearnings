Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$638B

The backlog is the story

Remaining performance obligations — work customers have signed for but not yet received — jumped $85B in one quarter to $638B, up 363% year over year, on giant AI contracts, some of them prepaid.

+47%

Cloud is now half the company

Cloud revenue rose 47% to $9.9B — 52% of everything Oracle sells — while old-style software shrank 2%. Infrastructure (OCI) alone grew 93%.

-$23.7B

Growth is eating cash

Free cash flow for fiscal 2026 was negative $23.7B — $32.0B of operating cash flow minus $55.7B spent on data centers and GPUs. Interest expense hit $1.4B in this quarter alone.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges of $823M — ten times the $83M of a year ago — mostly severance as Oracle reshaped its workforce.
  • Net income of $4,304M is before $81M of preferred-stock dividends; income available to common shareholders was $4,223M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$523B

The backlog exploded

Remaining performance obligations hit $523B, up 438% in a year and up $68B in the quarter — highlighted by new commitments from Meta and NVIDIA. Years of cloud revenue are already signed.

+68%

Infrastructure is the engine

Cloud infrastructure (OCI) revenue grew 68% to $4.1B; total cloud revenue was $8.0B, up 34% and now half of everything Oracle earns.

$2.7B

A one-time gain did the heavy lifting

Selling Oracle's stake in chip designer Ampere added a $2.7B pre-tax gain. Operating income was $4.7B, up 12%, and the tax bill was just $207M — together they explain why net income nearly doubled.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $2.7B pre-tax gain on the sale of Oracle's stake in Ampere Computing, its chip-design company — it sits inside the $2,668M of net non-operating income.
  • Restructuring charges of $406M — nearly five times the $84M of a year ago — sit inside operating expenses.

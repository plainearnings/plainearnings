Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$98B

The backlog surged 44%

Remaining performance obligations — work customers have signed for but Oracle has not yet delivered — jumped 44% to $98B. Oracle said it signed the largest sales contracts in its history in Q3 and Q4, including over 30 AI contracts worth more than $12.5B in Q4 alone, one of them with OpenAI to train ChatGPT in the Oracle Cloud.

+20%

Cloud is carrying the growth

Cloud revenue — infrastructure plus applications — rose 20% to $5.3B, with cloud infrastructure alone up 42% to $2.0B and cloud applications up 10% to $3.3B. That growth more than covered a 15% drop in new license sales and declines in hardware and services.

$878M

Debt and tax ate the operating gain

Operating income rose 13% to $4.7B, but net income still fell 5% to $3.1B: Oracle paid $878M of interest in the quarter, and the tax line swung from a $210M benefit a year ago to a $639M charge this time.

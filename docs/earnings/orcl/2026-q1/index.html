Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$455B

The backlog exploded

Remaining performance obligations — contracts signed but not yet delivered — rose 359% in a year to $455B after four multi-billion-dollar contracts were signed in the quarter. Oracle expects cloud infrastructure revenue to grow 77% this fiscal year.

+28%

Cloud is nearly half the company

Cloud revenue rose 28% to $7.2B — 48% of all revenue — while traditional software shrank 1%. MultiCloud database revenue, from running Oracle databases inside Amazon, Google and Microsoft clouds, grew 1,529%.

$2.9B

Profit flat while building

Net income was $2.93B, unchanged from a year ago: cloud delivery costs jumped 39% and restructuring charges hit $402M as Oracle races to build AI data-center capacity. Non-GAAP EPS was $1.47.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges of $402M — more than five times the $73M of a year ago — sit inside operating expenses.

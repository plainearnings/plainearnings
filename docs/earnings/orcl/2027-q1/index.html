Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+121%

The cloud build-out is the story

Cloud infrastructure revenue more than doubled to $7.4B, and Oracle delivered 850MW of new data-center capacity and over 300,000 GPUs to AI customers in one quarter.

$664B

A backlog bigger than most companies

Remaining performance obligations — contracts customers have signed but not yet paid for — hit $664B, up $209B in a year, including $30B+ of new AI cloud contracts in this quarter alone.

-$5B

Record cash in, even more spent

Operating cash flow hit a record $23B, yet free cash flow was −$5B because data-center construction is consuming cash even faster. Oracle also sold $20B of new stock to help fund it.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Oracle sold $20B of new common stock in the quarter through an at-the-market program to fund its data-center expansion — helpful for the balance sheet, dilutive for existing shareholders.

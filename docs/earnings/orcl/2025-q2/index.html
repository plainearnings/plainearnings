Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$97B

The backlog jumped 49%

Remaining performance obligations — work customers have signed for but not yet paid for — reached $97B, up 49% from a year ago. Cloud customers, including AI companies renting Oracle's data centers, are booking capacity well ahead.

+12%

Cloud is carrying the growth

Cloud services and license support revenue rose 12% to $10.8B — over three-quarters of all revenue — while old-style licenses barely grew and hardware and services shrank. The mix keeps shifting toward recurring cloud rent.

$866M

Debt is the quiet cost

Oracle paid $866M of interest in a single quarter on debt taken on to build data centers and buy back stock. Profit still rose 26%, but interest now eats about a fifth of operating income.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$553B

The backlog exploded

Remaining performance obligations hit $553B, up 325% in a year and up $29B in the quarter — mostly large AI contracts. Years of cloud revenue are already signed.

+84%

Infrastructure is the engine

Cloud infrastructure (OCI) revenue grew 84% to $4.9B; total cloud revenue was $8.9B, 52% of everything Oracle earns.

$1.2B

Debt is the drag

Interest expense rose 32% to $1.2B in the quarter as Oracle borrows to build data centers — the main reason net income ($3.7B) grew more slowly than operating income.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges of $153M — more than double the $63M of a year ago — sit inside operating expenses, alongside $20M of acquisition-related costs.

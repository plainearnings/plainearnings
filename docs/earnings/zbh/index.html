Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3%

Growth outside knees

Hips grew 6%, S.E.T. 7% and Technology & Data, Bone Cement and Surgical 12% — together two-thirds of sales — while Knees, still the biggest category, fell 3%.

$1.90

Underlying EPS held flat

Adjusted EPS of $1.90 matched last year even as GAAP EPS fell to $1.00, because the adjusted figure strips out $166M of acquisition amortization and the deal-cost swing.

$372M

Cash flow stayed strong

Operating cash flow was $560M and free cash flow $372M in the quarter, even though GAAP net earnings fell 17% to $192M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquisition, integration and divestiture swung from a $34M credit a year ago to a $20M cost — a roughly $54M year-over-year drag on operating profit.
  • Restructuring and cost-reduction charges were $14M in the quarter.
  • Adjusted net earnings were $363M versus $192M GAAP — the gap is mostly the $166M of intangible amortization plus restructuring and deal costs that Zimmer Biomet excludes from its adjusted figures.

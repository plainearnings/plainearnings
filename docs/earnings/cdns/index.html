Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8.2B

A record order book

Backlog hit a record $8.2B, with $4.2B of revenue already contracted for the next 12 months. Management raised the 2026 outlook to 21% revenue growth and $8.40 of non-GAAP EPS.

26.2%

GAAP margin compressed

GAAP operating margin fell to 26.2% from 31.8% as stock pay, acquisition amortization and restructuring grew faster than sales. On Cadence's non-GAAP basis the margin was 44.1%.

+30%

Systems is the growth edge

System Design & Analysis revenue grew 30%, IP 24% and Core EDA 20% — AI-driven chip design is pulling every category, and Cadence's hardware line had another record quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring cost $42M in the quarter, and amortization of acquired intangibles was $80M in total ($34M in operating expenses, the rest in cost of revenue) — both are excluded from Cadence's non-GAAP results.
  • Stock-based compensation — shares paid to employees — was $145M in the quarter. It is the main reason non-GAAP net income ($581M, $2.11 per share) is roughly double the GAAP figure.

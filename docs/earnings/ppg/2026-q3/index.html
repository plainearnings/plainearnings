Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Performance Coatings led

Segment sales rose 10% to $1.55B with organic growth of 8%, led by aerospace, protective & marine and refinish coatings. Its profit margin (EBITDA) widened to 23.9% of sales.

24.0%

Architectural margins jumped

Global Architectural Coatings' EBITDA margin rose 3 points to 24.0% of sales on Latin American strength, higher prices and cost cuts, even though paint volumes dipped slightly.

-11%

Industrial lagged

Industrial Coatings' EBITDA fell 11% and its margin shrank to 14.4% as raw-material inflation outran price increases — the one soft spot, despite double-digit growth in packaging coatings.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted EPS of $2.25 (non-GAAP) excludes $20M of acquisition-related amortization, $11M of restructuring costs and smaller items; GAAP EPS from continuing operations was $2.12.
  • A business PPG is exiting lost $22M after tax in discontinued operations, which is why total net income attributable to PPG ($451M) is lower than the continuing-operations figure used in the chart.

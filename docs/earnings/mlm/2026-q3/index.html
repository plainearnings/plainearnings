Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

Aug 21

The Lhoist deal reshaped the company

Combining with Lhoist North America made Martin Marietta the nation's leading producer of lime and limestone products. Specialties posted record revenue of $362M, including only five weeks of Lhoist.

27.5%

Growth diluted margins — for now

Gross margin fell to 27.5% from 33.1%: acquired volumes carry lower margins and the core East aggregates business earned less ($312M vs $335M) on softer shipments.

$138M

Leverage is the bill for growth

Interest expense more than doubled to $138M and the share count rose about 8% on stock issued for the deal — together they turned +28% revenue growth into a 34% drop in continuing EPS.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $84M of acquisition, divestiture and integration expenses tied mainly to the Lhoist North America combination, which closed August 21, 2026.

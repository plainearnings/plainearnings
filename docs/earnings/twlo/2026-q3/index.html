Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

Growth accelerated

Revenue rose 20% to $1,560M — 16% organic, the rest from carrier A2P fees passed through at cost and a small acquisition — and Twilio raised its full-year growth outlook to 20%.

$149M

GAAP profit is now real

GAAP operating income was $149M, up 263%, at a 49% gross margin. Non-GAAP operating income was $326M — the $177M gap is mostly $154M of stock-based pay, a real cost GAAP counts and non-GAAP does not.

$370M

Cash flow is the quiet headline

Free cash flow was $370M, 24% of revenue, up from $248M a year ago. Net income of $161M also included a $22M tax benefit and a $25M share of losses from an equity-method investment.

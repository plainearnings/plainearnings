Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Growth came from three places

Net sales rose 8.9% to $3,036M: comparable sales added 3.8%, new stores contributed, and the acquired Space NK luxury chain in the U.K. and Ireland added sales Ulta did not have a year ago.

39.1%

Margins held nearly steady

Gross profit rose 8.7% to $1,187M, but as a share of sales it eased to 39.1% from 39.2% because Space NK's mix carries a thinner margin. SG&A actually improved as a share of sales, to 26.4% from 26.6%, lifting operating income 10% to $380M.

$6.55

Profit rose, and guidance followed

Net income rose to $282M and diluted EPS rose 13% to $6.55, helped by buybacks shrinking the share count. Ulta raised its full-year outlook — sales growth of 6.7%–7.2% and EPS of $28.70–$29.00 — and lifted its planned buyback to $1.8B.

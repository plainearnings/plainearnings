Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+58%

AI servers drove a record quarter

Revenue rose 58% to $47.0B. Infrastructure Solutions Group revenue rose 89% to $31.8B — AI-optimized servers doubled to $16.4B, traditional servers and networking rose 122% to $10.5B and storage rose 26% to $4.9B. Client Solutions (PCs) rose 20% to $15.0B. Dell raised full-year revenue guidance by $25B to $192B.

$5.4B

Profit grew much faster than sales

Operating income tripled to $5.4B (an 11.5% margin, up from 6.0%) because running costs rose only 21% while revenue rose 58%. Net income was $4.1B, up 255%, and GAAP EPS was $6.34 vs $1.70 a year ago. Dell also returned a record $4.3B to shareholders in buybacks and dividends.

$95B

The order book says demand is not slowing

Dell booked a record $60.9B of AI server orders in the quarter and ended it with a $95B backlog — orders taken but not yet delivered or counted as revenue. That backlog, not just this quarter's sales, is why full-year AI server revenue guidance rose to $74B.

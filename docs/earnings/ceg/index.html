Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Calpine transformed the scale

Revenue rose 23% to $7.5B. The acquired Calpine fleet supplied 27,642 of 89,815 total GWh sold in the quarter, on top of 44,160 GWh of nuclear generation.

920 MW

Big customers are signing long deals

Constellation signed another 920 MW of 15–20-year power purchase agreements for nuclear power starting 2029–2032, and raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $11.50–$12.50.

$1.42

GAAP profit is the noisy number

GAAP EPS fell to $1.42 from $2.67 on $340M of after-tax unrealized fair-value losses and Calpine deal costs — while adjusted operating EPS rose 34% to $2.55. Watch both: the adjustments recur every quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Unrealized losses on fair-value adjustments (hedges and related items) cost $340M after tax in the quarter — the biggest single reason GAAP EPS ($1.42) sits far below adjusted operating EPS ($2.55).
  • Amortization of commodity contracts acquired with Calpine cost $149M after tax, and Calpine merger and integration costs were $84M after tax; decommissioning-related gains added $221M after tax the other way. Adjusted operating earnings were $920M in total.

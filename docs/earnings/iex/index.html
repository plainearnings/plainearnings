Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

AI demand is lifting HST

Health & Science Technologies sales jumped 14% (13% organic) on data-center power and semiconductor demand — 46% of IDEX's total sales now come from this segment.

$991M

Orders point to more growth

Orders rose 13% to $991M — more than the quarter's sales — and IDEX raised full-year guidance again, to organic sales growth of 6.5–6.8%.

$2.33

Two profit numbers

GAAP EPS was $1.95; the $2.33 'adjusted' figure the company headlines strips out amortization and restructuring. Profit margin widened either way.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted EPS of $2.33 excludes $3.4M of restructuring charges and asset impairments and $32.6M of intangible-asset amortization; GAAP EPS was $1.95.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.7%

The malls themselves are thriving

Portfolio net operating income rose 6.7%, occupancy hit 96.5%, base rent per foot rose 6.1% and retailer sales per foot jumped 14.8% to $852. Simon raised full-year Real Estate FFO guidance again.

-$130M

Paper losses cut GAAP profit

Unrealized mark-to-market losses of $130M — on Klépierre exchangeable bonds and a newly public equity holding — cut net income to $499M from $606M, a $99.6M after-tax drag of $0.27 per share.

$2.30

Dividend raised

The quarterly dividend rises to $2.30 per share, up 4.5% year over year, payable December 31. Simon also bought back $237M of stock in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $129.6M of unrealized mark-to-market losses on publicly traded equity instruments and derivatives (Klépierre exchangeable bonds and a revalued equity security) — $99.6M after tax, or $0.27 per share.

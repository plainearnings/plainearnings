Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Segment profit jumped

Segment profit rose 23% to $1,062 million and segment margin expanded to 21.3%, helped by productivity, pricing, and eliminating stranded costs left over from earlier spin-offs and sales.

$19B

Backlog near $19 billion

Backlog grew 7% year over year to nearly $19 billion (excluding the two businesses sold in the quarter), and management raised full-year guidance: sales of $20.0–20.1 billion and adjusted EPS of $8.40–8.45.

−23%

Industrial Automation shrank — on paper

Reported Industrial Automation sales fell 23%, but that is the sale of the warehouse (WWS) and productivity (PSS) businesses leaving the numbers; the remaining business grew 4% organically and its margin expanded 3.5 points to 18.4%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • First quarter as a standalone company following the Honeywell Aerospace spin-off (completed June 29, 2026): Aerospace results sit in discontinued operations, which lost $103M in the quarter.
  • Other income includes a $339M gain on selling non-strategic businesses and assets, and the quarter absorbed $94M of divestiture-related costs.
  • Reported EPS from continuing operations fell 20% only because the year-ago quarter contained a one-time gain from the Resideo termination agreement; adjusted EPS rose 34%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5%

Both hospital businesses grew

Net revenues rose 5.3% to $4,733M. Acute Care revenue rose 5% to $2,764M and Behavioral Health rose 6% to $1,966M, on more admissions in both businesses.

$26M

Liability reserves took a bite

UHS added $26M to its reserves for self-insured professional and general liability claims — tied to a tentative settlement — and interest expense rose to $56M from $38M. Income from operations fell to $454M from $522M despite the revenue growth.

$47M

Medicaid payments helped

Increases in Medicaid supplemental payments (extra state payments for treating Medicaid patients, mainly in Washington, D.C. and California) added a favorable $47M before tax, net of related provider taxes — partly offsetting the $26M reserve increase, for a net favorable $21M that was not in UHS's forecast.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Two items UHS says were not in its forecast netted to a favorable $21M before tax: a $47M favorable impact from higher Medicaid supplemental payments (net of provider taxes), partly offset by a $26M increase in self-insured liability reserves tied to a tentative legal settlement.

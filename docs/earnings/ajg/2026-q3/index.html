Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

26th

The streak continues

Adjusted EBITDAC grew 25% — the 26th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth — with margins still expanding. Brokerage organic growth was 5%.

$1.55

GAAP vs adjusted is the whole debate

GAAP EPS of $1.55 is barely half the adjusted $3.00: the gap is amortization ($212M after tax effect in adjustments), AssuredPartners integration and workforce/lease charges. Investors pay for the adjusted number; the GAAP one funds the roll-up.

-$170M

Corporate drag is the debt bill

The Corporate segment lost $170M — mostly $168M of interest on acquisition debt. It is the carrying cost of the roll-up model.

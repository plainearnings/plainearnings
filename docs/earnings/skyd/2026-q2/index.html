Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Streaming is the growth engine

Direct-to-consumer revenue rose 9% to $2.47B. Paramount+ revenue grew 16%, subscribers reached 81.6 million, and it was the service's best-ever quarter for keeping subscribers.

-9%

Legacy TV keeps shrinking

TV Media revenue fell 9% to about $3.13B as advertising dropped 14% and pay-TV affiliate fees fell 6%, though tighter costs lifted its profit margin.

$41M

Reported profit is still thin

Net earnings were just $41M on $6.91B of revenue, after $188M of restructuring and transaction costs and $255M of interest expense. Adjusted EBITDA was $1.1B, up 27%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and transaction-related costs of $188M in the quarter; the shareholder letter says operating income included $153M of transaction-related costs.
  • The company is preparing a proposed combination with Warner Bros. Discovery, which it flagged as a focus alongside its standalone strategy.

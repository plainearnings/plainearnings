Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

Users are growing faster again

Daily active users rose 29% to 64.9M — a second straight quarter of faster growth — helped by hundreds of small product experiments and game partnerships (Brawl Stars, Eggy Party) that brought in millions of learners. Monthly users reached 153.1M, up 13%.

$309M

Subscriptions do the heavy lifting

Revenue rose 14% to $309M. Subscriptions were $270M of it, up 18%, while advertising was flat and in-app purchases fell 38%. Bookings — cash committed by customers — rose 12% to $316M, and paid subscribers hit 13.1M.

$27M

Profit shrank as spending rose

Net income fell to $27M from $292M a year ago — but that year-ago figure included a one-time $223M tax benefit. The real story is cost: operating expenses rose 24% to $201M, including $49M of stock pay, so operating profit fell to $24M. The company's Adjusted EBITDA was $79M, a 25.5% margin.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter (Q3 2025) included a one-time $222.7M income-tax benefit from releasing a valuation allowance on deferred tax assets — that is why net income then was $292M and EPS was $5.95. This quarter's $27M profit has no such boost.
  • Research and development includes a $1.6M impairment of capitalized software, and stock-based compensation across the company was $49M in the quarter — a real cost to shareholders even though no cash left the building.

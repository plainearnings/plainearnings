Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-56%

Profit is funding the pivot

Operating income fell from $1,624M to $718M even though gross profit rose — Tesla chose to pour the difference into AI, robotics and stock-based pay.

+33%

Services is the quiet growth story

Services and other revenue hit a record $4,605M with record gross profit — charging, servicing and FSD subscriptions now bring in more than the entire energy business.

$113M

Regulatory credits are drying up

Credit sales fell from $417M a year ago to $113M — money rival carmakers pay Tesla for clean-air credits is disappearing, and it used to be almost pure profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based pay was $1,165M (vs $663M a year ago), swollen by the 2025 CEO Performance Award — a non-cash cost sitting inside operating expenses. Excluding it and mark-to-market swings, non-GAAP net income was $1,697M ($0.48 a share).
  • Other income includes paper swings: a $142M unrealized loss on Tesla's SpaceX stake and a $288M unrealized gain on digital assets. Neither involved cash.

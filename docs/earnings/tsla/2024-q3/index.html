Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

462,890

Deliveries bounced back

Tesla delivered 462,890 vehicles, up 6% YoY, and produced 469,796 — but it had cut prices to get there, so automotive revenue rose only 2% to $20.0B.

+75%

Energy is quietly surging

Energy storage deployments jumped 75% YoY to 6.9 GWh, energy revenue rose 52% to $2.4B, and the energy business earned a record 30.5% gross margin.

+54%

Cost cuts widened margins

Operating income rose 54% YoY to $2.7B as the gross margin widened to 19.8% from 17.9% a year ago — cost reductions per car more than offset the price cuts.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Self-driving revenue recognised early: Tesla recognised $326M of Full Self-Driving revenue in the quarter for Cybertruck and features such as Actually Smart Summon — money customers had paid earlier, booked as revenue when the features shipped.

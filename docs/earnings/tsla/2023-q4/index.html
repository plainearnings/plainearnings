Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3%

Revenue growth nearly stalled

Revenue rose just 3% YoY to $25.2B — automotive revenue grew only 1% as repeated price cuts offset record deliveries of 484,507 vehicles.

$5.9B

A tax bookkeeping gain did the profit

Net income of $7,943M includes a $5.9B one-time, non-cash tax benefit from releasing a valuation allowance on deferred tax assets. Operating income itself fell 47% YoY to $2,064M.

17.6%

Car margins kept thinning

Gross margin was 17.6%, down from 23.8% a year ago, as price cuts outran cost reductions — and Tesla warned 2024 volume growth could be notably lower while it launches its next-generation vehicle.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • One-time non-cash tax benefit of $5.9B — Tesla released the valuation allowance it had held against its deferred tax assets, judging future profits now make those assets usable. It turned $2,191M of pre-tax profit into $7,943M of net income (the total tax line was a $5,752M benefit); it is not cash and not a repeat item.

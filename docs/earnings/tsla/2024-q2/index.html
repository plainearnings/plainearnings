Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

9.4 GWh

Energy storage hit a record

Energy generation and storage revenue doubled to $3,014M as Tesla deployed a record 9.4 GWh of storage in the quarter, up 158% from a year ago on higher Megapack deployments.

-7%

Car revenue fell despite record credits

Automotive revenue fell 7% to $19,878M as lower average selling prices and 5% fewer deliveries outweighed a record $890M in regulatory credits, up from $282M a year ago.

$622M

Restructuring charge cut into profit

A $622M restructuring and other charge helped push operating income down 33% to $1,605M and narrowed the operating margin to 6.3% from 9.6% a year ago; $583M of the charge was employee termination expenses.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charge of $622M in Q2 2024, including $583M of employee termination expenses from restructuring actions initiated and substantially completed in the quarter to reduce costs and improve efficiency (Form 10-Q Note 13) — there was no such charge a year ago.

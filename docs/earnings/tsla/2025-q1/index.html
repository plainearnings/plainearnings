Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-13%

Deliveries fell

Tesla delivered 336,681 cars, down 13% YoY, after the Model Y update shut production lines at all four vehicle factories for several weeks — and lower prices cut automotive revenue 20%.

2.1%

Margins squeezed hard

Operating income fell 66% to $399M — a 2.1% operating margin — as lower prices and lost production overwhelmed cheaper costs per car and higher regulatory-credit sales.

+67%

Energy is quietly surging

Energy generation and storage revenue jumped 67% to $2.7B on record storage deployments of 10.4 GWh, and services revenue rose 15% — the non-car business kept growing through the car slump.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-57%

Operating profit is thinning

Operating income fell to $398M — a 1.4% margin — even as revenue hit a record. Price cuts and a 49% jump in R&D spending overwhelmed the delivery growth.

+142%

Capex went vertical

Spending on AI compute, factories and equipment hit $5.8B in one quarter, pushing free cash flow to -$1.1B — the growth bill is arriving before the growth pays.

$1.0B

A paper gain did heavy lifting

An unrealized $1,005M gain on Tesla's SpaceX stake sits inside other income. Without it, pre-tax profit would have been roughly $324M instead of $1,329M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1,005M unrealized (paper) gain on Tesla's SpaceX equity stake is inside the $590M of 'other income, net' in the release — no cash changed hands. A $112M unrealized loss on digital assets partly offset it.
  • Stock-based compensation was $1,151M (vs $635M a year ago), swollen by the 2025 CEO Performance Award — a non-cash cost inside operating expenses. Non-GAAP net income was $1,153M ($0.33 a share) vs GAAP $1,114M ($0.32).
  • The tax line includes a one-off benefit from releasing valuation allowances on California deferred tax assets, which is why the effective tax rate was only about 15%.

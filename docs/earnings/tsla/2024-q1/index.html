Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-56%

Car price cuts crushed operating profit

Operating income fell to $1,171M from $2,664M a year earlier. Lower average selling prices, fewer deliveries and the Cybertruck ramp outweighed cheaper raw materials, and overall gross margin narrowed to 17.4% from 19.3%.

+140%

Energy became a real profit engine

Energy generation and storage gross profit jumped to $403M from $168M, lifting its gross margin to 24.6% from 11.0%, helped by Megapack and IRA manufacturing credits — on record storage deployments of 4,053 MWh.

+49%

Spending on the future jumped while sales fell

Research and development rose to $1,151M from $771M on AI, battery-cell and other new programs, and selling, general and administrative costs rose 28% to $1,374M, so total operating expenses climbed 37% even as revenue fell 9%.

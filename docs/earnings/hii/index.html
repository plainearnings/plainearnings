Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$32.4B

A mountain of new orders

New contract awards of $32.4 billion in one quarter — including Block VI Virginia-class and Build II Columbia-class submarine contracts — have secured about 90% of expected shipbuilding revenue through 2030.

+18%

Newport News is carrying growth

Newport News Shipbuilding revenue rose 18% to $1.9 billion on submarine and aircraft-carrier work, and its segment profit jumped 65% on contract adjustments and incentives in the Virginia-class program.

$429M

Cash flow caught up

Free cash flow was $429 million, versus just $16 million a year ago, as operating cash flow more than quadrupled to $520 million. Profit growth is finally showing up as cash.

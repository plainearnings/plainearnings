Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-5%

Casino softness drove the dip

Casino revenue fell to $2,370M from $2,506M a year ago and total revenue slipped 3% to $3,216M, while every non-gaming line — rooms, food and beverage, mall and conventions — grew.

$191M

Sands China profit fell 30%

Sands China revenue rose 1.4% to $1.93B, but its net income dropped 29.8% to $191M from $272M, the main drag on the group result.

$304M

What LVS shareholders kept

Consolidated net income was $352M; after $48M attributable to noncontrolling interests (mainly Sands China's minority holders), $304M belonged to Las Vegas Sands shareholders, or $0.48 per diluted share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Loss on disposal or impairment of assets was only $4M this quarter, versus $68M a year ago.
  • Net income includes $48M attributable to noncontrolling interests; net income attributable to Las Vegas Sands Corp. was $304M.

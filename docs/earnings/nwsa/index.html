Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Three engines grew

Dow Jones rose 8%, Digital Real Estate 10% and Book Publishing 9% — only News Media fell, down 1%.

$403M

Segment EBITDA up 19%

Total Segment EBITDA — the company's segment profit measure — rose to $403M from $340M.

+43%

Net income jumped

Total net income rose 43% to $215M, helped by higher segment profit and the REA India divestiture gain in Other, net.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Impairment and restructuring charges were $20M.
  • Other, net of $38M included a gain related to the divestiture of REA India's remaining business.

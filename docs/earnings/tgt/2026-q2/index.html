Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.8%

Shoppers came back

Comparable sales rose 3.8% on 3.6% more traffic, with growth in all six core categories. Digital comparable sales rose 8.7%, led by same-day delivery up more than 25%.

$994M

Refunds flatter the margin

Gross margin was 33.7% vs 29.0% a year ago — but $994M of one-off tariff refunds sit inside it. Without them the margin was about 29.9%, still an improvement.

$4.11

Profit doubled — honestly, mostly the refund

EPS doubled to $4.11 from $2.05. Strip out the $1.65 refund benefit and EPS still rose 20%, which is the cleaner read on the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $994M of IEEPA tariff refunds received in the quarter were booked as a reduction of cost of sales, adding $752M to net earnings and $1.65 to EPS. Excluding the refunds, EPS rose 20% YoY and gross margin was about 29.9% instead of the reported 33.7%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$467M

High-value components led

High-Value Product Components sales rose 20% to $467M — 53% of all sales — as drug makers bought more premium stoppers, seals and plungers for injectable medicines.

30.6%

Margins jumped

Operating margin before the one-off SmartDose credit was 24.8%, up from a year ago on a richer product mix — high-value components are simply more profitable. As printed, including the gain, the margin was 30.6%; excluding one-offs, adjusted operating margin also improved year over year.

$9.38–9.48

Guidance raised

Full-year 2026 guidance went up: net sales of $3.41–3.42B and adjusted-diluted EPS of $9.38 to $9.48, up from $8.85 to $9.05 previously.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $55.7M pre-tax gain on the sale of the SmartDose 3.5mL On-Body Delivery System to AbbVie sits in other operating income (a $54.0M net benefit after related M&A costs, with about $22M of one-time tax on the gain) — it is the main reason GAAP EPS of $3.02 is above adjusted EPS of $2.66.
  • Restructuring and other charges were $6.3M pre-tax in the quarter under West's restructuring and legal-structure programs.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

8.41%

The card engine earns a wide spread

Net interest margin — what the bank earns on its loans minus what it pays for funds — rose 40 basis points from the second quarter to 8.41%. Period-end loans reached $462.5B, including $276.4B of credit card loans, now including Discover's portfolio.

$6.69

Deal costs hide the run-rate

Adjusted EPS was $6.69 versus $5.61 GAAP. The gap is deal items: $483M of acquisition amortization, $338M of Discover integration costs and $47M of Brex integration costs, all pre-tax. Management says the Discover integration is progressing well and synergy delivery is on track.

$2.9B

Credit costs eased

The provision for credit losses fell to $2.9B from $3.0B in the second quarter: $3.1B of loans were charged off as uncollectible, partly offset by releasing $225M of reserves set aside earlier. Deposits ended the quarter at $480.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusting items totaled $868M pre-tax: $483M of acquisition-related amortization, $338M of Discover integration expenses and $47M of Brex integration expenses (Brex, a payments company, was acquired on April 7, 2026). Excluding them, net income per share was $6.69 instead of $5.61.

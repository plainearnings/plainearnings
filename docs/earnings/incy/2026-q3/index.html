Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Sales growth is broadening

Jakafi grew just 4% to $827M, but Opzelura jumped 36% to $255M and hematology/oncology products 38% to $235M — the business beyond Jakafi is doing the growing.

$1.23B

One charge explains the loss

GAAP R&D of $1.79B includes a one-time $1.23B in-process R&D charge for buying Vega Therapeutics. Excluding it, R&D rose 11% and the quarter was solidly profitable before the charge.

$5.21–5.30B

Sales guidance raised

Incyte lifted its full-year 2026 net sales outlook to $5.21–5.30B, raising the ranges for Opzelura and for hematology/oncology products.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1.23B one-time in-process research & development charge for the acquisition of Vega Therapeutics sits inside R&D — it is a purchase price for research, not a quarterly running cost. GAAP net loss was $785M; even the company's non-GAAP figures show a $731M loss because the charge is only partly adjusted out.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

27.8%

Margins keep expanding

Operating margin rose 40 basis points to 27.8% — price increases beat rising raw-material costs, and ITW's enterprise initiatives added about 130 basis points.

~$40M

A tornado dented the top line

Late-July tornado damage at two Welding plants cost an estimated $40M of revenue; business-interruption insurance covered most of the lost profit.

$11.50

Guidance raised again

Full-year GAAP EPS guidance was narrowed to $11.45–$11.55 (midpoint $11.50, +10%), and the dividend was raised for the 63rd consecutive year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Tornado damage to two Welding facilities in late July reduced revenue by an estimated $40M; the lost operating income was largely offset by business interruption insurance.

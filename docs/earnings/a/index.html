Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8.1%

Growth with margin attached

Revenue rose 8.1% to $1.88B while GAAP operating margin widened 290 basis points to 23.6% — costs were essentially flat, so growth dropped through to profit.

$1.62

Tariff refunds flattered non-GAAP

Non-GAAP EPS of $1.62 included about $0.06 (~$17M) of net tariff-refund benefit, and non-GAAP operating margin got a ~110bp lift. GAAP EPS was $1.28, up 8%.

Raised

Full-year guidance lifted

Agilent raised FY26 revenue guidance to $7.49–7.51B (up 7.8–8.1%) and non-GAAP EPS to $6.18–6.21, citing continued operational momentum.

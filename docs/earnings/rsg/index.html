Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8.2%

Pricing did the heavy lifting

Revenue reached $4.56B. Core price increases added 5.3% and acquisitions 1.3%, while volumes slightly declined.

+22%

Environmental Solutions rebounded

Revenue rose to $529M from $433M and its adjusted profit margin improved to 25.4% from 21.0% a year ago.

$5B

Guidance up, new buyback approved

Republic raised its full-year revenue and profit guidance and the board approved a new $5B share-buyback plan running 2027–2029.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges of $4M in the quarter ($10M year to date) for designing a new accounts-receivable system.

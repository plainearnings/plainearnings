Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+138%

A record quarter, driven by product sales

Revenue hit $1,236M, with product revenue up 179% to $1,074M as data-center and other large customers bought far more fuel-cell systems. Bloom also joined the S&P 500 index in the quarter.

33.8%

Margins expanded as volume surged

Gross margin rose to 33.8% from 29.2% a year ago, and operating margin reached 18.2% — scale is starting to turn a fast-growing top line into real operating profit.

$4.4–4.6B

Full-year guidance raised again

Bloom raised its 2026 revenue outlook to $4.4–$4.6B — about 122% growth at the midpoint — its third raise of the year, citing continued customer demand for on-site power.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include $60M of stock-based compensation. Excluding it and small restructuring items, non-GAAP operating income was $286M and non-GAAP EPS was $0.94 (GAAP EPS $0.78).
  • Net profit was $249M; after a $1M loss attributable to noncontrolling interests, net income attributable to common stockholders was $250M — the figure EPS is based on.

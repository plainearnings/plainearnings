Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

A record quarter, half of it bought

Sales hit a record $1,614M, up 22%. Organic growth — excluding acquisitions and currency — was 11.3%, with Connected Frontline up 15% organically and Asset Visibility & Automation up 7%.

45.7%

Memory chips squeezed margins

Gross margin fell to 45.7% from 48.0% a year ago, mainly because memory components cost more and the product mix shifted, partly offset by pricing actions and currency.

$4.95

Profit grew faster than sales

Non-GAAP EPS rose 28% to $4.95 and GAAP EPS rose to $3.43 from $1.97. Zebra raised its full-year outlook and has repurchased over $640M of shares in the first nine months.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The Elo Touch acquisition lifted reported growth well above the 11.3% organic rate and pushed intangible amortization up to $37M from $25M a year ago; acquisition and integration costs themselves were just $1M this quarter (vs $10M a year ago).
  • Exit and restructuring costs printed at $0 in the quarter, so no line is shown for them.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8.48B

Skyrizi + Rinvoq carry the company

The two successor drugs brought in $8.48B — nearly half of all revenue — growing about 23–24% each, more than replacing Humira's decline.

$2.37

GAAP EPS more than doubled

A year ago, $2.68B of acquired IPR&D and milestone charges crushed GAAP profit to $0.10 a share. This year's charge was $216M, so GAAP EPS jumped to $2.37.

+5.2%

Dividend raised for 2027

AbbVie raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $13.95–13.99 and announced a 5.2% dividend increase starting with the February 2027 payment.

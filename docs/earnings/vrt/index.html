Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+61%

The AI buildout, in one number

Sales jumped 61% to $4.31B — 55% of it organic — and organic orders more than doubled. Every region grew at least 23%.

$765M

Profit nearly doubled

Net income rose 92% to about $765M and GAAP EPS to $1.95, as operating margin expanded on the surge in volume. Operating cash flow was $794M.

$14.65B

Guidance raised again

Vertiv lifted full-year 2026 sales guidance by $650M at the midpoint, to about $14.65B — management sees the data-center buildout accelerating, not pausing.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted (non-GAAP) operating profit was $1,087M — a 25.2% margin — vs $984M GAAP; the gap is mostly the $65M of acquisition-intangible amortization. Adjusted EPS was $2.21 vs $1.95 GAAP.

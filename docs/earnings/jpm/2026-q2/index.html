Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$4.6B

The Visa gain is the headline

A $4.6B net gain on Visa shares in Corporate, plus $1.0B of equity-investment gains, sits inside this quarter's revenue. Excluding significant items, net income was $16.9B ($6.14 a share) instead of $21.2B ($7.70).

29%

Returns no rival matches

With the one-offs included, return on tangible common equity hit 29% — profitability far beyond what other big banks produce.

-12%

Credit costs fell

The provision for credit losses fell 12% from a year ago to $2.5B — $2.4B of actual charge-offs plus a small $149M reserve build — even as lending kept growing.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $4.6B net gain related to Visa shares (in Corporate) and $1.0B of gains on certain equity investments ($763M of it in Corporate) are inside noninterest revenue. Excluding these significant items, net income was $16.9B and EPS $6.14 instead of $21.2B and $7.70.

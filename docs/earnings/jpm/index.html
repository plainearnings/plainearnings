JPMorgan reports revenue two ways: $50.2B reported (GAAP), used here, or $50.9B on a 'managed' basis that grosses up tax-exempt income. Net income is identical either way.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

Interest income is the engine

Net interest income rose 13% to $27.0B on bigger loan and deposit balances — average loans up 10% YoY. It is now 54% of all revenue.

$15.8B

One item hides the strength

Excluding $658M of investment securities losses in Corporate, net income was $15.8B and EPS $5.75 instead of $5.57 — a return on tangible equity of 21% rather than 20%.

$2.7B

Credit costs are contained

The bank set aside $2.7B for credit losses — $2.2B of actual charge-offs plus a $539M reserve build — down from $3.4B a year ago, even as lending grew.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $658M of net investment securities losses in Corporate — the quarter's flagged 'significant item'. Excluding it, net income was $15.8B ($5.75 per share) instead of $15.3B ($5.57).

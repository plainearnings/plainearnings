JPMorgan reports revenue two ways: $44.9B reported (GAAP), used here, or $45.7B on a 'managed' basis that grosses up tax-exempt income. Net income is identical either way. The rows reconcile to the $100 total within a cent of rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$7.9B

The year-ago mirage

Revenue fell 11% and net income 17% vs a year ago — but that quarter included a $7.9B net gain on Visa shares, plus a $1.0B donation of Visa shares inside its costs. Against that flattered comparison, the underlying business grew.

+8%

Banking and markets stayed hot

Investment banking fees rose 8% to $2.5B, asset management fees 12% to $4.8B, and trading (principal transactions) brought in $7.1B — the fee engine more than replaced the missing Visa gain.

21%

Profitability stayed elite

Return on tangible common equity was 21%, and the bank kept $15.0B — about 33 cents of every reported revenue dollar — while setting aside $2.8B for possible loan losses.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

23%

Returns no rival matches

Return on tangible common equity hit 23% (19% on common equity) — profitability far beyond what other big banks produce, on $16.5B of net income.

+31%

Dealmaking woke up

Investment banking fees rose 31% YoY to $2.9B — JPMorgan ranked #1 globally with a 9.8% wallet share — and Markets revenue rose 20%.

-24%

Credit costs fell

The provision for credit losses fell 24% from a year ago to $2.5B — $2.3B of actual charge-offs plus a small $191M reserve build — even as lending kept growing.

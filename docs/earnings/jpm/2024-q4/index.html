JPMorgan reports revenue two ways: $42.8B reported (GAAP), used here, or $43.7B on a 'managed' basis that grosses up tax-exempt income. Net income is identical either way. The rows reconcile to the $100 total within a cent of rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Markets and deal fees powered the quarter

Markets revenue rose 21% to $7.0B — equities up 22%, fixed income up 20% — and investment banking fees jumped 48%, more than covering the 3% dip in net interest income.

21%

Elite profitability to close the year

Return on tangible common equity was 21%, and the bank kept $14.0B — about 33 cents of every reported revenue dollar — capping a record $58.5B of profit for 2024 as a whole.

$2.6B

Credit costs stayed contained

The provision for credit losses was $2.6B — $2.4B of loans charged off, mostly in cards, plus a small net reserve build — down from $2.8B a year ago even as card balances grew.

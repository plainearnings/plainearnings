JPMorgan reports revenue two ways: $46.4B reported (GAAP), used here, or $47.1B on a 'managed' basis that grosses up tax-exempt income. Net income is identical either way. The rows reconcile to the $100 total within a cent of rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Fees and trading did the lifting

Noninterest revenue rose 17% YoY to $22.5B — investment banking fees up 17% to $2.6B and asset management fees up 14% — while net interest income grew 2% to $24.0B on the reported basis.

$14.4B

Profit rose 12%

Net income rose 12% YoY to $14.4B and EPS rose 16% to $5.07 — the bank kept 31 cents of profit out of every revenue dollar.

$810M

Reserves built modestly

Credit costs were $3.4B: $2.6B of loans were charged off and $810M was added to reserves, mostly in Consumer — a build, but smaller than the $1.0B added in the same quarter a year ago.

JPMorgan reports revenue two ways: $42.7B reported (GAAP), used here, or $43.3B on a 'managed' basis that grosses up tax-exempt income. Net income is identical either way. The rows reconcile to the $100 total within a cent of rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Deals and trading carried the quarter

Investment banking fees jumped 31% YoY to $2.2B — JPMorgan ranked #1 globally with a 9.1% wallet share — and Markets revenue rose 8%, lifting noninterest revenue 12%.

$3.1B

Credit costs more than doubled

The provision for credit losses was $3.1B — $2.1B of loans charged off, mostly cards, plus a $1.0B net reserve build — up 125% from a year ago, and the main reason profit dipped despite higher revenue.

19%

Still an elite return on capital

Return on tangible common equity was 19%, the bank kept $12.9B — about 30 cents of every reported revenue dollar — and its CET1 capital ratio stood at 15.3%.

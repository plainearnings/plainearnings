Q4 2025 · Quarter ended 2025-12-31

JPMorgan earned $13.0B in the quarter — revenue rose 7% to $45.8B, but credit costs jumped 77% to $4.7B as the bank built a $2.2B reserve for the Apple card portfolio it has agreed to take over, pulling profit down 7% from a year ago.

JPMorgan reports revenue two ways: $45.8B reported (GAAP), used here, or $46.8B on a 'managed' basis that grosses up tax-exempt income. Net income is identical either way.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

18%

Returns still lead the industry

Return on tangible common equity was 18% (15% on common equity) — profitability far beyond what other big banks produce, on $13.0B of net income.

+17%

Markets had a strong quarter

Markets revenue rose 17% YoY to $8.2B and asset management fees rose 17%, helping total revenue grow 7% even as card income fell.

+77%

Credit costs spiked — on purpose

The provision for credit losses jumped 77% YoY to $4.7B, but $2.2B of it was a reserve built ahead of taking over the Apple credit card portfolio — setting money aside early, not loans going bad today. Actual net charge-offs were $2.5B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $4.7B provision for credit losses includes a net reserve build of $2.1B, reflecting a $2.2B reserve established for the forward purchase commitment of the Apple credit card portfolio — money set aside before the portfolio arrives, not losses on loans already made.

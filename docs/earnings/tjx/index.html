Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

Growth everywhere except the flagship

Consolidated comparable sales rose 4%, above plan — but Marmaxx (T.J. Maxx/Marshalls U.S.) managed only +1%, while HomeGoods, Canada and International each rose 6–7%.

$0.14

Refunds padded the profit beat

EPS of $1.36 includes a $0.14 net benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds. The adjusted figure, $1.22, was still up 11% — and TJX raised its full-year outlook on the strength of the quarter.

7,500

A bigger long-term map

TJX lifted its long-term store target to 7,500 stores and plans to speed openings to 4% a year from FY28 — it ended the quarter with 5,285 stores.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Tariff refunds gave TJX a $219M net pretax benefit this quarter ($0.14 per share): refunds received, minus $112M of extra year-end bonus accruals the windfall triggered. Excluding it, adjusted EPS was $1.22 (up 11%) and adjusted pretax margin was 11.9% instead of 13.3%. More refunds are expected in Q3, in uncertain amounts.

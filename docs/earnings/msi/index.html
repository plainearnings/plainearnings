Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Public safety keeps spending

Sales rose 12% in both North America and international markets, with acquisition contributions of $211M; management raised full-year guidance to about $13.05B of revenue.

$4.69

The GAAP dip is in the adjustments

Non-GAAP EPS was $4.69 vs $3.06 GAAP. The $114M of other charges and $97M of acquisition amortization explain most of the gap between the two profit stories.

41%

Services are the ballast

Services brought in 41% of sales and grew 9% — multi-year contracts with governments that keep paying regardless of hardware cycles.

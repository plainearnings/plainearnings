Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Operating leverage did the work

Operating income rose 22% to $362M — a 27% margin — on just 6% revenue growth, because costs and operating expenses in total rose only about 1%.

$100M+

Airo is becoming real

The Airo AI website builder's annualized bookings run rate exceeded $100M, more than doubling in one quarter, and GoDaddy launched agent-friendly APIs for hosting, email and commerce.

20.5M

The customer base inched up

Total customers reached 20.5M, up 40 thousand in the quarter — growth now comes mainly from selling more to each customer (Applications & Commerce) rather than adding users.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Rates, not volume, drive growth

Electric revenue rose 9% to $4,395M. Under New York's rate plans, Con Edison earns on its rate base — the regulated value of grid assets — so approved rate increases for grid investment, not extra electricity use, did most of the work. Gas revenue rose 5% to $455M.

$766M

Profit rose 11%

Net income rose from $688M to $766M and diluted EPS from $1.90 to $2.06. Operating income rose 21% to $1,170M as operations and maintenance costs actually fell 2%, more than offsetting a 7% rise in property and other non-income taxes to $1,017M.

$1.2B

A quarter of revenue is pass-through

Purchased power, fuel and gas bought for resale cost $1,196M — about $24 of every $100 of revenue. Con Edison recovers these costs from customers dollar for dollar, so they inflate the top line without adding profit; the regulated delivery business underneath is what earns the $766M.

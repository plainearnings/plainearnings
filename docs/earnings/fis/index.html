Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.7%

Real growth is 3.7%, not 27%

The 27% reported jump mostly reflects adding Total Issuing Solutions. On a pro-forma basis — as if FIS had owned it all along — revenue grew 3.7% and adjusted EBITDA 5.2%.

$1.61

Adjusted EPS vs $0.69 GAAP

Adjusted net earnings were $831M ($1.61 a share) versus $356M GAAP ($0.69). The gap is mostly $320M of purchase-accounting amortization and $147M of acquisition and integration costs.

$20.8B

Deleveraging comes first

Debt stood at $20.8B after the acquisition. FIS has paused buybacks and tuck-in deals to pay debt down toward its ~2.8x leverage target, while keeping the dividend.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • FIS closed the acquisition of Total Issuing Solutions (the former Global Payments issuer business) in 2026; prior-year figures do not include it, which is why reported growth rates look so large.
  • On October 20, 2026, FIS sold a non-core prepaid business for gross proceeds of over $100M as part of its strategic portfolio review.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

Non-residential carried growth

US non-residential revenue rose 8% on large capital projects, while residential — about half of sales — returned to growth at +2% despite weak housing activity.

31.0%

Gross margin eased a touch

Gross margin was 31.0%, down 20 basis points, because last year was flattered by the timing of supplier price increases. Operating leverage still lifted reported operating margin to 10.2%.

5 deals

Buying growth, and guiding higher

Ferguson closed five acquisitions in the quarter and agreed to buy FloWorks; full-year calendar 2026 guidance was raised to mid-single-digit sales growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted figures exclude $37M of amortization of acquired intangibles and $2M of corporate restructuring costs; adjusted operating profit was $932M and adjusted EPS $3.39.
  • Ferguson changed its fiscal year-end from July 31 to December 31, so this is the second quarter of calendar 2026 — results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were scheduled for November 9, 2026, after this report's cutoff.

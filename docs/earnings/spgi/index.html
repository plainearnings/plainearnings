Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+20%

Indices is the growth engine

Indices revenue rose 20% to $554M as demand for benchmarks and the products built on them kept climbing — it drove a large share of the quarter's $235M revenue increase.

$200M

A one-off gain flatters profit

A $200M gain on a disposition in Energy lifted GAAP operating profit 26% to $1,956M. On an adjusted basis, operating profit grew 8% — still solid, but not the headline jump.

$4.45

A leaner company after the spin-off

With Mobility spun off on July 1 and Capital IQ now under strategic review, S&P Global is narrowing itself around ratings, benchmarks and data — continuing EPS of $4.45 rose 26% on profit growth and buybacks.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $200M gain on a disposition in the Energy segment, which lifted GAAP operating profit; adjusted results exclude it.
  • Mobility was spun off on July 1, 2026 and is treated as discontinued operations (a $58M loss in the quarter); S&P Global also announced a strategic review of Capital IQ and associated data businesses within Market Intelligence.

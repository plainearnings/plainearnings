Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

Growth held steady

Revenue rose 13% to $410M, and ARR — the annualized value of subscription contracts — rose 12% to $1.938B, with $37M of net new ARR. Existing customers expanded their spending at a 109% net retention rate.

80%

High margins, one soft spot

Gross margin was 80%. Licenses and subscriptions are very profitable to deliver; professional services is not — it brought in $20M and cost $39M to deliver, a loss-making line that grew 82%.

$32M

Back to GAAP profit

Operating income was $32M, vs a $20M loss a year ago, because revenue grew while total operating expenses fell 6% to $298M. Net income was $36M. On the company's non-GAAP basis (leaving out $45M of stock pay and other items), operating income was $89M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses include $45.0M of stock-based compensation and $5.0M of restructuring costs in the quarter — the main reasons GAAP operating income ($32M) is well below the company's non-GAAP figure ($89M).
  • General and administrative costs include a $0.8M increase in the fair value of contingent consideration owed on past acquisitions.

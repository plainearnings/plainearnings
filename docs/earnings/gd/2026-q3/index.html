Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

6.8x

Orders dwarfed deliveries

The defense segments booked 8.5 dollars of orders for every dollar of revenue — a huge order intake, led by submarines — taking total backlog to $220B, including $98.8B in the defense segments' funded backlog growth story.

+21%

Marine Systems is the growth engine

Marine Systems revenue rose 21% to $4.96B and its operating earnings rose 34% as Columbia- and Virginia-class submarine work ramped.

139%

Cash conversion was strong

Operating cash flow was $1.7B — 139% of net earnings — with free cash flow of $1.2B after $481M of capital spending.

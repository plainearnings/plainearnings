Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.9%

Growth in both businesses

Diagnostics revenue rose 6.5% to $2,949M (4.4% organic) and Biopharma Laboratory Services rose 8.5% to $867M, as specialty testing and new health-system and pharma partnerships kept volumes climbing.

$4.78

Adjusted EPS rose 14.5%

Adjusted EPS of $4.78 excludes $79M of intangible amortization and $16M of restructuring charges that sit in the GAAP figure of $3.22. Adjusted operating margin widened to 15.0% from 14.4%.

$18.30–$18.70

Guidance raised

Labcorp lifted its full-year outlook: revenue growth of 5.9%–6.7% and adjusted EPS of $18.30–$18.70. It also announced or closed four acquisitions in the quarter, each expected to add to adjusted EPS in year one.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges were $16M in the quarter (vs $6M a year ago), and GAAP results carry $79M of amortization of intangibles from past deals — both are excluded from Labcorp's adjusted EPS of $4.78.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Software & Control led growth

Software & Control sales rose 15% to $754M and Intelligent Devices rose 5% to $1.14B, while Lifecycle Services fell 9% on divestitures.

$103M

Tariff refunds cut both ways

Rockwell received $103M of IEEPA tariff refunds, which lowered cost of sales, but also accrued $85M to refund customers for tariff price increases, which lowered sales. The net effect added $0.11 to EPS.

$346M

Net income was $346M

Net income attributable to Rockwell was $346M, or $3.11 per share, up from $138M a year earlier when results included a $224M goodwill and intangible impairment.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $103M of IEEPA tariff refunds (plus $5M of related interest) reduced cost of sales in the quarter.
  • An $85M accrual for the customer tariff refund program reduced sales in the quarter.
  • A $50M contribution to the Rockwell Automation Charitable Corporation and $25M of restructuring charges were recorded in the quarter.

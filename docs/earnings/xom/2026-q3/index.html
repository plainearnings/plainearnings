Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$7.2B

Refining carried the quarter

Energy Products earnings (GAAP) rose to $7.2B from $1.8B a year ago — $9.1B adjusted — as refining margins surged, year-to-date fuels utilization hit a record excluding Middle East impacts, and crude units at the U.S. Gulf Coast refineries ran at 100%.

+104%

Profit more than doubled

Net income rose to $15.4B from $7.5B a year ago on sales up 33% to $110.5B. Upstream earned $7.1B on production of 4,496 thousand barrels of oil-equivalent a day, and both Guyana and Kearl passed 1 billion barrels produced.

$1.07

Dividend up for a 44th year

ExxonMobil declared a fourth-quarter dividend of $1.07 a share, payable December 10, 2026 — its 44th straight annual increase. It returned $9.4B to shareholders in the quarter ($4.3B of dividends and $5.1B of buybacks) and announced $20B of buybacks for 2027.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $675M gain on the sale of assets in U.S. Upstream, partly offset by $226M of other identified items, for a net $449M gain included in GAAP earnings.
  • Unfavorable timing effects of $2,541M — mainly unsettled derivatives marked to end-of-quarter prices before the matching physical sales are booked — cut GAAP earnings. Excluding timing effects and identified items, adjusted earnings were $17,505M, or $4.23 a share.

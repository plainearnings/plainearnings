Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.4B

Refining rescued the quarter

Energy Products earnings jumped to $3.4B from $0.4B a year ago as refining margins surged — without it, the quarter would have looked much worse, since Upstream earnings nearly halved on lower crude prices.

-46%

Upstream earnings nearly halved

Upstream earned $3.5B, down from $6.5B a year ago on lower crude prices, even as production hit a quarterly record of nearly 5.0 million barrels a day, with Guyana passing 700,000 gross barrels a day and advantaged assets now 59% of production.

$1.03

Dividend up for a 43rd year

ExxonMobil declared a first-quarter dividend of $1.03 a share, payable March 10, 2026 — its 43rd straight year of raising the annual dividend. It returned $9.5B to shareholders in the quarter ($4.4B of dividends and $5.1B of buybacks).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Impairments of $1,700M — including a $640M charge to optimize materials and supply inventory — cut GAAP earnings, partly offset by a $720M gain on the sale of assets in non-U.S. Energy Products.
  • Tax-related items added $288M and restructuring charges cost $64M. Total identified items were a net $755M drag; excluding them, earnings were $7,256M, or $1.71 a share.

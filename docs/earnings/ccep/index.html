Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4.4%

Steady growth in the first half

Revenue rose 4.4% to €10.7B ($12.3B) for the six months. Volume grew 2.2% once adjusted for extra selling days, and revenue per case rose 0.4% — growth came from selling more drinks, not from big price rises. Full-year guidance was reaffirmed.

+5.9%

Europe carried it; Asia was flat in euros

Europe's revenue rose 5.9% to €7.9B. The APS region (Australia, Pacific and Southeast Asia) rose only 0.4% in euros to €2.8B, but grew 5.4% before currency effects — the euro's strength hid the local growth.

€991M

Profit up, dividend set

Profit after tax was €991M ($1.1B), up 5.8%, and diluted earnings per share rose 9.1% to €2.17. The company declared an interim dividend of €0.82 per share and generated €435M of comparable free cash flow in the half.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported costs include €44M of restructuring charges for business transformation. On the company's 'comparable' basis — leaving out such items — operating profit was €1,481M rather than the reported €1,458M.
  • Other income includes a €23M gain on the sale of a property in Germany.

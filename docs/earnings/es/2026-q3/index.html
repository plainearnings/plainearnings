Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$0.92

A reset quarter

GAAP and recurring EPS were both $0.92 with no adjustments this quarter — versus $1.19 of recurring EPS a year ago, before the FERC return cut and the storm-cost disallowance.

$182M

Transmission is the anchor

Electric transmission earned $182M and electric distribution $201M, while natural gas lost $43M in its seasonally weak quarter after a Massachusetts rate-design change shifted revenue between quarters.

$4.66

Guidance midpoint raised

Management narrowed 2026 recurring EPS guidance to $4.62–$4.70 and lifted the midpoint to $4.66, keeping the 5–7% long-term growth target through 2030.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Year-to-date (not this quarter) GAAP results carry three large charges: $111M after tax on the Aquarion water sale, $164M for higher expected offshore-wind exit payments, and $44M for refunds after FERC cut the New England transmission return on equity from 10.57% to 9.57%.
  • Connecticut regulators (PURA) disallowed part of CL&P's previously deferred storm costs in a July 29, 2026 decision, which weighed on electric distribution earnings this quarter.

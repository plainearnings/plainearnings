Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+121%

U.S. commercial is exploding

U.S. commercial revenue more than doubled to $397M, and the pipeline points the same way: Palantir closed a record $1.31B of U.S. commercial contract value in the quarter, up 342% YoY, taking remaining deal value to $3.63B, up 199%.

114%

A Rule of 40 score of 114

Revenue grew 63% while adjusted operating income reached $601M — a 51% margin. Added together, that is Palantir's 'Rule of 40' score of 114%, and U.S. revenue overall grew 77% to $883M.

$540M

Cash generation followed

Adjusted free cash flow was $540M — 46% of revenue — and Palantir raised its full-year guidance for U.S. commercial revenue (to over $1.43B) and for free cash flow (to $1.9–2.1B).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes of about $208M sit inside cost of revenue and operating expenses; excluding them, adjusted operating income was $601M (a 51% margin) instead of $393M, and adjusted EPS was $0.21 vs $0.18 GAAP.

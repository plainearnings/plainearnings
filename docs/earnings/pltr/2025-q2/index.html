Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+93%

U.S. business customers nearly doubled

U.S. commercial revenue grew 93% YoY to $306M, and Palantir raised its outlook — it now expects full-year 2025 revenue of about $4.15B, with U.S. commercial growth of at least 85%.

$1.0B

First billion-dollar quarter

Total revenue reached $1,004M, up 48% YoY, with U.S. revenue up 68% to $733M — 73% of everything Palantir sells.

57%

Profit, not just growth

Adjusted free cash flow was $569M — 57 cents of every revenue dollar — and GAAP net income attributable to shareholders was $327M, a 33% margin.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+96%

Growth is accelerating, not cooling

Revenue growth hit 96% — extraordinary at this size — with U.S. revenue up 119% and 212 deals of at least $1M closed in the quarter.

158%

The 'Rule of 40' score

Palantir's own scorecard — growth rate plus adjusted operating margin — hit 158%, a level almost no software company reaches; adjusted operating margin was 62%.

$984M

Businesses pass government

U.S. commercial revenue ($984M) topped U.S. government revenue ($953M) for the first time in the company's history — the customer mix has flipped.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based pay cost $319M this quarter and sits inside the GAAP expenses; excluding it, 'adjusted' operating income was $1,427M (a 62% margin) versus $1,085M on a GAAP basis (47%).

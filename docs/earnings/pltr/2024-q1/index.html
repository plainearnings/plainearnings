Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+40%

U.S. business customers surged

U.S. commercial revenue grew 40% YoY to $150M, the U.S. commercial customer count grew 69% to 262, and U.S. commercial remaining deal value grew 74%.

$106M

Sixth straight profitable quarter

GAAP net income was $106M — a 17% margin — with GAAP operating income of $81M, a 13% margin, and GAAP EPS of $0.04, up from $0.01 a year ago.

$149M

Cash generation and a higher outlook

Adjusted free cash flow was $149M — 23 cents of every revenue dollar — with $3.9B of cash and short-term U.S. Treasury securities on hand, a Rule of 40 score of 57%, and Palantir raised full-year revenue guidance to $2.677–$2.689B.

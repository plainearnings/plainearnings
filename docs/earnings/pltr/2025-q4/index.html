Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+137%

U.S. commercial is exploding

U.S. commercial revenue more than doubled to $507M, and the pipeline points the same way: U.S. commercial remaining deal value hit $4.38B, up 145% YoY.

41%

A 41% operating margin

GAAP operating income was $575M — 41 cents of every revenue dollar — because revenue grew 70% while total operating expenses actually fell from a year ago.

$4.26B

Record deal value closed

Palantir closed $4.26B of total contract value in the quarter, up 138% YoY, including 180 deals of at least $1M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation of $196M sits inside cost of revenue and operating expenses; excluding it and related employer payroll taxes, adjusted operating income was $798M (a 57% margin) instead of $575M, and adjusted EPS was $0.25 vs $0.24 GAAP.

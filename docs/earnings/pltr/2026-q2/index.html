Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+149%

U.S. businesses are the growth engine

U.S. commercial revenue hit $764M and Palantir closed a record $2.13B of U.S. commercial contract value in a single quarter — the deal pipeline behind future revenue.

55%

Half of revenue became profit

Net income was $1,066M on $1,935M of revenue — a 55% margin, helped by an income-tax bill of just $15M.

$265M

Stock pay is the hidden cost

Stock-based compensation was $265M — 14% of revenue. Excluding it and related payroll taxes, adjusted operating income was $1,194M vs $912M on the GAAP view.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation of $265M sits inside cost of revenue and operating expenses; excluding it and $17M of related employer payroll taxes, adjusted operating income was $1,194M (a 62% margin) instead of $912M.

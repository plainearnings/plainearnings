Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+70%

U.S. business customers surged

U.S. commercial revenue grew 70% YoY to $131M, the U.S. commercial customer count grew 55% to 221, and U.S. commercial total contract value grew 107%.

$97M

Fifth straight profitable quarter

GAAP net income was $97M in total ($93M attributable to common stockholders), with GAAP operating income of $66M — an 11% margin — and GAAP EPS of $0.04, up from $0.01 a year ago.

$305M

Cash generation was the standout

Adjusted free cash flow was $305M — 50% of revenue — and cash from operations was $301M; Palantir ended the year with $3.7B of cash, cash equivalents and short-term U.S. Treasury securities.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+133%

U.S. commercial is exploding

U.S. commercial revenue more than doubled to $595M, and the pipeline points the same way: U.S. commercial remaining deal value hit $4.92B, up 112% YoY.

46%

A 46% operating margin

GAAP operating income was $754M — 46 cents of every revenue dollar — because revenue grew 85% while running costs grew far more slowly.

$2.41B

Record deal value closed

Palantir closed $2.41B of total contract value in the quarter, up 61% YoY, including 206 deals of at least $1M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation of $202M sits inside cost of revenue and operating expenses; excluding it and related employer payroll taxes, adjusted operating income was $984M (a 60% margin) instead of $754M.

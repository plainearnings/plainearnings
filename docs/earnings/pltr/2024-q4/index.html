Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+64%

U.S. business customers surged

U.S. commercial revenue grew 64% YoY to $214M, and Palantir closed a record $803M of U.S. commercial contract value — up 134% — as its AIP bootcamps kept converting into deals. It then guided 2025 revenue to grow 31%.

$116M

A one-time stock charge masked profit

GAAP operating income was just $11M because the quarter absorbed $116M of accelerated stock pay tied to the founders' market-vesting appreciation rights (SARs), plus related payroll taxes. Excluding the SAR items, operating income was $142M and net income $165M.

63%

Cash generation stayed elite

Adjusted free cash flow was $517M — 63 cents of every revenue dollar — and Palantir ended 2024 with $5.2B of cash and Treasury securities.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • One-time SAR-related expenses: $115.8M of accelerated stock-based compensation plus $15.5M of employer payroll taxes, triggered by the vesting of market-vesting stock appreciation rights (SARs). Palantir's own figures excluding them: net income of $165M and EPS of $0.07.
  • Total stock-based compensation was $282M in the quarter (including the SAR acceleration) — a real cost to shareholders through dilution, even though it is not cash.

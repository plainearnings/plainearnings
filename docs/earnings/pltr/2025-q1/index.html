Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+71%

U.S. business customers surged

U.S. commercial revenue grew 71% YoY to $255M, passing a $1B annual run rate, and Palantir booked its best-ever quarter of U.S. commercial contract value — $810M, up 183%.

55%

America is the engine

U.S. revenue rose 55% to $628M — 71% of everything Palantir sells — as government revenue grew 45% and commercial demand for its AI platform (AIP) accelerated.

42%

Profit, not just growth

Adjusted free cash flow was $370M — 42 cents of every revenue dollar — and GAAP net income was $214M for shareholders, a 24% margin, with $5.4B of cash and Treasury securities on hand.

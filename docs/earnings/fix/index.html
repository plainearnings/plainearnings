Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+54%

Growth most builders never see

Revenue grew 54% year over year — technology customers alone were 61% of revenue, reflecting the data-center construction boom — and gross margin held at 24.8% despite the pace.

$17.8B

Backlog nearly doubled

Backlog reached $17.81B, up from $9.38B a year ago and $14.06B just last quarter — about $3.8B of new work added in three months, most of it same-store rather than acquired.

$1.0B

Cash followed the profit

Operating cash flow was $1.01B in the quarter — more than double net income — as customers paid advances on the swelling project pipeline.

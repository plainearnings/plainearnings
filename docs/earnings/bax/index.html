Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5%

Sales rose to $2.98B

Both main segments grew: Medical Products & Therapies +6% on infusion strength, Healthcare Systems & Technologies +4%.

$253M

A gain from its own debt

With its bonds trading at a discount, Baxter bought debt back early and booked a $253M gain — the main reason net income swung from a $46M loss a year ago to a $294M profit.

$0.57

EPS of $0.57

Adjusted results strip out the debt gain and $95M of intangible amortization among other items; the underlying business grew steadily rather than spectacularly.

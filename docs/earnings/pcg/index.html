Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$0.34

A lower allowed return bit

GAAP EPS fell to $0.34 from $0.37, mainly because regulators lowered PG&E's return on equity for 2026 and last year's tax-method catch-up did not repeat.

2–4%

Cost cuts are on track

Operating and maintenance spending fell to $2,543M from $2,641M — PG&E says it is on track for its 2–4% non-fuel cost reduction target for 2026.

5,000 mi

Burying lines to cut fire risk

PG&E filed a plan to bury 5,000 miles of power lines, which it says cuts wildfire risk on those lines by 98% — the long-term project behind much of its spending.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-core items totaled $156M after tax ($0.07 per share) — mainly $102M of Wildfire Fund contribution amortization and $42M of strategic repositioning costs, partly offset by a $29M bankruptcy and legal benefit. Excluding them, core earnings were $928M ($0.41 per share).

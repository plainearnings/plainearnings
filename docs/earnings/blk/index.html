Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$205B

Clients poured in money

Clients added a net $205B in the quarter and $869B over twelve months, taking assets under management to $15.5 trillion. iShares ETFs alone took in $135B, and annualized organic base-fee growth was 11%.

30.0%

A deal charge flattened the margin

GAAP operating margin was 30.0% — the same as a year ago — because of the $564M contingent-consideration charge. Without deal-related items, the adjusted margin was 47.8%, up from 44.6%.

+14%

Technology keeps growing

Technology services and subscription revenue rose 14% to $588M on Aladdin and Preqin demand, and annual contract value grew 12% — a smaller, steadier business beside the market-driven fees.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $564M noncash increase in the fair value of contingent consideration — money potentially still owed for the GIP and HPS acquisitions, which rises and falls with BlackRock's share price. It was $93M a year ago.
  • Acquisition-related costs excluded from the company's adjusted results also included $276M of intangible amortization, $84M of acquisition-related compensation and $46M of acquisition-related admin costs.
  • Excluding these items, as-adjusted operating income was $3,330M (a 47.8% margin, vs 30.0% GAAP) and adjusted EPS was $15.93, vs $10.57 GAAP.

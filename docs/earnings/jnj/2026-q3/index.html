Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$0.09 vs $2.59

One charge, two stories

GAAP EPS was $0.09 after the talc settlement charge and deal costs; adjusted EPS of $2.59 shows the underlying quarter. Reported net earnings fell 96% to $210M from $5.15B.

+6.0%

The business underneath is accelerating

Operational sales grew 6.0% — Innovative Medicine +7.9% operationally — and management now targets double-digit growth by the end of the decade.

$101.2B

Guidance raised

J&J lifted its 2026 outlook to about $101.2B of sales (+7.4% at the midpoint) and adjusted EPS of $11.10.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A one-time special charge for the proposed talc litigation resolution sits inside the $5,780M of other expense, alongside Firefly Bio acquisition and Sail Biomedicines collaboration costs. Adjusted net earnings of $6,339M exclude these items.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.85B

The order book jumped 73%

Backlog hit a record $1.85B, helped by Rocket Lab's largest single contract to date — $816M from the Space Development Agency to build 18 satellites. Full-year revenue was a record $602M, up 38%.

38.0%

Margins keep improving

GAAP gross margin reached 38.0%, up from 27.8% a year ago, as higher-margin space systems work grows — services revenue alone rose 77%.

$53M

Losses are not shrinking yet

The net loss was $53M, about the same as a year ago despite 36% revenue growth: R&D rose to $79M as the Neutron program ramps, and an $11M tax charge widened the loss.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Unlike most loss-making quarters, Rocket Lab still recorded income tax: an $11.3M expense widened the net loss from $41.6M before tax to $52.9M.

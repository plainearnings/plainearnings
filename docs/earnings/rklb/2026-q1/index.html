Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.2B

The order book doubled

Backlog hit a record $2.2B, up 108% YoY: 31 new Electron and HASTE contracts plus five dedicated Neutron launches were signed in the quarter — more launches sold than in all of 2025.

38.2%

Best gross margin yet

GAAP gross margin reached a record 38.2%, up from 29% a year ago, as higher-margin space systems work grows faster than launch.

$45M

Losses are narrowing

The net loss shrank from $61M a year ago to $45M even as R&D rose to $81M — the Neutron program is the big deliberate spend standing between Rocket Lab and break-even.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Rocket Lab closed its acquisition of Mynaric (laser communications terminals for satellites) in the quarter and agreed to buy Motiv Space Systems — deal costs sit inside SG&A, and the acquired businesses add to revenue from here on.

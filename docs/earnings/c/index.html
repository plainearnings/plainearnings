Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Services is the standout

Services revenue rose 15% to $6.6B — Treasury and Trade Solutions up 16% and Securities Services up 13% — and the business earned $2.6B, up 27%, at a 31.2% return on tangible equity.

+14%

Interest income did the heavy lifting

Net interest income rose 14% to $17.0B on loan growth of 12% and deposit growth of 9%. It is now 71% of all revenue, more than covering the 4% dip in fee and trading income.

$2.85

EPS up 53% on an easy comparison

EPS rose 53% to $2.85. Part of the jump is the comparison base: a year ago included a $726M goodwill impairment tied to the Banamex stake sale. Excluding that item, year-ago EPS was $2.24 and growth was 27%. Citi returned $4.1B to shareholders in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter included a $726M goodwill impairment ($714M after tax) in operating expenses, tied to the agreement to sell a 25% stake in Banamex — it flatters this quarter's reported EPS growth (53% reported vs 27% excluding the item). No comparable notable item was flagged in Q3 2026.

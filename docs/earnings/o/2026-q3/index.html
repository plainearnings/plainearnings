Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10%

Revenue grew on rent and loans

Total revenue rose to $1,622M — rent up 7% and loan and preferred-equity interest income more than doubling to $105M.

$1.12

AFFO per share up 4%

AFFO — the REIT cash-earnings measure — was $1.12 per share, and Realty Income raised 2026 AFFO guidance to $4.45–$4.46.

98.8%

Portfolio stayed full

Occupancy was 98.8% across 15,536 properties, with a 103.5% rent recapture rate on re-leased properties.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Provisions for impairment of real estate were $112M and credit-loss provisions $14M in the quarter.
  • Gain on sales of real estate was $114M, largely offsetting the impairments.

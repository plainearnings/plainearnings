Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.5%

Both big brands grew

Net sales rose 3.5% to a record $1,481M. UGG grew 3.5% to $786M and HOKA grew 6.8% to $677M, while Other brands halved to $17M as Koolaburra was phased out. Wholesale sales rose 5.5% to $1,093M; direct-to-consumer sales (its own stores and websites) slipped 1.8% to $388M. International sales rose 9% and made up 44% of the total.

60.5%

Margins jumped — mostly a refund

Gross margin rose from 56.2% to 60.5%. About 3.1 points of that came from a $46M refund of tariffs (import taxes) previously paid, booked as a cut to cost of sales. Even without it, underlying margins improved, and operating income rose 19% to $389M.

$313M

Profit rose 17%, buybacks shrank the share count

Net income rose to $313M from $268M, and EPS rose faster — up 27% to $2.31 — because buybacks cut the share count: Deckers repurchased $299M of shares in the quarter ($638M so far this year). It also raised full-year EPS guidance to $7.80–$7.95.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Deckers received $46M of IEEPA tariff refunds in the quarter — money back for import tariffs it had paid under a policy later unwound — plus $3M of interest on the refunds. The $46M was booked as a reduction in cost of sales and the $3M in interest income, adding about $0.28 to EPS after tax. It is a one-off: without it, gross margin would have been about 57.4% instead of 60.5%.

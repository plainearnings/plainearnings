Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11 pts

Price up, volume down

Price increases added about 11 points to sales growth while volume and mix subtracted about 10 — shoppers paid more and bought less, and pricing won.

46.1%

Margins snapped back

Reported gross margin jumped from 32.6% to 46.1% on price realization, lower net commodity costs, productivity savings, tariff refunds and derivative gains; even the adjusted margin rose to 42.7%.

$950M

The core candy business earned it

North America Confectionery segment income rose 66% to $950M — a 35.8% segment margin — carrying the quarter while International segment results stayed in loss.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported gross profit includes $113M of unallocated mark-to-market gains on commodity derivatives — a paper gain that is why reported gross margin (46.1%) sits above the adjusted figure (42.7%).
  • Tariff refunds added $0.22 to adjusted EPS in the quarter.
  • Unallocated corporate costs include $36M of acquisition and integration spending, mainly for LesserEvil and Sour Strips.

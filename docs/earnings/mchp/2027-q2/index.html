Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+42%

The downturn is over

Sales rose 42% year over year and 9% sequentially, above the high end of guidance, with bookings strong and the book-to-bill ratio well above one — orders exceed shipments.

65.9%

Margins snapped back

Gross margin recovered to 65.9% from 55.9% a year ago as factories refilled, turning the sales rebound into a fivefold jump in operating income to $464M.

24

Data center design wins doubled

Microchip's PCIe Gen 6 switch and retimer design wins for AI data centers reached 24, up from 12 a quarter earlier; it also closed the Hailo edge-AI acquisition in September.

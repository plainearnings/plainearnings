Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Imaging is the growth engine

Digital Imaging sales rose 15% to $903M — over half of all revenue — on infrared detectors and subsystems for space and unmanned applications, plus growth in industrial vision and healthcare imaging.

20.6%

Record profit margin

Operating income rose 23% to $347M, a 20.6% margin vs 18.4% a year ago, as the higher-margin imaging mix grew fastest. Funded backlog reached nearly $5.3B.

$225M

Cash goes to buybacks and a deal

Teledyne generated $320M of free cash flow, repurchased $224.6M of stock in September–October, and has a pending ~$1.1B acquisition of Varex Imaging.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $55M of acquired intangible asset amortization — a non-cash cost of past acquisitions that Teledyne excludes from its non-GAAP earnings of $6.72 per share.

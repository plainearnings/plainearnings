Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+49%

The growth portfolio is taking over

Growth Portfolio revenue reached $1.26B, up 49%, and is now 63% of core pharmaceutical revenue — including the first full quarter from the acquired Apellis products Syfovre ($191M) and Empaveli ($52M).

$284M

A deal charge caused the loss

Acquired research expense of $284M, mainly for RayThera, plus $67M of restructuring, turned a profitable operating business into a $21M GAAP net loss. Non-GAAP EPS, which excludes these items, was $2.62.

-13%

The old MS franchise keeps shrinking

Multiple sclerosis product revenue fell 13% to $919M. Biogen's story now depends on growth products — Spinraza (+23%), Leqembi (+67%) and Skyclarys (+34%) — outgrowing that decline.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $284M of acquired in-process R&D, upfront and milestone expense, primarily tied to the RayThera acquisition — the main reason GAAP EPS was -$0.14 while non-GAAP EPS was $2.62.
  • $67M of restructuring charges and $173M of amortization and impairment of acquired intangible assets, from the Apellis and Reata deals, also weighed on GAAP results.

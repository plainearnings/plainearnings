Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Record revenue of $20.1B

Card spending (billed business) rose 8% to $454B, discount revenue grew 8%, and card fees jumped 16% after the Platinum refresh.

$982M

A one-off gain flattered EPS

Selling its ~30% stake in Global Business Travel Group added a $982M pre-tax gain ($1.11 a share); part was reinvested via a $336M restructuring charge. Adjusted EPS of $4.68 strips both out.

-23%

Credit costs fell

Provisions for credit losses dropped 23% to $985M on a reserve release, with write-off and delinquency rates stable — Amex's premium customers kept paying.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include a $982M pre-tax gain on the sale of Amex's equity stake in Global Business Travel Group (closed Sep 29, 2026) and a $336M restructuring charge; adjusted EPS of $4.68 excludes both.

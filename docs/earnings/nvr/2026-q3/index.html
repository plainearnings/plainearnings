Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-8%

Settlements fell

Home settlements fell 7% to 5,268 and the average settlement price fell 2%, taking homebuilding revenue down 8%.

18.3%

Margins squeezed

Homebuilding gross margin fell to 18.3% from 21.0% on higher lot costs, pricing pressure and $24.8M of land deposit impairments.

21%

Cancellations rose

The new-order cancellation rate rose to 21% from 19%, and new orders fell 7% to 4,391 — demand stayed soft.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Contract land deposit impairments totalled approximately $24.8M in the quarter, hurting homebuilding gross margin.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Sales rose to $3.56B

Americas led with 27% growth as new on-site plants started up; Asia grew 4% and Europe 8%.

$223M

Exit charges cut reported profit

Exiting two China coal-gasification plants cost $223M this quarter; excluding such items, operating income would have been $856M.

$3.61

Adjusted EPS beat guidance

Adjusted EPS of $3.61 topped the guidance midpoint, and fiscal 2027 guidance of $14.15–$14.50 implies another year of growth.

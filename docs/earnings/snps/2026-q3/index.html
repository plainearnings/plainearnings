Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+42%

Ansys transformed the scale

Revenue rose 42% to $2,477M, with Design Automation up 53%. Management raised full-year revenue guidance to about $9.72B at the midpoint.

$402M

Deal costs hide in GAAP profit

Amortization of bought intangibles was $402M in the quarter ($155M in operating expenses, the rest in cost of revenue) and stock pay $232M — the main gap between GAAP EPS of $2.84 and non-GAAP EPS of $3.91.

$460M

Other income did heavy lifting

Other income of $460M, helped by gains on business divestitures (about $381M over nine months), offset $133M of interest expense on acquisition debt.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gains on divestitures of about $381M (net, nine months) sit inside other income; the non-GAAP reconciliation removes a net $403M of acquisition/divestiture-related items this quarter.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $402M and restructuring charges $236M over the first nine months, largely from the Ansys acquisition.

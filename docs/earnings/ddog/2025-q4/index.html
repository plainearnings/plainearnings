The striking thing: R&D alone eats about $44 of every $100 — more than four times the operating profit it leaves behind. Datadog's GAAP profit is thin because stock-based pay ($205M in the quarter) runs through those same lines; on management's preferred non-GAAP basis the operating margin was 24%, not 1%. Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

Growth held at 29% into year-end

Revenue grew 29% to $953M, and the customers that matter most keep spending more: 603 customers now pay $1M+ a year, up 31% from 462 a year ago.

$291M

A cash machine underneath a thin GAAP profit

Free cash flow was $291M — 31 cents of every revenue dollar — while GAAP operating income was just $9M. The gap is mostly stock-based pay ($205M), which costs no cash.

1% vs 24%

Two margins, one gap

GAAP operating margin was 1%; non-GAAP was 24% after adding back stock pay and amortization. The full year tells the same story: a $44M GAAP operating loss vs $768M non-GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+36%

Growth stayed hot

Revenue grew 36% to $1.12B, and customers spending $100k+ a year rose 23% to about 4,720 — big companies keep putting more of their systems on Datadog.

$5M vs $257M

Stock pay is the whole gap

GAAP operating income was just $5M, while non-GAAP operating income was $257M. Almost the entire difference is $220M of stock-based compensation — pay delivered in shares rather than cash.

$279M

Cash flow tells a richer story

Free cash flow was $279M — 25 cents per revenue dollar — and Datadog held $5.0B of cash and marketable securities at quarter-end.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation was $220M in the quarter, and G&A included $2M of M&A transaction costs (Datadog acquired AI startup Adaptive ML in the quarter).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+35%

Growth re-accelerated

Revenue growth hit 35%, and customers spending $100k+ a year rose 20% to about 4,860 — big companies keep putting more of their systems on Datadog.

$251M

A cash machine underneath

Free cash flow was $251M in one quarter — 21 cents per revenue dollar — because subscriptions are paid upfront and stock pay costs no cash.

6% vs 28%

Two margins, one gap

GAAP operating margin was 6%; non-GAAP was 28%. Almost the entire gap is stock-based compensation — pay delivered in shares rather than cash.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+122.7%

Sales more than doubled

Net sales rose to $10.2B from $4.6B a year ago, driven by AI rack demand — though sales eased from $12.7B in Q2 as large deals timed differently.

9.9%

Margins recovered

Gross margin rose to 9.9% from 6.3% in Q2, which management credited to its growing Data Center Building Block Solutions business.

-$6.6B

Cash went the other way

Operations used $6.6B of cash in the quarter as receivables and inventories ballooned with the sales surge; cash ended at $1.3B against $8.8B of bank debt and convertible notes.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The May 5 release called its figures preliminary; the Form 10-Q filed May 11, 2026 reported the same quarter totals (net sales $10,243M, net income $483M).
  • Stock-based compensation was $125.9M in the quarter — the main gap between GAAP net income ($483M) and non-GAAP net income ($581M basic).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+123.4%

Sales more than doubled

Net sales rose to a record $12.7B from $5.7B a year ago on large AI datacenter deployments — more than double Q1's $5.0B in a single step.

6.3%

Margins squeezed hard

Gross margin fell to 6.3% from 11.8% a year ago as very large AI deals dominated the mix — revenue more than doubled, but gross profit rose only 19%.

$401M

Profit followed, slowly

Net income rose to $401M from $321M a year ago and $168M in Q1 — real growth, but a fraction of the sales surge, because so little of each sales dollar survived direct costs.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation was $90.5M in the quarter — the main gap between GAAP net income ($401M) and non-GAAP net income ($486M, diluted basis).
  • Management raised the full-year FY26 net sales outlook to at least $40B in this release.

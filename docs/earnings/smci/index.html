Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+297.5%

AI racks nearly quadrupled sales

Net sales hit $19,946M, up from $5,018M a year ago. The 10-Q attributes the jump mainly to Hyper Servers, GPU and Super Rack billings — up $12,535M, or 293% — led by liquid- and air-cooled GB200 and GB300 systems, on higher volumes and a richer mix.

12.7%

Margins are better than a year ago, thinner than last quarter

Gross margin rose to 12.7% from 9.3% a year ago on product and customer mix, though it fell from 17.5% in Q4 FY26. With operating costs at just 2.4% of sales, that margin still produced $2,064M of operating income.

-$434M

Profit did not turn into operating cash

Operating activities used $434M of cash: receivables grew by $3,560M and inventories by $2,477M to support the sales surge, partly offset by $3,054M more in deferred revenue — money customers paid in advance. Capex was only $42M, so the cash drain is working capital, not factories.

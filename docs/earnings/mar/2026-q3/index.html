Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Fees are the business

Net fee revenue rose 14% to $1.5B — franchise fees alone were $1.0B, up 15%. Marriott mostly collects tolls on other people's hotels rather than owning them.

+6.5%

Hotels were fuller and pricier

Worldwide revenue per available room rose 6.5% — up 7.4% in the U.S. & Canada and 4.4% internationally. Net rooms grew 4.6% year over year, with a record pipeline of 634,000 rooms.

$1.3B

Buybacks shrink the share count

Marriott repurchased 3.5 million shares for $1.3B in the quarter and has returned about $4.0B to shareholders so far this year — one reason EPS grew 14% while net income grew 9%.

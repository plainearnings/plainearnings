Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Growth picked up

Revenue rose 18% to $928M — subscription revenue up 16% to $767M and professional services up 24% to $161M. Veeva also raised its full-year outlook.

$273M

Profit jumped 37%

GAAP net income was $273M, up 37% from $200M a year ago, helped by last year's quarter carrying a $31M litigation charge in G&A. Non-GAAP net income was $387M.

180+

Vault CRM is winning

More than 180 customers are live on Vault CRM, including five top-20 biopharmas, in what Veeva called its best CRM quarter ever — and its new AI agents are the next pitch.

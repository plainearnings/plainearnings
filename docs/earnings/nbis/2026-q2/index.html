Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+454%

The AI cloud exploded

Revenue rose 454% to $582.3M. The AI cloud alone brought in $574.9M, up 514%, as Nebius added GPU capacity and filled it at strong prices and high utilization. The other businesses are now rounding errors by comparison.

$236M

Cash-profitable before the build-out costs

Adjusted EBITDA swung to a $236.2M profit from a $21.0M loss a year ago. The loss appears lower down: $259.7M of depreciation on all the new servers and data centers, and $119.1M of interest, turned the quarter to a $190.4M net loss.

$5.7B

Spending at full speed

Nebius spent $5,657.4M on property and equipment in this one quarter. Customers paying in advance helped operating cash flow reach $2,246.1M, and the company has since added a $3.5B credit facility (October 2026) to keep funding the build-out.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Share-based compensation was $102.5M in the quarter, versus $14.7M a year ago — a real cost of paying staff in shares, and the main reason the company's 'adjusted' net loss of $33.2M looks so much better than the GAAP loss of $190.4M.
  • The comparison a year ago was flattered the other way: Q2 2025 included a $597.4M gain from revaluing the ClickHouse stake, which turned that quarter's continuing result into a $502.5M profit. There was no such gain this quarter.

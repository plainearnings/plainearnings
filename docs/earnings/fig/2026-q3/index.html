Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+43%

Growth beat its own guidance

Revenue of $391M grew 43% and topped Figma's guidance range; management raised the full-year outlook to about $1.50B as AI features turned into paid credits.

$159M

Stock pay is the story

Stock-based compensation was about $159M in the quarter — more than the entire gap between the $119M GAAP operating loss and the $47M non-GAAP operating profit.

16,566

Big customers keep joining

Customers paying over $10k a year rose by more than 3,650 in a year to 16,566 — and over 80% of them now consume AI credits every week.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Figma listed on the NYSE in July 2025. Newly public companies often show giant GAAP losses as years of employee stock awards vest at once: Q3 2025's loss was $1,097M for exactly that reason. This quarter's $81M loss is the same effect, fading.

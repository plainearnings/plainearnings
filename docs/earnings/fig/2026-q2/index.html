Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+48%

Growth accelerated again

Revenue growth quickened for the third straight quarter, and Figma raised its full-year revenue outlook to $1.463–1.467B.

136%

Customers keep expanding

Net dollar retention was 136% — existing customers spent 36% more than a year ago — as teams bought more seats and, in the first full quarter of AI credits, more AI usage: over 80% of larger paid customers used AI credits weekly.

$148M

Stock pay is the loss

Stock-based compensation was $148M in the quarter. Without it and related items, Figma earned $36M of operating income and $43M of net income (non-GAAP) instead of the GAAP loss.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Figma listed on the NYSE in July 2025. Newly public companies often show large GAAP losses as employee stock awards vest: stock-based compensation was $148M this quarter, versus just $7M in Q2 2025 before the IPO.

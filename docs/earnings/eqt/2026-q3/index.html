Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

583 Bcfe

Volumes down on purpose

Sales volume fell 8% to 583 Bcfe, including 21 Bcfe of strategic curtailments — EQT held back gas rather than sell it at weak prices.

$1.09

Unit costs at the low end

Total operating costs were $1.09 per Mcfe, at the low end of guidance, and free cash flow attributable to EQT was $283 million; net debt fell to $5.47 billion.

-24%

Operating income fell

Operating income dropped to $459 million from $603 million as lower sales and smaller derivative gains ($100M vs $136M) outweighed cost control.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Derivative gains added $100M to revenue this quarter (vs $136M a year ago) — these hedging marks swing with gas prices and are not the same as selling more gas.

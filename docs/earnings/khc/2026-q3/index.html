Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-1.1%

Volumes keep sliding at home

Organic sales fell 1.1% — price was flat while volume/mix declined, led by North America meats, sauces and beverages.

+15.6%

Emerging Markets is the bright spot

Organic sales grew 15.6% in Emerging Markets, the only segment growing — now $720M of the quarter's sales.

Paused

The big split is on hold

Work on the previously announced separation of Kraft Heinz into two public companies is currently paused, the company disclosed, even as it updated its 2026 outlook.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter includes $72M of goodwill and intangible impairment charges. The nine-month picture is much heavier: $2.51B of goodwill and $4.93B of intangible impairments taken earlier in 2026 are why year-to-date results show a net loss.

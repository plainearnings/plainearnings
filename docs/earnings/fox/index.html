Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+47%

Advertising carried the quarter

Advertising revenue jumped 47% to $2.08B. In the Television segment, advertising rose 60% — live sports and a heavy political-advertising cycle — taking segment revenue up 32% and segment EBITDA from $399M to $934M.

$859M

Cable networks stayed dependable

Cable Network Programming (Fox News, FS1 and peers) grew revenue 8% to $1.80B and produced $859M of adjusted EBITDA — a ~48% margin on distribution fees that barely fluctuate.

$2.20

Profit per share rose two-thirds

Net income attributable to stockholders was $943M, or $2.20 a share, versus $1.32 a year ago — helped by the ad surge and by a share count that fell from 455M to 428M on buybacks.

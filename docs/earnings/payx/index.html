Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

PEO is the growth engine

PEO and Insurance revenue grew 12% to $368M on more worksite employees and higher insurance volumes — three times the growth rate of the core payroll business.

38.0%

Margins expanded

Operating margin rose to 38.0% from 35.2% as revenue grew and acquisition-related costs fell versus a year ago. Adjusted margin was 42.0%.

$1.21

EPS grew twice as fast as revenue

Diluted EPS rose 14% to $1.21 — margin gains and fewer shares outstanding turned 6% revenue growth into double-digit profit growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquisition-related costs of $65.5M (mainly from the Paycor deal) are inside GAAP results; excluding them, adjusted operating income was $684.7M and adjusted EPS $1.34.

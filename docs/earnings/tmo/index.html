Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Pharma services are the engine

Laboratory Products & Biopharma Services grew 15% to $6,863M — clinical research revenue alone rose 21% to $2,445M — and now supplies 57% of all revenue.

-6%

Diagnostics shrank on a sale, not demand

Specialty Diagnostics fell 6% to $1,098M, mainly because the microbiology business was divested in the quarter (its revenue dropped from $162M to $70M).

$6.68

Two profit numbers, one business

Adjusted EPS was $6.68 vs GAAP $4.64 — the $2.04 gap is mostly $519M of non-cash acquisition amortization and $199M of restructuring charges that adjusted results strip out.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other costs of $199M (severance, impairments and facility consolidations; about 2% of the workforce affected by the wider program) are excluded from adjusted results, as is $519M of acquisition-related intangible amortization — together they explain most of the gap between GAAP EPS of $4.64 and adjusted EPS of $6.68.
  • Thermo Fisher completed the sale of its microbiology business during the quarter, which is why Specialty Diagnostics revenue fell even as the rest of the company grew.

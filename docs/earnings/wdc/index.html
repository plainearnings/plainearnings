Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

88%

It is a cloud company now

Cloud was $3,868M of the $4,381M total, up 54% — data centers racing to store AI data bought more exabytes at about 25% higher prices per exabyte.

57.8%

Pricing power showed up in margin

Gross margin jumped to 57.8% from 43.5% a year ago, and operating margin reached 46.7% — scarce high-capacity drives are being sold at premium prices.

~$5.1B

Guidance says more of the same

For FY27 Q2 Western Digital guided to revenue of about $5.1B (up 66–72% YoY) and non-GAAP gross margin around 60.5% — and raised the quarterly dividend 17% to $0.175 a share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $43M of business realignment (restructuring) charges are inside operating expenses. Excluding them and similar adjustments, non-GAAP EPS was $4.64 versus GAAP EPS of $4.63 — a nearly clean quarter.

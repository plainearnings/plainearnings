Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-2.8%

The sales dip was pre-announced

Organic sales fell 2.8%, reflecting a 6.3-point reversal of advance orders customers placed in Q2 ahead of the ERP system cutover, which is now complete.

$387M

Underlying profit is far above GAAP

Adjusted operating income was $387M (19.9% of sales) versus $68M GAAP. The gap is amortization ($90M), restructuring ($50M) and 3M spin-off/separation costs ($157M).

$1,518M

Last year's comparison is distorted

A year ago GAAP EPS was $7.22 because of a $1,518M gain on selling the Purification & Filtration business. That business is gone, which also removes $128M of quarterly sales from the comparison.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring ($50M), 3M spin-off and separation costs ($157M) and acquisition-related items weighed on GAAP operating income; adjusted operating income was $387M vs $68M GAAP.
  • Year-ago results included a $1,518M gain on the sale of the Purification & Filtration business.

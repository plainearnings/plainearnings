Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

1%

Growth is thinner than it looks

Organic volume growth was just 1% (focus categories +3%) — the 7% sales headline is mostly resin cost pass-through, especially in Flexibles where it added 10 points.

$883M

Cost spike ate the growth

Adjusted EBITDA was flat at $883M despite higher sales: late-quarter energy and distribution inflation ate the gains. Management says mitigation actions keep it out of 2027 results.

$650M

Synergies on track

Berry synergy realization is tracking to the three-year $650M target — the core of the investment case while GAAP EPS ($0.52) still carries merger amortization and integration costs.

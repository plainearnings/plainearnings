Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11.5%

Demand, not just deals, drove growth

Organic sales rose 11.5% with increased demand across all market verticals and record backlog levels. Acquisitions — mainly Filtration Group — added about 7 points of the 18% reported growth.

28.6%

Record adjusted margins

Segment operating margin was 24.4% on a GAAP basis and a record 28.6% adjusted. Aerospace Systems earned a 26.6% GAAP margin on sales up 14%; Diversified Industrial earned 23.4% on sales up 21%.

$36.45–$37.25

The outlook went up

Parker raised fiscal 2027 guidance: reported sales growth of 16–19%, organic growth of 7.5–10.5%, and adjusted EPS of $36.45 to $37.25 — now including Filtration Group.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Deal costs are inside GAAP profit: $78M of acquisition-related expenses (including the fair-value step-up of acquired inventory), $23M of integration costs to achieve, $17M of business realignment charges and a net $22M tariff-refund adjustment. Excluding these and $189M of amortization of acquired intangibles, adjusted net income was $1,138M ($8.90 per share).
  • Net sales were reduced by $27M at Corporate for IEEPA tariff-related returns to customers — tariffs a court ruled unauthorized — partly offset by $6M received from the U.S. government.

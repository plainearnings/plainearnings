Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.0%

Dental merchandise led

Global dental distribution merchandise sales rose 7.0% (5.1% internal growth) — dentists are buying supplies again, and equipment sales also grew 2.3%.

$0.19

A tariff-refund boost

GAAP and non-GAAP results both include a $0.19-per-share net benefit from tariff refunds. Excluding it (and last year's remeasurement gain), non-GAAP EPS still rose 19% to $1.37.

9.24%

Margins inched up

Adjusted EBITDA rose to $321 million and its margin widened 40 basis points to 9.24% — slow, steady operating leverage in a distribution business where thin margins are normal.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter includes a $0.19-per-share net benefit from tariff refunds in both GAAP and non-GAAP results; the year-ago quarter included a $0.23-per-share remeasurement gain.
  • Restructuring and related costs of $22M are inside GAAP operating expenses; non-GAAP net income was $175M ($1.56 per share) after removing restructuring, amortization and similar items.

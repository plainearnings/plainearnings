Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$362M

Wisconsin carried the quarter

The Wisconsin segment contributed $362M of net income, up 29%, on new rates in effect since January, warmer summer weather and carrying costs on construction for very large customers (VLCs) — the data-center-style loads arriving in Wisconsin.

+3.3%

People used more power

Retail electricity deliveries rose 3.3% excluding the iron-ore mine and the very large customers — residential use rose 5.1% in the warm weather.

$5.60–$5.64

Guidance raised

WEC raised and narrowed its 2026 EPS guidance to $5.60–$5.64, citing favorable weather and capital investment supported by the very large customers.

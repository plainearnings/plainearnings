Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+75%

Networking exploded

Networking revenue rose 75% to $2.9 billion — routing revenue alone rose 270% — as the Juniper Networks business (acquired in 2025) filled out HPE's AI data-center offer.

$6.8B

AI servers are the engine

Server revenue rose 35% to $6.8 billion on AI demand, gross margin expanded almost 11 points to 40.1%, and management raised the outlook for both fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027.

$444M

A one-time gain flattered profit

A $444 million gain on selling an equity interest sits below operating profit — without it, pre-tax profit would have been about a quarter lower. The operating improvement is real, but not quite as big as the headline net income suggests.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $444M gain on the sale of an equity interest is included in pre-tax earnings this quarter.
  • Operating expenses include $315M of acquisition-related intangible amortization and $71M of acquisition, disposition and other charges, largely tied to the Juniper acquisition.

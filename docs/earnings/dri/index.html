Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.1%

Every brand grew

Blended same-restaurant sales rose 3.1%, and all four segments were positive — LongHorn led with +6.2%, Olive Garden added +1.1%. Total sales reached $3.2B, up 5.1%.

$2.05

Profit only looks down

Reported profit fell from $258M to $233M, but last year included a one-off $42M gain on selling the Canadian Olive Gardens. Against last year's adjusted $1.97 per share, continuing EPS of $2.05 is up 4.1%.

$222M

Cash back to shareholders

Darden bought back $222M of its own shares in the quarter, declared a $1.62 quarterly dividend, and reaffirmed its full-year outlook of $11.10–$11.35 of EPS from continuing operations.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A year ago, profit included a $42M gain on the sale of Olive Garden's Canadian restaurants — without it, last year's continuing earnings were $231M ($1.97 per share), so this year's $234M ($2.05) is actually up 4% on a like-for-like basis.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+260%

International partnerships delivered

Revenue rose to $389M from $108M a year ago, with $311M earned internationally — COVID vaccine deliveries under long-term government partnerships, including the first U.K. shipment, did the work while the U.S. contributed $78M.

-24%

The cost reset is real

Excluding the settlement, costs fell across the board: R&D down 24% to $649M, SG&A down 18% to $173M, and underlying cost of sales down as unused-capacity costs and write-downs shrank. Moderna reiterated up to 10% revenue growth for 2026 with lower operating expenses.

2

Two European approvals

The EU approved mNEXSPIKE and mCOMBRIAX — the world's first flu-plus-COVID combination vaccine — and broadened mRESVIA's approval, while a Phase 3 trial of the intismeran cancer therapy in early-stage lung cancer began.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Cost of sales as reported was $955M, of which $878M was litigation settlement-related expense booked in third-party royalties (total royalties were $895M). The related $950M cash payment was made in July 2026, after this quarter ended.
  • Excluding the settlement charge, Moderna's net loss would have been about $465M instead of $1,343M.

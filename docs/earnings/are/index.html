Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$638M

A write-down quarter

Alexandria marked down properties it intends to sell by $638M, the main reason for the $599M net loss. As a REIT it pays little income tax, so the loss flows straight through.

-11%

Revenue fell to $671M

Rent declined as the company sells campuses and life-science leasing demand stays soft — a deliberate shrink to cut debt.

$1.54

FFO per share held up

Funds from operations — which add back depreciation and the impairment — were $1.54 a share on an as-adjusted basis, down from $2.22 a year ago.

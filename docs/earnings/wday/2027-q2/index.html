Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Subscriptions keep compounding

Subscription revenue rose 14% to $2,471M — 93% of all revenue — and the 12-month backlog grew 14% to $9,034M. Workday raised its fiscal 2027 subscription outlook to about $9.94B.

$632M

Profit doubled — on a tax item

Net income rose from $228M to $632M, but $305M of that is a one-time tax benefit from moving IP between Workday's own companies. Operating income itself rose a steadier 26% to $313M.

25%+

AI is driving new deals

AI drove more than 25% of new annual contract value, and more than 5,500 customers now use at least one Workday AI agent — the growth story management is selling.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $305M income-tax benefit from an intra-entity transfer of certain intellectual property rights, part of an internal legal-entity restructuring — worth $1.52 of the $2.57 GAAP diluted EPS. Pre-tax income was only $327M, so the tax benefit is why net income ($632M) is nearly double pre-tax income. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $2.75 and non-GAAP operating income was $824M (versus GAAP $313M, the gap mostly $462M of share-based compensation).

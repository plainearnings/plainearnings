Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+54%

Product sales exploded

Product revenue rose 54% to $687M, powered by AI and hyperscale buyers — including a design win with a second top-five hyperscaler. Eight straight quarters of accelerating growth.

$5.05B

Guidance raised, a lot

Full-year FY27 revenue guidance jumped to $5.03B–$5.07B from $4.41B–$4.51B — management sees the demand lasting, not fading.

-$238M

Profit, but cash went out

GAAP profit was $74M, yet free cash flow was -$238M as prepaid costs jumped and Everpure paid $125M for an acquisition. Worth watching whether cash catches up next quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation was $160M in the quarter — the main reason GAAP operating income ($63M) sits far below non-GAAP operating income ($230M).
  • Cash flow was unusually weak: operating cash flow was -$136M and free cash flow -$238M, after a large jump in prepaid expenses and a $125M acquisition payment.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$506M

The utility is the steady core

PSE&G earned $506M, nearly matching last year's $515M, as infrastructure investment and customer growth offset higher depreciation and interest costs.

-$130M

Power swung to a loss

PSEG Power & Other lost $130M (vs a $107M profit a year ago) on mark-to-market and trust-investment losses — the entire reason group profit fell.

95.5%

Nuclear plants ran hard

PSEG's nuclear fleet produced 8.2 terawatt-hours of carbon-free power at a 95.5% capacity factor — running almost flat-out through the summer storm season.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Mark-to-market accounting losses of $251M before tax and a $21M loss on nuclear decommissioning trust investments hit GAAP results. Excluding reconciling items of $198M after tax, non-GAAP operating earnings were $574M ($1.15 per share).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.4%

Underlying growth is faster than it looks

Reported revenue rose 5.3%, but on an organic constant-currency basis — stripping out currency and portfolio changes — growth was 7.4%, with subscription revenue up 8.2%.

$1.96

Adjusted EPS jumped 14%

Diluted adjusted EPS rose 14.0% to $1.96, and GAAP EPS rose 5.6% to $1.70 — helped by buybacks, including a $100M accelerated repurchase in the quarter.

-2.8%

Investments and interest cut net income

Net income slipped 2.8% to $219M, mainly on a $17.5M investment loss and higher net interest expense, partly offset by operating leverage.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $17.5M investment loss (mostly a $16.7M loss on cost-based investments), versus a $1.6M loss a year ago — the main reason net income fell despite revenue and EBITDA growth.

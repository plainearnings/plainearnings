Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$5.3B

The subscription engine accelerated

Contract value — subscriptions already sold but not yet recognized as revenue — grew 2.5% year over year and 1.1% just in the quarter, its fastest sequential pace in a while.

$3.35

EPS comparisons are distorted

EPS rose 613% partly because last year's quarter absorbed a $150M goodwill impairment, and partly because buybacks shrank the diluted share count from 75.0M to 63.9M.

$1.7B

Buybacks are doing heavy lifting

Gartner repurchased $1.7B of its own stock over the last four quarters ($138M this quarter), and raised its full-year guidance for revenue, profit and free cash flow.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter included a $150M goodwill impairment, which is why profit growth rates look extreme.
  • Revenue from the divested 'Other' business fell from $55M a year ago to zero; Gartner's adjusted figures exclude it, and on that basis revenue grew 2.4%.

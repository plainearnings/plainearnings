Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.7B

Pro is the profit engine

Ford Pro, the business-vehicle unit, made $1,665M of EBIT (earnings before interest and tax) on $17.8B of revenue — more than the gas-vehicle unit — though profit fell 16% on commodity and launch costs.

-$1.1B

EVs still lose money

Ford Model e lost $1,058M in the quarter. The loss narrowed 25% from a year ago, but on much lower sales — Ford is spending on a new, cheaper EV platform while first-generation models shrink.

$803M

The bank inside Ford earned more

Ford Credit's earnings before tax rose 27% to $803M on healthy lending margins and strong used-car prices, cushioning the vehicle businesses.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Ford's adjusted figures exclude $127M of pre-tax special items in the quarter; GAAP net income was $2,049M versus adjusted net income of $1,978M.
  • Ford raised the low end of its full-year adjusted EBIT guidance to $10.5-$11B (from $10-$11B).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Fees grew with markets

Investment advisory fees rose 5.8% to $1,796.4M as average assets under management rose 9.5% to $1,887.2B, partly offset by a lower average fee rate of 37.8 basis points.

-$28.3B

Clients kept pulling money out

Net client outflows were $28.3B in the quarter, including $25.6B from equity. Ending AUM fell 1.6% in the quarter to $1,862.7B despite the revenue growth.

$612M

Profit dipped on smaller investment gains

Non-operating income fell to $17.6M from $238.4M a year ago, when investment gains were much larger. Net income for shareholders fell 5.2% to $612.3M, while operating income rose 19.7% to $769.8M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges were $4.0M in the quarter, down from $28.5M a year ago. On the company's adjusted basis — which excludes restructuring, acquisition-related items and similar costs — net income attributable was $629.5M and diluted EPS was $2.86, versus $612.3M and $2.79 on a GAAP basis.

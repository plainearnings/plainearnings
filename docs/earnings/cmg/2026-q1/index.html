Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+0.5%

Transactions turned positive

Comparable sales rose 0.5% — transactions up 0.6%, partly offset by a 0.1% smaller average check — the first positive transaction comp after a soft 2025. Digital sales were 38.6% of food and beverage revenue.

49

49 new restaurants opened

Chipotle opened 49 company-owned restaurants (42 with a Chipotlane), reaching 4,090 company-owned and 14 partner-operated locations. Full-year guidance was for about flat comparable sales and 350–370 openings.

23.3%

Margins squeezed at the restaurant

Restaurant-level margin fell to 23.3% from 26.2% as labor rose to 26.1% of revenue (including legal costs) and food, beverage and packaging rose to 29.6% on beef and freight inflation. Operating margin fell to 12.9% from 16.7%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted net income of $316.2M excludes $17.6M of items: $11.9M of legal-proceeding costs in labor, $0.6M of legal costs in G&A, $2.1M of Recipe for Growth restructuring costs and $3.0M of retention stock pay.
  • G&A of $203.7M included the biennial All Managers Conference, held in the first quarter of 2026.
  • Chipotle repurchased $700.8M of its own stock in the quarter at an average of $36.14; about $1.0B of buyback authorization remained at March 31.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2.2%

Traffic improved again

Comparable sales rose 2.2% — transactions up 1.0% and average check up 1.2% — a second straight quarter of improving transaction comps. Digital sales climbed to 38.3% of food and beverage revenue.

100

100 new restaurants, guidance raised

Chipotle opened 100 company-owned restaurants (80 with a Chipotlane) plus one partner-operated abroad, passing 4,200 total locations. Management raised full-year guidance to low-single-digit comparable sales growth and 350–370 openings.

25.2%

Margins squeezed at the restaurant

Restaurant-level margin fell to 25.2% from 27.4% as food, beverage and packaging costs rose to 29.7% of revenue (beef and freight inflation) and labor rose to 25.0%. Operating margin fell to 15.7% from 18.2%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted net income of $418.9M excludes $18.2M of items: a $10.0M legal reserve in G&A, $3.9M of restaurant asset impairment, $3.3M of Recipe for Growth restructuring costs and $0.9M of retention stock pay.
  • Chipotle repurchased $630.7M of its own stock in the quarter at an average of $32.55, and the board added $1.3B of buyback authorization; $1.7B remained at June 30.

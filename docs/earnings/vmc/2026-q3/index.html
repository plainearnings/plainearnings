Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$22.84

Pricing did the work

Freight-adjusted aggregates prices rose 4% to $22.84 a ton and gross profit held at $9.46 a ton even as shipments slipped 3% to 62.7M tons — pricing and cost discipline protected the margin.

-61%

Concrete is shrinking away

Concrete revenue fell 61% to $92M and asphalt fell 8% to $381M — Vulcan is increasingly an aggregates-only story, which is where its pricing power lives.

$2.4B

Full-year target held

Vulcan expects about $2.4B of adjusted EBITDA for 2026 and targets earnings growth again in 2027, while spending on aggregates-led acquisitions in growth markets.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3%

Growth was modest at the core

Ratings revenue rose 3% to $939M while Moody's Analytics slipped 1% to $1,138M — total revenue grew just 1% to $2.0B.

$115M

A one-off gain flatters profit

Selling businesses added a $115M pre-tax gain below the operating line. It is the main reason GAAP EPS rose 18% while adjusted EPS rose 6%.

24.0%

A friendlier tax rate helped

The effective tax rate fell to 24.0% from 25.4%, helped by the lapse of a statute of limitations on an old acquisition-related tax exposure.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $115M pre-tax gain on business divestitures sits in non-operating income. Excluding it and related items, adjusted EPS was $4.14, up 6% — a cleaner read on the quarter than the 18% GAAP jump.

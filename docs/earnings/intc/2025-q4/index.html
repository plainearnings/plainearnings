Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Data center carried the quarter

Data Center and AI revenue rose 9% to $4,737M, while client computing fell 7% to $8,193M. Total revenue fell 4% to $13,674M and gross margin eased to 36.1% from 39.2%.

$671M

Tax turned a profit into a loss

Intel made a $580M operating profit, but a $671M tax charge on $338M of pre-tax income (a 198.5% rate) produced a $333M total net loss. On Intel's non-GAAP basis, EPS was a $0.15 profit.

$4.3B

Cash generation was strong

Intel generated $4.3B of operating cash flow in the quarter, launched its first 18A products (Core Ultra Series 3), and completed the $5.0B sale of stock to Nvidia.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Tax: the $671M tax charge on just $338M of pre-tax income — a 198.5% effective rate, as reported — is what turned an operating profit into a net loss. Intel's non-GAAP tax rate was 12% and its non-GAAP EPS was a $0.15 profit.

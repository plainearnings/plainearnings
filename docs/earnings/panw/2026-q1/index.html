Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

Steady growth

Revenue grew 16% to $2.47B — product revenue rose 23% to $434M and subscription and support 14% to $2.04B.

+29%

The platform bet is working

Next-Generation Security ARR grew 29% to $5.9B, and remaining performance obligations rose 24% to $15.5B.

$334M

Profitably run

GAAP net income was $334M, just below $351M a year ago as G&A rose with acquisition activity — operating income still grew to $309M from $286M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Share-based compensation was $387M, acquisition-related costs $5M, amortization of acquired intangible assets $39M and litigation-related charges $6M — together they explain most of the gap between the $334M GAAP net income and $662M of non-GAAP net income.
  • Palo Alto announced its intent to acquire Chronosphere in this release; the previously announced CyberArk acquisition had not yet closed, so neither deal's revenue or costs are in these numbers.

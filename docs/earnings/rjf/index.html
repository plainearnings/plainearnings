Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Record revenue of $4.27B

Net revenues rose 15% from a year earlier to a record $4.27B, driven by asset management and related fees, which grew 23% to $2.31B.

$3.06B

Private Client Group did most of it

The advisor business for individual clients brought in a record $3.06B, about 72% of total net revenues, helped by record fee-based account assets of $1.16T.

$666M

Record profit of $666M

Net income available to common shareholders was a record $666M, or $3.40 per share, up 10% from a year earlier.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted results exclude $37M of acquisition-related expenses, net of tax; on that basis net income available to common shareholders was $703M, or $3.59 per share.

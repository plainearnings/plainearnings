Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

The LS Power deal drives the East

East revenue jumped 28% to $3,512M and its adjusted EBITDA rose from $99M to $469M, thanks to the generation assets and CPower business bought from LS Power and higher capacity prices.

-3%

Texas had a softer quarter

Texas revenue slipped 3% and adjusted EBITDA fell to $381M from $512M, on milder weather (heating degree days down about 30% in the first half), higher supply costs and the cost of running the new plants.

$7.90–$9.90

Full-year guidance reaffirmed

NRG kept its 2026 guidance: adjusted EPS of $7.90–$9.90 and adjusted EBITDA of $5,325–$5,825M. GAAP profit of $506M was well above adjusted net income of $315M because of unrealized hedge gains (see one-time items).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include unrealized mark-to-market gains on economic hedges — power contracts revalued each quarter while the customer contracts they hedge are not. That is the main reason GAAP net income of $506M was well above adjusted net income of $315M; these gains can reverse before the contracts settle.
  • A $37M gain on the sale of assets sits inside operating income this quarter.

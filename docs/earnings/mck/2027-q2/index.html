Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Oncology is the growth engine

Oncology & Multispecialty revenue jumped 24% to $15.0B on demand for cancer drugs, while the core North American Pharmaceutical business grew 6% to $91.4B.

$10.44

Buybacks amplify profit growth

Net income attributable to McKesson rose 10%, but EPS rose 17% — the diluted share count fell 6% year over year as buybacks shrank the denominator.

3.6%

A thin slice of a giant flow

Drug distribution runs on razor-thin margins: $107.0B of every $111.0B went straight to buying the medicines, leaving a 3.6% gross margin to run the network and earn from.

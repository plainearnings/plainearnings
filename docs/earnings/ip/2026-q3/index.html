Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$427M

North America is improving fast

North America segment profit jumped to $427M from $204M in Q2 and a $166M loss a year ago, on price increases and better mill performance.

$1.38B

Europe drove the headline loss

The $1.38B goodwill impairment is non-cash — it writes down what the EMEA business is carried at ahead of its planned separation. Excluding special items, adjusted operating EPS was +$0.37.

$843M

Cash profit held up

Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations was $843M, close to last year's $859M, even as GAAP results showed a $1.1B loss.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1.38B non-cash goodwill impairment on the Packaging Solutions EMEA business (before and after tax).
  • Special items overall added $2.70 per share of costs in the quarter, including EMEA separation costs and accelerated depreciation from site closures.
  • Discontinued operations — the former Global Cellulose Fibers business, sold in January 2026 — lost another $29M, taking the total net loss to $1,126M (EPS -$2.13).

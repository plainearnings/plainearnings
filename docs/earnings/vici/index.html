Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.8%

The rent roll keeps growing

Total revenue rose 5.8% to $1.07B, with built-in rent escalators doing most of the work — the triple-net model means tenants pay the property costs, not VICI.

$458M

A paper charge sank GAAP profit

The change in the CECL allowance — a non-cash reserve against lease and loan receivables — swung from a $20M release a year ago to a $458M charge, cutting net income attributable to common stockholders by 55% to $343M.

+7.5%

The cash measure grew anyway

AFFO, which adds the non-cash charge back, rose 7.5% to $685M, and management raised full-year AFFO guidance to $2,695M–$2,705M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $458M non-cash increase in the allowance for credit losses (CECL) — versus a $20M release in the same quarter last year. It is the sole reason GAAP net income fell 55%; AFFO excludes it.

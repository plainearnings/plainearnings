Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+27%

Segment profit jumped

Total segment operating income rose 27% to $3,876M: Experiences grew 8% to $3,105M, Entertainment more than doubled to $874M, and Sports narrowed its loss to $103M on the timing of College Football Playoff games.

-$138M

Streaming losses shrank fast

Entertainment direct-to-consumer (Disney+ and Hulu) lost $138M, versus a $984M loss a year ago, as price rises and ad revenue grew. Hulu added 1.2 million subscribers; Disney+ Core lost 1.3 million after a price increase.

$1.04

Profit rose even on flat revenue

Revenue was flat at $23,549M, but cost cuts — over $500M of savings realized in the quarter — lifted EPS 49% to $1.04 and free cash flow swung to $886M from -$2,155M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Disney's 'excluding certain items' EPS of $1.22 adds back $451M of amortization of the Fox (TFCF) and Hulu acquisition intangible assets ($0.18 per share) — a non-cash purchase-accounting charge Disney excludes every quarter. There were no restructuring or impairment charges this quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.5B

Experiences kept growing

Experiences — parks, resorts and cruises — grew operating income to $2,491M, with domestic parks up 13% to $1.8B on higher guest spending and consumer products up 14%.

$336M

Streaming profit jumped seven-fold

Entertainment operating income rose 61% to $1,258M: Direct-to-Consumer operating income jumped to $336M from $47M, with 180.7 million Disney+ and Hulu subscriptions (126.0 million Disney+). Content sales swung back to profit.

$314M

A tax benefit padded the bottom line

Reported net income was $3,401M ($1.81 a share) — helped by a $314M income-tax benefit, which is why net income came in above pre-tax income. Adjusted EPS, excluding such items, was $1.45, up 20%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Income taxes were a $314M benefit this quarter rather than an expense — the release does not attribute it to a single item — so reported net income ($3,401M) came in above pre-tax income ($3,087M).
  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $109M — a fraction of the $2,052M recorded a year ago, which had dragged the year-ago quarter to a small loss for Disney shareholders.

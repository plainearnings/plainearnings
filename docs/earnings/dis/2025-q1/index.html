Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.1B

Experiences stayed at full steam

Experiences — parks, resorts and cruises — earned $3,110M of operating income, flat with a year ago at a very high level, on revenue up 3% to $9.4B. It remains Disney's biggest profit engine by far.

$293M

Streaming swung to profit

Entertainment operating income nearly doubled to $1,703M: Direct-to-Consumer swung to a $293M profit from a $138M loss, content sales jumped 34% on theatrical hits, and Sports recovered to $247M from a $103M loss.

$1.76

Adjusted EPS rose 44%

Diluted EPS was $1.40, up 35%; excluding restructuring charges and acquisition amortization, adjusted EPS was $1.76, up 44%. Cash from operations rose 47% to $3.2B, though free cash flow was $739M after studio and parks spending.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $143M in the quarter.
  • Disney's adjusted EPS of $1.76 excludes the restructuring charges and $397M of amortization from the TFCF (Fox) and Hulu acquisitions; GAAP EPS was $1.40.

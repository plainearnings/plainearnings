Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Segment profit jumped

Total segment operating income rose 23% to $3.7B: Entertainment more than quadrupled to $1,067M as streaming swung to a profit and Inside Out 2 and Deadpool & Wolverine powered content sales, while Experiences eased 6% to $1,659M on softer international park attendance.

$253M

Streaming turned the corner

Entertainment's Direct-to-Consumer business earned $253M versus a $420M loss a year ago, on revenue up 15% to $5.8B; Disney's combined streaming businesses made $321M. Linear networks went the other way — operating income fell 38% to $498M.

$0.25

Charges masked the progress

Reported EPS was just $0.25 because the quarter absorbed $1,543M of restructuring and impairment charges. Excluding such items, adjusted EPS rose 39% to $1.14.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $1,543M — impairments of goodwill ($584M, at the general entertainment linear networks), retail assets ($328M), Star India ($210M), content ($187M) and equity investments ($165M), plus $69M of severance.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7%

Streaming and theaters carried growth

Revenue rose 7% to $25.2B. Entertainment revenue grew 10% — Avatar: Fire and Ash and Zootopia 2 in theaters, plus streaming subscribers paying higher rates — and Experiences (parks and cruises) grew 7%.

$2.6B

Parks are the profit engine

Experiences operating income rose 5% to $2.6B — more than Entertainment ($1.3B) and Sports ($652M) combined — as guests kept spending at the parks.

$1.27

Reported EPS fell — blame last year

GAAP EPS dropped from $1.81 to $1.27 even though pre-tax profit rose 9%: the year-ago quarter included a one-off ~$1.0B tax benefit from resolving a prior-year tax matter. Adjusted EPS, which strips out one-offs, rose 8% to $1.57.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $239M in the quarter.
  • The year-ago quarter included a one-off income-tax benefit of about $1.0B from the resolution of a prior-year tax matter — the main reason reported EPS fell year over year despite higher profit before tax.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Segment profit jumped

Total segment operating income rose 17% to $3,845M: Experiences grew 12% to $2,286M, Entertainment jumped 72% to $781M, and Sports eased 2% to $778M on the timing of College Football Playoff games.

1st

Entertainment streaming turned profitable

Disney's Entertainment direct-to-consumer business (Disney+ and Hulu) made its first quarterly profit, and Disney+ Core added more than 6 million subscribers. Disney still expected the combined streaming businesses to be profitable by Q4 of fiscal 2024.

$2.1B

Impairments masked the progress

Goodwill impairments of $2,052M — mainly Star India, which Disney agreed to contribute to a new joint venture with Reliance, plus its entertainment linear networks — swung the reported result to a $0.01-per-share loss, versus $1.21 excluding certain items.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Goodwill impairments of $2,052M — mainly Star India, written down as Disney agreed to contribute it to a new joint venture with Reliance, plus the entertainment linear TV networks. The charges are largely not tax deductible, which is why the tax bill stayed high relative to profit.

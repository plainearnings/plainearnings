Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5%

A record quarter at the parks

Revenue rose 5% to $26.0B. Experiences — parks, resorts and cruises — set a record with $10.0B of revenue, and Entertainment revenue grew 7% on a big theatrical slate (Zootopia 2, Avatar: Fire and Ash) and higher streaming rates.

$3.3B

Parks are the profit engine

Experiences operating income rose 6% to $3.3B — three times Entertainment's — as domestic park profit grew 8% on 1% higher attendance and 4% higher spending per guest.

-35%

Entertainment profit shrank

Entertainment operating income fell 35% to $1.1B: higher programming, production and marketing costs for the quarter's big releases outweighed the extra revenue. Sports profit also fell, partly on a temporary YouTube TV carriage suspension.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The temporary suspension of YouTube TV carriage cost the Sports segment about $110M of operating income in the quarter.
  • There were no restructuring and impairment charges this quarter (a year ago there were $143M).

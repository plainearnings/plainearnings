Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+64%

Entertainment profit jumped

Entertainment operating income rose 64% to $1.68B as streaming grew, lifting total segment profit 21% to $5.6B — well ahead of Disney's own guidance.

+20%

Parks kept packing them in

Experiences profit rose 20% to $3.0B, with spending per guest up 4% — helped by roughly $100M of tariff refunds booked in the quarter.

-17%

Sports had a soft quarter

Sports profit fell 17% to $858M: short NBA playoff series and a TV carriage dispute cost more than higher subscription and ad fees added.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $900M, dominated by an $812M writedown of Disney's 50% stake in A+E Global Media — which Disney has agreed to sell to Hearst for about $1.2B.
  • The year-ago quarter included a one-time $2.7B income-tax benefit, which is why reported EPS fell from $2.92 to $1.51 even though pre-tax profit rose 14%.

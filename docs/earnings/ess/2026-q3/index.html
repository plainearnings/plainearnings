Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+3.0%

Same-property growth

Revenue from apartments Essex owned in both periods rose 3.0%, led by Northern California at +5.4%; financial occupancy was 96.3%.

$4.00

Core FFO per share

Core funds from operations — the REIT cash-earnings measure — rose 0.8% to $4.00 a share, a cent above the guidance midpoint; the full-year Core FFO midpoint was raised to $16.16.

-41%

GAAP profit fell on comparisons

Net income available to common shareholders dropped to $98M from $165M mainly because Q3 2025 included $62M of gains on real estate sales, versus $7M this quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Essex sold three apartment communities in the quarter for a combined $111.9M pro-rata price and booked a $6.8M gain on sales, versus $62.3M of gains a year ago.
  • Expenses include a $10.7M impairment loss (none a year ago).
  • Essex agreed a $19.25M tenant settlement that also cancels $8.7M of claimed move-out balances, as disclosed with the quarter's results.

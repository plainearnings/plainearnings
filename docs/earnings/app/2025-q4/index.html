Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+66%

Growth stayed hot

Revenue grew 66% to $1.66B — a year ago the same platform did $999M in the quarter — as advertisers kept shifting budgets to AXON.

66%

Two-thirds kept as profit

Net income of $1.10B is 66% of revenue, up 84% in a year — an almost unheard-of margin for an ad business, because software costs almost nothing extra to deliver.

$1.31B

Cash in, buybacks out

Free cash flow was $1.31B in a single quarter, and AppLovin spent $482M buying back its own shares — $2.58B over the full year.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+71%

The platform kept compounding

Revenue from the AXON advertising platform rose 71% from a year ago to $1.16B, and the continuing business turned that into $840M of operating income — a 72% operating margin, because the platform's costs barely grow with its sales.

$189M

A goodwill write-down on the way out

In February AppLovin agreed to sell its own games/apps business. Writing that business's goodwill down by $189M ahead of the sale drove a $147M discontinued-operations loss — the reason reported net income ($576M) is well below the platform's own earnings ($724M).

$938M

Cash generation stayed extreme

Adjusted EBITDA — profit before interest, tax and non-cash charges — was $938M, 81 cents of every revenue dollar. Few companies anywhere convert sales into cash profit at that rate.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $147M loss from discontinued operations — the apps business AppLovin sold in June 2025 — including a $189M goodwill impairment recorded in this quarter ahead of the sale.

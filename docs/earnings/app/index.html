Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+55%

Growth is not slowing down

Revenue hit a record $2.18B, up 55% year over year — and AppLovin guided Q4 higher still, to roughly $2.37–2.40B.

66%

Two-thirds of revenue became profit

Net income was $1.44B on $2.18B of revenue. With the game studios sold, almost everything AppLovin sells now is software margin.

$1.4B

Buybacks shrink the share count

AppLovin spent $1.4B in the quarter repurchasing and withholding its own shares, ending with 331 million shares outstanding — the same profit split fewer ways.

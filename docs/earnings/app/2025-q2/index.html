Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+77%

Growth stayed hot

Revenue grew 77% to $1.26B as advertisers kept shifting budgets to AXON, AppLovin's AI ad platform. The comparison is clean — the sold apps business counts as discontinued operations in both periods, so this is the platform's own growth.

65%

Most of it became profit

Net income of $820M is 65% of revenue, up 164% in a year. The platform itself earned $772M from continuing operations; the apps business added a final $48M in the quarter it was sold.

$768M

Cash in, buybacks out

Free cash flow was $768M in a single quarter. AppLovin spent $1.27B buying back its own shares across the first half of 2025.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Income of $48M from the apps business AppLovin sold in June 2025, reported as discontinued operations — its last contribution, including the gain on the sale.

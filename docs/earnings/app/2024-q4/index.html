Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+73%

The platform's breakout year

Advertising revenue rose 73% from a year ago to $999M in the quarter — and $3.22B for the full year, up 75%. AXON, AppLovin's AI ad engine, kept finding better matches between advertisers and the users inside other companies' apps.

63%

Margins most companies can't match

The continuing platform turned $999M of revenue into $628M of operating income. Its total costs were just $371M — they barely grow with sales, so most of each new revenue dollar drops through to profit.

$61M

A tax benefit, not a tax bill

The continuing business recorded a $61M income-tax benefit this quarter, adding directly to profit. Together with a small $3M profit from the apps business being sold, reported net income reached $599M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $61M income-tax benefit in continuing operations — the tax line added to profit instead of subtracting from it.
  • Interest expense of $94M includes a loss on settling debt early, as AppLovin refinanced its borrowings in late 2024.

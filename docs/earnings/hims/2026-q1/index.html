Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

−8%

The U.S. engine stalled

U.S. revenue fell 8% as the weight-loss business pivoted from compounded GLP-1s to branded medicines. Monthly revenue per subscriber slipped to $80 from $85.

65%

Margins compressed

Gross margin fell to 65% from 73% a year ago — product costs rose in the pivot, and cost of revenue absorbed $28M of inventory write-downs tied to it.

$44M

Cash still flowed

Despite the $92M GAAP loss, Adjusted EBITDA was +$44M and free cash flow +$53M — much of the loss is one-off charges and non-cash items, listed below.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges of $33M tied to the 2026 shift in the U.S. weight-loss offering toward branded GLP-1 medicines — including $28M of inventory write-downs inside cost of revenue.
  • General and administrative costs include $15M of legal settlement costs and $13M of acquisition and transaction-related costs (per the company's Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation) — items it treats as non-recurring.

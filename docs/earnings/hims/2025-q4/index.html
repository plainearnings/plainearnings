Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+59%

A breakout year

Q4 revenue rose 28% to $618M, capping a year in which revenue jumped 59% to $2.35B and Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $318M. Monthly revenue per subscriber rose 11% to $83.

72%

Margins are thinner

Gross margin was 72%, down from 77% a year ago, as the product mix shifted — profitability now depends on marketing staying efficient at 39% of sales.

−$3M

Cash went into buildings

Adjusted EBITDA was +$66M, but free cash flow was −$3M in Q4: the company spent $59M in the quarter on property and equipment, building its own fulfillment and pharmacy capacity.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+79%

AI datacenters are the story

Datacenter & Communications revenue rose 79% to about $2.0B as AI datacenters transition from copper to optical connectivity. Industrial revenue fell 15% to $419M, mostly because of businesses Coherent sold. Management expects a first $3B revenue quarter by the end of fiscal 2027.

39.1%

Margins are climbing

GAAP gross margin rose to 39.1% from 36.6% a year ago on pricing, lower input costs and manufacturing yield gains. Coherent now targets its first quarter of non-GAAP gross margin above 45% before the end of calendar 2028.

$776M

Spending hard to keep up

Coherent spent $776M on property and equipment in the quarter and built up $737M of inventory, so operating cash flow was negative $93M despite the profit — it is investing ahead of expected demand. Second-quarter revenue is guided to $2.55B–$2.75B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The tax line was a $23M benefit rather than a cost, helped by deferred-tax effects — one reason net income grew faster than pre-tax income. Non-GAAP EPS of $2.09 excludes $74M of stock-based compensation, acquired-intangible amortization, restructuring and similar items that GAAP counts.

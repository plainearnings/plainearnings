Seed is a seasonal business: Q3 is the smallest quarter, and this one carried $99M of spin-off costs on top of a $268M R&D bill. For every $100 of sales, $63.75 went to the cost of the seed itself and $44.35 to selling and admin — together more than revenue — so the quarter ended $51.17 in the red per $100, funded from the balance sheet.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-$480M

A loss quarter is normal for seed — but this one was deeper

Q3 is the seasonally smallest quarter — most seed is sold in the first half — but the $480M GAAP loss still widened from $391M a year ago as $99M of separation costs hit and R&D rose 14% to $268M. Even excluding spin-off costs, adjusted operating EBITDA was a $269M loss, 33% worse than last year.

+6%

Prices rose while volumes fell

Volume fell 9% — lower corn acres in North America and Europe (drought), delayed monsoons in India, and the shift to a licensing model for Brazil soybeans — but price/mix rose 6% on demand for the latest hybrids and more licensing income. Corn sales still grew 4% to $607M; soybeans fell 26% to $133M.

$2.8B

The full year still points up

Year-to-date pro forma sales are up 4% to $8.53B with $1.01B of net income, and management reaffirmed full-year adjusted operating EBITDA of ~$2.8B and adjusted EPS of $2.25–$2.35. The board also started a $0.13 quarterly dividend and a $3B buyback — a standalone company acting like one.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Separation costs ($99M in Q3; $228M in the first nine months): legal, advisory and standalone-setup costs from the October 1 spin-off out of Corteva. Excluded from Adjusted Pro Forma Operating EBITDA.
  • Restructuring and asset-related charges, net: a $1M net credit in Q3 ($31M charge year-to-date).
  • A further ~$21M of one-time separation expenses are expected after the separation, per the release — not yet in these numbers.
  • Amortization of intangibles ($120M in Q3): a recurring non-cash charge on intangibles carried over from the DowDuPont/Corteva separation — excluded from adjusted EPS, but it recurs every quarter until the assets are fully amortized.
  • GAAP vs adjusted gap: a GAAP net loss of $480M vs an Adjusted Pro Forma Operating EBITDA loss of $269M. The bridge is mostly separation costs ($99M), amortization ($120M), R&D ($268M) and the tax benefit.

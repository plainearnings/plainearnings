Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

Azure is the growth engine

Azure and other cloud services grew 29% (30% in constant currency), and Microsoft Cloud overall reached $36.8B — about 57% of all revenue — as AI demand pulled customers onto the platform.

$36.8B

The cloud is most of Microsoft now

Microsoft Cloud revenue hit $36.8B, up 21%. The flip side of the growth story: the cost of revenue rose 17% as Microsoft built out the data centers behind it.

+61%

Xbox got an acquisition lift

Xbox content and services revenue jumped 61%, but 58 points of that came from the Activision acquisition — the underlying games business grew much more slowly, and Windows OEM revenue rose just 4%.

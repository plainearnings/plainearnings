Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+43%

Azure is still accelerating

Azure grew 43%, up from 40% the prior quarter, and passed $100B in revenue for the full fiscal year.

$678B

The backlog is enormous

Commercial remaining performance obligation hit $678B, up 84% — years of contracted cloud and AI spending not yet recognized.

$3.2B

The bottom line got a boost

A $3.2B gain on Microsoft's Anthropic investment lifted other income — and discrete items together added $0.27 to EPS.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $3.2B gain from Microsoft's investment in Anthropic, lifting other income and net income.
  • Lower-than-expected expenses from the Voluntary Retirement Program, partly offset by severance expense and impairment charges in Xbox — together the discrete items added $0.27 to diluted EPS.
  • $480M net gain from investments in OpenAI (+$0.07 EPS), excluded from non-GAAP results (non-GAAP net income was $35.3B, EPS $4.74).

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+39%

Azure growth stayed hot

Azure revenue grew 39% and Microsoft Cloud reached $46.7B, up 27%. Intelligent Cloud overall grew 26% to $29.9B.

$281.7B

A record fiscal year

Fiscal 2025 revenue was $281.7B, up 15%, and net income was $101.8B, up 16% — Microsoft's first year past $100B of profit.

$9.4B

Cash returns continued

Microsoft returned $9.4B to shareholders in dividends and share repurchases during the quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+40%

Azure growth accelerated

Azure revenue grew 40% and Microsoft Cloud reached $49.1B, up 26%. Customers have committed to $392B of future spending (commercial remaining performance obligation, +51%).

$3.1B

OpenAI losses cut into GAAP profit

Losses from investments in OpenAI reduced GAAP net income by $3.1B ($0.41 a share). Excluding that (non-GAAP), profit was $30.8B, up 22%, and EPS was $4.13 rather than $3.72.

$10.7B

Cash returns continued

Microsoft returned $10.7B to shareholders in dividends and share repurchases during the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Losses from investments in OpenAI decreased GAAP net income by $3.1B ($0.41 per share) this quarter. Non-GAAP net income excluding the OpenAI impact was $30.8B ($4.13 per share), up 22%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+48%

AI is the growth engine

Azure grew 48%, and Microsoft Cloud revenue hit $63.9B — about 69% of all revenue. The growth story is the data centers.

$677B

Customers have already committed

Commercial remaining performance obligation — contracted future cloud revenue — reached $677B, up 73%. That is years of AI spending already signed.

-3%

The consumer side is shrinking

Devices & Consumer fell 3%, dragged by Windows OEM and devices revenue down 22%. PCs, Xbox and search ads are now just 17% of revenue — a side business next to the cloud.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP results exclude a $207M net gain from investments in OpenAI in Q1 FY27 (vs a $3,086M net loss a year ago) — the reason GAAP net income (+32%) grew much faster than non-GAAP net income (+18%).

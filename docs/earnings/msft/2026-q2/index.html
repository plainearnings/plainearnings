Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$51.5B

The cloud crossed $50B

Microsoft Cloud revenue was $51.5B, up 26%, and Azure grew 39%. Customers have committed to a record $625B of future spending (commercial remaining performance obligation, +110%).

+60%

Headline profit is flattered by OpenAI

GAAP net income jumped 60% to $38.5B, but $7.6B of it was a paper gain on the OpenAI investment. Excluding that (non-GAAP), profit was $30.9B, up 23%, and EPS was $4.14 rather than $5.16.

-3%

The consumer side shrank

More Personal Computing — Windows, Xbox, Surface and search ads — fell 3% to $14.3B, the only declining piece, while the two business segments grew 16% and 29%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Net gains from investments in OpenAI increased GAAP net income by $7,583M ($1.02 per share). Non-GAAP net income excluding the OpenAI impact was $30,875M ($4.14 per share), up 23%.

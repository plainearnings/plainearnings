Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Azure accelerated

Azure and other cloud services grew 31% — faster than the quarter before — as AI demand added to regular cloud growth; Microsoft Cloud overall reached $35.1B, up 23%.

$61.9B

Growth was broad-based

All three segments grew at double-digit rates — Intelligent Cloud +21%, More Personal Computing +17% (Xbox content +62% on the Activision deal) and Productivity and Business Processes +12%.

35¢

A third of sales became profit

Net income rose 20% to $21.9B — about 35 cents of every revenue dollar — even as Microsoft kept spending heavily on the data centers behind its AI push.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+40%

Azure is still accelerating

Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 40%, Intelligent Cloud rose 30% to $34.7B, and Microsoft Cloud overall reached $54.5B — two-thirds of all revenue.

$30.9B

The data-center bill is staggering

Microsoft spent $30.9B on property and equipment in one quarter — nearly double a year ago — yet still generated $46.7B of operating cash flow and $15.8B of free cash flow.

$627B

Customers have pre-committed $627B

Commercial remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for but not yet paid — jumped 99% to $627B, a backlog that underwrites future cloud revenue.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Losses from Microsoft's investment in OpenAI trimmed just $14M from net income this quarter — a rounding error next to the $583M drag in the same quarter a year ago.

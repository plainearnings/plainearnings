Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Azure is the growth engine

Azure and other cloud services grew 31%, and Microsoft said its AI business has passed an annual revenue run rate of $13 billion — up 175% year over year.

$40.9B

The cloud is most of Microsoft now

Microsoft Cloud revenue hit $40.9B, up 21% — about 59% of all revenue. The growth story is the data centers.

+0%

The PC side stood still

More Personal Computing was flat at $14.7B: search ads (+21%) and Windows (+4%) offset a soft quarter for Xbox content and services (+2%).

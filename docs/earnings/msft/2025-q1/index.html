Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+33%

Azure is the growth engine

Azure and other cloud services grew 33%, and Microsoft Cloud overall reached $38.9B — about 59% of all revenue — as AI demand pulled customers onto the platform.

$38.9B

The cloud is most of Microsoft now

Microsoft Cloud revenue hit $38.9B, up 22%. The growth story is the data centers — and the cost of revenue rose 23% as Microsoft built them out.

+61%

Xbox got an acquisition lift

Xbox content and services revenue jumped 61%, but 53 points of that came from the Activision acquisition closing a year earlier — the underlying games business grew much more slowly.

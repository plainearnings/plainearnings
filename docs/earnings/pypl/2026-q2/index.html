Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$486B

The pipes keep growing

Total payment volume rose 10% to $486.4B and transactions rose 8% to 6.8 billion, with 439 million active accounts. Venmo and Braintree kept their momentum, and branded checkout stabilized.

+1%

What PayPal keeps barely grew

Transaction margin dollars rose just 1% to $3.9B (3% excluding interest on customer balances). Revenue grew 5%, but transaction costs grew faster — the margin squeeze is the story of the turnaround.

$1.5B

Buybacks and a guidance raise

PayPal returned $1.5B to shareholders via buybacks in the quarter ($6.0B over twelve months), paid a $0.14 dividend, and raised full-year non-GAAP guidance while reaffirming GAAP EPS guidance.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP EPS includes a roughly $0.07 negative impact from PayPal's strategic investment portfolio and crypto assets held for investment, versus a $0.01 positive impact a year ago — that swing sits inside the $117M of other expense.
  • Restructuring and other charges were $113M in the quarter as the transformation plan reshapes the company.

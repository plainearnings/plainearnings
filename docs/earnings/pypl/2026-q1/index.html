Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$464B

The payment pipes keep growing

Total payment volume rose 11% to $464.0B and transactions rose 7% to 6.5 billion, with 439 million active accounts.

+7%

Growth picked up a little

Revenue grew 7%: transaction revenues +7% and value-added services +10%. U.S. revenue rose 9% while international revenue grew 4%.

$1.5B

Shareholders are getting the cash

PayPal returned $1.5B to shareholders via buybacks in the quarter — $6.0B over twelve months — funding the returns from its cash pile even as GAAP profit fell.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include $101M of losses on PayPal's strategic investment portfolio (inside other expense), versus gains a year ago — a swing that explains much of the profit decline.
  • Restructuring and other charges were $74M in the quarter as new leadership simplifies the organization.

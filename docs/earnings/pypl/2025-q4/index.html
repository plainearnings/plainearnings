Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$475B

The payment pipes keep growing

Total payment volume rose 9% to $475.1B and transactions rose 2% to 6.8 billion, with 439 million active accounts.

+4%

Steady, slower growth

Revenue grew 4%: transaction revenues +3% and value-added services +10%. Transaction margin dollars rose 3% to $4.0B — the core business grew, just slowly.

$1.53

Investment gains flattered EPS

GAAP EPS rose 38% to $1.53, including a roughly $0.10 boost from PayPal's strategic investment portfolio and crypto assets, versus a $0.04 drag a year ago. Non-GAAP EPS was $1.23.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include gains on PayPal's strategic investment portfolio and crypto assets worth about $0.10 of EPS in the quarter, versus a roughly $0.04 drag in Q4 2024 — a swing that explains much of the GAAP profit jump.
  • Restructuring and other charges were $80M in the quarter as PayPal reshapes the organization; the board also announced Enrique Lores as the next President and CEO alongside these results.

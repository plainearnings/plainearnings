Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15.5%

Top line and margin both up

Revenue rose to $3.87B and operating margin widened from 21.9% to 25.4% — renewables growth plus stronger regulated and contracted generation.

$91M

Impairments and interest bite

Asset impairments of $91M and a $361M interest bill are the drag: of $982M of operating margin, interest alone consumed more than a third.

$553M

Attributable beats consolidated

Net income attributable to AES ($553M) is higher than the consolidated $527M because outside partners in AES subsidiaries absorbed a $26M loss — an unusual sign convention, disclosed in the footer.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$190.4B

Clients brought in record new money

Core net new assets hit a record $190.4B, up 38%, and clients opened 1.2 million new brokerage accounts, taking total client assets to $13.33 trillion.

+17%

Every revenue line grew

Net interest revenue rose 14%, asset management fees 14% and trading revenue 16% as daily average trades jumped 44%.

$1.62

Earnings hit a record

GAAP EPS rose 29% to $1.62. Excluding $151M of pre-tax transaction-related costs, adjusted EPS was $1.69.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $151M of pre-tax transaction-related costs in the quarter, made up of $141M amortization of acquired intangible assets and $10M of acquisition and integration costs. Adjusted net income was $3,021M versus $2,906M GAAP.

Financials adaptation: Schwab nets interest expense inside net interest revenue, so cost of revenue is the provision for credit losses on bank loans — $1M in Q3 2026 per the allowance rollforward in the Form 10-Q (the provision is netted inside Schwab's 'other revenue' line, so $1M is backed out of other expenses here to avoid double-counting). Operating income is income before taxes, other income is $0, and preferred dividends are reconciled through equity so net income is the $2,811M available to common stockholders.

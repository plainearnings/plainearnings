Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Fuel drove the top line

Total revenue rose 24% to $5,678M, but gallons sold rose just 2.5% — this was mostly higher fuel prices flowing through the pump price, plus 64 more stores than a year ago.

47.8¢

Margins did the real work

Fuel margin widened to 47.8 cents a gallon from 41.0 cents, and the inside margin ticked up to 42.2%. Together they lifted gross profit to $1,239M and net income 27% to $274M.

+4.8%

Pizza is still the inside engine

Prepared food same-store sales rose 4.8%, led by whole pizzas, at a 59.3% gross margin — the highest-margin food Casey's sells. Inside sales overall grew 5.6% to $1,778M.

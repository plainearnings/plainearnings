Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+60%

UFC is carrying the growth

UFC revenue rose 60% to $519M — media rights jumped on the new Skydance deal, and UFC 329 set records. UFC alone supplied almost all of TKO's $240M revenue increase.

41%

Margins expanded sharply

Adjusted EBITDA — the company's preferred profit measure, before interest, tax and non-cash charges — rose 54% to $556M, a 41% margin vs 32% a year ago.

$179M

Shareholders keep a minority slice

Of $278M of net income, $179M belongs to noncontrolling interests such as Endeavor; $99M was attributable to TKO shareholders ($1.33 per diluted Class A share). The company still raised full-year guidance and returned over $1.6B to equity holders this year.

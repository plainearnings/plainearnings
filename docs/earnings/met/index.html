Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Premiums drove real growth

Premiums rose 12% to $11.8B, helped by pension risk-transfer business — MetLife taking over pension obligations from employers — and solid group benefits sales.

+$698M

Hedges explain the GAAP jump

Derivative gains of $698M replaced a $929M loss a year ago. These hedges protect the balance sheet, but they make GAAP profit swing hard; adjusted EPS (+17%) is the calmer gauge.

$2.78

Adjusted earnings rose steadily

Adjusted EPS of $2.78 rose 17% year over year — the number management steers by, because it removes investment and derivative noise.

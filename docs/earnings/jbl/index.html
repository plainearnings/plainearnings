Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

AI infrastructure is the driver

Growth was led by AI and data-center manufacturing, with strength across automotive, healthcare and energy infrastructure too. Full-year revenue grew 21% to $36.0B.

$44.5B

Next year's guide is bold

For fiscal 2027 Jabil expects revenue of $44.5B (+24%) and core EPS of $17.55 (+34%), with core operating margin expanding to 6.1%.

$1.5B+

Cash generation is strong

Adjusted free cash flow topped $1.5B in fiscal 2026 — the asset-light model turns most profit into cash.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4.6%

Same stores earned more

Stores open in both years took in 2.7% more rent and spent 1.8% less to run, so their net operating income rose 4.6% to $505M. Occupancy ended the quarter at 94.0%.

$2.17

Cash profit per share rose

Core FFO — funds from operations, the cash-profit measure landlords use because it adds back property depreciation — rose 4.3% to $2.17 per share, and the company raised its full-year guide to $8.40-$8.50.

$327M

Buying back its own shares

Extra Space repurchased 2.4 million of its own shares for $327M in the quarter and paid a $1.62-per-share dividend — returning cash instead of chasing growth.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $6M loss on real estate assets held for sale and sold, versus a $105M loss in Q3 2025 — the main reason GAAP net income attributable to shareholders rose 49% to $248M.
  • A $14M impairment and loss on joint-venture interests, partly offset by $17M of earnings and dividend income from unconsolidated ventures.

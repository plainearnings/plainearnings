Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1.5%

Growth stalled after a cyber incident

Sales grew just 1.5% — US sales rose only 0.5% — in a quarter disrupted by the August 2026 cybersecurity incident the company says its teams worked to recover from.

$582M

One-off charges cut GAAP profit

GAAP profit attributable to shareholders fell 23% to $582M ($0.40 per share) as litigation ($81M), restructuring ($63M) and impairment ($39M) charges landed. Adjusted EPS, which strips those out, was $0.73 vs $0.75 a year ago.

-3% to -1%

Management expects a Q4 sales dip

The company guided Q4 sales down 3% to 1% vs a year ago, with full-year adjusted EPS of $3.16 to $3.18 — a soft finish after the disrupted quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Litigation-related charges of $81M, restructuring charges of $63M and a $39M intangible-asset impairment in the quarter — the main reasons GAAP EPS ($0.40) sits well below adjusted EPS ($0.73).
  • An August 2026 cybersecurity incident disrupted operations in the quarter; the company does not break out a separate dollar cost for it in the release.

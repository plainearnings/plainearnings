Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$4,317

Price did the heavy lifting

Newmont sold 1,211 thousand ounces of gold at a net realized $4,317 an ounce, so revenue rose 15% even though gold volumes fell 8% versus a year ago (1,319 thousand ounces).

$2.5B

Profit followed the price

Net income attributable to Newmont rose to $2,475M ($2.35 a share) from $1,832M a year ago, helped by $113M less income and mining tax than the prior quarter on the company's own sequential walk.

1,302k oz

Production steadied

Attributable gold production rose 1% from the second quarter to 1,302 thousand ounces as Cadia recovered from a seismic event and Brucejack improved — partly offset by planned maintenance at Lihir and Boddington.

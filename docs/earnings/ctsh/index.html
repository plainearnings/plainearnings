Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Financial Services carried the growth

Revenue rose 4.6% to $5.66B. Financial Services jumped 12.0% to $1.77B and Communications, Media and Technology rose 10.5% to $939M, while Health Sciences fell 1.1% and Products and Resources fell 0.9%. Trailing 12-month bookings were $28.7B, up 4%.

15.4%

Restructuring held back the margin

Operating income was $872M, a 15.4% margin, down from 16.0% a year ago. The $51M of Project Leap restructuring charges explains the gap: leaving those out, the company's adjusted operating margin was 16.3%, up from 16.0%.

$1.39

EPS more than doubled — off a low base

Net income was $627M and GAAP EPS $1.39, up from $0.56 a year ago. That comparison flatters the quarter: a year ago a one-time, non-cash tax charge cut EPS by $0.80. Adjusted EPS, which strips out such items, rose a steadier 7.2% to $1.49.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating costs include $51M of restructuring charges for Project Leap, a program launched in Q2 2026 to reshape Cognizant's operating model around AI — funding new offerings and partnerships and reshaping the workforce. The company now expects total Project Leap costs of $180M–$250M, with $140M–$160M of that in 2026.

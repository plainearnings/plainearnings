Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$12.3B

A record quarter

Revenue hit a record $12.25B, up 5% — and QCT chip revenue set its own record at $10.61B.

+15%

Cars keep climbing

Automotive chip revenue topped $1B for a second straight quarter, at $1.10B, and IoT grew 9% to $1.69B — diversification beyond phones continued.

$2.78 vs $3.50

Two profit pictures

GAAP EPS was $2.78 against $3.50 non-GAAP — share-based compensation and acquisition items sit in the GAAP column only. GAAP net income eased 6% YoY to $3.00B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Qualcomm completed its acquisition of Alphawave Semi in the quarter, accelerating its expansion into data-center chips.
  • The Q2 FY26 guidance in this release includes the estimated impact of industry-wide memory supply constraints on several handset customers.

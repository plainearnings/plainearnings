Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+89%

Cars are the growth engine

Automotive revenue reached $1,990M — Qualcomm's first $2B automotive quarter — up 89% from a year ago. Together, Automotive and IoT grew 36% YoY and are carrying the business while handsets shrink.

-26%

Handsets pulled revenue down

Handset revenue fell to $5,137M, down 26% YoY, dragging QCT down 8% and total revenue down 8% to $10,322M. Qualcomm renewed long-term QTL license agreements with Apple, Huawei and Transsion during the quarter.

$1.25B

Profit bounced back from last year's tax hit

Net income was $1,251M, against a $3,117M net loss a year ago — that loss came from a $6,088M tax expense, not from operations. This quarter tax was actually a $78M benefit on $1,173M of pre-tax profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and restructuring-related charges of $235M are the whole of the 'Other' operating-expense line this quarter; Qualcomm excludes them from non-GAAP results, along with $141M of acquisition-related charges spread across its cost lines.
  • GAAP revenue includes a $120M payment to a customer resulting from a recent legal settlement, recorded as unallocated revenue. That is why non-GAAP revenue of $10,442M is higher than GAAP revenue of $10,322M.
  • The year-ago quarter is a distorted comparison: in Q4 FY25 Qualcomm booked a $6,088M income tax expense (largely a valuation allowance on deferred tax assets) and a net loss of $3,117M. In fiscal 2026 it released that valuation allowance, a $5.7B tax benefit recorded earlier in the year.

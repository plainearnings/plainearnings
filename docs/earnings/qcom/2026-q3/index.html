Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-20%

Handsets dragged

Handset chip revenue fell to $5.09B as phone demand softened — the whole reason total revenue slipped 4% despite growth everywhere else.

+61%

Cars keep climbing

Automotive chip revenue set another record at $1.59B and IoT grew 9% to $1.83B — Qualcomm's diversification beyond phones is working.

$1,014M

Investments padded profit

Investment and other income of $1,014M — mostly securities gains — is why net income held at $2.0B even though operating income fell to $1.63B from $2.76B a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $68M 'Other' operating-expense line is restructuring and restructuring-related charges, substantially severance (10-Q) — excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Most of the $1,014M investment and other income was paper gains: $726M of net gains on marketable securities and $149M on deferred-compensation plan assets (10-Q), plus $771M attributable to the QSI strategic-investment segment in Qualcomm's non-GAAP bridge.
  • Qualcomm flagged broad input-cost inflation — wafers, assembly, test, packaging and memory — and said pricing actions to offset it should benefit gross margins over time.

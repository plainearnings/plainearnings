Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-13%

Handsets dragged

Handset chip revenue fell to $6.02B in a challenging memory environment — the whole reason total revenue slipped 3% despite growth elsewhere.

+38%

Cars keep climbing

Automotive chip revenue set a record at $1.33B and IoT grew 9% to $1.73B — combined, the two grew 20% YoY as diversification beyond phones continued.

$5.7B

A tax paper gain

Releasing a deferred-tax valuation allowance added a $5.7B benefit — profit on paper that brought no cash with it. Underlying non-GAAP profit was $2.84B, down 10% YoY.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include a $5.7B income-tax benefit ($5.33 a share) from releasing a valuation allowance set up in Q4 FY25, after new corporate alternative minimum tax guidance — the entire reason GAAP EPS ($6.88) dwarfs non-GAAP EPS ($2.65). The total tax benefit on the income statement is $5,138M.
  • The $29M 'Other' operating-expense line is restructuring and restructuring-related charges (10-Q) — excluded from non-GAAP results.

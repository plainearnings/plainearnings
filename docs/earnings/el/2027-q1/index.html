Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7%

Growth in nearly everything, everywhere

Net sales rose 7% to $3,715M (6% organic). Fragrance led the categories, up 19% to $861M, and every region grew on a reported basis — Mainland China sales rose 15% to $614M and Asia Pacific 9% to $952M. Management raised its fiscal 2027 outlook for sales, margin and EPS.

75.7%

Margins did the heavy lifting

Gross margin widened to 75.7% from 73.4% on savings from the Profit Recovery and Growth Plan, pricing and sales leverage, and operating margin jumped to 11.0% from 4.9%. Net earnings rose to $233M from $47M, also helped by the tax rate falling to 32.5% from 56.9%.

$0.72

Adjusted EPS is a bit higher than GAAP

Adjusted diluted EPS was $0.72 vs $0.64 reported. The gap is mainly $41M of returns charges tied to the restructuring program, which the company excludes from its adjusted operating income of $449M (vs $408M as reported); restructuring and other charges in operating expenses were $40M, down from $89M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges of $40M sit in operating expenses (down from $89M a year ago), tied to the restructuring program within the Profit Recovery and Growth Plan.
  • A further $41M of returns charges associated with restructuring and other activities is excluded from the company's category and regional operating results — adjusted operating income was $449M vs $408M as reported — and net sales include a $1M returns charge on the same program.

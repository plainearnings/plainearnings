Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$9.12B

AI capex, monetized

Record revenue, up 25%, with the highest sequential growth in company history — AI chip demand for leading-edge logic, DRAM and advanced packaging is pulling equipment orders.

50.3%

Margins keep climbing

Gross margin expanded for the 13th consecutive quarter year over year — Applied keeps capturing more value per tool as chips get harder to make.

$369M

A tax tailwind too

The tax bill was unusually light (12.7% of pre-tax income vs 30.6% a year ago), flattering GAAP EPS growth of 43%. Operating performance alone was still a record.

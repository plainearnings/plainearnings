Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Safety carried the growth

Safety and Industrial sales rose 9% to $3.2B, led by Personal Safety (+8%) and Fire Safety and Rescue, which nearly doubled to $206M. The other two segments were flat.

$2.63

GAAP beat adjusted — unusually

GAAP EPS of $2.63 came in above adjusted EPS of $2.50: this quarter's special items (including the other-income swing) added to profit instead of subtracting from it.

$9.10–$9.15

Guidance raised

3M lifted full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $9.10–$9.15 — management's signal that the underlying business, not just the one-offs, is improving.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter included a $161M loss on business divestitures and heavy litigation-related costs inside 'other expense' ($300M). This quarter those lines were quiet — other items were a net $112M of income — which explains most of the 65% profit jump.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

0%

Sales were flat at $941M

North America's 3% growth exactly offset a 7% decline in the rest of the world, which is mostly China.

81%

The China business is being sold down

On October 27 the company agreed that CPE will acquire 81% of its China water-treatment business; A. O. Smith keeps a 19% stake rather than full control of a difficult market.

$0.93

EPS barely moved

EPS of $0.93 was down just 1% despite flat sales, helped by North America margins and share buybacks; net income was $126M.

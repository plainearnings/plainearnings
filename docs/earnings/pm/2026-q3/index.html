Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Record operating income

Revenue rose 5.5% to a record $11.4B (7.1% organically), with $739M of price increases the biggest driver. Operating income rose 14% to a record $4.9B and gross margin expanded.

>40%

Smoke-free is now two-fifths of sales

Smoke-free products passed 40% of revenue, are sold in 110 markets, and shipped 2.7% more units — IQOS heated-tobacco units rose 1.5% to 41.5 billion and VEEV shipments jumped 62%.

$2.36

Adjusted EPS tells a different story

Reported EPS fell 5% to $2.12, hit by a $0.06 fair-value loss on equity stakes, a $0.04 Dutch pension buy-out loss and a tax rate up to 21.5%. Excluding adjusting items, EPS rose 5% to $2.36.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusting items cut reported EPS by $0.24: $0.13 of amortization of acquired intangibles, a $0.06 non-cash fair-value loss on equity security investments in India and Sri Lanka, a $0.04 Dutch pension plan buy-out loss, and $0.01 of tax related to Swedish Match financing. Adjusted EPS was $2.36.

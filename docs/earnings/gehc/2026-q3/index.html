Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

Diagnostics led growth

Pharmaceutical Diagnostics grew 13.0% (13.8% organically) at a 29.9% segment EBIT margin — the most profitable business — while the big imaging segment grew 6.1%.

$1.36

EPS jumped 39%

Diluted EPS of $1.36 rose $0.38 year over year as operating income grew 23% to $805M and the tax bill fell; adjusted EPS was $1.34.

1.03x

Orders kept pace

Orders grew 4.0% organically with a book-to-bill of 1.03 and a $23.7B backlog — demand is healthy, if not booming, across hospital capital budgets.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$8,782M

Refining had a monster quarter

Refining operating income was $8,782M, up from $1,649M a year ago, on near-record throughput of 3.0 million barrels a day — wide refining margins turned straight into profit.

$353M

Renewable diesel swung to profit

Diamond Green Diesel earned $353M versus a $28M loss a year ago, and Ethanol earned $272M on record production of 4.8 million gallons a day.

52%

Half the cash went back to shareholders

Valero generated $6.9B of operating cash flow, spent only $432M on capital investments, and paid out 52% of adjusted operating cash flow to stockholders.

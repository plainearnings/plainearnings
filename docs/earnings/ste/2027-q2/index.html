Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Life Sciences leads the quarter

Life Sciences revenue rose 18% to $171M as capital equipment sales jumped 53%, and its operating income climbed to $76M — strong enough for STERIS to raise the segment's full-year expectations.

$1,122M

Healthcare stays the core

Healthcare, about 71% of revenue, grew 9% to $1,122M on 10% growth in both services and consumables, with operating income of $269M.

~$25M

A new restructuring plan starts

STERIS approved another targeted restructuring — facility realignment and product rationalization, mainly in Healthcare — booking about $25M of charges in the quarter toward an expected $45M total, for about $9M of annual savings.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • About $25M of restructuring charges in the quarter, including a new targeted plan (facility realignment, product rationalization and organizational changes, mainly in Healthcare) expected to cost about $45M in total, of which about $10M is cash.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $65M in the quarter — a non-cash cost of past acquisitions that STERIS excludes from its adjusted results.

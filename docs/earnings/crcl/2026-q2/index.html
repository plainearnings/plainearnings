Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$73.3B

USDC kept growing

USDC in circulation ended the quarter at $73.3B, up 19% YoY, and onchain transaction volume hit $14.8 trillion — the network is expanding even as crypto markets cooled.

3.5%

Falling rates bite

The return Circle earns on USDC reserves fell 66 basis points to 3.5%. Balances grew fast enough to offset it this quarter, but reserve income — 95% of all revenue — rises and falls with interest rates.

$48M

Back in profit

Net income was $48M versus a $482M loss a year ago. The difference is mostly stock pay: $54M this quarter versus $435M in Q2 2025, when the IPO made years of awards vest at once.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses include a $5M charitable donation of shares to the Circle Foundation and $10M of one-off legal expenses — items Circle excludes from its non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $143M.
  • The year-ago comparison is distorted by the June 2025 IPO: Q2 2025 operating expenses included $435M of stock-based compensation, versus $54M this quarter.

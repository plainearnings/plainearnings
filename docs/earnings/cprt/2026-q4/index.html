Service revenues are shown at $969M (reported $970M) and general and administrative at $97M (reported $98M) so the rounded lines tie exactly. Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2.4%

Sales edged up

Revenue rose 2.4% to $1,152M. Service revenues — the auction fees that are the core business — rose 1.4% to $970M, while vehicle sales rose 8.3% to $183M.

41.8%

Costs squeezed the yards' margin

Gross margin fell from 45.3% to 41.8%. Facility (yard) operations cost $451M, up 7.7%, and the cost of vehicles Copart itself bought and resold rose 11.4% — both far faster than revenue. Gross profit fell 5.5% to $481M despite higher sales.

$327M

Profit fell even as sales rose

Net income fell 17% to $327M and EPS fell to $0.35 from $0.41. Higher yard and admin costs did most of the damage, and other income also dropped from $66M to $36M as interest income fell 21%.

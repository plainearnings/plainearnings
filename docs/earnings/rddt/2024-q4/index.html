Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

101.7M

Daily users hit 101.7 million

Daily Active Uniques rose 39% from a year ago to 101.7M — crossing 100M for the first time. International and logged-in users grew fastest, helped by machine translation into new languages.

+60%

Ads are the business

Advertising brought in $395M of the $428M total, up 60% from a year ago. Reddit's pitch — reach people inside communities built around what they're interested in — kept winning bigger brand budgets.

$85M

Stock pay is the quiet cost

Reddit paid $85M of this quarter's costs in stock rather than cash — much of it inside R&D. It sits inside the GAAP figures, but it dilutes shareholders, and it is a big reason the adjusted numbers look so much stronger than the reported ones.

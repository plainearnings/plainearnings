Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+68%

Growth stayed hot

Revenue growth hit 68%, powered by advertising (+74%) as daily users reached 116.0M, up 19% — Reddit is still getting bigger and better at selling ads against its communities.

$163M

Profit rose five-fold

Net income rose from $30M a year ago to $163M — 28% of revenue. Operating income was $139M, versus just $7M a year ago, because costs grew far more slowly than revenue.

$183M

Cash flow followed

Free cash flow was $183M — 31% of revenue — on operating cash flow of $185M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+70%

Growth accelerated

Revenue growth hit 70%, powered by advertising (+75%) as daily users reached 121.4M, up 19% — Reddit is still getting bigger and better at selling ads against its communities.

$252M

Profit more than tripled

Net income rose from $71M a year ago to $252M — 35% of revenue. Costs grew far more slowly than revenue, so most of the new money dropped straight to the bottom line.

$264M

Cash machine, now buying back stock

Free cash flow was $264M — 36% of revenue — and Reddit announced a $1B share buyback program alongside the results.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+69%

Growth accelerated

Revenue growth picked up to 69%, powered by advertising (+74%) as daily users hit 126.8M — Reddit is still getting bigger and better at selling ads against its communities.

$204M

Profit jumped eightfold

Net income rose from $26M a year ago to $204M. Total costs grew just 24% while revenue grew 69%, so most of the new revenue dropped straight to the bottom line.

$311M

A cash machine

Free cash flow was $311M — 47% of revenue — and Reddit ended the quarter holding $2.8B of cash and marketable securities.

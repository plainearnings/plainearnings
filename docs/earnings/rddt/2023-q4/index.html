Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+25%

Ads carried the quarter

Revenue rose 25% year-over-year to $250M, and $247M of it was advertising. Gross margin was 88% — Reddit's content is written by its users, so serving it is cheap.

$18.5M

A rare profit

Net income of $18.5M was Reddit's first profitable quarter since Q4 2021. The operating profit was a slim $3M — $16.5M of interest and other income did most of the work.

73.1M

The audience kept growing

Daily active uniques averaged 73.1M in the quarter. Weeks later, on February 22, 2024, Reddit filed its IPO prospectus — the document these figures come from — and listed on the NYSE that March.

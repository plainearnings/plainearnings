Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+54%

Ads are booming

Ad revenue rose 54% to $847M as brands pay up to reach Reddit's communities — and data licensing to AI companies added most of the other $48M.

130.3M

Daily users hit a record

Daily active uniques rose 12% to 130.3 million, and weekly users reached 515.2 million — a bigger audience to sell ads against every quarter.

$402M

45 cents of every dollar

Adjusted EBITDA was $402M — 45% of revenue — and free cash flow was $363M. Reddit also joined the S&P 500 index this quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

97.2M

Daily users surged 47%

Daily Active Uniques reached 97.2 million, up 47% from 66.0 million a year ago — the audience growth behind the 56% jump in ad revenue, as more people spent more time in Reddit's communities.

$29.9M

First GAAP profit as a public company

Net income was $29.9 million — an 8.6% margin — versus a $7.4 million loss a year ago, Reddit's first profitable quarter since its March 2024 IPO. Operating income itself was a thin $6.9 million; $23.0 million of interest and other income did the rest.

$83M

Stock pay still dwarfs the profit

Stock-based compensation and related taxes were $83.3 million in the quarter ($74.8 million of stock pay alone) — nearly three times net income and 24% of revenue. That gap is why Adjusted EBITDA of $94.1 million looks so much stronger than the $29.9 million GAAP profit.

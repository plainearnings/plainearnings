Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

91.2M

Daily users surged 51%

Daily Active Uniques reached 91.2 million, up 51% from 60.4 million a year ago, and Weekly Active Uniques rose 57% to 342.3 million — the audience growth behind the 41% jump in ad revenue.

$39.5M

Adjusted EBITDA turned strongly positive

Adjusted EBITDA was $39.5 million, a 14.0% margin, up $74.9 million from -$35.4 million a year ago. The net loss also narrowed to $10.1 million from $41.1 million a year ago, with gross margin improving to 89.5% from 84.2%.

$64.3M

Stock pay still dwarfs the result

Stock-based compensation was $64.3 million in the quarter — $66.8 million including related taxes — more than six times the $10.1 million net loss and 23% of revenue. That gap is why Adjusted EBITDA looks so much stronger than the GAAP loss; this quarter's charge is the normal post-IPO run rate, not the Q1 2024 IPO catch-up charge.

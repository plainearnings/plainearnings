Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+48%

First quarter as a public company

Revenue grew 48% from a year ago to $243M in the quarter Reddit listed on the NYSE, with Daily Active Uniques up 37% to 82.7 million and Weekly Active Uniques up 40% to 306.2 million.

$596M

One charge explains the loss

When Reddit went public in March, years of employee restricted stock units vested at once, booking $595.5M of stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes across the cost lines. That single non-cash-heavy charge is why a fast-growing ad business still posted a $575M GAAP net loss.

$10.0M

Adjusted EBITDA turned positive

Adjusted EBITDA was $10.0M — Reddit's first positive first quarter on that basis, versus -$50.2M a year ago — and operating cash flow was $32.1M despite the GAAP loss, with gross margin at 88.6%.

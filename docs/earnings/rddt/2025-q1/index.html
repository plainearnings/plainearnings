Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$26M

Profitable — a swing from the IPO quarter

A year earlier Reddit lost $575M in a single quarter — almost all of it a one-time stock-award charge triggered by its IPO ($578M of stock-based pay). With that gone, stock pay fell to $85M and the same business model produced a $26M profit.

+61%

Ads are the engine

Ad revenue rose 61% to $359M as advertisers followed the audience: daily users grew 31% to 108.1M, and advertisers paid more to reach them. Other revenue — mostly content licensing — rose 66% to $34M.

29%

Margins jumped with scale

Adjusted EBITDA — profit before interest, tax and non-cash charges — was $115M, 29 cents of every revenue dollar, up from a 4% margin a year ago. Scale is starting to do its work.

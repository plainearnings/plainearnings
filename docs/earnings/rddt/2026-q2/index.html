Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+61%

Eight straight quarters above 60%

Revenue growth stayed above 60% for the eighth consecutive quarter, driven by ads (+64%). International revenue grew even faster, +84% to $167M.

130.3M

Half a billion weekly users

Daily active uniques rose 18% to 130.3 million, and weekly users crossed half a billion at 514.6 million — more audience for the ad machine to sell.

$253M

Profit is scaling faster than revenue

Net income more than doubled to $253M — 31% of revenue — because revenue grew 61% while total costs grew about a third. Reddit also bought back $235M of its own stock.

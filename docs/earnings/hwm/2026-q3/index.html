Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

Fastest growth on record

Revenue rose 29% — 25% of it organic — led by commercial aerospace (+32%), gas turbines (+45%) and commercial transportation (+28%).

39.1%

Engine Products prints money

Engine Products' adjusted EBITDA margin widened to 39.1% as spares demand and gas-turbine volumes surged; group adjusted EBITDA rose 43% to $879M.

$5B

Cash is going back to holders

Free cash flow of $562M funded $500M of buybacks in the quarter, the board added $5B to the repurchase program, and the dividend rose 17% to $0.14.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Special items were small: $9M pre-tax ($7M of CAM/Brunner acquisition costs and $2M of restructuring) plus net discrete tax charges — adjusted net income was $584M vs $567M GAAP.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

A record quarter, and guidance raised

Sales rose 17% to a record $2.22B and GAAP profit rose to a record $426M. Management raised its full-year outlook after the strong quarter, citing broad-based organic growth plus contributions from recent acquisitions.

$1.44B

Electronic Instruments did the heavy lifting

EIG sales rose 16% to a record $1.44B with segment operating income of $407M. During the quarter Ametek also closed its acquisition of Indicor Instrumentation, adding another group of industrial technology businesses.

124%

Profit turned into cash, and then some

Operating cash flow rose 27% to $561M, and free cash flow (cash left after buying equipment) equaled 124% of net income — the company collected more cash than its accounting profit, a sign of disciplined working capital.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted results add back $0.36 per share of acquisition-related intangible amortization and deal/integration costs, taking adjusted EPS to $2.21 versus the $1.85 GAAP figure — the business itself was not changed by these items.

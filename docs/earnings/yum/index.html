Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.68B

The Pizza Hut sale is the quarter

Selling Pizza Hut booked a $1,683M pre-tax gain and took GAAP net income to $1,623M. Excluding Special Items, EPS was $1.56, and core operating profit excluding Pizza Hut grew 1%.

+8%

KFC carried the growth

KFC revenue rose 8% to $948M on $10.0B of system sales, with 878 gross new restaurants and unit growth of 7%. KFC is now well over half of Yum!'s ongoing business.

-4%

Taco Bell U.S. had a soft quarter

Taco Bell U.S. same-store sales fell 4% and division operating profit fell 5% to $255M, though Yum! says Taco Bell U.S. has outpaced the fast-food industry on same-store sales since the end of September.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax gain of $1,683M on selling Pizza Hut — mainland China to Yum China (August 7, 2026) and the rest of the world to LongRange Capital (September 1, 2026). It is why GAAP EPS was $5.92 versus $1.56 excluding Special Items.
  • Pizza Hut contributed $202M of revenue and $50M of operating profit through its sale dates; neither will recur, and year-ago comparisons included a full quarter of Pizza Hut.
  • Refranchising gain of $22M from selling company restaurants to franchisees.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

132M

The audience kept growing

Daily active users rose 35% to 132 million and hours engaged jumped 43% to 31 billion — even as a Russia platform ban and the age-check rollout tempered growth.

$1.73B

Bookings jumped 43%

Bookings — Robux cash sales — reached $1,731M, and free cash flow was $596M despite the accounting loss: players pay upfront, and $275M of costs were stock pay, not cash.

$57M

A legal charge sits in the loss

GAAP results include $57M of legal settlement expenses from negotiations with U.S. states over youth consumer-protection and digital-safety matters. Excluding them and other non-cash items, Adjusted EBITDA was $99M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP expenses include $57M of legal settlement expenses related to settlement negotiations with certain states regarding youth-related consumer protection and digital safety matters — Roblox excludes them from its Adjusted EBITDA of $99M.
  • Revenue is not the cash in: bookings were $1,731M but revenue was $1,442M. Roblox defers most bookings and recognizes them over the 27-month average paying-user lifetime, so reported revenue lags the cash by design.

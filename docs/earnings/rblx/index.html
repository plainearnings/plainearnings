Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

138M

The audience is enormous

Daily active users hit 138M and hours engaged reached nearly 34 billion in a single quarter — the platform's pull is not the problem.

−12%

Bookings slipped

Bookings — Robux cash sales — fell 12% YoY after 2025's viral surge, even as reported revenue rose 16%: revenue is recognized over ~27 months, so past bookings are still flowing through.

$364M

Losses, but cash-positive

Free cash flow was +$364M despite the $230M accounting loss: players pay upfront, and $298M of the quarter's costs were stock pay, not cash.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • G&A includes $49M of legal settlement expenses tied to state youth-safety and consumer-protection matters — management treats them as outside the ordinary course of business.
  • Revenue is not the cash in: bookings were $1,697M but revenue was $1,572M. Roblox defers most bookings and recognizes them over the 27-month average paying-user lifetime, so reported revenue lags the cash by design.

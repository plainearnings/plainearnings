Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Interest income surges

Net interest income rose 21% to $864M as the margin on earning assets widened by 16 basis points — the single biggest contributor to the 13% revenue growth.

$59.2T

Record fees on record assets

Servicing fees rose 10% and management fees 20%, both records, helped by higher market levels — assets under custody reached $59.2 trillion and assets under management $6.3 trillion.

34.6%

Eleventh straight quarter of operating leverage

Revenue grew faster than the 8% rise in expenses, expanding the pre-tax margin to 34.6% — the eleventh consecutive quarter of positive year-over-year operating leverage excluding notable items, of which there were none.

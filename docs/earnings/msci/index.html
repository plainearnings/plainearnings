Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

The market does MSCI's selling

Index revenue jumped 18%: $49M of the quarter's $99M revenue increase came from asset-based fees, which rise automatically when markets lift the value of index-tracking funds.

56.3%

A toll-booth margin

Once an index or dataset exists, serving one more customer costs little — hence a 56.3% operating margin and $39 of every $100 kept as profit after interest and tax.

-6%

Buybacks shrink the share count

Diluted shares fell to 72.3M from 76.6M a year ago — buybacks are why EPS grew 14% on 8% net income growth.

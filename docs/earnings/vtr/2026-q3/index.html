Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Senior housing is powering growth

Total company net operating income rose 18% YoY, led by the senior-housing operating portfolio, where same-store cash NOI grew 16% — 17% in the U.S.

$5.2B

Buying into the demographic wave

Ventas has closed $5.2B of senior-housing investments so far in 2026 and raised full-year guidance again, betting on the wave of Baby Boomers turning 80.

$0.99

Cash earnings vs accounting profit

GAAP net income was just $67M after $446M of depreciation and $161M of interest — but Normalized FFO, the cash-style measure, was $514M, or $0.99 per share, up 10%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Growth in both halves

Revenue rose 6% to $73,683M, driven by both Evernorth Health Services and Cigna Healthcare. Evernorth's adjusted revenues were $63,774M — pharmacy benefit services $37,276M and specialty and care services $26,498M.

85.0%

Medical costs held steady

Cigna Healthcare's medical care ratio — medical costs as a share of premiums — was 85.0%, close to 84.8% a year ago, and the segment's adjusted operating income rose 15%. Medical customers grew to 18.5M, up 2% from year-end.

$30.60

Full-year outlook raised

Cigna raised its 2026 outlook for adjusted income from operations to at least $30.60 per share, and narrowed its expected medical care ratio to 84.2% plus or minus 0.25 points. GAAP EPS of $5.47 compares with adjusted EPS of $7.98 — the gap is mainly amortization and special items.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted income from operations of $2,104M excludes special items booked in the quarter — an $85M strategic optimization (modernization) charge, $60M of litigation charges and $23M of integration and transaction costs, all pre-tax — as well as the $589M amortization of acquired intangibles and net investment losses.
  • A year ago, shareholders' net income was lifted by an after-tax gain on the sale of businesses; there was no such gain this quarter, which is the main reason GAAP profit fell even though adjusted profit was flat.

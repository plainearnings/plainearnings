Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-18%

Pool was the whole decline

Pool sales fell 18% to $292M and segment income fell 38% to $72M as channel partners completed an inventory realignment ahead of the 2027 pool season. Pentair guides Q4 sales up 7–9%.

28.7%

Water Solutions got more profitable

Water Solutions income rose 16% to $121M — a 28.7% return on sales, up 3.7 points — and Flow income rose 15% to $69M. The two growing segments cushioned Pool.

$242M

Adjusted profit vs GAAP profit

GAAP operating income was $196M (20.1% of sales). Excluding $46M of restructuring, transformation, amortization and deal costs, adjusted operating income was $242M (24.8%).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjustments between GAAP and adjusted results were $46M before tax: $11M restructuring and other, $16M transformation costs, $15M intangible amortization, and $3M of deal costs for the Taco acquisition (closed October 1, after quarter-end).
  • Results include about $8M of IEEPA (tariff) refunds in the quarter — a benefit that is not part of normal product sales.

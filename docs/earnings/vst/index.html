Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2,378M

Underlying profit jumped 50%

Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA rose more than 50% to $2,378M — Texas contributed $1,227M and the East $979M — on higher energy and capacity prices, the Lotus plants, and record summer demand in ERCOT and PJM.

~95%

The fleet showed up in the heat

In one of the hottest third quarters on record, Vistra's fleet ran at nearly 95% commercial availability — exactly when power is worth the most.

5,500 MW

Still buying growth

In October Vistra closed the Cogentrix acquisition — about 5,500 MW of gas generation — and authorized another $1.5B of share buybacks, taking remaining authorization to about $2.5B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $559M of unrealized gains on hedges expected to settle in future years sits inside GAAP net income — paper profit that flatters the headline. Vistra strips unrealized hedge moves out of its adjusted EBITDA.
  • $269M impairment of long-lived assets, including the partial impairment of the Moss Landing 350 battery facility in California.

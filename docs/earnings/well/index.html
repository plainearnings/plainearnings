Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+38%

Acquisitions transformed the top line

Revenue jumped to $3.72B as resident fees rose 51%. Welltower completed $13.3B of investments in the first nine months and had $21.0B closed or under contract for the year.

21.0%

Seniors housing is booming

Same-store NOI in the seniors housing operating portfolio grew 21.0%, on 320 basis points of occupancy growth and 5.3% growth in revenue per occupied room; total portfolio same-store NOI grew 15.8%.

$1.67

Cash earnings per share leapt

Normalized FFO — the cash-earnings yardstick for REITs — rose 24.6% per share to $1.67, and Welltower raised full-year guidance to $6.47–$6.53. Net debt was just 3.02x adjusted EBITDA.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $115M gain on real estate dispositions and acquisitions of controlling interests lifted net income (versus a $4M gain a year ago).
  • Impairment charges of $33M and a $7M loss on extinguishment of debt also sit in this quarter's expenses.

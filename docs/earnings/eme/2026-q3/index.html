Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+29%

Record quarter, led by construction

Revenue hit a record $5.54B, up 28.8% — 25.5% of that was organic growth, with acquisitions and the 2025 sale of the UK business making up the rest. US mechanical revenue rose 43% to $2.55B and US electrical 44% to $1.85B. The company raised its 2026 revenue guidance to $20.6B–$21.0B.

$18.57B

The order book is the story

Remaining performance obligations — work under contract but not yet done — reached a record $18.57B, up 47.2% from $12.61B a year ago, on new awards across most of the sectors EMCOR serves. That is more than three quarters of revenue already booked.

$9.60

One impairment hides a cleaner number

GAAP diluted EPS was $9.04, up 37.6%. Excluding a $34M non-cash impairment of intangible assets ($24.7M after tax), non-GAAP EPS was $9.60 and non-GAAP operating income $579.8M at a 10.5% margin. The company also added $500M to its share buyback program.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $34.0M non-cash impairment loss on identifiable intangible assets ($24.7M after tax) is included in operating income. Excluding it, non-GAAP operating income was $579.8M and non-GAAP net income was $424.7M, or $9.60 per diluted share.

Three things to take away

29.5%

Operating income of $3.02B was 29.5% of sales — a high margin for a manufacturing business, even while integrating a large acquisition.

$2.05B

Profit rose from $1.26B a year ago to $2.05B, even with interest expense nearly tripling to $212M on acquisition debt.

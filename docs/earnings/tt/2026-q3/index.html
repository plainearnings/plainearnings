Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

The Americas did the heavy lifting

Americas revenue rose 21% to $5,648M with a 21.9% operating margin, across commercial HVAC, residential and transport refrigeration. EMEA grew just 1% and Asia Pacific 8%, with margins down in both.

$12.5B

Future work is piling up

Bookings rose 21% to $7.2B — more than the quarter's revenue in every segment — and the backlog reached $12.5B, up 73%, with Americas commercial HVAC backlog up about 80%.

$4.86

Adjusted EPS rose 25%

Adjusted continuing EPS was $4.86, up 25%, vs $4.79 GAAP continuing EPS. Year-to-date free cash flow was $2.8B, up about 60%, funding $930M of dividends, $1.1B of acquisitions and $1.4B of buybacks so far this year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Pre-tax non-GAAP adjustments were $21M in the quarter: $16M of amortization of an acquired backlog intangible, $4M of restructuring costs and $1M of deal costs — the gap between GAAP operating income of $1,343M and adjusted operating income of $1,364M.
  • Discontinued operations lost $11M after tax in the quarter (legacy businesses in Chapter 11-related separation), reducing net EPS to $4.74 from $4.79 for continuing operations.

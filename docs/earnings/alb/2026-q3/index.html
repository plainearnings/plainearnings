Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+186%

It's the lithium price, again

Adjusted EBITDA nearly tripled to $646M. Nothing about the cost base explains that — lithium prices do. Energy Storage EBITDA alone rose 351%.

$205M

The JVs out-earn the parent

Equity earnings from joint ventures (mainly Talison) exceeded Albemarle's own operating income. The JV structure is where much of the lithium profit is booked.

$300–350M

Capex discipline

Full-year capex guidance was cut again (from ~$500M) on capital-efficiency gains and resequenced projects — discipline while prices recover, with volumes guided to 225–235 kt LCE for the year.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+19%

Growth came back

Revenue rose 19% to $3,786M, with Industrial & IoT up 39% and Communications Infrastructure up 54%.

57.8%

High gross margin

GAAP gross margin was 57.8% — $2,189M of gross profit on $3,786M of revenue.

$5B

More buybacks authorised

The board authorised a new $5B share repurchase program, on top of $1.3B remaining under the prior one.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Non-GAAP results exclude $114M of share-based compensation, $36M of purchase-price-accounting effects and other items — the gap between GAAP EPS of $3.48 and non-GAAP EPS of $4.20.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

0%

Revenue flat, regions split

Revenue was flat at $12,429M: Greater China grew 5%, North America and Asia Pacific & Latin America 3% each, while Europe, Middle East & Africa fell 3% and Converse dropped 19%.

44.8%

Margin widened, spending rose faster

Gross margin rose 150 basis points to 44.8% on strategic pricing and lower freight and logistics costs — but demand creation spending rose 10% and operating overhead 6%, so operating income fell.

$0.77

Profit eased against a strong comparison

Net income fell 5% to $1,172M and EPS 3% to $0.77. Inventories fell 13% year over year to $7.7B as Nike cleared the prior year's excess stock.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7%

Wholesale carried the quarter

Revenue rose 1% to $11.7B (down 1% without currency help). Wholesale revenue grew 7% and North America 4%, but Nike Direct fell 4% — digital sales dropped 12% — and Greater China fell 9%.

42.2%

Discounts and tariffs squeezed the margin

Gross margin fell 320 basis points to 42.2%, mainly on lower average selling prices — higher discounts and channel mix — plus higher tariffs in North America. Inventories were $8.1B, down 2%.

$0.49

Profit fell 31%

Net income fell 31% to $727M and EPS to $0.49. Selling and administrative expense fell 1% to $4.0B — demand creation down 3% and overhead flat — but not enough to offset the margin squeeze.

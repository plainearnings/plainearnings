Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$986M

A tariff refund remade the quarter

The expected recovery of IEEPA tariffs added $986M back into gross profit — roughly 900 basis points of margin and $0.52 of the $0.72 EPS. Strip it out and this was a flat, difficult quarter.

−1%

Sales still shrinking

Revenue fell 1% to $11.0B, unevenly: North America grew 3%, but Greater China fell 12% and Converse 32%. Wholesale revenue rose 4% while Nike's own stores and app fell 7%.

49.2%

Margin jumped — on paper

Gross margin leapt from 40.3% to 49.2% — almost entirely the tariff recovery, an expected refund rather than money already in the bank. It flatters this quarter and will not repeat.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The expected recovery of $986M of IEEPA tariffs (ruled unlawful) is booked inside cost of sales — it lifted gross margin by about 900 basis points and EPS by $0.52, and accounts for most of the jump from last year's $211M net income to this year's $1,069M. It is an expected refund, not cash already received, and it will not repeat.

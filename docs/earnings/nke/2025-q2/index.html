Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-8%

Revenue shrank in the reset

Revenue fell 8% to $12.4B: NIKE Direct dropped 13% to $5.0B as digital sales fell 21% on fewer promotions, and wholesale fell 3% to $6.9B. Every region declined; North America fell 8%.

43.6%

Discounts squeezed margins

Gross margin narrowed 100 basis points to 43.6% on higher discounts and inventory reserves; inventories were flat at $8.0B. Demand creation spending rose 1% while operating overhead fell 5%.

$1.2B

Profit fell by a quarter

Net income fell 26% to $1.2B and EPS to $0.78 from $1.03. New CEO Elliott Hill, who took over in October, framed the quarter as the start of a turnaround built on sport and full-price selling.

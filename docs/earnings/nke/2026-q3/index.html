Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

0%

Sales went sideways

Revenue was flat at $11.3B (-3% without currency help). Wholesale revenue rose 5% and North America grew 3%, but Nike Direct fell 4%, Greater China fell 7% and Converse dropped 35%.

40.2%

Tariffs squeezed the margin

Gross margin fell 130 basis points to 40.2%, primarily on higher tariffs in North America. Inventories were $7.5B, down 1% — cleaner, but carrying higher tariff-driven product costs.

20.0%

The tax rate explains the profit drop

Pre-tax profit fell 23%, but net income fell 35% to $520M: last year's 5.9% tax rate included a one-time, non-cash deferred tax benefit, while this year's rate was 20.0%. EPS was $0.35.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter's unusually low 5.9% tax rate included a one-time, non-cash deferred tax benefit from U.S. tax regulations on foreign-currency gains and losses — the main reason net income fell 35% while pre-tax profit fell 23%.
  • Operating overhead included employee severance costs from the 'Win Now' actions (the amount was not broken out).

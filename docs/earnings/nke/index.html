Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2%

North America is stabilizing

Home-market revenue rose 2% to $5.1B — the only region in growth — and wholesale overall fell just 1%, suggesting the product reset is landing with retailers.

-22%

Greater China is the drag

China revenue fell 22% (26% before currency) and Converse dropped 28%. Nike says it is repositioning both brands rather than discounting its way out.

$2.5B

Pace: the big cost program

Nike's new transformation program targets about $2.5B of savings through fiscal 2031, at roughly $1.0B of charges along the way. Overhead already fell 6% this quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Nike's 'Pace' transformation brings severance and employee-related charges — about $0.3B expected in fiscal 2027 (roughly $0.15 per share) — that sit inside its normal cost lines rather than on a separate line. Nike's adjusted EPS leaves them out; the GAAP diluted EPS here is $0.48.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1%

Wholesale carried the quarter

Revenue rose 1% to $12.4B (flat without currency help). Wholesale revenue grew 8% and North America 9%, but Nike Direct fell 8% — digital sales dropped 14% — and Greater China fell 17%.

40.6%

Tariffs squeezed the margin

Gross margin fell 300 basis points to 40.6%, primarily on higher tariffs in North America. Inventories were $7.7B, down 3% — fewer units, but carrying higher tariff-driven product costs.

$0.53

Profit fell 32%

Net income fell 32% to $792M and EPS to $0.53. Demand creation spending rose 13% as Nike leans into brand marketing for its 'Win Now' reset, while operating overhead fell 4%.

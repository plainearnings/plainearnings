Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

All three businesses grew sales

Paint Stores rose 5%, Consumer Brands 22% (helped by buying Suvinil in Brazil) and Performance Coatings 9%, taking total sales to $6.86B.

47.8%

Margins were squeezed by costs

Gross margin fell from 49.2% to 47.8% as raw material and supply chain costs rose, so net income slipped 1% to $821M despite higher sales.

$749M

Cash went back to shareholders

Sherwin-Williams returned $749M to shareholders in the quarter through dividends and share buybacks.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted EPS of $3.63 excludes Valspar acquisition-related amortization and severance and restructuring costs; GAAP EPS was $3.38. Full-year guidance includes $0.82 per share of Valspar amortization and $0.11 of severance and restructuring.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

7.54 Bcfe/d

Production at the high end

Output averaged 7.54 billion cubic feet equivalent per day — 6.9 Bcf/d of gas plus 17 MBbls/d of oil and 87 MBbls/d of NGLs — at the high end of full-year guidance. Hedging improved realized prices by $0.46 per Mcfe.

$1.46

Adjusted EPS tells the steadier story

Adjusted net income was $338M, or $1.46 a share, versus $519M and $2.24 GAAP — the gap is mostly the $464M of derivative gains that adjusted results remove. Adjusted EBITDAX was $1,229M.

$900M

Buybacks and a low debt load

Expand repurchased $305M of stock in the quarter, about $900M year to date (roughly 4% of shares). Net debt was $4.7B, or 0.8x leverage, and the quarterly dividend is $0.575 a share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Derivative gains of $464M are included in GAAP revenue; adjusted results exclude them, which explains most of the gap between GAAP net income ($519M) and adjusted net income ($338M).
  • The Twin Eagle Holdings acquisition closed on September 16, 2026, adding marketing and midstream capabilities — and $349M of goodwill and $1.1B of intangible assets — so only about two weeks of it sit in this quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$329M

The float keeps paying

Interest earned on the $36.9B of client money ADP holds between payday deductions and payouts rose 15% to $329M, as the yield on those balances climbed 30bp to 3.6% — high rates are a real profit engine.

26.8%

Margins expanding, guidance raised

Adjusted EBIT margin expanded 130bp to 26.8% — ADP beat its own plan on revenue, margin and EPS, and raised full-year guidance for revenue and adjusted EPS growth.

771k

PEO scale keeps growing

The PEO now pays about 771,000 worksite employees (+2%). Its reported revenue overstates the business — much of the $1.8B is zero-margin benefits money passing through.

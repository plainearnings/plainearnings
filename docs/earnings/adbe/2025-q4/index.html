Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$6.19B

A record quarter to close a record year

Revenue hit a record $6,194M, up 10% (10% in constant currency), and GAAP net income rose to $1,856M, up 10% year over year. For the full fiscal year, revenue was $23.77B, up 11%, and remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — stood at $22.52B.

+12%

Subscriptions keep compounding

Subscription revenue grew 12% to $5.99B, and total ARR reached $25.20B at year end, up 11.5%. Business Professionals & Consumers subscription revenue grew 15% to $1.72B, faster than the creative professional line's 11% to $4.25B.

$3.16B

Cash flow hit a record too

Operating cash flow was a quarterly record $3.16B — more than the quarter's $1.86B of net income, because customers pay for subscriptions up front. Adobe also repurchased 7.2 million shares in the quarter, 30.8 million for the full year.

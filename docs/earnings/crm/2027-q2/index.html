Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Subscriptions keep compounding

Subscription and support revenue grew 12% to $10.8B — 95% of all revenue — helped by $440M from the newly acquired Informatica.

$2.6B

A paper gain did the heavy lifting

A $2.6B mark-to-market gain on strategic investments is why GAAP EPS jumped to $4.29 from $1.96. Strip it out and operating income was flat — the gain is real on paper, but it isn't the software business selling more.

-15%

Far fewer shares outstanding

Buybacks cut the diluted share count from 962M to 821M in a year, so each remaining share claims a bigger slice of the same profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $2,613M gain on strategic investments — a paper (mark-to-market) gain, not cash from customers — accounts for most of the jump in net income from $1,887M to $3,526M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.4B

Agentforce is scaling

Agentforce and Data 360 ARR reached nearly $1.4B, up 114% YoY, with more than 9,500 paid Agentforce deals closed — Salesforce's AI agents are becoming a real revenue line, though still small next to $9.7B of subscription revenue.

$59.5B

Backlog keeps climbing

Remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for but not yet paid — rose 12% to $59.5B, with $29.4B of it due within a year. Future revenue is unusually visible.

$263M

A paper gain padded profit

Gains on strategic investments added $263M before tax. Core operating income was $2.19B, up 16% from a year ago — solid growth even without the gain.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $263M gain on strategic investments — paper (mark-to-market) gains on stakes Salesforce holds in other companies.
  • Restructuring charges of $260M — up from $56M a year ago — sit inside operating expenses.

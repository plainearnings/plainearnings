Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

19.1%

Margins hit a record

GAAP operating margin reached 19.1%, up 190 basis points from a year ago, as operating income rose 21% to $1.78B on revenue up just 8% — Salesforce grew profit much faster than sales, and raised its full-year margin guidance.

$26.5B

Backlog keeps climbing

Current remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for and will pay for within a year — rose 10% to $26.5B. That locks in a large share of future subscription revenue in advance.

$99M

Restructuring doubled

Restructuring charges were $99M, double the $49M a year ago, and losses on strategic investments cost another $37M. Even after both, net income rose 13% to $1.43B, and Salesforce returned $4.3B to stockholders through share repurchases plus $0.4B in dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $37M loss on strategic investments, net — paper (mark-to-market) losses on stakes Salesforce holds in other companies.
  • Restructuring charges of $99M — up from $49M a year ago — sit inside operating expenses.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1B+

AI revenue passed $1B

Data Cloud and AI annual recurring revenue passed $1B, up more than 120% YoY. Salesforce had closed over 8,000 Agentforce deals since launch, half of them paid.

$29.6B

Backlog keeps climbing

Current remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for and will pay for within a year — rose 12% to $29.6B. Future revenue is unusually visible.

19.8%

Margins inched up

GAAP operating margin reached 19.8%, up from 18.7% a year ago, and operating cash flow hit $6.5B. During the quarter Salesforce also announced its agreement to acquire Informatica.

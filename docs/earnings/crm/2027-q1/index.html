Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+13%

Growth, with an acquisition assist

Revenue rose 13% to a record $11.1B, but $444M of it came from Informatica, acquired in 2025 — roughly 4 points of the growth was bought, not organic.

$1.2B

Agentforce is becoming real money

Agentforce ARR passed $1.2B, up 205%, and combined AI and Data ARR reached $3.4B — the agent story is now a measurable slice of the business, not just a pitch.

$2.42

Profit per share jumped 52%

GAAP EPS rose 52% to $2.42, helped by a $558M paper gain on investment stakes and a shrinking share count after heavy buybacks.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $558M gain on strategic investments — paper gains on stakes Salesforce holds in other companies; it added $0.49 to diluted EPS.
  • Revenue includes $444M from Informatica, acquired in late 2025 — roughly 4 points of the 13% growth is the acquisition, not organic growth.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$72.4B

Backlog at a record

Remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for but not yet paid — hit $72.4B, up 14%, with $35.1B of it due within a year. Future revenue is unusually visible.

$811M

A paper gain padded profit

Gains on strategic investments added $811M before tax ($0.66 a share after tax). Core operating income was $1.87B, barely up from $1.82B a year ago — the gain is real on paper, but it isn't the software business selling more.

$800M

Agentforce is scaling

Agentforce ARR reached $800M, up 169% YoY, with 29,000 deals closed — Salesforce's AI agents are becoming a real revenue line, though still small next to $10.7B of subscription revenue.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $811M gain on strategic investments — paper (mark-to-market) gains on stakes Salesforce holds in other companies; it added $0.66 to diluted EPS.
  • Revenue includes $399M from Informatica, acquired in late 2025 — roughly 3–4 points of the 12% growth is the acquisition, not organic growth.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.2B

AI revenue is scaling

Data Cloud and AI annual recurring revenue passed $1.2B, up 120% YoY. Salesforce has closed over 12,500 Agentforce deals since launch, more than 6,000 of them paid.

$29.4B

Backlog keeps climbing

Current remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for and will pay for within a year — rose 11% to $29.4B. Future revenue is unusually visible.

22.8%

Margins kept expanding

GAAP operating margin reached 22.8% — a tenth consecutive quarter of expansion — and net income rose 32% to $1.89B. Salesforce also added $20B to its buyback program.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

17.2%

The margin plan worked

GAAP operating margin reached 17.2%, up from 5.9% a year ago, as the January 2023 restructuring cut spending — research, sales and admin costs all fell in dollar terms even as revenue grew 11%.

$1.53B

Cash poured in

Operating cash flow was $1.53B, up 389% year-over-year, and free cash flow was $1.37B — the profit is turning into cash, not just accounting gains.

$23.9B

The order book kept growing

Current remaining performance obligation — work customers have contracted for but Salesforce has not yet billed as revenue — reached $23.9B, up 14% year-over-year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $55M of restructuring charges in the quarter from the January 2023 plan (workforce cuts and office exits); $815M was booked across the first nine months of FY24.
  • A $72M mark-down on strategic investments — stakes Salesforce holds in other companies — which cut GAAP EPS by about $0.06.

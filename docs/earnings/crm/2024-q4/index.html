Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11%

A steady finish to the fiscal year

Revenue rose 11% from a year ago to $9.29B, led by subscription and support at $8.75B, up 12%. Professional services shrank 9% to $539M. Current remaining performance obligations — work signed but not yet billed — rose 12% to $27.6B.

$1.47

From a loss to $1.47 a share

Net income was $1.45B, versus a $98M loss a year ago when restructuring charges peaked; GAAP EPS swung from -$0.10 to $1.47. Fourth-quarter operating cash flow was $3.40B, up 22%, and free cash flow $3.26B.

$0.40

Salesforce's first-ever dividend

Salesforce declared its first quarterly dividend of $0.40 a share and added $10B to its share-buyback authorization — a marker of its shift toward returning cash to shareholders as growth settles to around 10%.

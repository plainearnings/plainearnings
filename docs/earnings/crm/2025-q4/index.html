Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$900M

AI revenue became real

Data Cloud and AI recurring revenue reached $900M a year, up 120%, and Salesforce had closed 5,000 Agentforce deals since October — more than 3,000 of them paid. The AI-agent business was small next to $10B of quarterly revenue, but it was the fastest-growing thing in the company.

$63B

A $63B backlog

Remaining performance obligation — work customers have signed for but Salesforce hasn't yet delivered or billed — reached $63.4B, up 11%. The current portion, due within a year, rose 9% to $30.2B, locking in most of next year's growth in advance.

$298M

Restructuring weighed, cash flow didn't

A $298M restructuring charge — severance and facility costs — sat in running costs this quarter. Even with it, net income rose 18% to $1.71B, and for the full year Salesforce generated a record $13.1B of operating cash flow.

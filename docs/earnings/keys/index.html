Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.09B

Orders are outrunning sales

Orders of $2.09B exceeded revenue for a second straight quarter — a backlog signal that demand for AI data-center testing is still building.

~$1.94B

Q4 guided to another record

Keysight guided fiscal Q4 revenue to $1.93–1.95B (about +37% YoY at the midpoint) and raised its full-year outlook.

Free cash flow was $403M in the quarter, and the cash pile reached $2.62B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

85%

Margins are extraordinary

Gross margin was 85.0%, up from 29.8% a year ago. Scarce flash supply and long-term customer deals let Sandisk keep almost every sales dollar after production costs.

$5.0B

Cash is pouring back to owners

Sandisk spent $5.0B buying back about 3.2M shares in the quarter and announced an 8-for-1 stock split effective November 19, 2026.

$513M

A paper gain flatters profit

Net income includes a $513M mark-to-market gain on equity securities. Excluding it and stock pay, non-GAAP EPS was $49.51 versus the GAAP $52.60.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5%

Sales rose to $2.33B

Organic growth of 3.7% plus currency gains; the Materials Group did the heavy lifting with sales up 8.5%.

$2.42

Adjusted EPS of $2.42

Up 2% — slower than sales, reflecting margin pressure in Materials (adjusted margin down 110 basis points) as growth categories carry start-up costs.

Full-year EPS guidance set

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$6.5B

A non-cash reckoning

The goodwill impairment does not use cash, but it formally writes down the value of past acquisitions. Excluding it and other items, adjusted operating income was $1,512M — still down from $1,822M a year ago as margins compressed.

0%

Organic revenue went flat

Organic revenue was flat overall: Merchant Solutions fell 2% organically while Financial Solutions grew 2%. Fiserv affirmed its full-year outlook of -1% to 0% organic growth and $7.20–$7.40 adjusted EPS.

$1B

Cost cuts are the plan

Fiserv raised its 'Project Elevate' savings target from $500M to $1B after a portfolio review, sold its student-loan servicing business, and plans to resume buybacks with $500M of repurchases in Q4.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $6,452M goodwill impairment is non-cash and sits in the Corporate and Other segment; GAAP operating margin was -105.7% because of it (adjusted operating margin: 31.2%).
  • Adjusted results also exclude $297M of amortization of acquisition-related intangibles, $158M of 'One Fiserv' transformation costs and $83M of severance.

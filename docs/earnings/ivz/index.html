Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.38B

Look at 'net revenues' instead

About $0.49B of GAAP revenue passes straight through to third parties. Invesco's own yardstick, net revenues, was $1.38B, up 16.5%, and its adjusted operating margin on that was 40.7%.

+61%

Operating leverage showed up

GAAP operating income jumped 61% to $437M as the QQQ fee change lifted revenue while employee compensation actually fell 5%.

$2.54T

Assets keep growing

Assets under management reached $2.54 trillion, up 19% year over year, on market gains and inflows.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • QQQ's conversion to an open-end fund ETF in Q4 2025 reshaped the revenue lines: management fees gained roughly $328M year over year while service and distribution fees lost about $88M of pass-through revenue. It is a presentation change more than new business.

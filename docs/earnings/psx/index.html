Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+46%

Sales jumped on higher prices

Sales rose to $50.5B from $34.5B a year ago, mainly on higher refined-product, crude and NGL prices.

$38.00

Refining margins surged

Realized refining margin was $38.00 per barrel, up from $12.15 a year ago, and refining swung from a $518M pre-tax loss to a $6,031M profit.

$2.8B

Cash went to buybacks and debt cuts

The company returned $2.8B to shareholders in the quarter and cut total debt by $2.0B to $18.5B.

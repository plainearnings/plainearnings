Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+$57M

Revenue grew 3% to $1.97B

Revenue rose $57M from a year earlier to $1.97B, with net interest income up 3% to $1.30B and fee income up 2% to $675M.

$68M

Credit costs stayed contained

The bank set aside $68M for possible loan losses, down from $105M a year earlier, and net charge-offs fell to 0.29% of average loans.

$631M

Net income was $631M

Net income was $631M. After $20M of preferred dividends (dividends on a special class of shares), $611M, or $0.71 per share, was available to common shareholders.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported noninterest revenue includes $15M of securities losses from repositioning trades (selling investments at a loss to reinvest); the company's adjusted revenue of $1,988M excludes them.

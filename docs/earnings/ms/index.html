Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$9.1B

Wealth is now half the firm

Wealth Management set a revenue record at $9.1B with a 30.4% pre-tax margin — nearly matching the $10.0B from the historically dominant trading and banking side.

+18%

Markets businesses fired

Institutional Securities revenue rose 18%: trading brought in $5.6B (+12%) and investment banking $2.4B (+4%) as dealmaking recovered.

-12%

Asset management lagged

Investment Management revenue fell 12% to $1.5B and its pre-tax profit fell to $259M — the one business moving the wrong way.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Pharma distribution is carrying the growth

Pharmaceutical and Specialty Solutions profit rose 17% to $783M on brand and specialty drug growth and a strong generics program — it earns more than the rest of the company combined many times over.

-28%

Medical products is the weak spot

Global Medical Products and Distribution profit fell 28% to just $33M on flat $3.2B revenue, hit by distribution and currency effects, partly offset by its own-brand products.

$12.80–$13.00

Full-year guidance raised

Management lifted fiscal 2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance to $12.80–$13.00 and completed a $500M share buyback in the quarter. GAAP EPS of $2.38 sits below non-GAAP $3.26 mainly because of $182M of amortization and acquisition costs.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $47M litigation recovery (a benefit) inside operating expenses this quarter, vs no such item a year ago.
  • Non-GAAP results exclude $182M of amortization and other acquisition-related costs, $94M of acquisition-related compensation and $23M of restructuring — the gap between GAAP EPS of $2.38 and non-GAAP EPS of $3.26.

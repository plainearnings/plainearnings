Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7%

Net revenues rose to $5.12B

Advice and management fees jumped 15% as client assets grew 12%, more than offsetting a 28% drop in insurance premiums.

+23%

Net income rose to $1.13B

Profit grew much faster than revenue because market-related insurance charges shrank — the change in fair value of market risk benefits fell from $302M to $202M.

$12.32

EPS of $12.32, up from $9.33

Buybacks amplify the growth: Ameriprise has shrunk its share count from about 98M to 91M diluted shares in a year.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.7%

Shoppers kept coming

Comparable sales rose 5.7% (8.0% excluding gasoline prices and currency moves), and e-commerce sales grew 14.8%.

$1.24B

Membership fees are the profit engine

Fees rose 10% to $1.24B — nearly half of Costco's $2.53B operating income came from what members pay before they buy anything.

905

Still expanding

Costco ended the quarter with 905 warehouses, including 624 in the United States and Puerto Rico.

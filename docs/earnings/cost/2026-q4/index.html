Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9.4%

Shoppers keep coming

Comparable sales rose 9.4% (6.7% excluding gasoline-price and currency swings), and digitally-enabled sales grew 19.5%. Net sales hit $93.9B in the 16-week quarter.

$1.9B

The membership is the profit

Membership fees of $1,850M covered 62% of the quarter's $2,998M net income. Costco can price goods close to cost because members pay an annual fee just to walk in.

+$0.15

A tariff refund helped

EPS included a non-recurring +$0.15 per share from IEEPA tariff refunds received in the quarter, partly reinvested in member prices. Even without it, EPS of about $6.60 would still be up roughly 12%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A non-recurring benefit of $0.15 per diluted share from IEEPA tariff refunds received in the quarter, net of amounts reinvested in lower prices and member values.

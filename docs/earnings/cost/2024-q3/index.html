Costco — Q3 FY24 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q3 FY24 · Quarter ended 2024-05-12
Reported 2024-05-30

Costco's net sales rose 9.1% to $57.4B as comparable sales grew 6.6%, and with $1.12B of nearly cost-free membership fees on top, profit jumped 29% to $1.68B — though last year's quarter carried a one-off $298M shipping charge that flatters the comparison.

Membership fees were only $1.92 of every $100 of revenue, yet they covered about 51% of Costco's operating profit — the membership model, not the markup, is where the money is.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.6%

Shoppers kept coming

Comparable sales rose 6.6% — U.S. +6.2%, Canada +7.7% and other international +7.7% — or 6.5% after adjusting for fuel prices and currency, with a small extra boost from the shift of the fiscal calendar.

$1.12B

Membership is the profit engine

Membership fees rose 7.6% to $1.12B. They are just 1.9% of revenue, yet they alone cover 51% of the quarter's $2.2B in operating income.

+20.7%

Online is catching up

E-commerce comparable sales jumped 20.7%, and Costco ended the quarter with 878 warehouses, including 605 in the United States and Puerto Rico.

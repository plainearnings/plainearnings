Costco — Q1 FY25 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q1 FY25 · Quarter ended 2024-11-24
Reported 2024-12-12

Costco's net sales rose 7.5% to $61.0B as comparable sales climbed 5.2% — and with $1.17B of nearly cost-free membership fees on top, profit rose 13% to $1.80B, helped by a $100M tax benefit.

Membership fees are only $1.88 of every $100 — but they arrive with almost no cost attached, which is why they cover about two-thirds of the $2.89 Costco kept as profit.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5.2%

Comparable sales still climbing

Comparable sales rose 5.2% across the company (7.1% adjusted for gas prices and currency), and e-commerce sales jumped 13.0% — members kept spending in the warehouses and online.

+7.8%

Membership fees keep growing

Membership fee income rose 7.8% to $1.17B — steady, recurring income with no cost of goods attached to it. Costco ended the quarter with 897 warehouses.

$1.80B

Profit up 13% with a tax assist

Net income rose to $1.80B ($4.04 per share) from $1.59B a year ago. Operating income itself rose 10.7% to $2.20B, and this year's figure also included a $100M tax benefit linked to stock-based compensation.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $100M tax benefit ($0.22 per diluted share) related to stock-based compensation flattered net income; a year ago the same benefit was $44M ($0.10 per share).

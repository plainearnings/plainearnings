Costco — Q2 FY26 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q2 FY26 · Quarter ended 2026-02-15
Reported 2026-03-05

Costco's net sales rose 9.1% to $68.2B as comparable sales climbed 7.4% — and with $1.36B of nearly cost-free membership fees on top, profit rose 14% to $2.04B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.4%

Comparable sales still climbing

Comparable sales rose 7.4% across the company (6.7% adjusted for gas and currency), and digitally-enabled sales jumped 22.6% — members kept spending in the warehouses and online.

+13.6%

Membership fees outgrow sales

Membership fee income rose 13.6% to $1.36B — growing faster than net sales, with no cost of goods attached to it.

$2.04B

Profit up 14%

Net income rose to $2.04B ($4.58 per share) from $1.79B a year ago — the usual Costco pattern: thin margins on goods, fat margins on memberships.

Costco — Q1 FY24 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q1 FY24 · Quarter ended 2023-11-26
Reported 2023-12-14

Costco's revenue rose 6.2% to $57.8B as comparable sales grew 4%, and with $1.08B of nearly cost-free membership fees on top, earnings per share rose 16.6% to $3.58 — and Costco declared a $15-per-share special dividend.

Membership fees were only $1.87 of every $100 of revenue, yet they covered about 55% of Costco's operating profit — the membership model, not the markup, is where the money is.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

Shoppers kept coming

Comparable sales rose 4% — U.S. +2%, Canada +6% and other international +11% — and e-commerce comparable sales rose 6%. Net sales grew 6.1% to $56,717M, and the net-sales gross margin improved 43 basis points to 11.04%.

$1.08B

Membership is the profit engine

Membership fees rose 8.2% to $1,082M. They are just 1.9% of revenue, yet they alone cover about 55% of the quarter's $1,984M in operating income. Costco ended the quarter with 72.0M paid household members, 129.5M cardholders and 871 warehouses.

$15

A $15 special dividend

On December 13, 2023, Costco declared a special cash dividend of $15 per share — about $6.7B in total, payable January 12, 2024 — on top of its regular dividend. Net income was $1,589M, or $3.58 a share, up 16.6%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Income taxes included a $44M benefit ($0.10 per diluted share) related to stock-based compensation.
  • The year-ago quarter included a $93M pre-tax charge tied to charter shipping, which flatters this quarter's margin comparison by about 17 basis points.

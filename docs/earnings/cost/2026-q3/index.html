Membership fees are only $1.95 of every $100 — but they arrive with almost no cost attached, which is why they cover nearly two-thirds of the profit Costco keeps. Rows may not add exactly due to rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9.8%

Shoppers kept coming

Comparable sales rose 9.8% (6.6% after adjusting for gasoline prices and currency), and digitally-enabled sales jumped 21.5% — more members spending more per trip.

$1.37B

Fees quietly fund the profit

Membership fees rose 10.7% to $1.37B. Merchandise itself earns a thin 12.8% gross margin by design — the fees are where the money is.

+15%

Profit outgrew sales

Net income rose 15% to $2.19B, faster than the 11.6% sales growth, as warehouse operating costs (SG&A) grew more slowly than revenue.

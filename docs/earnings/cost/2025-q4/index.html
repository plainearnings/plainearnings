Costco's sales rose 8.0% to $84.4B in its 16-week fourth quarter as comparable sales grew 6.4% — and with $1.72B of nearly cost-free membership fees on top, profit rose 11% to $2.6B.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.4%

Shoppers kept coming

Comparable sales rose 6.4% after adjusting for fuel prices and currency — U.S. +6.0%, Canada +8.3%, other international +7.2% — and e-commerce grew 13.5%.

$1.72B

Membership is the profit engine

Membership fees rose 14% to $1.72B — money that costs almost nothing to collect and alone covers more than half of the quarter's $3.3B operating profit.

914

Still expanding

Costco ended the year with 914 warehouses, 629 of them in the U.S. and Puerto Rico, and full-year profit of $8.1B, up 10%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The year-ago quarter included a one-time $63M tax benefit ($0.14 per share) from a transfer pricing settlement and reserve true-ups — so this year's 11% profit growth was earned against a flattered comparison.

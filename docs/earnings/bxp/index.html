Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

89.4%

Occupancy keeps climbing

Total portfolio occupancy rose 100 basis points to 89.4% — a fourth straight quarterly gain — and the CBD portfolio was 91.8% occupied. BXP signed 74 leases for about 1.2M square feet at a 10.3-year average term.

$1.89

FFO is the number REIT readers watch

Funds from Operations (non-GAAP) were $302M, or $1.89 per diluted share, up from $1.74 a year ago. FFO adds back building depreciation, which is why it sits well above GAAP EPS of $0.71.

$10M

Building sales flattered GAAP profit

Selling Sumner Square and 1330 Connecticut Avenue in Washington, DC for about $155M produced about $10M of gains. Management raised full-year guidance to EPS of $2.35–$2.37 and FFO of $7.06–$7.08 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gains on sales of real estate of $10M in the quarter (about $10.5M on the two Washington, DC sales, per the release) — included in GAAP profit above and excluded from FFO.
  • Headline non-GAAP measure: FFO of $302M ($1.89 per diluted share). This page is built on the GAAP income statement; FFO adds back $229M of depreciation and amortization and removes the sales gains.

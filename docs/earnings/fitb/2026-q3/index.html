Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+49%

Comerica made the bank much bigger

Average loans were $179B versus $123B a year ago and average deposits $238B versus $165B. The systems conversion finished this quarter with, the bank says, strong client and banker retention.

$1.13

Adjusted EPS vs $0.90 reported

Reported EPS of $0.90 includes $0.23 of certain items — mainly Comerica merger charges. Excluding them, EPS was $1.13 and adjusted return on tangible common equity was 20.6%.

0.31%

Credit stayed calm

Net charge-offs were just 0.31% of loans and the provision for credit losses fell 38% year over year to $122M — credit quality held up through the merger integration.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Certain items cut EPS by $0.23 (after tax $211M): merger-related charges ($171M after tax; $215M pre-tax in expenses and $10M against revenue), interchange litigation matters ($26M) and technology-related asset impairments ($14M).
  • Year-over-year comparisons span the Comerica acquisition, which closed earlier in 2026 — most growth rates reflect the bigger combined bank, not organic growth.

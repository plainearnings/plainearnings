Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1,817M

Electric utilities carried the quarter

Electric Utilities and Infrastructure earned $1,817M, up 10% from $1,658M, driven by recovering infrastructure investment in customer rates and favorable weather. Gas lost $19M in its off-season and Other lost $247M.

$1.99

A clean quarter, guidance raised

Reported EPS of $1.99 exactly matched adjusted EPS — there were no special items to strip out. Duke narrowed its 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $6.70–$6.80, toward the top of its range.

16 GW

A record building program

Duke is executing a record generation build to add 16 gigawatts of capacity by 2031 — roughly 16 large power plants' worth — to serve fast-growing states. Depreciation ($1,709M) and interest ($967M) already reflect the growing asset base.

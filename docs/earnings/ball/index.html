Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+24%

Sales jumped to $4.19B

Most of the increase is aluminum: can prices carry a metal pass-through, so revenue inflates with the aluminum price. Underneath, shipments grew a healthy 4.7%.

$475M

A pension buy-out charge

Ball transferred its U.K. pension obligations to an insurer; accounting rules required recognizing a $475M non-cash settlement charge, swinging the quarter to a GAAP loss.

$1.10

Underlying EPS rose 7%

Excluding the pension charge and similar items, comparable EPS was $1.10 and comparable operating earnings rose 2.5% to $451M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $475M pension settlement charge is non-cash: it recognizes previously deferred pension losses in one go as the U.K. plan is bought out. It does not change Ball's cash position or its ongoing pension costs (which fall).

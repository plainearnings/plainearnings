Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$35.8B

A record year, a quiet quarter

Quarterly revenue of $9,295M was up just 4% YoY, but fiscal 2023 revenue hit a record $35,819M, up 8%, and free cash flow was a record $17.6B — 49% of revenue.

$50B

VMware changes everything next year

Broadcom closed the VMware acquisition on November 22, 2023 — after this quarter ended — and guided fiscal 2024 revenue to approximately $50B including VMware, up about 40%.

51%

Half of revenue became free cash

Free cash flow was $4,723M in the quarter — 51% of revenue — and Broadcom raised its quarterly dividend by 14% to $5.25 a share for fiscal 2024.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit carries $786M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets ($438M inside cost of revenue and $348M inside operating expenses) — a bookkeeping charge, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $4,810M instead of $3,524M.
  • Stock-based compensation expense was $638M in the quarter ($62M in cost of revenue, $448M in research and development and $128M in selling, general and administrative).

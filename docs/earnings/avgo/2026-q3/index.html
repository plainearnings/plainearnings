Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+221%

AI chips are the story

AI semiconductor revenue hit $16.7B — more than Broadcom's entire company revenue a year ago — and management guided it to $21.7B next quarter.

$34.8B

Next quarter looks bigger

Guidance calls for revenue of about $34.8B in Q4, up 93% YoY — growth is accelerating, not cooling.

46%

A cash machine

Free cash flow was $13.7B — 46 cents of every revenue dollar — because software-like margins now sit on top of a chip business.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit carries $2,006M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets (split between cost of revenue and operating expenses) and $2,019M of stock-based pay — mostly non-cash charges. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, EPS was $3.32 instead of $2.68.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+196%

VMware transformed the mix

Infrastructure software revenue nearly tripled to $5.8B with a full quarter of VMware included — Broadcom is now as much a software company as a chip company, with software at 41% of revenue.

$12.2B

AI carried the chip business

Semiconductor revenue was a record $30.1B for fiscal 2024, driven by $12.2B of AI revenue — up 220% — from custom AI accelerators (XPUs) and Ethernet networking gear.

$442M

A tax benefit padded profit

GAAP net income of $4,324M was helped by a $442M income-tax benefit. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, which strips out $2,415M of acquisition amortization and $1,314M of stock pay, net income was $6,965M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit carries $2,415M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets ($1,602M inside cost of revenue and $813M inside operating expenses) — mostly bookkeeping from the VMware deal, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $6,965M instead of $4,324M.
  • A $442M income-tax benefit — a benefit, not a bill — is drawn as a green 'Tax benefit' source in the chart.
  • Discontinued operations added $119M below the line — profit from businesses being exited; profit from continuing operations was $4,205M.

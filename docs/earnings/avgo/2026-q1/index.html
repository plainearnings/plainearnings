Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+106%

AI chips doubled

AI semiconductor revenue hit $8.4B — more than double a year ago and above Broadcom's own forecast — on demand for custom AI accelerators and AI networking. Broadcom guided to $10.7B of AI chip revenue for the next quarter.

+1%

Software barely grew

Infrastructure software (mostly VMware) grew just 1% to $6.8B. Right now, essentially all of Broadcom's momentum is in silicon, not software.

41%

A cash machine

Free cash flow was $8.0B — 41% of revenue — because capital spending was only $250M. Few companies this size convert revenue into cash this efficiently.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit carries $1,969M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets ($1,462M inside cost of revenue and $507M inside operating expenses) — mostly bookkeeping from the VMware deal, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $10,185M instead of $7,349M.

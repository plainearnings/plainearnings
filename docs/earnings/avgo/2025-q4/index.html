Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+74%

AI chips are the story

AI semiconductor revenue grew 74% YoY, and Broadcom guided it to roughly double in the next quarter to $8.2B — on custom AI accelerators and Ethernet AI switches.

$1.6B

A tax benefit, not a tax bill

The tax line added $1,649M instead of subtracting — including a one-time, non-cash benefit of about $2.1B as tax statutes of limitations lapsed. That is why net income of $8,518M is bigger than pre-tax income of $6,869M.

41%

A cash machine

Free cash flow was $7,466M — 41% of revenue — because capital spending was only $237M. Broadcom raised its quarterly dividend 10% to $0.65 a share on the strength of it.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $1,649M tax benefit includes a one-time discrete non-cash tax benefit of about $2.1B from the lapse of statutes of limitations on old tax positions — it flatters this quarter's bottom line and will not repeat on schedule.
  • GAAP profit carries $2,052M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets ($1,545M inside cost of revenue and $507M inside operating expenses) — mostly bookkeeping from the VMware deal, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $9,714M instead of $8,518M.

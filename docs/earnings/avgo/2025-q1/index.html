Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+77%

AI chips are the growth engine

AI semiconductor revenue grew 77% YoY to $4.1B, and Broadcom guided it to $4.4B in the next quarter as hyperscale customers kept investing in custom AI chips and data-center networking.

+47%

VMware is the steady half

Infrastructure software revenue rose 47% to $6.7B — 45% of all revenue — giving Broadcom a subscription base that smooths the ups and downs of chip sales.

$6.0B

A cash machine

Free cash flow was $6.0B — 40% of revenue — even while carrying the debt taken on to buy VMware; interest on that debt cost $873M in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit carries $1,995M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets ($1,484M inside cost of revenue and $511M inside operating expenses) — mostly bookkeeping from the VMware deal, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $7,823M instead of $5,503M.

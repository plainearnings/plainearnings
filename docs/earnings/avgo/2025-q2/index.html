Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+46%

AI chips keep climbing

AI semiconductor revenue grew 46% YoY to over $4.4B, and Broadcom guided it to $5.1B in the next quarter — a tenth straight quarter of AI growth, on custom accelerators and AI networking gear.

+25%

VMware is the steady half

Infrastructure software revenue rose 25% to $6.6B — 44% of all revenue — giving Broadcom a subscription base that smooths the ups and downs of chip sales.

$6.4B

A cash machine

Free cash flow was $6.4B — 43% of revenue — even while carrying the debt taken on to buy VMware; interest on that debt cost $769M in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP profit carries $1,989M of amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets ($1,483M inside cost of revenue and $506M inside operating expenses) — mostly bookkeeping from the VMware deal, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $7,787M instead of $4,965M.

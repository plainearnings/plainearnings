Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+63%

AI revenue accelerated

AI semiconductor revenue grew 63% YoY to $5.2B — custom accelerators and networking — and Broadcom guided it higher again, to $6.2B in Q4, an eleventh straight quarter of AI growth.

$7.0B

Record free cash flow

Free cash flow was a record $7,024M — 44% of revenue — because capital spending was only $142M. Broadcom returned $2.8B to shareholders in the quarter through cash dividends.

$8.4B

Non-GAAP profit was double

GAAP profit carries $2,026M of amortization of acquisition-related intangibles plus $213M of restructuring — mostly VMware bookkeeping, not cash. On Broadcom's non-GAAP view, net income was $8,404M instead of $4,140M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $163M gain on the sale of a business sits inside other income, partly offsetting the quarter's $807M of interest expense.
  • The year-ago quarter (Q3 FY24) showed a net loss only because of a one-time $4,238M tax provision tied to moving intellectual property between subsidiaries; this quarter's tax was a normal $1,145M expense.

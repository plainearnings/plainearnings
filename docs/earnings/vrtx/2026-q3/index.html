Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

The base kept growing

Revenue rose 12% to $3.44B. CF products grew 8% to $3.27B, led by ALYFTREK at $706M, and Vertex raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $13.15B–$13.25B.

$168M

Diversification is showing up

Non-CF products — CASGEVY $99M, JOURNAVX $58M and new PALSONIFY $10M — brought in $168M, more than four times a year ago, as Vertex adds endocrine and (soon) kidney franchises.

$654M

Profit fell on deal costs

GAAP net income dropped from $1,083M to $654M almost entirely because of $502M of Crinetics acquisition costs. Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $9.8B after paying for the deal.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • About $502M of this quarter's GAAP expenses were Crinetics Pharmaceuticals acquisition-related costs — $282M in research & development and $220M in selling, general & administrative, mostly compensation for cash-settled Crinetics equity awards plus deal costs. Excluding these and other non-GAAP items, non-GAAP net income was $1,214M ($4.76 per share) versus GAAP net income of $654M ($2.56 per share), and non-GAAP operating income was $1,412M versus GAAP $713M.

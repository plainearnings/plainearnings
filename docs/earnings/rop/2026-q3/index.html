Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

Steady growth across the portfolio

Revenue reached $2.17B with 7% organic growth (growth from the existing businesses, not deals). Technology Enabled Products grew fastest at about +10%.

+22%

Profit boosted by an investment gain

GAAP net earnings rose to $485M, lifted by a $56M paper gain on the Indicor stake. Stripping out deal and investment effects, adjusted profit rose a more modest 5% to $586M.

$22.35–$22.40

Full-year guidance raised

Roper lifted its 2026 adjusted EPS forecast to $22.35–$22.40 and says it has more than $6B of capacity to spend on acquisitions.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $56M pre-tax gain from revaluing the Indicor minority stake sits in GAAP profit; in the first nine months Roper also received $1.24B of cash proceeds from Indicor's sale of its instrumentation businesses.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+$354M

Operations back in profit

A year ago operations lost $4.8B on a huge 777X charge. This quarter all three businesses contributed, led by services ($1,019M of earnings) and defense ($492M).

$573M

Interest is the drag

Debt taken on through the crisis years cost $573M of interest in one quarter — more than the operating profit — leaving a $116M net loss.

$745B

Record backlog of $745B

Orders grew to a record, including over 5,900 commercial jets — years of work, if Boeing can build them on schedule.

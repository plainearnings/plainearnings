Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1%

Tower rent is steady, services slumped

Site leasing revenue rose 1% to $665M, but site development revenue fell 36% to $49M, pulling total revenue down to $714M.

$3.19

Cash profit per share held up

AFFO (adjusted funds from operations, a REIT cash-profit measure) was $3.19 per share, and SBA bought back $300M of its own stock in the quarter.

$231M

Profit dipped slightly

Net income attributable to SBA was $231M, down from $237M a year ago, after a $20M loss on paying off debt early and $13M of impairment and decommission costs.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $20M loss on extinguishment of debt in the quarter.
  • $13M of asset impairment and decommission costs.
  • SBA sold all of its towers in Chile and Ecuador and agreed to sell its towers in Peru, exiting South America outside Brazil.

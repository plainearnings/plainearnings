Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Volume, not just price

Sales rose $3.0B to $20.7B. About $2.2B of that was selling more equipment to end users and $663M was higher prices — demand, led by a record backlog, did most of the work.

20.4%

Margins expanded sharply

Operating profit rose 38% to $4,214M and the operating margin widened to 20.4% from 17.3% a year ago, as the extra volume spread fixed costs — even with higher material and tariff costs in mining.

$2.0B

Cash went back to shareholders

Caterpillar returned $2.0B in the quarter — $1.2B of share buybacks and $0.7B of dividends — and ended the quarter with $7.9B of enterprise cash.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring costs of $41M before tax ($32M after tax, $0.07 per share) are included in GAAP results. Excluding them, adjusted EPS was $7.20 instead of $7.13.
  • Operating profit included $128M of IEEPA tariff recoveries — money back on tariffs previously paid — which flattered the 20.4% operating margin.

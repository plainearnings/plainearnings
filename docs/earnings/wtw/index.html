Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

Organic growth held up

Revenue rose 8% as reported, of which 4% was organic — the rest came from acquisitions and currency. Health, Wealth & Career grew 7% and Risk & Broking 8% on a reported basis.

$3.63

Adjusted EPS jumped 18%

Adjusted diluted EPS was $3.63 versus GAAP $3.24, and the adjusted operating margin widened to 21.9% from 20.4% — the deal and restructuring costs are the gap between the two views.

$56M

One-off costs flattened GAAP profit

Restructuring ($14M) plus transaction and program expenses ($42M) are why net income was nearly flat at $309M versus $306M despite 8% revenue growth; the GAAP operating margin slipped to 17.4% from 18.3%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring costs of $14M for Propel, WTW's AI Acceleration Plan (there were none in this line a year ago).
  • Transaction and program expenses of $42M, mainly share-based compensation and other transaction-related costs for the Newfront and Cushon acquisitions and Propel integration — up from $2M a year ago.

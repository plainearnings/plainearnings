Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

The cloud is doing the growing

SaaS revenue grew 21% to $242M and subscription revenue overall grew 15%, while old-style maintenance fell 5%. Annualized recurring revenue reached $2.28B, up 11% — most of Tyler's sales now repeat every year.

$277M

Cash flow set a record

Free cash flow was $277M, up 12%, a quarterly record. Tyler spent it two ways: about $28M to buy public-safety software maker CODY Systems, and $379M buying back 1.1 million of its own shares.

$3.52

Adjusted profit is much higher than GAAP

Non-GAAP EPS was $3.52 versus $2.33 GAAP. The gap is mostly $39M of stock-based pay and $25M of amortization of bought software and intangibles — real costs, but ones Tyler's preferred measure leaves out.

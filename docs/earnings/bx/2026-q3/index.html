Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

The fee base keeps compounding

Management and advisory fees rose 6% to $2.2B and fee-earning assets reached $990B. Fee Related Earnings — profit from fees alone, a non-GAAP measure — were $1.6B, or $1.26 per share.

-74%

Performance revenue swung down

GAAP performance allocations fell to $205M from $781M a year ago. That lumpiness is normal for carried interest, but it held total revenue flat even as fees grew.

$1.32

Distributable earnings fund the dividend

Distributable Earnings (non-GAAP) were $1.7B, or $1.32 per share, down 9% YoY. Blackstone declared a $1.12 per-share dividend for the quarter.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2%

Sales grew, profit shrank

Net sales rose 2% to $8,161M, but cost of goods sold rose faster — gross profit fell 8% to $1,440M — as freight, energy and other input costs surged.

$700M

Cost inflation is the story

Management expects to absorb about $700M of extra freight and energy costs alone in 2026, and cut its full-year adjusted EBITDA estimate to about $4.7B. Price increases already announced are meant to more than offset this, mostly landing in 2027.

$514M

Still investing heavily

Capital expenditures — money spent on buildings and equipment — were $514M in the quarter, against $874M of operating cash flow, and the company paid a quarterly dividend of $0.4523 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $26M of impairment and restructuring costs in the quarter, as the company continues reshaping the combined Smurfit Kappa / WestRock footprint.

Cost of revenue breaks down as labor and fringe $834M, fuel $451M, purchased services and other $657M, and equipment and other rents $92M. Depreciation is the only remaining operating-expense line, so every expense line sits in exactly one bucket and revenue less total operating expenses equals the reported $1,570M operating income.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Record revenue, on more freight and higher prices

Revenue hit a record $4.02B, up 12%. Total volume rose 5% to 1.69M units, and pricing and fuel surcharges — extra charges that rise with diesel prices — added the rest. Intermodal revenue jumped 20% and merchandise revenue 11%.

39.1%

Costs stayed flat, so the margin leapt

Total operating costs were $2.45B, down 2%, even though fuel costs jumped $170M as locomotive diesel prices rose 67%. Purchased services fell $73M on efficiency savings, and last year's $164M goodwill impairment (a write-down of an earlier acquisition) did not repeat. Operating margin widened from 30.3% to 39.1%.

$1.05B

Profit rose by half

Net earnings were $1.05B, up 52% from $694M a year ago, and EPS rose 54% to $0.57. Against last year's adjusted figures (which strip out the goodwill charge), operating income still rose 25% and EPS 30%.

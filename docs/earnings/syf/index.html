Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$49.7B

Spending hit a record

Purchase volume rose 8% to $49.7B — a record third quarter — and loan receivables grew 3% to $103.3B, with growth across all five sales platforms and the strongest gains from Gen Z and Millennial customers.

$1,096M

Partners took a bigger share

Retailer share arrangements rose 7% to $1,096M and the provision for credit losses rose 10% to $1,266M (a $30M reserve build, versus a $152M release a year ago). Together they more than ate the growth in interest income, cutting net earnings 17%.

$1.2B

Credit held up, capital flowed back

Net charge-offs improved to 4.79% of average loans from 5.16%, and loans 30+ days past due eased to 4.34%. The company returned $1.2B to shareholders in the quarter, including $1.1B of buybacks.

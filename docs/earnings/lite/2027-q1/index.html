Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+136.5%

AI demand is the whole story

Revenue rose 137% year over year and 25% in just one quarter, with growth in every business. Gross margin jumped to 51.0% from 34.0% a year ago as richer products filled the mix.

$5B+

A second giant photonics win

Lumentum secured its second major NPO/CPO win (near-package and co-packaged optics — putting photonics right next to the AI chip), backed by purchase commitments exceeding $5B, and expects optical switching to become a multi-billion-dollar annual revenue stream.

45%+

Guidance steps up again

Second-quarter guidance is revenue of $1.575–1.625B — about $350M more in one quarter — with non-GAAP operating margin of 45–46% and non-GAAP EPS of $5.90–6.20, versus a 42.3% margin this quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $7M gain on selling a business reduced operating expenses. On a non-GAAP basis — stripping out stock compensation, amortization and similar items — net income was $461M ($4.57 per share) versus GAAP net income of $360M ($3.54).

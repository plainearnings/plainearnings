Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14.9%

Underlying growth is even faster

On a daily, organic constant-currency basis sales grew 14.9% — the reported 11.3% is held back by the exit from the U.K. market.

16.1%

Margins expanded on volume

Adjusted operating margin rose to 16.1% as double-digit volume growth spread fixed costs, lifting adjusted operating earnings 18% to $836 million.

$47-48

Full-year outlook raised

Grainger now expects 2026 sales of $19.8-20.0 billion and adjusted EPS of $47.00-48.00, up from $45.50-47.25.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Reported operating earnings include $8 million of transaction costs for the Adroit technology acquisition; adjusted operating earnings of $836 million exclude them.
  • Year-ago results included a large loss on the exit of the U.K. market, which is why reported operating earnings appear to jump 62% — the adjusted increase is 18%.

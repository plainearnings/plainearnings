Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+125%

The mills did the heavy lifting

Steel mills' pre-tax earnings more than doubled to $1,788M from $793M, and steel products earnings rose to $465M from $319M, on higher volumes and higher realized prices. Raw materials earnings were $105M.

$1,493

Prices and volumes rose together

Nucor's average sales price was $1,493 per ton, up 19% from a year ago, and it shipped 7,533 thousand tons to outside customers. Sales rose 32% to $11,249M and net earnings attributable to stockholders rose 117% to $1,319M.

$2.7B

Cash is piling up

Nucor generated about $2.7B of free cash flow in the first nine months of 2026 and returned nearly $1.4B to shareholders, ending the quarter with $3.64B of cash and short-term investments and its credit facility undrawn.

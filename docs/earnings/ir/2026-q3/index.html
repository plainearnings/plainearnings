Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$355M

A settlement did the heavy lifting

GAAP net income of $671M includes about $355M of operating gains, mostly ILC Dover settlement recoveries. Adjusted net income — the underlying run-rate — was $415M, up 24%.

+21%

Orders are booming

Orders rose 21% to $2.35B (18% organic), including a roughly $195M engineered-to-order infrastructure order; both segments grew.

$623M

Cash flow doubled

Free cash flow was $623M versus $326M a year ago, helped by $162.5M of settlement cash collected in the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other operating income of $355M: Ingersoll Rand reached settlements with insurers and other parties over the ILC Dover transaction totaling about $425M of gross recoveries ($25M collected in Q2, $162.5M in Q3, the rest expected in Q4), alongside IEEPA tariff refunds. Excluding tariff refunds, adjusted EPS was $1.01 instead of $1.07.

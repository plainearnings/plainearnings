Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+12%

Fees did the heavy lifting

Fee revenue rose 12% to $4,078M on new business, higher client activity and higher market values. Assets under custody rose 10% to $63.4 trillion and assets under management 2% to $2.2 trillion.

+15%

Interest income kept climbing

Net interest income rose 15% to $1,423M as maturing securities were reinvested at higher yields and loans grew 19% on average — even though deposit margins narrowed.

$1.4B

Most profit went back to shareholders

BNY returned $1.4B in the quarter — $937M of share buybacks plus $436M of dividends — while keeping a 38.5% pre-tax margin and a 10.9% CET1 capital ratio.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Notable items were small this quarter: $23M of severance expense, partly offset by a $1M litigation-reserve release — a net $22M pre-tax drag. Excluding them, adjusted EPS was $2.37 vs $2.34 GAAP.

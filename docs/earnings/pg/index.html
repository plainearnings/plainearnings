Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4%

Growth was volume-led

Organic sales rose 4% on a 2% volume increase and 1% favorable mix, with pricing roughly neutral. Every segment grew — Beauty led at +6%.

-160 bps

Costs squeezed the margin

Cost of products sold rose 7% — much faster than sales — cutting gross margin from 51.4% to 49.8%. P&G now expects about $1.4B of after-tax commodity cost headwinds in fiscal 2027.

$3.9B

Cash returns continued

Operating cash flow was $5.8B and P&G returned $3.9B to shareowners — $2.6B of dividends and $1.3B of share repurchases. It held its full-year sales and EPS guidance.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Incremental restructuring charges of $98M before tax ($44M in cost of products sold and $54M in SG&A) are excluded from P&G's Core results — the difference between GAAP EPS of $1.97 and Core EPS of $2.00.

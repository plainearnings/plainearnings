Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+2.7%

Volumes are growing again

U.S. Foodservice local case volume rose 2.7% and total volume 2.6%, driving sales up 2.8% to $21.7B despite soft restaurant foot traffic and only 0.3% food-cost inflation.

$234M

A financing gain props up profit

Net earnings rose 10.5% to $526M, helped by a $234M gain on rate-lock deals taken out to finance the Jetro Restaurant Depot purchase. Excluding deal items, adjusted EPS was $1.19, up 3.5%.

$717M

Deal costs hit operating profit

Operating income fell 10.4% to $717M because acquisition, restructuring and transformation costs added $210M to expenses. Before those items, operating income rose 3.2% to $927M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $234M gain on deal-contingent rate-lock transactions (in other income) taken out to lock in borrowing costs for the Jetro Restaurant Depot acquisition.
  • $90M of bridge-loan cost amortization inside the $261M of interest expense, also tied to the Jetro deal.
  • $108M of acquisition-related costs and $102M of restructuring and transformation costs inside operating expenses; adjusted EPS was $1.19 vs $1.09 GAAP.

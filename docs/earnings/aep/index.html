Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

84 GW

The load queue is the story

AEP's contracted incremental load is expected to reach 84 GW by 2031 under signed agreements — an enormous data-center and industrial pipeline for an 11-state utility.

$100B

Capex super-cycle

The five-year capital plan jumped to $100B (rate base CAGR 13%+, to $162B by 2031), underpinning guided operating EPS growth of 9–11% a year for 2027–2031.

$21B

Affordability pitch

Take-or-pay contracts with large new customers are expected to offset up to $21B of fixed costs for existing residential customers — AEP's answer to the 'data centers raise my bill' argument.

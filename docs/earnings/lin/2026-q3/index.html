Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

Record sales, broad growth

Sales hit a record $9,404M, up 9% — underlying sales up 5% on 3% pricing and 2% volumes, with electronics, chemicals & energy and manufacturing leading. Every segment grew.

$425M

Charges hide the underlying trend

GAAP operating profit fell to $2,150M because of $425M of cost-reduction and other charges. Adjusted operating profit rose 8% to $2,763M and adjusted EPS rose 9% to $4.59 — both records.

$11B

Backlog keeps building

The project backlog reached $11B, including a $7.9B contractual sale-of-gas backlog. Full-year capex is guided at $5.5–6.0B and adjusted EPS at $17.82–$17.92, up 8–9%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Cost reduction program and other charges were $425M in the quarter. Excluding these charges and Linde AG purchase-accounting effects, adjusted operating profit was $2,763M (up 8%, a 29.4% margin), adjusted net income $2,122M and adjusted EPS $4.59 (up 9%).

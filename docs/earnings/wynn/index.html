Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-8%

All four properties shrank

Revenue fell at every property — Wynn Palace -$52M, Las Vegas -$46M, Wynn Macau -$30M and Encore Boston Harbor -$17M versus a year ago — as Macau demand softened.

$464M

Property profit fell nearly a fifth

Adjusted Property EBITDAR dropped 19% to $464M from $570M, with the biggest dollar declines in Las Vegas (-$45M) and at Wynn Palace (-$35M).

$0.25

Dividend held, UAE build continues

The board declared a $0.25 per-share dividend payable November 30, 2026, and finishing work continues at Wynn Al Marjan Island ahead of its planned September 2027 opening.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Las Vegas results were hurt by a table-games hold rate outside the expected range, and Encore Boston Harbor operated through a strike that began in early September — neither effect is separately quantified in the release.
  • Adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts was $33.9M, or $0.33 per diluted share (non-GAAP), versus GAAP EPS of $0.17.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.5B

International did the heavy lifting

International revenue rose 3% from last quarter to $3.5 billion, led by an 8% jump in Middle East/Asia, while North America fell 5% to $2.2 billion.

12.8%

Adjusted margin improved

Excluding $107 million of impairments and other charges, operating income was $731 million — a 12.8% margin, up from 12.0% last quarter.

~15%

2027 outlook: margins near 15%

Management expects mid-single-digit revenue growth in 2027 with operating margins of about 15%, assuming a flat North America market.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Results include a $107 million pre-tax charge for impairments and other items; excluding it and tax adjustments, adjusted net income was $501 million, or $0.60 per share.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+37%

A strong soft-season quarter

Net income available to common shareholders rose to $129.5M ($0.27 a share) from $94.7M a year ago — a strong result for the summer quarter, which is seasonally NiSource's weakest because gas heating demand is low.

$40B

A bigger, longer build-out

NiSource extended its capital plan to 2033: $40B of spending ($32B base plan plus $8B for data centers), targeting 10–11% compound annual adjusted EPS growth for 2027–2033, with 2027 adjusted EPS guided to $2.22–$2.30.

$1.75B

Microsoft deal grows

A new agreement with Microsoft expanded expected savings for existing customers from the GenCo data-center program to about $1.75B — big tech's power bills are being used to hold down everyone else's rates.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The quarter's GAAP result needed almost no adjustments — adjusted net income was $130.9M versus $129.5M GAAP. The small gap is weather (about $4M unfavorable versus normal) and $6M of 'Value Captured' initiative costs, partly offset in the reconciliation.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-3%

Customers came less often

Global comparable store sales fell 3% as transactions dropped 5% — a 2% higher average ticket only partly offset the lost visits. North America comparable sales fell 2% and China's fell 14%.

16.7%

Margins squeezed by labor and deals

GAAP operating margin contracted 60 basis points to 16.7%: Starbucks spent more on store partner wages and benefits and ran more promotions while selling less. Store operating expenses rose 4% to $3,829M even as revenue fell.

$0.93

Profit slipped, stores kept opening

EPS fell 6% to $0.93 and net earnings 8% to $1,056M. Starbucks still opened 526 net new stores in the quarter, ending with 39,477 — 52% company-operated and 48% licensed.

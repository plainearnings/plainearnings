Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.9%

North America comps snapped back

Comparable sales rose 7.9% in North America — average ticket +5.0% and transactions +2.7% — the clearest sign yet that 'Back to Starbucks' is working.

10.8%

Margins rebuilt

Operating margin reached 10.8%, up from 2.9% a year ago when restructuring charges peaked; excluding one-off items, the margin was 14.0%.

41,607

Still growing the footprint

Starbucks opened 303 net new coffeehouses in the quarter, ending with 41,607 worldwide — even while closing about 250 weak North America stores.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $273M in the quarter (store closures), versus $755M a year ago. GAAP EPS of $0.59 compares with non-GAAP EPS of $0.80, which leaves these charges out.
  • Revenue comparisons are distorted by China: converting company-run stores there to a licensed joint venture in Q3 FY26 removed their sales from revenue and replaced them with royalties — the main reason International revenue fell 37%.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-2%

Comps were still negative

Global comparable store sales fell 2% on 2% fewer transactions, partly offset by a 1% higher average ticket. North America comps fell 2% (U.S. −2%); International was flat, with China up 2% on 6% more transactions.

$0.11

One-offs cost 11 cents of EPS

GAAP EPS was $0.49 and non-GAAP EPS $0.50. Management said the Leadership Experience 2025 investment for 14,000+ store leaders, plus a discrete tax item, cut EPS by $0.11 in the aggregate.

41,097

Still opening stores

Starbucks opened 308 net new stores in the quarter, ending with 41,097 — 53% company-operated and 47% licensed. New stores helped revenue grow 4% to a record $9.5B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A non-recurring investment in Leadership Experience 2025 — a three-day conference for more than 14,000 North American store leaders — plus a discrete tax item reduced EPS by $0.11 in total; much of the conference cost sits in general & administrative expense, which rose 18% to $677M.
  • Restructuring charges were $21M under 'Back to Starbucks' — minor next to the $755M of store-closure charges that landed the following quarter.

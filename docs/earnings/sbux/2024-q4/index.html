Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-7%

Customers came less often

Global comparable store sales fell 7% as transactions dropped 8% — a 2% higher average ticket only partly offset the lost visits. North America comparable sales fell 6% and China's fell 14%.

14.4%

Margins squeezed by labor and deals

Operating margin contracted 380 basis points to 14.4%: Starbucks spent more on store partner wages and benefits and ran more promotions while selling less. Store operating expenses rose 4% to $3,882M even as revenue fell.

$0.80

A reset quarter

EPS fell 25% to $0.80 and net earnings 25% to $909M. New chairman and CEO Brian Niccol, who started in September, said Starbucks needs to 'fundamentally change our strategy' to win back customers — his 'Back to Starbucks' plan.

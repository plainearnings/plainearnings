Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+5%

Customers came more often and spent more

Global comparable store sales rose 5% — transactions up 3% and average ticket up 2%. North America rose 5%, International 7%, and China 10% as transactions there jumped 21%.

15.8%

Margins widened

GAAP operating margin expanded 140 basis points to 15.8% on sales leverage and in-store efficiency work under the Reinvention plan, partly offset by higher store wages and benefits. North America's margin reached 21.4%.

34.3M

Loyalty kept growing, stores kept opening

U.S. Starbucks Rewards 90-day active members rose 13% to 34.3 million, and Starbucks opened 549 net new stores to end the quarter with 38,587 — 51% company-operated and 49% licensed.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-1%

Comps were still negative

Global comparable store sales fell 1% on 2% fewer transactions, partly offset by a 1% higher average ticket. North America comps fell 1% (U.S. -2%); International rose 2%, with China flat.

$116M

Investment and restructuring hit profit

Operating income fell 45% to $601M as store labor spending rose under 'Back to Starbucks' and $116M of restructuring charges landed. GAAP EPS halved to $0.34; non-GAAP EPS was $0.41.

40,789

Still opening stores

Starbucks opened 213 net new stores in the quarter, ending with 40,789 — 53% company-operated and 47% licensed. New stores helped revenue grow 2% to $8.8B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring charges were $116M in the quarter as Starbucks simplified its global support organization under 'Back to Starbucks' — the main reason GAAP EPS of $0.34 sat below non-GAAP EPS of $0.41.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+1%

Comp growth is back

Global comparable store sales rose 1% — the first growth in seven quarters — on 1% more transactions. U.S. comps were flat (ticket +1%, transactions -1%) and turned positive in September; International comps rose 3% and China's 2%.

$755M

The bill for closing 627 stores

Restructuring and impairment charges were $755M as Starbucks closed 627 coffeehouses under the plan announced September 25, 2025 — over 90% in North America — and simplified its support organization. The GAAP operating margin shrank to 2.9%; excluding such items it was 9.4%.

$0.52

The underlying quarter was steadier

GAAP EPS was $0.12, down 85%. Excluding the restructuring charges and other one-offs, non-GAAP EPS was $0.52 — down 35%, but a fairer read on a business whose revenue grew 5%, ending the quarter with 40,990 stores after 107 net closures.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $755M under 'Back to Starbucks' — closing 627 coffeehouses (584 of them in North America) and simplifying the support organization; the year-ago quarter had none.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6.2%

The stores are turning

Global comparable store sales rose 6.2% — more transactions (+3.8%) and bigger tickets (+2.3%). North America comps rose 7.1%: the 'Back to Starbucks' plan is showing up where it matters, in the stores.

+9%

Revenue growth, at last

Revenue rose 9% to $9.5B. Channel Development jumped 39% on the Global Coffee Alliance, International rose 10%, and North America 7%. Starbucks raised its full-year guidance for comparable sales and non-GAAP EPS.

$511M

Profit up — with quieter charges

Net earnings rose 33% to $511M and the GAAP operating margin widened to 8.7%, helped by restructuring charges falling to $25M from $116M a year ago and by China stores no longer being depreciated. The tax rate jumped to 29.8% on China reorganization items.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $25M — store closures under 'Back to Starbucks' — versus $116M a year ago.
  • Starbucks classified its China retail operations as held for sale in Q1 FY26 (they moved into the Boyu Capital joint venture just after quarter-end), which stopped depreciation on those stores and lowered reported costs; the quarter's 29.8% tax rate included an $8M discrete charge tied to that classification.

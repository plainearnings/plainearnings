Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-4%

Fewer cigarettes, more money

Domestic cigarette shipment volume fell about 4%, but higher prices more than covered it — smokeable revenue rose 0.8% and segment operating income rose 3.8%.

$242M

The beer stake chipped in

Altria booked a $242M gain on equity investments, mainly its holding in Anheuser-Busch InBev — a meaningful slice of the quarter's 6% profit growth.

64.5%

An extraordinary margin

The smokeable segment converts about 65 cents of every net-of-tax revenue dollar into operating income — the economics that fund Altria's dividend even as volumes shrink.

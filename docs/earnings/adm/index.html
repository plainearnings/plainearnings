Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1.70

Profit surged off a low base

EPS of $1.70 versus $0.22 a year ago — last year's quarter carried $246M of impairment and restructuring charges plus weaker crushing margins; this year segment operating profit grew in all three segments.

$778M

Oilseeds did the heavy lifting

Ag Services & Oilseeds operating profit more than doubled to $778M (crushing alone $311M vs $13M), riding biofuel demand and ADM's North American footprint.

$5.50–5.80

Guidance raised again

Management raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to about $5.50–$5.80 (from $5.15–$5.60) and expects Q4 operating profit to improve year over year in every segment.

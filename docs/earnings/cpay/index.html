Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

Growth was broad, led by corporate payments

Revenue rose 18% to $1,382M. Corporate Payments jumped 41% to $571M (18% without the Alpha deal), Vehicle Payments rose 8% to $580M and Lodging rose 3% to $131M. Management raised full-year revenue guidance to about $5.35B.

57.4%

Very high margins held up

Adjusted EBITDA (a cash-profit measure that leaves out interest, tax, depreciation and one-offs) was $794M, up 17%, for a 57.4% margin — almost unchanged from 57.7% a year ago. On a GAAP basis, operating profit was $621M, a 45% margin.

$378M

Profit rose faster than revenue

Net income attributable to Corpay rose 36% to $378M, and EPS rose 45% to $5.66, helped by buybacks: Corpay repurchased 1.9M shares for $747M in the quarter alone, so each remaining share claims a bigger slice of profit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Integration and deal-related costs were $17M in the quarter and restructuring costs were $6M. Corpay excludes both from its adjusted results — a big part of why adjusted EPS ($7.54) sits well above GAAP EPS ($5.66).
  • Corpay booked a $3M gain on disposition (selling a business or asset) in the quarter; in the flow above it is netted into general and administrative costs. Its adjusted results also add back a $19M adjustment at an equity-method investment (a minority stake whose results Corpay shares in).

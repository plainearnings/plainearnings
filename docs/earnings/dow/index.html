Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Prices did all the work

Sales rose 13% to $11,267M because local prices rose 14% — led by polyethylene, up 17% in Packaging & Specialty Plastics — while volumes actually fell 1%. Every segment grew, with Packaging & Specialty Plastics up 18% to $5,778M.

$887M

Operating profit nearly quintupled

Operating EBIT — Dow's preferred profit measure — rose to $887M from $180M a year ago on higher prices and self-help cost savings, more than offsetting raw-material and logistics costs. Operating cash flow was $1.8B and free cash flow $1.1B.

-$0.09

One-offs trimmed reported EPS

GAAP EPS was $0.62 vs operating EPS of $0.71. Significant items cost $0.09 a share: $69M of Transform to Outperform costs, $31M of restructuring-program implementation and a $53M loss on paying off debt early, partly offset by a $69M litigation gain (all pre-tax).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Transform to Outperform program costs of $69M pre-tax ($60M in SG&A, $9M in restructuring charges) — Dow's company-wide productivity overhaul.
  • 2025 Restructuring Program implementation costs of $31M pre-tax ($30M in cost of sales, $1M in SG&A).
  • A $53M pre-tax loss on early extinguishment of debt, partly offset by $69M of litigation-related gains — both inside sundry income. Total significant items: $84M pre-tax, $0.09 per share.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

8.05M

Member growth stayed strong

Netflix added 8.05M paid memberships, up from 5.89M a year ago, to end the quarter at 277.65M — paid memberships were up 16.5% year over year, with Asia-Pacific adding the most members (2.83M).

+42%

Profit grew much faster than revenue

Operating income rose 42% year over year to $2.6B on revenue growth of 17% — the operating margin reached 27.2%, five points better than 22.3% a year ago, and net income rose 44% to $2.1B.

26%

Cash flow, and a higher full-year bar

Free cash flow was $1.2B in the quarter, and Netflix raised its full-year 2024 outlook to revenue growth of 14% to 15% (from 13% to 15%) and an operating margin of 26% (from 25%).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Currency gain: 'interest and other income' includes a $43M non-cash unrealized gain from F/X remeasurement on Netflix's euro-denominated debt — a paper gain below operating income, not revenue from running the service.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

9.33M

Member growth surged

Netflix added 9.33M paid memberships, up from 1.75M a year ago, to end the quarter at 269.60M — paid memberships were up 16.0% year over year, with every region adding at least 1.7M members.

28.1%

Profit grew much faster than revenue

Operating income rose 54% year over year to $2.6B on revenue growth of 15% — the operating margin reached 28.1%, seven points better than 21.0% a year ago, and net income rose 79% to $2.3B.

$131M

A paper currency gain helped profit

Net income included a $131M non-cash unrealized gain from F/X remeasurement on Netflix's euro-denominated debt, recognised below operating income in interest and other income — a paper gain, not money from running the service.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

32%

The margin keeps expanding

Operating income rose 27% YoY to $3.3B — revenue grew 13% while the cost of revenues grew just 6%, so the operating margin widened from 28% a year ago to 32%.

+23%

Every region grew

Asia-Pacific led at +23%, EMEA rose 15%, the U.S. & Canada 9% and Latin America 8% — price rises and the young ads business did the work in every market.

$2.7B

Cash piled up

Free cash flow was $2.7B, up from $2.1B a year ago, and Netflix still expected about $8B for the full year — funding $3.5B of share buybacks in the quarter and an $800M debt repayment.

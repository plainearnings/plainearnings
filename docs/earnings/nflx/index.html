Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

34.6%

Margins keep climbing

Operating margin rose from 28.2% to 34.6% in a year; Netflix guides to 31.5% for 2026 as a whole, even after a Q4 charge for job cuts.

$2.7B

Ads: growing, but slower than hoped

Netflix cut its 2026 ad-revenue expectation to about $2.7B from roughly $3B — the ad business is real money now, but still only about 5% of revenue.

$4.8B

Record buybacks

Netflix repurchased $4.8B of its own stock in one quarter — its biggest buyback ever — with $22.3B of capacity still unused.

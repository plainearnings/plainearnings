Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

34.1%

The margin keeps expanding

Operating income rose 45% YoY to $3.8B — revenue grew 16% while the cost of revenues grew just 3%, so the operating margin widened from 27% a year ago to 34%.

+24%

Every region grew

Asia-Pacific led at +24%, EMEA rose 18%, the U.S. & Canada 15% and Latin America 9% — growth no longer depends on any single market.

$8–8.5B

Cash-flow guidance raised

Netflix raised its full-year 2025 free-cash-flow forecast to $8–8.5B and guided to 17% revenue growth in Q3 — price rises and the young ads business are doing the work.

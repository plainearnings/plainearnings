Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

5.1M

Steady member growth, richer members

Netflix added 5.07M paid memberships to reach 282.7M, and average paid memberships rose 15% YoY — paid sharing and the ads plan kept pulling new households in.

+52%

Profit grew three times faster than revenue

Operating income rose 52% YoY to $2.9B on revenue growth of 15% — the operating margin reached 30%, seven points better than a year ago, and net income rose 41% to $2.4B.

$2.2B

Cash rich, and raising the bar

Free cash flow was $2.2B in the quarter, Netflix raised its full-year FCF outlook to $6.0–6.5B, and it bought back $1.7B of stock while net debt fell to $6.8B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Currency loss: 'interest and other income' includes a $91M foreign-exchange loss, mostly from remeasuring Netflix's euro-denominated debt (net of hedges) — a paper loss below operating income, not a cost of running the service.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

325M

A membership milestone

Netflix crossed 325 million paid memberships during the quarter — the last milestone it reported before moving to engagement and revenue as its headline measures.

+30%

Profit grew much faster than sales

Operating income rose 30% YoY to $3.0B and the operating margin widened about two points to 24.5% — revenue growth of 18% dropped through to profit at a richer rate.

+15%

Every region grew double digits

UCAN and EMEA both grew 18%, Asia-Pacific 17% and Latin America 15% — broad growth, not one market carrying the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • About $60M of costs related to the Warner Bros. bridge loan and associated financings were booked inside interest expense — one-off deal financing costs, not part of the streaming business, and not in Netflix's own guidance for the quarter.

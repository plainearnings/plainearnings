Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

13.12M

A record fourth quarter for sign-ups

Paid net additions were 13.12M — Netflix's largest Q4 ever, versus 7.66M a year ago — taking paid memberships to 260.28M as paid sharing and the ad-supported plan kept converting borrowers into members.

16.9%

Margins jumped as spending lagged

Operating income rose to $1,496M from $550M a year ago — a 16.9% margin versus 7.0% — on revenue upside and lower-than-planned spending; full-year 2023 operating margin reached 21%, ahead of Netflix's 20% target.

$239M

Currency noise cut into EPS

EPS of $2.11 includes a $239M non-cash unrealized loss from remeasuring euro-denominated debt as the dollar weakened in the quarter; even so, net income was $938M, up from just $55M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Currency loss: 'interest and other income (expense)' includes a $239M non-cash unrealized loss from F/X remeasurement on Netflix's euro-denominated debt — a paper loss below operating income, not a cost of running the service.

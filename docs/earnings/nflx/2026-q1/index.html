Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$2.8B

A one-off fee flattered everything

Netflix collected a $2.8B termination fee after its Warner Bros. transaction was called off. It is the main reason EPS of $1.23 came in far above Netflix's own $0.76 forecast — and it will not repeat.

+18%

The core business still grew

Even before the fee, operating income rose 18% YoY to $4.0B at a 32.3% margin — the streaming engine itself had a strong quarter.

+20%

Every region grew double digits

Asia-Pacific grew fastest at +20%, Latin America +19%, EMEA +17% and UCAN +14% — broad growth, not one market carrying the quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $2.8B termination fee from the Warner Bros. transaction sits inside 'interest and other income' ($2,852M in total). It is one-off cash: operating cash flow of $5.3B and free cash flow of $5.1B both include it, and Netflix raised its full-year free-cash-flow expectation to about $12.5B largely because of it.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+18%

HIV is growing again

HIV product sales rose 18% to $6.25B: Biktarvy grew 10% to $4.1B and HIV prevention sales nearly doubled as long-acting prevention rolled out — the franchise is three-quarters of all product sales.

-20%

EPS fell on a comparison quirk

Diluted EPS fell to $1.94 from $2.43 even though operating income rose 4% — the year-ago quarter included $569M of other income (net) versus a $130M expense this year.

$11.3B

Remember the first half

Over the first nine months Gilead recorded $11.3B of acquired in-process R&D charges from buying research-stage assets, which is why its nine-month result is a $6.0B loss despite profitable quarters like this one.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquired in-process R&D expense was just $18M this quarter (versus $170M a year ago) — but the nine-month total is $11,307M, concentrated earlier in 2026.
  • SG&A rose 21% year over year to $1,641M as Gilead spends behind new launches in HIV and oncology.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-7%

The van channel stumbled

Snap-on Tools Group sales fell 7% to $471M. The company blames an unusually sharp rise in diesel prices, which pushed franchisees to trim inventories even though demand from technicians stayed stable.

+10%

Critical industries set a record

Commercial & Industrial sales rose 10% to $404M, with operating earnings up 30% to $75M, led by aerospace, military and precision-torque work.

51%

Margins held firm

Gross profit was $604M on product sales — about 51% of net sales — showing pricing and cost discipline even as volumes softened. The year-ago quarter also included a $22M legal-settlement benefit that did not repeat.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+57%

The brokerage is booming

ICS revenue surged 57% on 22% more volume and 29% higher revenue per load, swinging the segment from a small loss to a $9.6M profit.

$671M

Fuel inflates the growth rate

Fuel surcharge revenue nearly doubled to $671M as fuel prices rose. Excluding surcharges, revenue grew 13% — still strong, but not 20%.

-22%

Dedicated profit got squeezed

DCS grew revenue 11% yet its operating income fell 22% to $82M, hit by driver pay, group medical claims and insurance costs.

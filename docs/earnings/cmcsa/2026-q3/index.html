Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+0.5%

The headline fall is mostly last year's story

Reported revenue fell 6.1%, but last year's quarter is not comparable — Versant has been separated and Sky Germany sold. On Comcast's pro forma basis, revenue rose 0.5% and Adjusted EBITDA rose 0.8%.

$261M

Peacock is profitable — again

Peacock made $261M of Adjusted EBITDA on $1.8B of revenue, its second straight profitable quarter, helped by the FIFA World Cup. A year ago it lost $217M. Media Adjusted EBITDA more than tripled to $769M.

-3.5%

The cable core keeps eroding

Residential Connectivity revenue fell 3.5% as broadband and video shrink; wireless is the offset — 368,000 lines added in the quarter, 10.6 million in total — and Business Services grew 2.7%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP EPS of $0.97 vs adjusted EPS of $1.07: the adjusted figures strip out items Comcast lists in its reconciliation, including amortization of acquisition-related intangibles and other gains and losses it does not count as operating performance.

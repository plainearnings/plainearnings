Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+44%

AI data centers are buying the fiber

Optical Communications sales rose 44% to $2.38B and its net income rose 80% to $532M. Enterprise Networks grew 84%, with Gen AI product sales up more than 100% — outpacing GPU unit growth, Corning says.

+37%

Solar is the second growth leg

Solar sales grew 37% to $436M as U.S. production ramped (more slowly than planned) and new U.S. trade action on solar imports helped pricing; the segment was roughly breakeven (-$7M).

$0.91

Core EPS vs $0.78 GAAP

Core EPS of $0.91 rose 36%; the GAAP figure of $0.78 (+56%) includes items Corning excludes from 'core', and the quarter's other income includes a $138M currency-contract gain.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+8%

The lending engine is earning more

Net interest income reached $16,449M as loans and deposits grew and old fixed-rate assets repriced to today's higher rates. The net interest yield widened to 2.16%, up 15 basis points from a year ago.

$1.2B

One charge explains the profit drop

A $1.2B non-deductible regulatory settlement turned a solid quarter into a reported profit decline: net income fell 17% to $6,933M, but excluding the charge it was $8.1B with EPS of $1.08 instead of $0.91.

$8.3B

Capital is strong and going back to shareholders

The bank returned $8.3B to shareholders ($6.0B of buybacks, $2.2B of dividends) while keeping its CET1 capital ratio at 10.9%. Net charge-offs were $1,349M — 0.44% of loans, slightly better than a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $1.2B regulatory settlement charge sits inside 'Other general operating' expenses. It is not tax-deductible and cost $0.17 of EPS — excluding it, net income was $8.1B, EPS $1.08, and the efficiency ratio was 62% instead of the reported 66%.
  • Preferred stock dividends of $392M sit between net income ($6,933M) and the $6,541M attributable to common shareholders.

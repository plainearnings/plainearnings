Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$167.3M

Royalties set a record

Oil and gas royalty revenue hit a record $167.3M, up 54% from a year ago. TPL's share of production was a record 42.3 thousand barrels of oil equivalent a day, and realized oil prices averaged $83.57 a barrel.

+35%

Best quarter in company history

Revenue, net income and free cash flow all set records. Easement and surface income more than doubled to $36.2M as operators built more pipelines and infrastructure across TPL's land.

-11%

The water segment cooled

Water Services revenue fell 11% to $71.8M: water sales dropped 36% even as produced-water royalty volumes hit a record 5,541 thousand barrels a day and produced-water royalties rose 28% to $41.4M.

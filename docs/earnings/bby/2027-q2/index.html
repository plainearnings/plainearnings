Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4.1%

Comparable sales grew 4.1%

Best Buy's best growth in years: computing and home theater led, with new categories (AI glasses, trading cards) adding novelty demand.

+69%

Profit jumped to $315M

The comparison is flattering — last year's quarter absorbed $114M of restructuring charges — but gross margin also improved to 23.9% on better product margins.

$6.70–$6.90

Guidance raised

Best Buy lifted its FY27 outlook: comparable sales of +1.9% to +3.0% and adjusted EPS of $6.70–$6.90.

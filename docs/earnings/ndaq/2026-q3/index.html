Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+26%

Trading volumes surged

Market Services gross revenue jumped to $1,221M on surging trading volumes. After paying out $912M of rebates and pass-through fees, Nasdaq kept $1,481M of net revenue — up 13% — and a record quarter for its new Digital Liquidity Networks business.

+39%

The index business is booming

Index revenue grew 39% to $286M as investors poured $49B into Nasdaq-indexed products in the quarter ($141B over twelve months) — high-margin money that arrives whether or not trading is busy.

$3.4B

Recurring revenue keeps compounding

Annualized recurring revenue reached $3.4B, up 12%, and Solutions revenue grew 17%. The steady software-and-data income is why profit rose 27% even in a business built on volatile trading.

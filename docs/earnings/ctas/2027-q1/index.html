Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+10.9%

Growth in every segment

Revenue rose 10.9% to $3.01B. The core Uniform Rental and Facility Services business grew 9.7% to $2.29B, First Aid and Safety grew about 16% to $389M, and All Other grew about 13% to $331M. Organic growth — the company's own measure stripping out deals, currency and an extra workday — was 8.9%.

51.5%

Margins hit records

Gross margin rose from 50.3% to 51.5% of revenue, and operating margin from 22.7% to 23.6%, both records. Operating income rose 15.2% to $712M — and that is after absorbing $14M of UniFirst deal costs.

$552M

Profit up 12%, guidance raised

Net income rose 12.3% to $552M and EPS 13.3% to $1.36, even though the tax rate rose from 17.6% to 20.0%. Cintas raised its full-year outlook to $12.15B–$12.27B of revenue and $5.45–$5.54 of adjusted EPS.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating costs include $14M ($14.4M exactly) of transaction expenses for the proposed acquisition of UniFirst Corporation, which Cintas expects to close before the end of calendar 2026 while the deal is reviewed by the US Federal Trade Commission. The costs cut diluted EPS by $0.03; excluding them, EPS was $1.39.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+57%

Record revenue, led by Electric

Revenue rose to $12.01B from $7.63B a year ago. Electric segment revenue grew 59% to $9.80B.

$63.7B

Backlog hit a record

Total backlog reached $63.7B, and Quanta raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $41.3B–$41.6B.

$4.55

GAAP profit more than doubled

Net income attributable to common stock was $695M, or $4.55 per diluted share, versus $339M, or $2.24, a year ago; adjusted EPS was $6.06.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Acquisition and integration costs of $35M (excluded from Quanta's adjusted results)
  • Increase in fair value of contingent consideration liabilities of $20M

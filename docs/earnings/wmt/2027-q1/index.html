Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+26%

eCommerce is the growth engine

Online sales grew 26% globally, U.S. comparable sales rose 4.1%, and International net sales jumped 18.0% — growth is coming from every segment at once.

+5.0%

Profit grew slower than sales

Operating income rose 5.0% to $7.5B against 7.3% revenue growth — higher fuel costs in distribution and fulfillment took roughly 2.5 points off operating-income growth.

$0.67

EPS jumped on investment swings

GAAP EPS rose 19.6% to $0.67, helped by a $275M gain on equity investments versus a $597M loss a year ago. Adjusted EPS, which strips that out, was $0.66.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other (gains) and losses swung from a $597M loss a year ago to a $275M gain — mostly fair-value moves on equity investments. Adjusted EPS of $0.66 excludes a $0.02 net gain from these items.
  • Higher fuel costs in distribution and fulfillment cut operating-income growth by roughly 250 basis points (in the company's adjusted framing).

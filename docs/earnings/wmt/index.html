Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28.8%

Operating profit surged — with help

Operating income jumped 28.8% to $9.4B, but tariff refunds (partly offset by price investments) did much of the lifting: on an adjusted constant-currency basis the rise was 17.4%.

−$1.2B

Investments cut the bottom line

Below the operating line, a $1,200M loss on equity and other investments (vs a $2,708M gain a year ago) turned a strong operating quarter into a 9.4% drop in attributable net income, to $6,366M.

+23%

eCommerce keeps compounding

Global eCommerce sales grew 23% and Walmart U.S. comparable sales rose 2.6% on more transactions. Management raised full-year FY27 guidance: net sales +4.0–5.0% and adjusted operating income +7.0–8.5% in constant currency.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Tariff refunds received in the quarter lifted the gross profit rate by about 96 basis points and boosted operating income, partly offset by deliberate price investments. Walmart plans to plow remaining refunds into prices in the second half — and says to judge Q2 and Q3 together.
  • A $1,200M loss on equity and other investments sits below operating income. Adjusted EPS of $0.81 excludes a $0.12-per-share loss on those investments and a $0.11-per-share benefit from a specific tax matter.

Walmart — Q4 FY24 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q4 FY24 · Quarter ended 2024-01-31
Reported 2024-02-20

Walmart's revenue rose 5.7% to $173.4B and operating income jumped 30.4% as U.S. comparable sales rose 4.0% and eCommerce grew 23% — but headline profit fell 12.4% to $5.5B because a year ago an even bigger $3.8B paper gain on its investment stakes had flattered the comparison.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30.4%

Operating income grew far faster than sales

Operating income rose 30.4% to $7,254M on 5.7% revenue growth — the gross margin rate widened 39 basis points, led by Walmart U.S., membership income grew 13.0%, and global eCommerce sales rose 23%, passing $100B for the full year. On an adjusted basis, which adds back $849M of reorganization charges in the year-ago quarter, operating income still grew 13.2%.

$813M

A smaller paper gain, not weaker stores

Below the operating line, Walmart booked an $813M gain on equity and other investments — mostly fair-value moves on stakes it holds. A year ago the same line was a $3,848M gain; that swing is the main reason headline profit fell 12.4% to $5,494M even though the stores had a much stronger quarter. Adjusted EPS of $0.60, split-adjusted, strips the gain out, versus GAAP EPS of $0.68.

$0.83

Dividend raised 9%

Walmart raised its annual dividend 9% to $0.83 per share on a post-split basis — its largest increase in over a decade and its 51st consecutive annual rise — after generating $35.7B in operating cash flow and $15.1B in free cash flow for the full year.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other (gains) and losses was an $813M gain this quarter versus a $3,848M gain a year ago — unrealized fair-value moves on equity investments Walmart holds, led by Symbotic and Asda debt securities and partly offset by JD.com. Adjusted EPS of $0.60 (split-adjusted; $1.80 as reported before the 3-for-1 split) excludes the net-of-tax effect of the investment gain ($0.08 per share split-adjusted; $0.23 pre-split).
  • The year-ago quarter included $849M of business reorganization and restructuring charges in Walmart International, which flatters the reported 30.4% operating-income growth; adjusted operating income, which excludes those year-ago charges, rose 13.2%.

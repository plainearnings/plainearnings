Walmart — Q3 FY24 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q3 FY24 · Quarter ended 2023-10-31
Reported 2023-11-16

Walmart grew revenue 5.2% to $160.8B on 4.9% U.S. comparable sales and 15% global eCommerce growth, and raised its full-year guidance — but a $4.75B unrealized loss on its investment stakes held headline profit to $453M, while adjusted EPS was $1.53.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+4.9%

U.S. stores stayed strong

Walmart U.S. comparable sales rose 4.9% excluding fuel, with transactions up 3.4% and eCommerce up 24%; global eCommerce rose 15% and the global advertising business about 20%, with Walmart Connect up 26%.

-$4.75B

A paper loss swamped the stores

A $4,750M loss on equity and other investments — unrealized fair-value moves on stakes Walmart holds — held GAAP profit to $453M. Adjusted EPS, which strips the loss out, was $1.53 as reported ($0.51 split-adjusted).

$6.40–6.48

Guidance raised

Walmart raised its fiscal 2024 outlook to net sales growth of 5.0–5.5% and adjusted EPS of $6.40–$6.48. Reported operating income rose 130.1% to $6,202M — flattered by a charge-hit year-ago quarter; adjusted operating income rose 3.0%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other (gains) and losses was a $4,750M loss this quarter — unrealized fair-value declines on equity and other investments Walmart holds. Adjusted EPS of $1.53 (as reported before the split) excludes the loss's $1.36-per-share effect, net of tax.
  • The year-ago quarter's operating expenses included a large opioid-litigation charge, which flatters this quarter's reported comparisons: operating expenses as a share of net sales fell 182 basis points, and reported operating income rose 130.1% against 3.0% on an adjusted basis.

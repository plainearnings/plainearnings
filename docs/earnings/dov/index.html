Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.5%

Growth is broad — and orders are stronger

Revenue rose 7.5% to $2,234M (+7% organic). Climate & Sustainability grew 18% and Clean Energy & Fueling 12%, helped by AI and power-generation demand. Bookings rose in all five segments and outpaced shipments, with much of the backlog due for delivery in 2027.

$2.88

Adjusted EPS vs the $2.50 GAAP figure

Adjusted earnings were $388M ($2.88 a share, +10%), excluding $46M of purchase-accounting expenses — mostly amortization of intangibles from past acquisitions — and $22M of restructuring costs, both pre-tax. GAAP EPS from continuing operations was $2.50.

$330M+

Buybacks and bolt-on deals continue

Dover repurchased over $330M of its shares in the quarter and announced two small acquisitions, while investing in new capacity in its high-growth markets. Segment profit margin reached 24.3% of revenue, up from 24.2% a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Purchase accounting expenses of $46M pre-tax in the quarter — primarily amortization of intangible assets created by past acquisitions. Dover excludes these from adjusted earnings.
  • Restructuring and other costs of $22M pre-tax — headcount reductions, facility consolidations and site closures, and product-line exits. Also excluded from adjusted earnings.
  • A $3M loss from discontinued operations took total net earnings to $333M, below the $337M from continuing operations used in the flow above.

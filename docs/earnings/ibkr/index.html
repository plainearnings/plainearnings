This chart starts from gross revenue: $2,433M of interest income plus $760M of non-interest income. Paying $1,279M of interest to customers leaves the $1,914M of net revenue Interactive Brokers headlines (up 16%). Of the $1,348M bottom line, $1,034M belongs to non-controlling interests (the founding group's IBG LLC stake); $314M was available to common stockholders.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

78%

A 78% pre-tax margin

Automation keeps costs tiny relative to revenue: $418M of non-interest expenses against $1,914M of net revenue left $1,496M of pre-tax profit.

$1.15B

Interest is the biggest earner

Net interest income rose 19% to $1,154M as customer margin loans grew 36% to $105B and customer credit balances reached $186B.

$965B

Customer money keeps arriving

Customer equity rose 27% to $965B and accounts grew 35% to 5.58 million — the base that future commissions and interest income are earned on.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+15%

Interest income is the growth story

Net interest income rose 15% to $4.2B as average loans grew to $372B and yields rose; the net interest margin was 2.94%. Revenue overall rose 11% to $6.6B.

57%

Costs fell and efficiency improved

Noninterest expense fell 8% from the second quarter to $3.8B, taking the efficiency ratio to 57% from 60%. Excluding integration costs and significant items, expenses still fell 3%.

$189M

Credit costs stayed contained

The provision for credit losses was $189M. Net charge-offs were $257M, including $35M tied to moving a $1.4B student loan portfolio to held-for-sale; the allowance covers 1.42% of loans.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • FirstBank integration costs were $36M before tax ($27M in expenses and $9M against noninterest income), and Visa-related significant items were a net negative $25M (an $11M gain on Visa shares, less $36M of Visa Class B-3 derivative adjustments). Together they cut net income by $48M, or $0.12 per share; adjusted EPS was $5.09 vs $4.97 reported.

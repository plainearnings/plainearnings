Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.6%

Growth was broad-based

All four segments grew. Interventional led at +7.6% to $1.47B, BioPharma Systems rose 6.5% and Medical Essentials 5.9%; only Connected Care was soft, at +1.6%.

+93%

Operating income nearly doubled

Operating income rose to $1,032M from $536M. Cost of products sold actually fell 2% despite higher sales, and other operating expense dropped from $232M to $76M as last year's legal charges eased.

$2.9B

Cash flow jumped for the full year

Fiscal 2026 free cash flow — cash from continuing operations minus capital spending — rose 42.5% to $2.9B, giving the reshaped company room to pay down debt and buy back shares.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Adjusted EPS of $4.15 excludes $346M of pre-tax purchase-accounting adjustments (mostly amortization from past acquisitions), $42M of integration costs, $35M of restructuring costs and $32M of separation costs tied to the Life Sciences spin-off.
  • Cost of products sold included a $66M benefit from refunds of tariffs imposed under IEEPA, partly offset in other operating expense by $45M of product-liability and other legal charges.
  • This page uses continuing operations. Total net income including discontinued operations was $696M ($2.54 per diluted share).

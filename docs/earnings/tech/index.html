Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+9%

The core business carries growth

Protein Sciences sales rose about 9% to $219.7M with a 37.9% segment operating margin, while Diagnostics and Spatial Biology was flat at $80.2M — all of the quarter's growth came from the core.

$28M

Deal costs cut into profit

Net earnings fell to $28.3M from $38.2M as SG&A jumped 15% — unallocated costs included about $17.8M of acquisition-related expenses for the pending Merck KGaA transaction, plus $3.8M of restructuring costs.

$73M

Cash generation improved

Cash from operations rose to $73.1M from $27.6M a year ago, and the cash balance grew to $350M even after paying a $12.6M dividend.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • About $17.8M of acquisition-related expenses and other costs (pending acquisition by Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany) in unallocated corporate costs, plus $3.8M of restructuring and restructuring-related costs.

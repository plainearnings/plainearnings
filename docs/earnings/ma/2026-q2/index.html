Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Spending held up everywhere

Gross dollar volume rose 8% in local currency, cross-border volume rose 12% and switched transactions rose 9% — the growth was broad, not one market carrying the rest.

+20%

Services are the growth engine

Value-added services net revenue grew 20% to $3.8B — fraud, data and consulting products now bring in over 40% of Mastercard's revenue.

60%

Six dollars in ten became operating profit

The operating margin was 60.2%, up from 58.7% a year ago. Net income rose 19% to $4.4B despite an $82M litigation provision flagged as a special item.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $82M litigation provision — covering ATM non-discrimination surcharge complaints, a change in estimate on claims from merchants who opted out of the U.S. merchant class litigation, and other legal matters — is flagged by Mastercard as a special item. Excluding it and a $2M loss on equity investments, adjusted net income was $4.5B and adjusted EPS was $5.04.

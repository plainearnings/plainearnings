Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+6%

Revenue rose to $1.41B

Revenue rose 6% to $1.41B, driven by sleep devices (+9% to $716M) and masks and other products (+10% to $540M).

$62M

A business sale boosted profit

Other income includes a $62M gain on selling the MatrixCare software business. Without it, operating income of $453M was up just 1% from a year earlier.

$418M

Net income rose 20%

Net income was $418M, up from $349M a year earlier, and the company returned $1.0B to shareholders in the quarter through dividends and buybacks, helped by the MatrixCare sale proceeds.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Other income includes a $62M gain on the divestment of the MatrixCare subsidiary, completed in the quarter.
